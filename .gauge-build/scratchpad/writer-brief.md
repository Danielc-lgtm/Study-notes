## 0. Non-negotiables

1. **Every theorem mentioned is proved.** A `Thm -` page carries a COMPLETE proof in its `# Formal Proof` section, fed by a `# Lemma Decomposition` whose every lemma has a complete nested `Full proof`. No sketches, no "clearly", "obviously", "it is easy to see", "similarly", "analogously", "one checks", "left to the reader", "omitted", "standard", "well known". Every result you *invoke* is wikilinked to a page whose proof is complete (a page in this series, or an existing vault page listed as proof-complete in `vault-index.txt`) and its statement is restated at the point of use. Never link to a page whose name contains "(Statement)". If the only vault page for a result you need is unproved or does not exist, either prove the result inside your page (as a lemma) or tell the orchestrator in your return value that a new page is needed — do not silently cite.
2. **The thesis floor on every proof** (prose-and-proof-standard.md §6): open by naming what is assumed and what must be shown; organise by labelled blocks (Direction 1 / Direction 2, Case 1 / Case 2, Part I / Part II, Step 0, 1, 2, …), each announced with what it establishes; introduce each move with a **bold lead-in** naming it; put a justification on EVERY displayed equality/inequality/implication ("(by Lemma 2)", "(since …)", "(by the Leibniz rule)", "(by the dominated convergence theorem, dominating function $g$)"); invoke every hypothesis by name where it is used; write out well-definedness, existence, and equivalence-relation checks clause by clause (Step 0 for preconditions); prove both directions, all cases, all parts; combine numbered intermediate lines explicitly; close in words ("Therefore …"); type every new symbol; name the contradiction in a proof by contradiction; cite the theorem licensing every interchange of limit/sum/integral/derivative and verify every regularity condition where used.
3. **The single exception — an imported result.** Only a genuinely book-length result (Freedman's classification, Donaldson's diagonalisation theorem's full analytic package, Uhlenbeck compactness, Atiyah–Singer, and essentially nothing else) may be used without proof, and only inside
   ```
   > [!warning] Imported without proof: <name>
   > **Statement.** <exact statement, all hypotheses and conventions>
   >
   > **Source of a complete proof.** <Author, *Title*, edition, section/pages>
   >
   > **Architecture of the proof.** <one paragraph: main steps, where the difficulty lies>
   >
   > **Why it is imported rather than proved here.** <one or two sentences>
   >
   > Everything below that uses this result says so explicitly at the point of use.
   ```
   never in a `> **Theorem.**` blockquote and never as its own `Thm -` page; every consequence drawn from it is proved with the import named as an explicit hypothesis. Report every import in your return value so the topic page can list it under `# Imported Results`.
4. **Prose register = the owner's thesis** (prose-and-proof-standard.md Part I). Measured academic first-person plural ("we now show", "we need to show", "note that"), direct, unornamented, expansive. Shape of every explanation: orient → motivate (name the problem, the alternatives, what goes wrong without the construction) → state formally → unpack immediately in the smallest concrete case (rank one, $U(1)$, the trivial bundle, $n = 2$, a specific matrix) → re-explain the intuition → close in words. Every claim travels with its reason. Standard terminology only, with the field named when useful; no coined compound nouns, no Capitalised Pseudo-Terms; every symbol declared and typed at first use, one meaning per page. Prose over bullets except for genuinely enumerative items. No abbreviations in prose ("with respect to", "if and only if"). No hedge stacking, no inspirational filler ("beautiful", "deep"), no whiteboard chattiness, no labelled fragments ("Trigger: … Action: …") outside the one permitted inline `*Trigger:*` / `*Pattern:*` idiom in Legal Operations. Every geometric object (region, neighbourhood, contour, surface, distribution) comes with an explicit set-builder or parametric formula beside the words; prefer coordinate proofs to picture proofs.
5. **Source fidelity.** Your page is built from the source excerpt you are given (Haydys and/or Bär, with PDF page numbers). Every definition, statement, remark, example, and exercise assigned to your page must be covered; nothing in the assigned source items may be dropped. Where the two sources differ in convention, follow the series conventions in §6 of this brief and say so in the Notation section. Where the source's proof has gaps, fill them from your own knowledge — correctly — and, if you are not certain of a step, mark it inline with `⚠️ [unverified: …]` rather than smoothing over it.
6. **Obsidian mechanics.** `$...$` inline and `$$...$$` display for ALL mathematics — every variable and symbol in prose is in LaTeX, never a bare Unicode math symbol in prose. Never any whitespace immediately after an opening `$` or before a closing `$`; never end inline math with a dangling operator followed by a space. Only core KaTeX commands (no `\fint`, `\abs`, `\norm`, `\bra`, `\ket`, `\dv`, `\pdv`, `\eqref`, no `\newcommand`); write `\operatorname{Ad}`, `\operatorname{tr}`, `\operatorname{End}`, `\mathfrak{g}`, `\lVert \cdot \rVert`. Inside `\text{...}` never re-enter `$...$` without surrounding spaces — prefer the split idiom `\text{ is a } \sigma \text{-algebra}`. Wikilinks: `[[Exact Page Name]]` or `[[Exact Page Name|display text]]`; NO LaTeX, NO markdown, NO nesting inside `[[ ]]` — use Unicode in display text (σ, ℝ, →, ⊗, U(1)); put bold outside: `**[[Def - X|x]]**`. Only wikilink pages that exist in `vault-index.txt` or are in the series manifest you were given; anything else is **bold plain text**. Transclusion: `![[Def - X#The Definition]]`, `![[Thm - Y#Statement]]` (these anchors exist on every Def/Thm page of the series by construction). Collapsible callouts: `> [!note]- Title` (collapsed), body lines prefixed `> `, blank line inside = `>`; nested callout = `> > [!note]- Title`. Never HTML `<details>`; never a wikilink inside HTML. Never wrap a wikilink in square brackets (`[[[`); group link lists in parentheses. Filenames are Windows-portable: no `< > : " / \ | ? *` — spell out (`S^2` is fine, `1/2` is not: write "Half"). Every page begins with YAML frontmatter.
7. **Self-containedness.** A reader landing cold on your page must be able to read it without clicking: the Notation section restates every symbol used; every definition or theorem used is transcluded or restated-with-wikilink at first use; the prerequisite chain resolves through existing pages.
8. **Return value.** Your final message is data for the orchestrator, not prose for a human: list the exact paths written, every wikilink target you used that is NOT in `vault-index.txt` and NOT in the manifest, every result you invoked whose proof you could not locate (with the page you would want created), every `Imported without proof` callout you wrote, and every `⚠️` marker. Nothing else.

## 2. Definition page — exact section order

`# Notation` (restate every symbol; open with the standing-convention preamble if the page depends on a convention; `> [!warning] Convention:` callout where sources diverge; for a compound page add one sentence "This is a compound page: it defines N interlocking notions — … — because …") → `# Axiom Motivation` (4+ paragraphs for any non-trivial definition; desiderata; what it must capture and exclude; **per-clause failure analysis** — for every clause, what breaks if it is dropped, with a concrete counterexample, and what strengthening would exclude; forward-reference to a theorem that would fail otherwise where apt; test: could a reader invent the definition from this alone?) → `# The Definition` (formal, primary form first, then equivalent formulations, each equivalence proved or wikilinked to its proof) → `# Categorical Definition` (or `# Categorical / Structural Definition`; only when a natural universal-property/functorial reading exists — bundles, connections as splittings, associated bundles, principal bundles as torsors, Lie group actions; self-contained) → `# Relate to Other Fields / Compression` (precise connections; a `**True name:**` paragraph giving the operational characterisation when it differs from the official definition, or one sentence saying the definition is already operational) → `# Examples / Corollaries` (instances AND at least one non-instance, each VERIFIED clause by clause on the page, every line justified; corollaries proved or wikilinked; ends with a `**Calibration check.**` paragraph of 2–3 verifications) → `# Unlocked by This` (optional; `> [!tip] Concept *(from Field)*` callouts; forward references to non-existent pages in **bold**, never wikilinked).

## 3. Theorem page — exact section order

`# Notation` → `# Statement` (REQUIRED, exactly this header, immediately after Notation; a blockquote `> **Theorem (name).** …` or `> **Lemma.**`/`> **Proposition.**`/`> **Corollary.**` with hypotheses and conclusion in one block, parts labelled;
