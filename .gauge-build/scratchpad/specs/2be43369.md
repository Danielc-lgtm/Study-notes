## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: B-T3.3.11, B-I3.3.5, B-R3.3.4 (→ inside, Statement: the convention of identifying $H^4_{dR}(M)$ with $\mathbb R$ and treating $p_1$ as a number); prereqs: [Def - de Rham Cohomology, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds, Thm - The Poincaré Lemma on a Star-Shaped Region, Def - Partition of Unity on a Manifold, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity, Def - Orientation of a Smooth Manifold, Thm - Integration is Well-Defined on Oriented Manifolds]; statement: Let $M$ be a closed, connected, oriented $n$-manifold. Then $\int_M:H^n_{dR}(M)\to\mathbb R$, $[\alpha]\mapsto\int_M\alpha$, is a well-defined linear isomorphism; in particular a top form is exact if and only if its integral vanishes; proof: Bär states it; follow Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds*, 2nd ed., Lemma 17.27 (a compactly supported $n$-form on $\mathbb R^n$ with zero integral is $d$ of a compactly supported $(n-1)$-form — induction on $n$ via fibre integration), Thm 17.30–17.31 (the connected-manifold argument: cover by charts, connect any two by a finite chain, use a partition of unity to write $\alpha$ as a sum of chart-supported forms and move mass between adjacent charts); every lemma spelled out: Lemma 1 (Poincaré lemma with compact supports on $\mathbb R^n$), Lemma 2 (two chart-supported forms with equal integrals in overlapping charts differ by an exact compactly supported form), Lemma 3 (chain of charts by connectedness), Lemma 4 (well-definedness of $\int$ on classes by Stokes); spec: the only use in VII is the identification of $p_1$ with a number, but the theorem is reused in XI (`Thm - Finiteness of the Seiberg-Witten Invariant`, lattice argument via $H^2$) and XIII; Bridges: `Thm - Poincare Duality via Hodge Star` (Hodge I) states the general duality with a sketch — this page proves the top-degree case fully; the Hodge-theoretic proof (harmonic $n$-forms are constant multiples of $\mathrm{vol}$) is a Cross-Field Exercise once chapter IX is available.
