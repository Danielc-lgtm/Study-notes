## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: the torus $T^n$, Fourier coefficients, $H_k(T^n)$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$, vector-valued version, the $L^2$ pairing); source items: (the series' construction; feeds A-T5.1.1, A-T5.2.1, A-T5.2.5); prereqs: [Def - Sobolev Space of Sections, Def - Inner Product Space, Def - Cauchy Sequence and Complete Metric Space]; spec: $T^n := \mathbb R^n/2\pi\mathbb Z^n$ with the flat metric; for $u\in C^\infty(T^n;\mathbb C^r)$ the Fourier coefficients $\hat u(\xi) := (2\pi)^{-n}\int_{T^n}u(x)e^{-i\langle\xi,x\rangle}dx$, $\xi\in\mathbb Z^n$; for $k\in\mathbb Z$ the norm $\|u\|_k^2 := \sum_{\xi\in\mathbb Z^n}(1+|\xi|^2)^k|\hat u(\xi)|^2$ and $H_k(T^n;\mathbb C^r)$ := completion of $C^\infty$ in $\|\cdot\|_k$, identified with the weighted sequence space $\{(c_\xi) : \sum(1+|\xi|^2)^k|c_\xi|^2<\infty\}$ (the identification proved: the completion of the rapidly decreasing sequences in the weighted $\ell^2$ norm is the weighted $\ell^2$ space, by truncation); $H_0 = L^2(T^n)$ (Parseval, next page); the $L^2$ pairing $\langle u,v\rangle := (2\pi)^n\sum_\xi\hat u(\xi)\overline{\hat v(\xi)}$ extends to $H_k\times H_{-k}\to\mathbb C$ with $|\langle u,v\rangle|\le(2\pi)^n\|u\|_k\|v\|_{-k}$ (Cauchy–Schwarz on the weighted sequences); elements of $H_k$ for $k<0$ are called distributions of order $-k$ in the Motivation (no distribution theory is used); real-valued subspaces; examples: $e^{i\langle\xi,x\rangle}$ has $\|\cdot\|_k = (1+|\xi|^2)^{k/2}$; the Dirac comb $\sum_\xi e^{i\langle\xi,x\rangle}$ lies in $H_k$ iff $2k<-n$ (with the lattice-sum lemma); non-example: a sequence $(c_\xi)$ with $|c_\xi| = 1$ is in no $H_k$ with $k\ge-n/2$.
