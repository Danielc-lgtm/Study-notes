## Spec (from the manifest)

**[anchor]** — type: definition (compound); source items: A-D3.3.4, A-R3.3.4, A-X3.3.1 (statement; corollary and exercise), B-R2.6.1; prereqs: [Thm - Properties of Parallel Transport, Def - Parallel Transport in a Principal Bundle]; spec: for a principal connection and $p\in P_m$, the holonomy $\operatorname{hol}_p(c)\in G$ of a loop $c$ at $m$ defined by $\Gamma(c)(p)=p\cdot\operatorname{hol}_p(c)$; $\operatorname{Hol}_p(\omega):=\{\operatorname{hol}_p(c)\}$; for a vector-bundle connection, $\operatorname{Hol}_m(\nabla):=\{PT_c\}\subset GL(E_m)$ (A); corollaries proved on the page: $\operatorname{Hol}$ is a subgroup (concatenation and reversal, from the properties theorem); $\operatorname{hol}_{pg}(c)=g^{-1}\operatorname{hol}_p(c)g$ and $\operatorname{Hol}_{m'}=PT_\gamma\operatorname{Hol}_mPT_\gamma^{-1}$ for a path $\gamma$ from $m$ to $m'$ (A-R3.3.4, "standard argument" written); the holonomy of a metric/Hermitian connection lies in $O(k)$/$U(k)$ and of a complex connection in $GL_{k/2}(\mathbb C)$ (A-X3.3.1 as corollary of the properties theorem (6)); the restricted holonomy group (loops null-homotopic) defined for later use. Verified examples: product connection: trivial holonomy; the constant connection on the trivial line bundle over $S^1$: $\operatorname{Hol}=\{e^{-2\pi ika}\}$; the Hopf connection: $\operatorname{Hol}=U(1)$ (from the exercises); non-example: $\operatorname{Hol}$ is not in general a closed subgroup (record as a scope remark — not claimed either way). Convention callout: A drops the base point after fixing a basis; B does not name the holonomy group. Calibration: holonomy of a reversed loop is the inverse; holonomy is unchanged under reparametrisation.
