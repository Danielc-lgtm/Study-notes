**[anchor]** — type: definition (compound); source items: A-D2.2.15, A-R2.2.7, B-D2.4.1, B-D2.4.3, B-D2.4.4, B-R2.4.1, B-R2.4.2; prereqs: [Def - Connection on a Principal Bundle, Def - Horizontal Subspace and Horizontal Lift, Thm - Bundle-Valued Forms on the Base are Basic Equivariant Forms on the Total Space, Def - Adjoint Bundles ad P and Ad P]; spec: Primary definition (B): $\Omega(X,Y):=d\omega(\pi_HX,\pi_HY)$, a $\mathfrak g$-valued 2-form on $P$; secondary description (A): $d\omega+\tfrac12[\omega\wedge\omega]$ — the equality of the two is the structure equation theorem, and the page states it with a forward link; corollaries proved on the page: $\Omega$ is horizontal (by definition) and, granting the structure equation, A-R2.2.7's computation that $d\omega+\tfrac12[\omega\wedge\omega]$ is basic (Cartan's formula, $\mathcal L_{\xi_P}\omega=-[\xi,\omega]$ from equivariance) and equivariant; hence there is a unique $F_\omega\in\Omega^2(M;\operatorname{ad}P)$ with $\pi^*F_\omega=\Omega$ (through the basic-forms theorem), which is B's $\bar\Omega$; local curvature forms $F_\alpha=s_\alpha^*\Omega$; abelian case $F\in\Omega^2(M;\mathfrak g)$. Convention callout: A calls both $\Omega$ ("$\hat F_A$") and $F$ the curvature; B writes $\Omega$, $\Omega_\alpha$, $\bar\Omega$; the series writes $\Omega\in\Omega^2(P;\mathfrak g)$, $F_\omega\in\Omega^2(M;\operatorname{ad}P)$, $F_\alpha$ locally. Verified examples: product connection has $\Omega=0$ (via the Maurer–Cartan equation); the Hopf connection has $\Omega=da\ne0$ (exercise); non-example: $d\omega$ alone is not horizontal for non-abelian $G$. True name: "the vertical part of the bracket of horizontal lifts": $\Omega(\tilde X,\tilde Y)=-\omega([\tilde X,\tilde Y])$ (proved as corollary from the invariant formula for $d$). Calibration: $\Omega$ vanishes if either argument is vertical; $F_\omega$ is a genuine 2-form on $M$ with values in a vector bundle, not in $\mathfrak g$, unless $G$ is abelian.
