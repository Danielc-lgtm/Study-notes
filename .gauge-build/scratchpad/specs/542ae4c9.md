Then $p(F_1)-p(F_0)=d\,Tp(\omega_0,\omega_1)$ with the transgression form $Tp(\omega_0,\omega_1):=d\int_0^1p(b,F_t,\dots,F_t)\,dt\in\Omega^{2d-1}(M)$ (descended from $P$). In particular, on a trivial bundle with $\omega_0$ the product connection and $\omega_1$ given by $A\in\Omega^1(M;\mathfrak g)$: for $p(\xi)=\operatorname{tr}(\xi^2)$ on a matrix group, $\operatorname{tr}(F_A\wedge F_A)=d\operatorname{tr}\big(A\wedge dA+\tfrac23A\wedge A\wedge A\big)$ (A's (97)), and the 3-form $\operatorname{cs}(A):=\operatorname{tr}(A\wedge dA+\tfrac23A^{\wedge3})$ is the Chern–Simons form; proof: differentiate $p(F_t)$ in $t$ using $\tfrac{d}{dt}F_t=d^{\omega_t}b$ (II's shifted-curvature theorem in the principal setting), the invariance identity, and Bianchi, to get $\tfrac{d}{dt}p(F_t)=d\,\big(d\,p(b,F_t,\dots,F_t)\big)$; integrate in $t$; the special case by direct expansion with $F_A=dA+A\wedge A$; reference: Chern–Simons, *Characteristic forms and geometric invariants* (1974) Prop 3.2; Kobayashi–Nomizu II Ch.
