## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (the stabilisation lemma used by the stability theorem here and by the determinant line bundle in X); source items: A-I6.4.1, A-I6.4.2, A-R6.4.2 (the exact sequence (170)); prereqs: [Def - Fredholm Operator and Index, Thm - Closed Range is Automatic for Finite-Dimensional Cokernel, Thm - Banach–Steinhaus and Open Mapping (Application of Baire)]; statement: Let $X,Y$ be Hilbert spaces, $T:X\to Y$ Fredholm, and $V\subset Y$ a finite-dimensional subspace with $\operatorname{Im}T + V = Y$. Define $T_V:X\oplus V\to Y$, $T_V(x,v) := Tx + v$. Then: (i) $T_V$ is surjective and Fredholm with $\dim\ker T_V<\infty$, and there is an exact sequence $0\to\ker T\to\ker T_V\to V\to V/(\operatorname{Im}T\cap V)\to0$ with $\operatorname{coker}T\cong V/(\operatorname{Im}T\cap V)$; consequently $\operatorname{index}T = \dim\ker T_V - \dim V$. (ii) (Openness of surjectivity) If $S:X\oplus V\to Y$ is bounded and surjective with finite-dimensional kernel, then every $S'$ with $\|S'-S\|<\delta(S)$ is surjective with $\dim\ker S' = \dim\ker S$. (iii) (Continuity of the kernel) For a continuous family $p\mapsto S_p\in\operatorname{Hom}(Z,Y)$ ($Z$ Hilbert) of surjective operators with finite-dimensional kernels, near any $p_0$ there is a continuous family of linear isomorphisms $\ker S_{p_0}\to\ker S_p$; hence $\{\ker S_p\}$ is a locally trivial (continuous) vector bundle over the parameter space; proof: (i) exactness by direct verification (the maps: inclusion, $(x,v)\mapsto v$, $v\mapsto[v]$); dimension count; (ii)–(iii): write $Z = \ker S\oplus Z_1$ (orthogonal complement); $S|_{Z_1}:Z_1\to Y$ is a bounded bijection, hence has a bounded inverse $R$ (open mapping); for $\|S'-S\|<\|R\|^{-1}$, $S'|_{Z_1} = S|_{Z_1}(1 + R(S'-S)|_{Z_1})$ is invertible (Neumann series, Lemma 1), so $S'$ is surjective and $\ker S' = \{k - (S'|_{Z_1})^{-1}S'k : k\in\ker S\}$ is the graph of a bounded map $\ker S\to Z_1$ depending continuously on $S'$ — this gives (iii) with the isomorphism $k\mapsto k - (S_p|_{Z_1})^{-1}S_pk$; spec: reference McDuff–Salamon *J-holomorphic Curves* Thm A.2.2 (Haydys's [MS12]) and Lawson–Michelsohn III.§7; mechanism: **adding a finite-dimensional space makes a Fredholm operator surjective, and surjectivity with finite-dimensional kernel is an open condition whose kernel varies as a graph**.
