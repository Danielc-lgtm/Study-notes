## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor); source items: A-D6.2.2, A-R6.2.1 (→ inside, Motivation: the finite-dimensional degree counts preimages of a regular value); prereqs: [Def - Fredholm Map and Its Index, Thm - Proper Maps are Closed and Fredholm Maps are Locally Proper, Def - Regular Value and Transversality for Fredholm Maps, Thm - Regular Value and Transversality Theorems for Fredholm Maps]; spec: let $F:X\to Y$ be a proper Fredholm map of index $0$ between (second countable) Banach manifolds with $Y$ connected; for a regular value $y$, $F^{-1}(y)$ is a compact $0$-manifold (dimension $= \operatorname{index}F = 0$ by the regular-value theorem, compact by properness), hence a finite set; $\deg_2 F := \#F^{-1}(y)\bmod 2 \in\mathbb Z/2$; the **well-definedness (independence of the regular value) and homotopy invariance are proved on `Thm - Well-Definedness and Homotopy Invariance of the Degree` (§10.4)**, which treats the mod-2 and the integer degree together; existence of a regular value is `Thm - Sard-Smale Theorem`; examples: a finite-dimensional proper map $S^n\to S^n$ (Brouwer degree mod 2); $z\mapsto z^k$ on $S^1$ ($\deg_2 = k\bmod 2$); a linear Fredholm isomorphism ($\deg_2 = 1$); non-example: a non-proper map (the count is not locally constant — e.g. $\arctan:\mathbb R\to(-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2)$ has empty fibres over nearby regular values); Convention callout: Haydys writes the mod-2 degree for maps between paracompact Banach manifolds; the series works with second countable (hence, being metrisable, paracompact) manifolds and records the reduction.
