## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: B-T1.4.1, B-T1.4.2, B-D1.4.1, B-X1.4.1, B-I1.4.4, B-I1.4.1, B-R1.4.1; prereqs: [Def - Left-Invariant Vector Field, Def - Exponential Map of a Lie Group, Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of Integral Curves, Thm - Fundamental Theorem on Flows]; statement: Let $\gamma\colon\mathbb R\to G$ be smooth with $\gamma(0)=e$. Then $\gamma$ is a group homomorphism iff $\gamma$ is an integral curve of a left-invariant vector field. Every left-invariant field is complete, its integral curve $\gamma_X$ through $e$ satisfies $\gamma_X(t)=\exp(tX)$, hence $\exp((s+t)X)=\exp(sX)\exp(tX)$, $\exp(0)=e$, $\exp(-X)=\exp(X)^{-1}$; the flow of $X$ is $R_{\exp tX}$; $\exp$ is smooth; proof: "⇒" source full; "⇐" omitted — write it: if $\gamma$ is an integral curve of $X\in\mathfrak g$ then $s\mapsto\gamma(t)\gamma(s)$ and $s\mapsto\gamma(t+s)$ are both integral curves of $X$ through $\gamma(t)$ (left-invariance), so they agree by uniqueness; completeness (X1.4.1): an integral curve on $(-\epsilon,\epsilon)$ extends by left translation to all of $\mathbb R$ (Lemma with the gluing argument); smoothness of $\exp$ from smooth dependence of the flow on the point of $G\times\mathfrak g$ (link DG V `Thm - Fundamental Theorem on Flows`); reference: Lee ISM 2e Prop 20.5, Thm 20.8 (parts a–d); spec: also states as corollary that $\gamma_X$ is the unique one-parameter subgroup with initial velocity $X$ (link `Def - One-Parameter Subgroup`).
