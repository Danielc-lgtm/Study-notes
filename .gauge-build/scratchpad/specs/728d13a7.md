type: theorem; source items: I4.3.1, I4.3.2 (as corollaries), I5.1.4 (chapter XIII's unimodularity input), I5.2.1 (homological definition of $Q_X$); prereqs: [Def - Cap Product, Def - Compactly Supported Cohomology and Direct Limits, Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class, Thm - Mayer-Vietoris Sequence for Singular Homology (Gauge Theory), Thm - Excision Theorem for Singular Homology, Thm - Universal Coefficient Theorem for Cohomology, Def - Local Homology and Local Orientation]; statement: (A) (Hatcher Thm 3.30) Let $M$ be a closed $R$-oriented $n$-manifold with fundamental class $[M]$. Then $D_M : H^k(M;R) \to H_{n-k}(M;R)$, $D_M(\phi) := [M] \frown \phi$, is an isomorphism for all $k$. (B) (Non-compact form, Hatcher Thm 3.35) For an $R$-oriented $n$-manifold $M$ (not necessarily compact), $D_M : H^k_c(M;R) \to H_{n-k}(M;R)$, defined as the direct limit of the maps $H^k(M, M \setminus K) \to H_{n-k}(M)$, $\phi \mapsto \mu_K \frown \phi$, is an isomorphism. (C) (Bär's first imported form, I4.3.1) For $M$ closed connected $R$-oriented and $B$ a chart ball around $x$, $\partial : H_n(M;R) \to H_{n-1}(\dot B;R)$ is an isomorphism — this is `Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class` (d), restated here because Bär attributes it to Poincaré duality. (D) (Bär's second imported form, I4.3.2) is `Thm - Bounding Classes Vanish` (i), likewise restated. (E) For $M$ closed connected $R$-oriented, $H^n(M;R) \cong R$ and $H_0(M;R) \cong R$, and the Kronecker pairing $H^n(M;R) \times H_n(M;R) \to R$ is perfect; proof: source: none (imported); the page follows Hatcher Thm 3.35 with (A) as the compact case: (Lemma 1) $D_U$ is an isomorphism for $U \cong \mathbb{R}^n$ (both sides $R$ in degree $n$ resp. $0$, and the cap product of the local generator with the dual generator is the point class: explicit check); (Lemma 2) if $M = U \cup V$ with $U$, $V$ open and $D_U$, $D_V$, $D_{U \cap V}$ isomorphisms then $D_M$ is an isomorphism (the Mayer–Vietoris sequence for $H^*_c$ — derived on the page from the relative MV sequence and the direct limit's exactness — mapped by $D$ to the Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology, commutativity up to sign of every square proved (Hatcher's computation with the naturality of cap products and the sign $(-1)^k$ in the connecting square), and the five lemma (proved on `Thm - De Rham Theorem via Smooth Singular Chains` as a lemma; restated here)); (Lemma 3) if $M = \bigcup_i U_i$ is an increasing union of open sets with all $D_{U_i}$ isomorphisms then $D_M$ is an isomorphism (compactness of singular chains: every class in $H_*(M)$ and every class in $H^*_c(M)$ lives on some $U_i$; the direct limit argument); (Lemma 4) every open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ is handled by Lemmas 1–3 (write $U$ as a countable increasing union of finite unions of convex open sets, induct on the number of convex sets with Lemma 2 — intersections of convex sets are convex, hence homeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^n$ or empty); (Lemma 5) general $M$: a Zorn's lemma argument on open subsets where $D$ is an isomorphism, or for second countable $M$ (all manifolds here) a countable chart cover and Lemmas 2–4. (E) from (A) with $k = n$ and `Thm - Universal Coefficient Theorem for Cohomology` (b)/(a) — precisely: $H^n(M;R) \cong H_0(M;R) \cong R$ by (A).
