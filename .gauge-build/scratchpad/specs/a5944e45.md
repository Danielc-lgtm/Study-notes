## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: B-T1.5.3, B-I1.5.1, B-E1.5.7 (application), A-I2.2.2 (partial), B-I2.2.2, B-I2.2.3; prereqs: [Def - Free, Transitive, Effective, and Proper Group Actions, Def - Fundamental Vector Field of a Group Action, Thm - Regular Value Theorem on Manifolds, Def - Equivariant Map, Thm - Universal Property of the Quotient]; statement: Let $G$ act smoothly, freely and properly on $M$ (either side). Then the orbit space carries a unique smooth manifold structure of dimension $\dim M-\dim G$ such that the quotient map $\pi$ is a smooth submersion; the quotient is Hausdorff and second countable; $\pi$ admits smooth local sections; and for every smooth $f\colon M\to N$ constant on orbits there is a unique smooth $\tilde f$ with $\tilde f\circ\pi=f$. In particular this holds when $G$ is compact and the action free; proof: source "idea of proof" only. Follow Lee ISM 2e Thm 21.10 in full: Step 0 (Hausdorff and second countable: Prop 21.5 / Lemma 21.11 — properness gives closed orbit relation and quotient Hausdorffness; open quotient map preserves second countability); Step 1 (slice construction: for $p\in M$ the orbit is an embedded submanifold and there is a slice $S$ through $p$ transverse to it, via the equivariant rank theorem — DG XI `Def - Equivariant Map` records it; write the proof — and the regular value theorem); Step 2 (the restricted action $G\times S\to M$ is a diffeomorphism onto an open set after shrinking; injectivity uses properness and freeness); Step 3 (the slices give charts of the quotient; transition maps smooth); Step 4 (uniqueness of the structure and the universal property from the local sections); reference: Lee ISM 2e Thm 21.10, Lemma 21.11, Thm 21.13; spec: Sources: compact group + free; discrete group + properly discontinuous (next theorem); Targets: $\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$, $G/H$ (link DG XI `Thm - Homogeneous Space is a Smooth Manifold` — this theorem is its generalisation), associated bundles (III), principal bundles from free actions (III). Convention callout: A calls the free action on $P\times V$ "properly discontinuous"; the correct hypothesis is free and proper.
