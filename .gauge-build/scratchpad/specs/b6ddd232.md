Sobolev norm $\|u\|_{W^{k,p}} := (\sum_{i\le k}\|\nabla^iu\|_{L^p}^p)^{1/p}$ where $\nabla^iu$ is the $i$-th iterated derivative (on $\mathbb R^n$: the tensor of all $i$-th partials; on a Riemannian $M$ with bundle $E$: $\nabla^iu\in\Gamma((T^*M)^{\otimes i}\otimes E)$ using $\nabla^E$ and $\nabla^M$ on $T^*M$ — `[NEEDED FROM I–VI: induced connections]`); $C^r(M;E)$ with $\|u\|_{C^r} := \sum_{i\le r}\sup|\nabla^iu|$; notation remark (Haydys's Remark 135 on the inconsistency of $L^s_r$ notations); dependence on choices (R5.1.3) stated here and proved on the next page; **Convention callout (group decision 2):** the series proves everything for $p = 2$; Haydys's $W^{k,p}$ for $p>1$ is recorded; the vault's `Def - Lp Spaces` (Lebesgue) is context: the identification of the completion with the Lebesgue space is stated with the reference (Riesz–Fischer plus density of $C_0^\infty$, e.g. Folland *Real Analysis* Thm 6.6 and Prop 8.17) and marked "not used in this series"; examples: on $S^1$, $u\in W^{1,2}$ iff $\sum(1+n^2)|\hat u(n)|^2<\infty$ (proved in §9.1 below — forward link); non-example: the indicator of an interval is in $L^2(S^1)$ but not in $W^{1,2}(S^1)$ (its Fourier coefficients decay like $1/n$;
