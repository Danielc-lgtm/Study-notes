## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor; compound: the equations, the Seiberg–Witten map, the configuration space); source items: A-D7.1.2 (equation (188)); prereqs: [Def - The Quadratic Map on Positive Spinors, Def - Spin-c Dirac Operator Twisted by a Connection, Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Connections, Def - Curvature of a Principal Connection, Def - The Yang-Mills Field Strength]; spec: fix a closed oriented Riemannian 4-manifold $M$ and a spin$^c$ structure with determinant line $L_{\det}$; the *configuration space* is $\mathcal C := \Gamma(\slashed S^+)\times\mathcal A(P_{\det})$, where $\mathcal A(P_{\det})$ is the (affine, over $\Omega^1(M;i\mathbb R)$) space of unitary connections on $L_{\det}$ (equivalently on $P_{\det}$; `Thm - The Space of Connections is an Affine Space`); the *Seiberg–Witten equations* for $(\psi,A)\in\mathcal C$ are $\slashed D_A^+\psi = 0$ and $F_A^+ = \mu(\psi)$ (with $F_A^+\in\Omega^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)$ the self-dual part of the curvature of $A$, `Def - The Yang-Mills Field Strength` in the abelian case, and $\mu(\psi)$ read as an imaginary self-dual 2-form via (187)); the *Seiberg–Witten map* $SW:\mathcal C\to\Gamma(\slashed S^-)\times\Omega^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)$, $SW(\psi,A) := (\slashed D_A^+\psi, F_A^+ - \mu(\psi))$, so solutions are $SW^{-1}(0)$; Axiom Motivation: the equations are the abelian analogue of the anti-self-duality equations coupled to a spinor; the first is linear in $\psi$ and the second couples curvature to the spinor bilinear, and their solutions are the absolute minima of a Weitzenböck energy (§11.3); Convention callout: the factor $\tfrac12$ in $\slashed D_A$'s dependence on $A$ (`Thm - Variation of the Twisted and Spin-c Dirac Operators with the Connection`) and the gauge factor $2$ in $A\cdot g = A + 2g^{-1}dg$ both come from $L_{\det}$ being the square of the spinor line (R4.3.5); examples: a Kähler surface with its canonical spin$^c$ structure has explicit reducible and irreducible solutions (bold context, Taubes/Witten); the trivial solution $(0,A)$ with $F_A^+ = 0$ (reducible); non-example: $\slashed D_A^+\psi = 0$ alone (no curvature constraint) is underdetermined.
