type: exercise; difficulty: ⭐⭐; source items: (additional; the abelian case of T3.3.12 — the exact statement Haydys uses in Corollary 199, A-T7.1.11 and Appendix B item 17); prereqs: [Thm - Energy Identity and the Topological Bound for Yang-Mills, Thm - First Chern Class of a Line Bundle from Curvature, Thm - Self-Dual Decomposition of 2-Forms in Four Dimensions]; spec: for a unitary connection $A$ on a Hermitian line bundle $L$ over a closed oriented Riemannian 4-manifold, with $F_A\in\Omega^2(M;i\mathbb R)$ and $c_1(L) = [\tfrac{i}{2\pi}F_A]$: prove the energy identity with the signs tracked for imaginary-valued forms: writing $F_A = iG$ with $G$ real, $F_A\wedge F_A = -G\wedge G$ while $|F_A|^2 = |G|^2$, so $c_1(L)^2 := \int_M\big(\tfrac{i}{2\pi}F_A\big)\wedge\big(\tfrac{i}{2\pi}F_A\big) = -\frac{1}{4\pi^2}\int F_A\wedge F_A = \frac{1}{4\pi^2}\int G\wedge G = \frac{1}{4\pi^2}(\|G^+\|^2-\|G^-\|^2) = \frac{1}{4\pi^2}(\|F_A^+\|^2-\|F_A^-\|^2)$, so $\|F_A^-\|^2 = \|F_A^+\|^2 - 4\pi^2c_1(L)^2$; this fixes the sign convention flagged in A-T7.1.11 (source typo: Haydys p. 65 writes the identity with a sign requiring $\int F^+\wedge F^+ = -\|F^+\|^2$; with $F$ imaginary-valued and the norm taken of the real form this is consistent — the exercise settles it explicitly and chapter XI cites this page); ⭐⭐.
