Then the homomorphism $\Phi : \pi_1(U,x) * \pi_1(V,x) \to \pi_1(X,x)$ induced by the inclusions is surjective, and its kernel is the normal subgroup generated by the elements $i_{U\sharp}(\gamma)\, i_{V\sharp}(\gamma)^{-1}$, $\gamma \in \pi_1(U \cap V, x)$.
