type: theorem; source items: (foundation; feeds A-T5.1.1, A-T5.2.1, A-T5.2.5); prereqs: [Thm - Sobolev Norms on the Torus via Fourier Coefficients, Thm - Sobolev Norms are Independent of the Metric and Connections up to Equivalence, Def - Partition of Unity on a Manifold, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity, Def - Local Trivialization, Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact $n$-manifold and $E\to M$ a vector bundle of rank $r$. Fix a finite atlas of charts $\kappa_i:U_i\to\kappa_i(U_i)\subset(-\pi,\pi)^n\subset T^n$ over which $E$ is trivialised, and a subordinate partition of unity $(\phi_i)$. For $k\in\mathbb Z$ and $u\in\Gamma(E)$ set $\|u\|_{k}^2 := \sum_i\|(\phi_iu)\circ\kappa_i^{-1}\|_{H_k(T^n;\mathbb C^r)}^2$ (extended by zero). Then: (i) for $k\ge0$ this norm is equivalent to the connection norm $\|\cdot\|_{W^{k,2}}$ of `Def - Sobolev Space of Sections`; (ii) for every $k\in\mathbb Z$ the norms obtained from two such choices of data are equivalent; $H_k(M;E)$ := completion, a Hilbertable space, $H_k = W^{k,2}$ for $k\ge0$; (iii) $\Gamma(E)$ is dense in $H_k(M;E)$; $H_k\subset H_m$ continuously for $k\ge m$; (iv) the $L^2$ pairing $\Gamma(E)\times\Gamma(E)\to\mathbb R$ extends to a perfect pairing $H_k(M;E)\times H_{-k}(M;E)\to\mathbb R$ identifying $H_{-k}$ with the dual of $H_k$; (v) multiplication by a smooth function, and more generally a smooth bundle map $E\to F$, is bounded $H_k(M;E)\to H_k(M;F)$ for every $k\in\mathbb Z$; (vi) (interpolation) as on the torus; proof: Lawson–Michelsohn III.§5 (the paragraph after Def 5.1) and Wells IV §1; Lemma 1 (for $k\ge0$, multiplication by a smooth compactly supported function is bounded on $H_k(T^n)$ — Leibniz and (i) of the torus page; for $k<0$ by duality (iv)); Lemma 2 (for a diffeomorphism $\psi$ between open subsets of $T^n$ and $u$ supported in the domain, $\|u\circ\psi\|_k\le C\|u\|_k$: $k\ge0$ by the chain rule and induction; $k<0$ by duality with the transposed map $v\mapsto|\det D\psi^{-1}|\,v\circ\psi^{-1}$); Lemma 3 (change of partition of unity/atlas: combine Lemmas 1–2); (i) by comparing $\nabla^i(\phi_iu)$ with coordinate derivatives (the connection differs from $d$ by a smooth zero-order term; the previous equivalence theorem); (iv) from the torus duality and the partition of unity (the writer must show the two pairings agree and that $\sum_i\phi_i^2$-type weights do not matter — LM's argument); spec: this page is the bridge from the torus to manifolds; every later "on a compact manifold" statement reduces to the torus through it; mechanism: **a compact manifold is finitely many pieces of a torus glued by smooth maps, and Sobolev norms only see smooth maps as bounded operators**.
