## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-D3.3.6 (the mechanism $\pi^*\nabla s=d\hat s$), B-T2.5.6 (product connection), the classical "flat = locally product" (needed for A-I3.3.3); prereqs: [Def - Flat Connection, Thm - The Frobenius Theorem, Thm - Sections of a Principal Bundle and Triviality, Thm - Transformation of Local Connection and Curvature Forms, Def - Transition Functions and the Cocycle Condition]; statement: For a connection $\omega$ on $P\to M$ the following are equivalent: (a) $\omega$ is flat; (b) the horizontal distribution is integrable; (c) every point of $M$ has a neighbourhood $U$ with a local section $s$ such that $s^*\omega=0$ (a flat local gauge), equivalently $\omega|_{\pi^{-1}(U)}$ is the product connection in the trivialisation defined by $s$; (d) there is a trivialising atlas whose transition functions $g_{\alpha\beta}$ are locally constant; and then parallel transport along any curve inside a flat chart depends only on its endpoints; proof: (a)⇔(b) via $\Omega(\tilde X,\tilde Y)=-\omega([\tilde X,\tilde Y])$ and Frobenius (DG X, restated); (b)⇒(c): the integral manifold of $H$ through $p$ is transverse to the fibre and $\pi$ restricts to a local diffeomorphism onto a neighbourhood $U$ (inverse function theorem via DG IV's rank theorem), whose inverse is a horizontal section; (c)⇒(d): two flat gauges differ by $g$ with $g^*\theta=0$, hence $dg=0$ (matrix case: $g^{-1}dg=0$; general: $\theta_g\circ dg=0$ and $\theta_g$ is an isomorphism), so $g$ is locally constant; (d)⇒(a): $F_\alpha=0$ in each chart; the parallel-transport clause from (c); reference: Kobayashi–Nomizu I Ch. II §9 (Thm 9.1), Taubes *Differential Geometry* Ch. 13; spec: Mechanism: "zero curvature makes the horizontal planes tangent to a foliation whose leaves are local horizontal sections." Targets: monodromy; flat bundles as bundles with discrete structure group (bold).
