type: theorem (four parts); source items: A-X5.3.3 (Exercise 160 — its content), A-D5.3.6 (the de Rham complex with $d^*$, the Hodge–de Rham Laplacian, Betti numbers; the $d^*$ formula is `Thm - The Hodge-de Rham Operator is a Dirac Operator`), A-I5.3.2, A-R5.3.5; prereqs: [Thm - Hodge Theorem for Elliptic Complexes, Thm - The Hodge-de Rham Operator is a Dirac Operator, Def - de Rham Cohomology, Thm - Homotopy Invariance of de Rham Cohomology, Def - Hodge Laplacian, Def - Harmonic Form]; statement: Let $M$ be a closed oriented Riemannian $n$-manifold. (i) The de Rham complex $0\to\Omega^0\xrightarrow{d}\Omega^1\to\cdots\to\Omega^n\to0$ is elliptic: its symbol sequence $0\to\Lambda^0\xrightarrow{\xi\wedge}\Lambda^1\xrightarrow{\xi\wedge}\cdots\to\Lambda^n\to0$ is exact for $\xi\ne0$. (ii) Its Laplacian is the Hodge Laplacian $\Delta = dd^*+d^*d$ with $d^* = (-1)^{n(j-1)+1}\star d\star$ on $j$-forms. (iii) $\mathcal H^j(M) := \ker\Delta_j$ is finite-dimensional, $\mathcal H^j\cong H^j_{dR}(M)$, and $\Omega^j = \mathcal H^j\oplus d\Omega^{j-1}\oplus d^*\Omega^{j+1}$ orthogonally. (iv) The Betti numbers $b_j(M) := \dim\mathcal H^j(M) = \dim H^j_{dR}(M)$ are independent of the metric and invariant under diffeomorphism and smooth homotopy equivalence; proof: (i) the Koszul-complex lemma: for $\xi\ne0$ choose $e_1 = \xi/|\xi|$; the operator $\iota_{e_1}$ is a contracting homotopy: $\xi\wedge\iota_{e_1} + \iota_{e_1}(\xi\wedge\cdot) = |\xi|\,\mathrm{id}$ (proved from the derivation property of $\iota$), so the sequence is exact; (ii) from the Dirac-operator page ($D = d+d^*$, $D^2 = dd^*+d^*d$ since $d^2 = 0 = (d^*)^2$); (iii) the Hodge theorem; (iv) metric independence since $H^j_{dR}$ does not involve the metric; diffeomorphism invariance of $H^j_{dR}$ by pullback; homotopy invariance by `Thm - Homotopy Invariance of de Rham Cohomology` (DG X); **the identification with singular Betti numbers (topological invariance in the strong sense) is the de Rham theorem, whose vault page is flagged: stated as bold context, not invoked**; spec: this page proves in full what the vault's `Thm - Harmonic Forms Represent de Rham Cohomology` and `Thm - Hodge Decomposition Theorem` state; mechanism: **the wedge-with-$\xi$ complex is exact because contraction with $\xi$ is a chain homotopy to a multiple of the identity**; Bridges: $b_2^\pm$ and the intersection form (XIII), $\ker(d^++d^*)$ in XI.
