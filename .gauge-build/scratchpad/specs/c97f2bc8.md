## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: I5.1.4 (the precise form needed for T5.1.10), I5.2.1; prereqs: [Thm - Poincare Duality, Thm - Universal Coefficient Theorem for Cohomology, Def - Cup Product, Def - Cap Product, Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated]; statement: Let $M$ be a closed connected $R$-oriented $n$-manifold. (i) For $R$ a field, the pairing $H^k(M;R) \times H^{n-k}(M;R) \to R$, $(\alpha, \beta) \mapsto \langle \alpha \smile \beta, [M] \rangle$, is non-degenerate. (ii) For $R = \mathbb{Z}$ and $M$ with finitely generated homology (e.g. compact smooth), the pairing $H^k(M;\mathbb{Z})/\text{torsion} \times H^{n-k}(M;\mathbb{Z})/\text{torsion} \to \mathbb{Z}$ is unimodular: it induces an isomorphism $H^k/\text{torsion} \to \operatorname{Hom}(H^{n-k}/\text{torsion}, \mathbb{Z})$. (iii) In particular for a closed oriented $4$-manifold with $H_1(M;\mathbb{Z}) = 0$: $H^2(M;\mathbb{Z})$ is free, $H_2(M;\mathbb{Z}) \cong H^2(M;\mathbb{Z})$ is free, and the form $Q^{\smile}(\alpha, \beta) := \langle \alpha \smile \beta, [M] \rangle$ on $H^2(M;\mathbb{Z})$ is symmetric and unimodular, i.e. has determinant $\pm 1$ in any basis; proof: source: none; the page follows Hatcher Prop. 3.38: precisely: $\langle \alpha \smile \beta, [M]\rangle = \langle \beta, [M] \frown \alpha \rangle$ (the cap–cup identity from `Def - Cap Product`), so the pairing is the composite of $D_M$ (an isomorphism) with the evaluation $H_{n-k}(M) \to \operatorname{Hom}(H^{n-k}(M), R)$ which is an isomorphism for a field, and an isomorphism modulo torsion for $\mathbb{Z}$ by the universal coefficient theorem (the map $H_{n-k}/T \to \operatorname{Hom}(H^{n-k}/T, \mathbb{Z})$ is an isomorphism when homology is finitely generated: proved via UCT (c)); symmetry in (iii) from graded commutativity with $k = l = 2$ (`Thm - Properties of the Cup Product`); the determinant statement: a bilinear form on $\mathbb{Z}^r$ inducing an isomorphism onto the dual has matrix in $GL_r(\mathbb{Z})$, hence determinant $\pm 1$ (proved). Reference: Hatcher Prop. 3.38 and Cor. 3.39, p. 250. spec: Targets: `Thm - Intersection Form is Unimodular` (chapter XIII); Betti number symmetry exercise.
