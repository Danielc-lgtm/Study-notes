## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (two parts); source items: A-X6.4.1 (Exercise 171); prereqs: [Def - Alternating Multilinear Form, Def - Tensor Product of Vector Spaces, Thm - Fundamental Theorem of Linear Maps, Def - Quotient Space]; statement: For finite-dimensional real vector spaces write $\det V := \Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}V$ (top exterior power, a line). (i) A short exact sequence $0\to U_0\to U_1\to U_2\to0$ induces a canonical isomorphism $\det U_1\cong\det U_0\otimes\det U_2$. (ii) Any exact sequence $0\to U_0\to U_1\to\cdots\to U_k\to0$ induces a canonical isomorphism $\bigotimes_{j\text{ even}}\det U_j\cong\bigotimes_{j\text{ odd}}\det U_j$; proof: (i) choose a splitting $U_1\cong U_0\oplus U_2$ and use $\det(A\oplus B)\cong\det A\otimes\det B$ (from a basis, with the sign of the shuffle absorbed into the canonical identification — the writer checks independence of the splitting: two splittings differ by a map $U_2\to U_0$, which acts trivially on $\det$ because it is unipotent, $\det = 1$); (ii) split the long exact sequence into short exact sequences $0\to Z_j\to U_j\to Z_{j+1}\to0$ with $Z_j := \operatorname{im}(U_{j-1}\to U_j) = \ker(U_j\to U_{j+1})$ and multiply the isomorphisms of (i), the interior $\det Z_j$ cancelling in pairs; spec: mechanism: **the top exterior power is multiplicative on exact sequences because a splitting adds dimensions and a unipotent change of splitting has determinant one**; targets: the determinant line of a Fredholm operator (next page); the orientation of the Seiberg–Witten moduli space (XI).
