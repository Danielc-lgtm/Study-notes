## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (Hatcher Props 3.10, 3.11, Thm 3.14); prereqs: [Def - Cup Product, Thm - Small Chains Theorem via Barycentric Subdivision, Def - Chain Complex and Its Homology]; statement: On $H^*(X;R)$, $R$ commutative: the cup product is associative, has the unit $1 \in H^0(X;R)$ (the class of the augmentation cocycle), is natural ($f^*(\alpha \smile \beta) = f^*\alpha \smile f^*\beta$), and is graded commutative: $\alpha \smile \beta = (-1)^{kl} \beta \smile \alpha$ for $\alpha \in H^k$, $\beta \in H^l$; proof: associativity, unit and naturality at cochain level (verified); graded commutativity by Hatcher Thm 3.14: the chain map $\rho : C_n(X) \to C_n(X)$, $\rho(\sigma) = \varepsilon_n \, \sigma \circ \bar\rho_n$ (reversal of vertex order, $\varepsilon_n = (-1)^{n(n+1)/2}$), is chain homotopic to the identity (the explicit prism-like chain homotopy $P(\sigma) = \sum_i (-1)^i \varepsilon_{n-i} \, (\sigma \circ \pi)|_{[v_0, \dots, v_i, w_n, \dots, w_i]}$ with the verification of $\partial P + P \partial = \rho - \mathrm{id}$ written out), and $\rho^*(\phi \smile \psi) = (-1)^{kl} \rho^*\psi \smile \rho^*\phi$ at cochain level. Reference: Hatcher pp. 210–212. spec: Targets: symmetry of the cup-product intersection form in dimension $4$ ($k = l = 2$, sign $+1$); Poincaré duality's cap-product identities.
