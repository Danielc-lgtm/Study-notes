## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor); source items: A-T5.1.1 (i), (iii), A-I5.1.1, A-R5.1.4 (→ inside, Motivation / Why Is It True: Haydys's $S^1$ argument with the mean value theorem and Cauchy–Schwarz written out, as the one-dimensional model), A-I7.1.2 (the instances $W^{5,2}\subset C^2$ and $W^{5,2}\subset C^0$ in dimension four, recorded as Corollary 1); prereqs: [Thm - Sobolev Spaces of Sections on a Compact Manifold via Charts, Thm - Fourier Series of Smooth Functions on the Torus, Thm - Convergence of the Lattice Sum]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact $n$-manifold, $E\to M$ a vector bundle. (i) For integers $k\ge m$, $H_k(M;E)\subset H_m(M;E)$ with $\|u\|_m\le C\|u\|_k$. (ii) For integers $k, r\ge0$ with $k - \frac n2>r$ there is a continuous injection $H_k(M;E)\hookrightarrow C^r(M;E)$: every element of $H_k$ is (represented by) a $C^r$ section and $\|u\|_{C^r}\le C\|u\|_{H_k}$. (iii) $\bigcap_{k\ge0}H_k(M;E) = C^\infty(M;E)$: if $u\in H_k$ for all $k$ then $u$ is smooth. **Convention callout:** Haydys's Theorem 136 (i),(iii) for $W^{k,p}$, $p>1$: $W^{k,p}\subset W^{m,q}$ continuously when $k-\frac np\ge m-\frac nq$, $k\ge m$, and $W^{k,p}\subset C^r$ when $k-\frac np>r$ — recorded verbatim as unproved context with references (Evans 5.6.1–5.6.3 and 5.6.5; Adams–Fournier Thm 4.12); proof: (i) is the torus statement plus charts; (ii) on the torus: for $u\in C^\infty$ and $|\alpha|\le r$, $\sup|\partial^\alpha u|\le\sum_\xi|\xi|^{|\alpha|}|\hat u(\xi)|\le\big(\sum_\xi(1+|\xi|^2)^{r-k}\big)^{1/2}\big(\sum_\xi(1+|\xi|^2)^k|\hat u(\xi)|^2\big)^{1/2} = C\|u\|_k$ (Cauchy–Schwarz; the lattice sum converges since $2(k-r)>n$); the estimate on smooth sections and completeness of $C^r$ (Lemma 1: $C^r(T^n)$ with the $C^r$ norm is complete — uniform limits of derivatives) give the embedding of the completion; injectivity: the $C^r$ representative determines the $H_k$ element because the map $C^r\to L^2\to H_0$ is injective on continuous functions (Parseval/Fejér); patch to $M$ via the charts page; (iii) from (ii) for every $r$; spec: mechanism: **$k$ derivatives in $L^2$ control $r$ derivatives uniformly exactly when the leftover weight $(1+|\xi|^2)^{r-k}$ is summable, which is dimension counting**; targets: $C^0$ bound in XI, smoothness of kernels of elliptic operators.
