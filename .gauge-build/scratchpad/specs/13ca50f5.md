type: theorem; source items: B-T2.2.2, B-I2.2.2, B-I2.2.3, A-R2.2.3 (the manifold structure on $(P\times V)/G$), A-I2.2.2; prereqs: [Def - Principal G-Bundle, Thm - Quotient Manifold Theorem for Free Proper Actions]; statement: If a Lie group $G$ acts smoothly, freely and properly on the right on a manifold $P$ (in particular if $G$ is compact and the action free), then $(P,\pi,P/G)$ with $\pi(p)=pG$ is a principal $G$-bundle. Moreover, if $P$ is a principal $G$-bundle and $G$ acts on a manifold $F$ on the left, then the diagonal action $(p,f)\cdot g=(pg,g^{-1}f)$ on $P\times F$ is free and proper and $P\times_GF:=(P\times F)/G\to M$ is a fibre bundle with fibre $F$ (the associated fibre bundle; the vector and principal cases are §3.4); proof: source sketch; write: the quotient manifold theorem gives the manifold structure, submersion, and local sections $s$; define $\psi_U^{-1}(x,g):=s(x)g$ and prove it is an equivariant diffeomorphism (injective by freeness, surjective by transitivity on fibres, smooth inverse via the slice charts); for the second part prove properness of the diagonal action from properness of the $G$-action on $P$ (a sequence argument) and construct the local trivialisations from local sections of $P$; reference: Lee ISM 2e Thm 21.10 + Kobayashi–Nomizu I Prop I.5.? ; spec: Convention callout: A calls the $P\times V$ action "properly discontinuous"; correct hypothesis is free and proper (properly discontinuous is the discrete case).
