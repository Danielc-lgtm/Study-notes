**[anchor]** — type: theorem; source items: A-T2.1.2, A-R2.3.1 (Target); prereqs: [Def - Bundle-Valued Differential Forms, Thm - Existence of Smooth Bump Functions, Def - Section of a Vector Bundle]; statement: Let $A\colon\Gamma(E)\to\Omega^p(M;F)$ be $\mathbb R$-linear and $C^\infty(M)$-linear, $A(fs)=fA(s)$.
