**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: none in A/B §1–3 (conventions.md: $p_1(E)=-c_2(E\otimes\mathbb C)$; required by the anchors for VII/XIII); prereqs: [Def - Chern Classes, Def - Constructions on Representations, Def - Euclidean Vector Bundle and Metric Connection, Def - Euler Class of an Oriented Vector Bundle]; spec: for a real vector bundle $E$ of rank $n$, $p_k(E):=(-1)^kc_{2k}(E\otimes\mathbb C)\in H^{4k}_{dR}(M)$; corollaries proved on the page: $c_j(E\otimes\mathbb C)=0$ for odd $j$ (the complexification is isomorphic to its conjugate, and $c_j(\bar E)=(-1)^jc_j(E)$ from the dual-bundle exercise, so $2c_j=0$ in real cohomology); the Chern–Weil formula: for a metric connection with skew curvature $F$, $\det(1-\tfrac{F}{2\pi})=1+p_1(F)+p_2(F)+\dots$ (the odd terms vanish pointwise as forms because $\operatorname{tr}$ of odd powers of skew matrices vanish — proved), so $p_1(E)=[-\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)]$; naturality and Whitney formula modulo 2-torsion (the de Rham version is exact: $p(E_1\oplus E_2)=p(E_1)\smile p(E_2)$, proved by block-diagonal determinants); $e(E)^2=p_m(E)$ for oriented rank $2m$ (via $\operatorname{Pf}^2=\det$); for an $SU(2)$-bundle $P$ and the underlying real rank-4 bundle $E$ of $P\times_{SU(2)}\mathbb C^2$: $E\otimes\mathbb C\cong\mathbb C^2\oplus\overline{\mathbb C^2}\cong2\cdot\mathbb C^2$ as $SU(2)$-representations (the quaternionic structure gives $\overline{\mathbb C^2}\cong\mathbb C^2$), so $c(E\otimes\mathbb C)=c(P)^2=(1+c_2(P))^2$ and $p_1(E)=-c_2(E\otimes\mathbb C)=-2c_2(P)$ — recorded as a verified example with every step proved. Convention callout: the sign $(-1)^k$ makes $p_1$ of the tangent bundle of $\mathbb{CP}^2$ positive (stated as a scope remark; not computed here). Non-example: $p_k$ of a bundle with a flat connection vanishes. Calibration: $p_k(E)=0$ for $4k>\dim M$; $p_1$ of a trivial bundle is $0$. Reference: Milnor–Stasheff §15, Kobayashi–Nomizu II Ch. XII §4.
