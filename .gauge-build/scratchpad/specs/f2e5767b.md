## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor; three parts, with the wall-crossing caveat); source items: A-T7.2.1 (the invariance half), A-R7.2.1, A-R7.2.2; prereqs: [Def - Seiberg-Witten Invariant, Thm - The Perturbed Moduli Space is a Compact Oriented Manifold, Thm - Orientation and Bordism Invariance of the Equivariant Moduli Space, Def - Cobordism Class and Characteristic Numbers of a Parametric Moduli Space]; statement: Assume $b_2^+(M)\ge2$. (i) $\mathrm{sw}(\sigma)$ is independent of the generic perturbation $\eta$ and of the Riemannian metric $g$ (through the space of pairs $(g,\eta)$, which is connected and whose good locus avoids reducibles for $b_2^+\ge2$). (ii) $\mathrm{sw}$ depends only on the diffeomorphism type of $(M,\text{orientation})$ and the spin$^c$ structure: an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism $\phi:M\to M'$ pulls back spin$^c$ structures and $\mathrm{sw}_{M'}(\sigma) = \pm\mathrm{sw}_M(\phi^*\sigma)$ (the sign from the orientation of $H^0\oplus H^1\oplus H^2_+$). (iii) (**Wall-crossing / $b_2^+$ caveat**) for $b_2^+ = 1$ the space of good pairs $(g,\eta)$ is disconnected by the reducible wall (codimension $1$), and $\mathrm{sw}$ jumps by a computable wall-crossing term across the wall — so $\mathrm{sw}$ is only a *chamber-wise* invariant; for $b_2^+ = 0$ it is not defined; proof: (i),(ii) $\mathcal M_{\eta_0}$ and $\mathcal M_{\eta_1}$ (and for two metrics $g_0,g_1$ the corresponding moduli spaces) are oriented-bordant through a family avoiding reducibles ($b_2^+\ge2$, path-transversality; `Thm - The Perturbed Moduli Space ...`, `Thm - Orientation and Bordism Invariance ...`), and the characteristic number $\langle\hat\mu^{d/2}, [\mathcal M]\rangle$ is a bordism invariant (`Def - Cobordism Class and Characteristic Numbers ...`, Stokes; the framing bundle extends over the bordism); (iii) the wall-crossing statement is recorded with the codimension-$b_2^+$ computation of `Thm - Generic Absence of Reducible Solutions` (for $b_2^+ = 1$ the wall is a hypersurface; the jump term is the Euler number of an obstruction bundle over the reducible — stated, with the reference Morgan, *The Seiberg–Witten Equations*, Ch. 6, as **bold context, not proved**, since the full wall-crossing formula is beyond the sources); spec: **the metric/perturbation independence is exactly the bordism invariance of chapter X applied to the parametric moduli space; the $b_2^+ = 1$ caveat is the reason the invariant is stated for $b_2^+\ge2$**; mechanism: **two generic choices are joined by a generic bordism, along which the signed characteristic number is unchanged, provided the reducible wall (codimension $b_2^+$) is missed — which needs $b_2^+\ge2$**; targets: the invariant is a diffeomorphism invariant of smooth four-manifolds, distinguishing smooth structures (below).
