type: theorem; source items: I4.3.2 (the input for Rem. 4.3.14), (Hatcher proves the relative fundamental class inside the proof of Thm 3.43 via a collar); prereqs: [Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class, Def - Manifold with Boundary and Induced Orientation, Def - Smooth Manifold with Boundary, Thm - Long Exact Sequence of a Pair in Singular Homology, Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated]; statement: Let $W$ be a compact connected $R$-oriented topological $n$-manifold with boundary whose boundary has a collar (a neighbourhood of $\partial W$ homeomorphic to $\partial W \times [0,1)$ with $\partial W \leftrightarrow \partial W \times \{0\}$); every smooth manifold with boundary has a collar (proved on the page as Lemma 1 via the flow of an inward vector field, Lee Thm 9.25, or cited from `Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated` Lemma C). Then there is a unique class $[W, \partial W] \in H_n(W, \partial W; R)$ restricting to $\mu_x$ in $H_n(W, W \setminus \{x\}; R)$ for every interior point $x$; $H_n(W, \partial W; R) \cong R$; and $\partial [W, \partial W] = [\partial W] \in H_{n-1}(\partial W; R)$ where $\partial W$ carries the induced orientation (the page fixes the induced orientation of $\partial W$ homologically — as the one for which this formula holds with the outward-normal-first convention — and proves in Lemma 2 that for smooth $W$ it agrees with `Def - Manifold with Boundary and Induced Orientation`, by a local computation in the half-space model $H^n = \{x_n \ge 0\}$: Lemma 2 computes, for the half-ball $D_+ = \bar B^n \cap H^n$, that the connecting map $H_n(D_+, \partial D_+) \to H_{n-1}(\partial D_+) $ followed by restriction to the flat part sends the relative generator to the generator of $H_{n-1}(\bar B^{n-1}, S^{n-2})$ determined by the outward-normal-first convention, by an explicit chain-level computation with one singular $n$-simplex; proof: source: none; the page follows Hatcher's proof of Thm 3.43 (pp. 253–254): let $W' := W \setminus (\partial W \times [0,1/2))$, a compact subset of the open manifold $M := W \setminus \partial W$; apply Hatcher Lemma 3.27 (from `Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class`) to $A = W'$ in $M$ to get $\mu_{W'} \in H_n(M, M \setminus W')$, identify $H_n(M, M \setminus W') \cong H_n(W, \partial W)$ via the collar (excision and homotopy invariance), and check the boundary formula via the pair sequence and the identification $H_{n-1}(\partial W \times (0, 1/2)) \cong H_{n-1}(\partial W)$; uniqueness from the uniqueness in Lemma 3.27; $\partial[W,\partial W]$ is the fundamental class of $\partial W$ because it restricts to a generator at every point of $\partial W$ (local computation of Lemma 2) and the fundamental class is characterised locally. Reference: Hatcher Thm 3.43 proof, Lemma 3.27; Bredon VI.9. spec: Targets: `Thm - Bounding Classes Vanish`, Poincaré–Lefschetz duality, cobordism invariance of the signature (chapter XIII).
