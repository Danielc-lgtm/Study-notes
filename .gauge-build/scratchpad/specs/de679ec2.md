## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (the regularity engine; feeds A-T5.2.1, A-I7.1.6); prereqs: [Def - Fourier Series on the Torus and Sobolev Spaces of Integer Order, Thm - Sobolev Norms on the Torus via Fourier Coefficients, Thm - Fourier Series of Smooth Functions on the Torus]; statement: Fix $\chi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ with $\chi = 1$ on $|\xi|\le1$, $0\le\chi\le1$, and for $0<\epsilon\le1$ let $J_\epsilon u := \sum_\xi\chi(\epsilon\xi)\hat u(\xi)e^{i\langle\xi,x\rangle}$. Then: (i) $J_\epsilon:H_k\to C^\infty$ for every $k$, $\|J_\epsilon u\|_k\le\|u\|_k$, and $J_\epsilon u\to u$ in $H_k$ as $\epsilon\to0$ for $u\in H_k$; (ii) $J_\epsilon$ commutes with constant-coefficient operators; (iii) for $a\in C^\infty(T^n)$ and every $t\in\mathbb Z$: $\|[a,J_\epsilon]v\|_{t+1}\le C_{a,t}\|v\|_t$ uniformly in $\epsilon$, and $[a,J_\epsilon]v\to0$ in $H_{t+1}$ as $\epsilon\to0$ for $v\in H_t$; (iv) consequently for a differential operator $P$ of order $\ell$ with smooth coefficients: $\|[P,J_\epsilon]v\|_{t}\le C\|v\|_{t+\ell-1}$ uniformly in $\epsilon$ and $[P,J_\epsilon]v\to0$ in $H_t$ for $v\in H_{t+\ell-1}$; (v) (lower semicontinuity) for $u\in H_m$ with $\sup_\epsilon\|J_\epsilon u\|_{s}<\infty$ for some $s>m$: $u\in H_s$ and $\|u\|_s\le\liminf_\epsilon\|J_\epsilon u\|_s$; proof: (i) weights $\chi(\epsilon\xi)\le1$ and dominated convergence for series (monotone/dominated convergence for sums proved directly: tail estimate); (iii) $\widehat{[a,J_\epsilon]v}(\xi) = \sum_\eta\hat a(\xi-\eta)(\chi(\epsilon\eta)-\chi(\epsilon\xi))\hat v(\eta)$; Lemma 1: $|\chi(\epsilon\eta)-\chi(\epsilon\xi)|(1+|\xi|)\le C(1+|\xi-\eta|)^2$ uniformly in $\epsilon\le1$ (if $\chi(\epsilon\eta)\ne\chi(\epsilon\xi)$ then $\min(|\xi|,|\eta|)\le R/\epsilon$ with $R$ the support radius, and $|\chi(\epsilon\eta)-\chi(\epsilon\xi)|\le\epsilon|\xi-\eta|\sup|\nabla\chi|$ by the mean value theorem — the two cases written out); then Peetre and the rapid decay of $\hat a$ give a convolution bound $\|[a,J_\epsilon]v\|_{t+1}\le C\sum_\zeta|\hat a(\zeta)|(1+|\zeta|)^{2+|t|+1}\cdot\|v\|_t$ via Young's inequality $\ell^1*\ell^2\subset\ell^2$; convergence to zero: true for trigonometric polynomials $v$ (finite sums, each term $\to0$), and the uniform bound plus density gives the general case ($\epsilon/3$); (iv) $P = \sum_\alpha a_\alpha\partial^\alpha$, $[P,J_\epsilon] = \sum_\alpha[a_\alpha,J_\epsilon]\partial^\alpha$ by (ii); (v) $\|u\|_s^2 = \sum(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\hat u|^2 = \lim_{N}\sum_{|\xi|\le N}\dots$ and for each finite set of $\xi$, $\sum_{|\xi|\le N}(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\hat u|^2 = \lim_\epsilon\sum_{|\xi|\le N}\chi(\epsilon\xi)^2(\dots)\le\liminf_\epsilon\|J_\epsilon u\|_s^2$; spec: reference Lawson–Michelsohn III.5 (Friedrichs' lemma) and Wells IV.2 ("Friedrichs mollifiers"); mechanism: **a smooth cutoff in frequency almost commutes with multiplication by a smooth function, with the error gaining one derivative because the cutoff varies slowly on the scale of its support**.
