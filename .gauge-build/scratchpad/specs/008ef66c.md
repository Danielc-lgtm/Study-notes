**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: A-D2.1.9; prereqs: [Def - Connection on a Vector Bundle, Def - Bundle-Valued Differential Forms, Def - Exterior Derivative on a Manifold]; spec: $d^\nabla(\omega\otimes s):=d\omega\otimes s+(-1)^p\omega\wedge\nabla s$ extended linearly; well-definedness on $\Omega^p(M;E)=\Omega^p(M)\otimes_{C^\infty}\Gamma(E)$ proved (check compatibility with $f\omega\otimes s=\omega\otimes fs$); the graded Leibniz rule $d^\nabla(\alpha\wedge\omega)=d\alpha\wedge\omega+(-1)^q\alpha\wedge d^\nabla\omega$ proved as a corollary; local formula $d^\nabla(e\sigma)=e(d\sigma+A\wedge\sigma)$ for a column $\sigma$ of $p$-forms proved; the sequence $\Omega^0(M;E)\to\Omega^1(M;E)\to\cdots$ need not be a complex (verified example: $d+A$ on the trivial bundle over $\mathbb R^2$ with $A=x\,dy\otimes\operatorname{id}$ has $d^\nabla d^\nabla s=dx\wedge dy\otimes s\ne0$); example: for $E=\underline{\mathbb R}$ with $\nabla=d$ it is the de Rham differential. Calibration: $d^\nabla=\nabla$ on $\Omega^0$; $d^\nabla(f\omega)=df\wedge\omega+fd^\nabla\omega$.
