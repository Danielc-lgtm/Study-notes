type: theorem (three parts); source items: A-T5.1.1 (iv)(a),(b), A-I5.1.3, A-I7.1.7 (the instance "$W^{k,2}(M^4)$ is an algebra for $k\ge3$" as Corollary 1; source typo: Haydys Theorem 136 (iv)(b) omits the side conditions $k\le\min(k_1,k_2)$ and strictness of the inequality; the corrected statement is the one below); prereqs: [Thm - Sobolev Norms on the Torus via Fourier Coefficients, Thm - Convergence of the Lattice Sum, Thm - Sobolev Spaces of Sections on a Compact Manifold via Charts, Thm - Sobolev Embedding Theorem]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact $n$-manifold. (a) If $2k>n$ then pointwise multiplication extends to a bounded bilinear map $H_k(M)\times H_k(M)\to H_k(M)$, $\|uv\|_k\le C\|u\|_k\|v\|_k$: $H_k(M)$ is a Banach algebra (after renorming). (b) For integers $k_1,k_2\ge k\ge0$ with $k_1+k_2-k>\frac n2$, multiplication extends to a bounded bilinear map $H_{k_1}(M)\times H_{k_2}(M)\to H_k(M)$; more generally for a smooth bilinear bundle map $E_1\otimes E_2\to E_3$, $H_{k_1}(E_1)\times H_{k_2}(E_2)\to H_k(E_3)$. (c) For $2k>n$ and $u\in H_k(M)$, $v\in H_m(M)$ with $|m|\le k$: $\|uv\|_m\le C\|u\|_k\|v\|_m$ (multiplication by an $H_k$ function is bounded on $H_m$ for $|m|\le k$). **Convention callout:** Haydys's general-$p$ (iv) recorded as context; proof: on the torus, $\widehat{uv}(\xi) = \sum_\eta\hat u(\xi-\eta)\hat v(\eta)$ (for smooth $u,v$, from the Fourier page); by Peetre, $(1+|\xi|^2)^{k/2}\le2^{k/2}\big((1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k/2}+(1+|\eta|^2)^{k/2}\big)$ for $k\ge0$ (Lemma 1, the standard splitting: either $|\xi-\eta|\ge|\xi|/2$ or $|\eta|\ge|\xi|/2$); split the convolution accordingly and estimate each piece by Cauchy–Schwarz against a summable weight; the writer follows the standard proof of $H^{s_1}\cdot H^{s_2}\subset H^s$ for $s\le\min(s_1,s_2)$, $s_1+s_2-s>n/2$ (e.g. Taylor *PDE III* §13.3 Prop 3.7 / Palais *Foundations of Global Non-Linear Analysis* §9, in the periodic setting): write $(1+|\xi|^2)^{k/2}|\widehat{uv}(\xi)|\le\sum_\eta[(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k/2}+(1+|\eta|^2)^{k/2}]|\hat u(\xi-\eta)||\hat v(\eta)|$ up to $2^{k/2}$, then in the first sum insert $1 = (1+|\eta|^2)^{k_2/2}(1+|\eta|^2)^{-k_2/2}$ and use Cauchy–Schwarz in $\eta$ with $\sum_\eta(1+|\eta|^2)^{-k_2}\cdot(\dots)$ — since $k\le k_1$, $(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k/2}|\hat u(\xi-\eta)|\le(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k_1/2}|\hat u(\xi-\eta)|$; the resulting $\ell^2$-in-$\xi$ norm is bounded by Young's inequality for sequences ($\ell^2*\ell^1\subset\ell^2$, proved as Lemma 2) using that $(1+|\eta|^2)^{-k_2/2}(1+|\eta|^2)^{k_2/2}|\hat v(\eta)|\in\ell^1$ requires $\sum(1+|\eta|^2)^{-k_2}<\infty$, i.e. $2k_2>n$ — **this is the case (a) and the case $k_2>n/2$ of (b)**; the general case of (b) with $k_1+k_2-k>n/2$ but neither $k_i>n/2$ needs the refined splitting, which the writer proves in full following the reference: on the region $|\eta|\le|\xi-\eta|$ bound $(1+|\xi|^2)^{k/2}\le C(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k/2}$ and write $(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k/2} = (1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{k_1/2}(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{(k-k_1)/2}$; since $k-k_1\le0$ and $|\eta|\le|\xi-\eta|$, $(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{(k-k_1)/2}\le(1+|\eta|^2)^{(k-k_1)/2}$, so the weight on $\eta$ becomes $(1+|\eta|^2)^{(k-k_1-k_2)/2}\cdot(1+|\eta|^2)^{k_2/2}|\hat v(\eta)|$, and $\sum(1+|\eta|^2)^{k-k_1-k_2}<\infty$ iff $2(k_1+k_2-k)>n$ — that is the full proof); (c): the case $m\ge0$ by the same argument with the roles arranged as in (b) ($k_1 = k$, $k_2 = m$, target $m$: needs $k+m-m>n/2$ ✓); $m<0$ by duality from the case $-m\ge0$; on $M$ via charts and $\phi_i$'s; spec: mechanism: **convolution of Fourier coefficients spreads regularity: the product has the lesser regularity provided the excess $k_1+k_2-k$ pays for the dimension**; targets: $\mathcal G^{k,2}$ is a group (XI), smoothness of the Seiberg–Witten map, bootstrapping in the compactness proof.
