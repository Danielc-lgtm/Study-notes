## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-T7.1.14 (Proposition 203), A-X7.1.1; prereqs: [Thm - Coulomb Gauge Fixing with Elliptic Estimates, Thm - The C0 Bound on the Spinor, Thm - Curvature Bounds for Seiberg-Witten Solutions, Thm - Elliptic Regularity and the Elliptic Estimate, Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem]; statement: For every $k\ge0$ there is $C_k>0$ such that every solution $(\psi,A)$ of the Seiberg–Witten equations is gauge equivalent, via some $g\in\mathcal G^{k+1,2}$, to a configuration $(\psi', A_0 + \alpha)$ (with $\psi' = \bar g\psi$, $d^*\alpha = 0$) satisfying $\|(\psi',\alpha)\|_{W^{k,2}}\le C_k$; proof: induction on $k$: $k = 0$ — $\|\psi\|_{L^2}$ bounded (`Thm - The C0 Bound on the Spinor`) and $\|F_A^+\|_{L^2}$ bounded (`Thm - Curvature Bounds`), so `Thm - Coulomb Gauge Fixing` bounds $\|\alpha\|_{L^2}$; $k = 1$ — from $0 = \slashed D_A^+\psi = \slashed D_{A_0}^+\psi + \tfrac12\alpha\cdot\psi$, the term $\alpha\cdot\psi$ is $L^2$-bounded ($\psi\in C^0$, $\alpha\in L^2$), so the elliptic estimate for $\slashed D_{A_0}^+$ bounds $\psi$ in $W^{1,2}$; then $F_A^+ = \mu(\psi)\in L^2$ bounds $\alpha$ in $W^{1,2}$; $k = 2$ — the pointwise estimate $|\nabla_{A_0}\mu(\psi)|\le C|\nabla_A\psi||\psi|$ ($\mu$ quadratic; **the content-map gap mixing $\nabla^{LC}$ and $\nabla_A$ is resolved by writing $\nabla_{A}\mu(\psi)$ and controlling the difference $\alpha\cdot\psi$**) gives $F_A^+\in W^{1,2}$, hence $\alpha\in W^{2,2}$, and Sobolev multiplication gives $\alpha\cdot\psi\in W^{1,2}$, so $\psi\in W^{2,2}$; the induction step $k\ge3\Rightarrow k+1$ uses that $W^{k,2}(M^4)$ is an algebra for $k\ge3$ (`Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem`), so $\mu(\psi)\in W^{k,2}$, `Thm - Coulomb Gauge Fixing` bounds $\alpha\in W^{k+1,2}$, and the elliptic estimate for $\slashed D_{A_0}^+$ bounds $\psi\in W^{k+1,2}$; **the $k = 3$ step (Haydys's Exercise X7.1.1) is written out; the compatibility of the gauge transformations across levels is handled by using one Coulomb gauge (unique modulo constants and $H^1(M;\mathbb Z)$)**; spec: mechanism: **the elliptic estimate lets each derivative of the curvature be traded for a derivative of the spinor and vice versa, and the Sobolev algebra closes the bootstrap once past the critical exponent**; targets: compactness.
