**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: B-D2.5.7 (Euler class half), B-E2.5.4, B-E2.5.5, B-T2.5.9, conventions.md ($e(E)=[\operatorname{Pf}(F/2\pi)]$, $e(TS^2)[S^2]=2$); prereqs: [Def - Pfaffian, Thm - Properties of the Pfaffian, Thm - Chern-Weil Theorem, Thm - Euclidean and Hermitian Structures are Reductions of the Structure Group, Def - Chern Classes, Ex - The Levi-Civita Connection of the Round Sphere as a Connection Matrix]; spec: for an oriented real vector bundle $E$ of rank $2m$, choose a Euclidean structure, so $\operatorname{Fr}_{SO}(E)$ is an $SO(2m)$-bundle, and a metric connection with curvature $F$ (skew-symmetric in oriented orthonormal frames): $e(E):=[\operatorname{Pf}(\tfrac{F}{2\pi})]\in H^{2m}_{dR}(M)$; well defined by Chern–Weil and independent of the Euclidean structure (convexity + III's homotopy theorem, as for Chern classes; proved); B's normalisation $\operatorname{Pf}(\bar\Omega)/((2\pi)^mm!)$ with B's $\operatorname{Pf}=\lambda(\sigma,\dots,\sigma)=m!\operatorname{Pf}_{series}$ agrees (Convention callout, computed); corollaries proved on the page: $e(E)=c_1(E)$ for an oriented rank-2 bundle viewed as a Hermitian line bundle (B-T2.5.9: $\mathfrak{so}(2)\cong\mathfrak u(1)$, $\operatorname{Pf}(\sigma)=\sigma$, and the identification of $\tfrac{i}{2\pi}\operatorname{tr}$ with $\tfrac{1}{2\pi}\operatorname{Pf}$ — the sign is where the orientation convention enters; the writer fixes orientations so that $e=c_1$ with the series' $c_1$); $e(TS^2)[S^2]=2$ verified from the round-sphere curvature ($\operatorname{Pf}(F/2\pi)=K\,dA/2\pi$); $e(E)=0$ if $E$ has a nowhere-vanishing section (proved: reduce the structure group to $SO(2m-1)$ and the Pfaffian vanishes on $\mathfrak{so}(2m-1)\subset\mathfrak{so}(2m)$ — written); $e(E)^2=p_m(E)$ (from the Pfaffian theorem (b), after the Pontryagin page); reversing the orientation negates $e$. Verified examples: $TS^2$; trivial bundles ($e=0$); non-example: a non-orientable bundle has no Euler class in this sense. Bridges: RG IV `Thm - Gauss-Bonnet Theorem for Surfaces` (the identity $\int_\Sigma e(T\Sigma)=\chi(\Sigma)$ is not proved here; the series proves only $\int_{S^2}e(TS^2)=2$; a bold scope remark). Calibration: $e$ of a rank-$2m$ bundle vanishes if $2m>\dim M$; $e(E_1\oplus E_2)=e(E_1)\smile e(E_2)$ (proved via block-diagonal Pfaffians).
