type: theorem; source items: T4.3.7, R4.3.2, I4.3.1 (first form of Poincaré duality Bär imports: $\partial : H_n(M;R) \to H_{n-1}(\dot B; R)$ is an isomorphism), D4.3.4; prereqs: [Def - Local Homology and Local Orientation, Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class, Thm - Mayer-Vietoris Sequence for Singular Homology (Gauge Theory), Thm - Excision Theorem for Singular Homology, Thm - Homology of Punctured Manifolds and Connected Sums]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact connected topological $n$-manifold, $R$ a commutative ring. (a) If $M$ is $R$-oriented with orientation $(\mu_x)$, there is a unique class $[M] \in H_n(M;R)$ whose image in $H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R)$ is $\mu_x$ for every $x \in M$; the map $H_n(M;R) \to H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R)$ is an isomorphism for every $x$, so $H_n(M;R) \cong R$ generated by $[M]$. (b) $H_i(M;R) = 0$ for $i > n$. (c) If $M$ is not $\mathbb{Z}$-orientable then $H_n(M;\mathbb{Z}) = 0$ and $H_n(M;\mathbb{Z}/2) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2$. (d) (Bär's form, Rem. 4.3.9, corrected) For $M$ closed connected $R$-oriented and $B \ni x$ a chart ball with $\dot B = B \setminus \{x\}$, the Mayer–Vietoris connecting map $\partial : H_n(M;R) \to H_{n-1}(\dot B;R)$ for the cover $M = \dot M \cup B$ is an isomorphism, and the composite $H_n(M;R) \xrightarrow{\partial} H_{n-1}(\dot B;R) \xrightarrow{(F_{x,r})_*^{-1}} H_{n-1}(S^{n-1};R) \cong R$ sends $[M]$ to $1$ when the orientation of $M$ is the one induced by the chart; `(source typo 25: Rem. 4.3.9 claims $H_{n-1}(\dot M) \oplus H_{n-1}(\{p\}) = 0$, which is false in general, e.g. for $M = T^n$; only the injectivity of $H_n(\dot M) \oplus H_n(B) \to H_n(M)$, i.e. $H_n(\dot M;R) = 0$, and the fact that $\partial$ is an isomorphism are needed, and both are proved here)`; R)$ restricting to all $\mu_x$, and $H_i(M, M \setminus A; R) = 0$ for $i > n$; proved by induction: (1) $A$ compact convex in a chart, (2) $A = A_1 \cup A_2$ via Hatcher's relative Mayer–Vietoris sequence $\cdots \to H_i(M, M \setminus (A_1 \cup A_2)) \to H_i(M, M \setminus A_1) \oplus H_i(M, M \setminus A_2) \to H_i(M, M \setminus (A_1 \cap A_2)) \to \cdots$, derived on the page from the small-chains theorem applied to the cover $\{M \setminus A_1, M \setminus A_2\}$ of $M \setminus (A_1 \cap A_2)$, (3) $A$ compact in $\mathbb{R}^n$ by approximation with finite unions of cubes and a direct-limit/compactness argument, (4) general $A$ by a finite chart cover) and Thm 3.26 with $A = M$; (d) is proved by identifying the MV connecting map for $M = \dot M \cup B$ with the composite $H_n(M) \to H_n(M, \dot M) \cong H_n(B, \dot B) \cong H_{n-1}(\dot B)$ (naturality of connecting maps, excision) and using (a) plus $H_n(\dot M;
