## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: A-D4.4.2; prereqs: [Def - Spinor Bundle and Dirac Operator, Def - Curvature of a Vector-Bundle Connection]; spec: for a Dirac bundle $E$ with curvature $R\in\Omega^2(\operatorname{End}E)$, $\mathcal R(s) := \tfrac12\sum_{i,j}e_i\cdot e_j\cdot R_{e_i,e_j}(s)$ in a local orthonormal frame — the image of $R$ under the Clifford-multiplication map $\Lambda^2T^*M\otimes\operatorname{End}(E)\to\operatorname{End}(E)$; frame-independence proved (it is the composition of tensorial maps); $\mathcal R$ is self-adjoint (the writer proves $\langle\mathcal Rs,t\rangle = \langle s,\mathcal Rt\rangle$ using condition (3) and antisymmetry in $i,j$); examples: flat bundle ($\mathcal R = 0$); $\Lambda T^*M$ (computed on 1-forms in the exercise below); non-example: $\sum_{i,j}e_ie_jR_{e_i,e_j}$ without the $\tfrac12$ is twice $\mathcal R$ — a normalisation, not a different object; Convention callout: Lawson–Michelsohn write $\mathcal R = \tfrac12\sum e_ie_jR_{e_i,e_j}$ — same.
