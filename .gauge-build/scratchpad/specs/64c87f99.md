## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (five parts); source items: (foundation; feeds A-T5.1.1, A-T5.2.1); prereqs: [Thm - Fourier Series of Smooth Functions on the Torus, Def - Fourier Series on the Torus and Sobolev Spaces of Integer Order, Def - Sobolev Space of Sections]; statement: (i) For integer $k\ge0$ and $u\in C^\infty(T^n)$: $c_k\|u\|_k\le\|u\|_{W^{k,2}}\le C_k\|u\|_k$ (with the flat metric and trivial connection), hence $W^{k,2}(T^n) = H_k(T^n)$ with equivalent norms. (ii) $H_k\subset H_m$ with $\|u\|_m\le\|u\|_k$ for $k\ge m$; $\bigcap_kH_k = C^\infty(T^n)$ (as sets; with the topology of all the $\|\cdot\|_k$). (iii) (Peetre's inequality) $(1+|\xi|^2)^s\le2^{|s|}(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)^{|s|}(1+|\eta|^2)^s$ for all $\xi,\eta\in\mathbb R^n$, $s\in\mathbb R$. (iv) (Duality) the pairing induces an isometric anti-linear isomorphism $H_{-k}\to(H_k)^*$; and $\|u\|_k = \sup\{|\langle u,\phi\rangle| : \phi\in C^\infty, \|\phi\|_{-k}\le1\}$ (up to the factor $(2\pi)^n$). (v) (Interpolation) For integers $r<t<s$ and every $\epsilon>0$ there is $C_\epsilon$ with $\|u\|_t\le\epsilon\|u\|_s + C_\epsilon\|u\|_r$; proof: (i) $\|\partial^\alpha u\|_{L^2}^2 = (2\pi)^n\sum\xi^{2\alpha}|\hat u|^2$ and $\sum_{|\alpha|\le k}\xi^{2\alpha}\asymp(1+|\xi|^2)^k$ (elementary inequalities with explicit constants); (ii) from the weights and part (iv) of the previous page; (iii) $1+|\xi|^2\le2(1+|\xi-\eta|^2)(1+|\eta|^2)$ by the triangle inequality, then raise to the power; (iv) Riesz representation for weighted $\ell^2$ (prove directly: a bounded functional on weighted $\ell^2$ is given by a sequence, via evaluation on basis vectors and Cauchy–Schwarz); (v) Young: $(1+|\xi|^2)^t\le\epsilon(1+|\xi|^2)^s + C_\epsilon(1+|\xi|^2)^r$ pointwise (prove: $x^{t-r}\le\epsilon x^{s-r} + C_\epsilon$ for $x\ge1$); spec: mechanism: the Sobolev scale on the torus is a family of weighted $\ell^2$ spaces indexed by the weight exponent.
