## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: (Hatcher §3.3; needed for Poincaré duality); prereqs: [Def - Cup Product, Def - Singular Homology with Coefficients]; spec: $\frown : C_k(X;R) \times C^l(X;R) \to C_{k-l}(X;R)$, $\sigma \frown \phi := \phi(\sigma|_{[v_0, \dots, v_l]}) \, \sigma|_{[v_l, \dots, v_k]}$ for $k \ge l$; the boundary formula $\partial(\sigma \frown \phi) = (-1)^l (\partial \sigma \frown \phi - \sigma \frown \delta \phi)$ (proved on the page, Hatcher p. 240); descent to $H_k(X;R) \times H^l(X;R) \to H_{k-l}(X;R)$; relative versions $H_k(X,A) \times H^l(X) \to H_{k-l}(X,A)$ and $H_k(X, A) \times H^l(X, A) \to H_{k-l}(X)$; the page states precisely the versions Hatcher uses in the proof of 3.30 and 3.43: $H_k(X, A \cup B) \times H^l(X, A) \to H_{k-l}(X, B)$ for $A, B$ open (or with interiors covering); naturality: $f_*(\alpha) \frown \phi = f_*(\alpha \frown f^*\phi)$ (proved at chain level); compatibility with the Kronecker pairing: $\langle \phi, \alpha \frown \psi \rangle = \langle \psi \smile \phi, \alpha \rangle$ (proved). Examples verified: cap with $1 \in H^0$ is the identity; for $\alpha \in H_n$ and $\phi \in H^n$, $\alpha \frown \phi = \langle \phi, \alpha \rangle \cdot [\text{point}] \in H_0$. Calibration check: compute the boundary formula's sign for $l = 1$; write $\sigma \frown \phi$ for a $2$-simplex and a $1$-cochain; verify naturality on an inclusion.
