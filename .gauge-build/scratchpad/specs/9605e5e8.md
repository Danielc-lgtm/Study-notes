## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-R5.2.1; prereqs: [Def - Elliptic Differential Operator and Principal Symbol, Thm - Tensoriality Lemma for C-Infinity-Linear Maps]; statement: (i) For a differential operator $L$ of order $\ell$, $x\in M$, $\xi\in T_x^*M$ and $v\in E_x$: $\sigma_L(x,\xi)v = \frac1{\ell!}L(f^\ell s)(x)$ for any $f\in C^\infty(M)$ with $f(x) = 0$, $df_x = \xi$ and any $s\in\Gamma(E)$ with $s(x) = v$; hence the symbol is independent of charts and trivialisations and defines a smooth bundle map $\sigma_L:\pi^*E\to\pi^*F$ over $T^*M$, fibrewise a homogeneous polynomial of degree $\ell$ in $\xi$. (ii) $\sigma_{L_2\circ L_1} = \sigma_{L_2}\circ\sigma_{L_1}$ and $\sigma_{L^*}(\xi) = (-1)^\ell\sigma_L(\xi)^*$ (the latter after `Def - Formal Adjoint of a Differential Operator`). (iii) Ellipticity is invariant under changes of charts, trivialisations, and under composition and adjoints; proof: (i) Leibniz: $\partial^\alpha(f^\ell s)$ at $x$ is non-zero only when all $\ell$ derivatives fall on the $f$-factors, giving $\ell!\,\xi^\alpha v$ for $|\alpha| = \ell$ and $0$ for $|\alpha|<\ell$; (ii) from (i) applied to $f^{\ell_1+\ell_2}$ (Leibniz count) and integration by parts for the adjoint; spec: mechanism: the top-order part of a differential operator is detected by conjugating with $e^{tf}$ or by the $\ell$-th power trick.
