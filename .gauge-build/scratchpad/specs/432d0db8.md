**[backlink]** — type: definition; source items: A-D2.1.7, A-R2.1.4, A-D2.1.11, A-E2.1.3, A §2.1 conventions ("connection" = "covariant derivative", $\mathcal A(E)$); prereqs: [Def - Vector Bundle, Def - Section of a Vector Bundle, Def - Bundle-Valued Differential Forms]; spec: A covariant derivative (the page states both names, "connection" and "covariant derivative", as required by backlink-map.md) is an $\mathbb R$-linear $\nabla\colon\Gamma(E)\to\Omega^1(M;E)$ with $\nabla(fs)=df\otimes s+f\nabla s$; directional derivative $\nabla_vs:=\iota_v\nabla s$, partial derivatives $\nabla_is$, $\nabla s=\sum dx_i\otimes\nabla_is$ (D2.1.11); equivalence with RG I's $(X,s)\mapsto\nabla_Xs$ formulation proved on the page (both directions, including $C^\infty$-linearity in $X$), with a bridge to `Def - Affine Connection on a Vector Bundle`. Axiom Motivation from R2.1.4 (the difference quotient of a section is undefined; the Leibniz rule is the whole axiom; per-clause failure: drop linearity — $s\mapsto\nabla s+\|s\|^2\theta$; drop the $df\otimes s$ term — a tensor, not a derivative). Verified examples: $d$ on the product bundle (coefficientwise derivative); the projected connection $\nabla s=\operatorname{pr}(ds)$ on $TM\subset\underline{\mathbb R^N}$ (E2.1.3, Leibniz rule computed on the page); the difference of two connections is $C^\infty$-linear (proved; it feeds the affine-space theorem); non-example: $s\mapsto ds$ on a non-trivial bundle is not defined (frame dependence exhibited on the Möbius bundle). Categorical: a connection is a splitting of the Atiyah sequence — stated only as an outlook in bold. True name: "a Leibniz-rule derivative in the base direction". Calibration: the connection matrix of $d$ is $0$; $\nabla_v$ of a constant section of the trivial bundle vanishes; $\nabla(fs)$ for $f$ locally constant.
