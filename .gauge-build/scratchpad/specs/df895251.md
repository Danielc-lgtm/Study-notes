## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: T4.1.17, I4.1.2; prereqs: [Def - Covering and the Universal Covering, Thm - Lifting Criterion for Coverings, Thm - Path Lifting and Homotopy Lifting, Def - Topological Manifold]; statement: Every connected topological manifold $X$ (more generally every path connected, locally path connected, semilocally simply connected space) admits a covering $p : \bar X \to X$ with $\bar X$ simply connected; if $p' : \bar X' \to X$ is another such covering then for any $\bar x \in \bar X$, $\bar x' \in \bar X'$ over the same point there is a unique homeomorphism $h : \bar X \to \bar X'$ with $p' \circ h = p$ and $h(\bar x) = \bar x'$; if $X$ is a smooth manifold, $\bar X$ carries a unique smooth structure making $p$ a local diffeomorphism; proof: source omitted; the page follows Hatcher Thm 1.38 (construction of $\bar X$ as homotopy classes of paths from a basepoint, the basis of open sets $U_{[\gamma]}$, the verification that $p$ is a covering and that $\bar X$ is simply connected) and Prop. 1.37/1.34 for uniqueness (lift $p$ through $p'$ both ways, use uniqueness of lifts); manifolds are locally contractible (charts), hence semilocally simply connected; the smooth structure is pulled back through charts of $X$ restricted to evenly covered sets (verify the transition maps are smooth). Cross-reference `Def - Universal Cover` and `Thm - Galois Correspondence for Covering Spaces` (AT II) as the classification context. spec: Why-true: "a point of $\bar X$ is a point of $X$ together with a homotopy class of ways of reaching it; simple connectivity of $\bar X$ is the statement that a loop of ways is trivial".
