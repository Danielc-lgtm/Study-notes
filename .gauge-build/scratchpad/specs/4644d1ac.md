## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound); source items: A-D2.2.12, A-D2.2.19 (the bundle $\operatorname{Ad}P=P\times_GG$ only), B-D2.7.4; prereqs: [Def - Associated Bundle, Def - Adjoint Representation]; spec: $\operatorname{ad}P:=P\times_{\operatorname{Ad}}\mathfrak g$ (a vector bundle; for $G\subset GL_k$ a subbundle of $\operatorname{End}E$ — proved), $\operatorname{Ad}P:=P\times_{\alpha}G$ with $G$ acting on itself by conjugation (a fibre bundle of groups: fibrewise multiplication $[p,g][p,g']=[p,gg']$ well defined — proved; each fibre isomorphic to $G$; not a principal bundle in general — B-D2.7.4; has the global section $[p,e]$; the Lie algebra bundle of $\operatorname{Ad}P$ is $\operatorname{ad}P$ — proved fibrewise). Calibration: $\Gamma(\operatorname{Ad}P)$ is a group under pointwise multiplication (proved).
