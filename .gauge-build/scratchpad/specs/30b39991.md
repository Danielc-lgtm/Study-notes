type: theorem; source items: A-T7.1.7 (Proposition 195); prereqs: [Def - Sobolev Configuration Space and Gauge Group, Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem, Thm - Composition with Analytic Functions on the Sobolev Algebra, Thm - Differential of the Seiberg-Witten Map, Thm - Variation of the Twisted and Spin-c Dirac Operators with the Connection]; statement: For $k\ge1$ and $2(k+1)>4$, the Seiberg–Witten map extends to a smooth map of Hilbert manifolds $SW:W^{k+1,2}(\slashed S^+)\times\mathcal A^{k+1,2}(P_{\det})\to W^{k,2}(\slashed S^-)\times W^{k,2}(\Lambda^2_+T^*M\otimes i\mathbb R)$; the gauge action of $\mathcal G^{k+2,2}$ on $W^{k+1,2}(\slashed S^+)\times\mathcal A^{k+1,2}$ is smooth and $SW$ is equivariant; in particular $SW:\mathcal C^{5,2}\to W^{4,2}(\slashed S^-)\times W^{4,2}(\Lambda^2_+T^*M\otimes i\mathbb R)$ is smooth and $\mathcal G^{6,2}$-equivariant; proof: with $A = A_0 + a$, $a\in W^{k+1,2}$: $\slashed D_A^+\psi = \slashed D_{A_0}^+\psi + \tfrac12 a\cdot\psi$ (Dirac variation), and $a\cdot\psi\in W^{k,2}$ by `Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem` (with $a\in W^{k+1,2}$, $\psi\in W^{k+1,2}$, product in $W^{k,2}$ since $2(k+1) - k>2$); $F_A^+ = F_{A_0}^+ + d^+a\in W^{k,2}$; $\mu(\psi)$ is quadratic, in $W^{k,2}$ (algebra property, $2(k+1)>4$); smoothness (not just well-definedness) of these polynomial and Clifford-bilinear maps between Sobolev spaces from `Thm - Sobolev Multiplication Theorem` (bilinearity + boundedness ⇒ smooth) and `Thm - Composition with Analytic Functions on the Sobolev Algebra` for the gauge action ($g^{-1}dg$ needs $g\in W^{k+2,2}$, explaining the $+2$); spec: **Haydys asserts smoothness via Theorem 136(iv); the series proves it from the $p = 2$ multiplication theorem and the analytic-composition theorem of chapter IX**; mechanism: **the Seiberg–Witten map is a polynomial in $(\psi,a)$ with Clifford-bilinear coefficients, and Sobolev multiplication makes each monomial a bounded, hence smooth, multilinear map**.
