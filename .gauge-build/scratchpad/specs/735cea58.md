spec: (a) for a spin structure $\tau:P\to\operatorname{Fr}_{SO}$ and the Levi-Civita connection form $\omega\in\Omega^1(\operatorname{Fr}_{SO};\mathfrak{so}(n))$, $\tau^*\omega\in\Omega^1(P;\mathfrak{spin}(n))$ (via $d\xi^{-1}:\mathfrak{so}(n)\to\mathfrak{spin}(n)$) is a connection on $P$ (both axioms verified using $\tau$ equivariant and $\xi$ a local diffeomorphism); (b) fix a complex Hermitian $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathbb R^n)$-module $\slashed S$ with the restricted $Spin(n)$-action $\rho$ (for $n = 3,4$ the quaternionic modules; general $n$: any irreducible one, existence/uniqueness recorded as bold context with reference LM I.5); $\slashed S := P\times_{Spin(n),\rho}\slashed S$ is a bundle of Clifford modules (the Clifford multiplication descends because $\rho(g)(v\cdot s) = \xi(g)v\cdot\rho(g)s$ — proved from $\rho(g) = g\cdot$ in the Clifford algebra and $\xi(g)v = gvg^{-1}$), and with the induced connection it is a Dirac bundle (conditions (1)–(3) verified: (3) because the connection on $\mathrm{Cl}(M)$ and on $\slashed S$ are both induced from $\tau^*\omega$ and the module structure is $Spin(n)$-equivariant); $\slashed D$ its Dirac operator; in dimension 4, $\slashed S = \slashed S^+\oplus\slashed S^-$, Clifford multiplication by 1-forms is odd, $\slashed D = \begin{pmatrix}0&\slashed D^-\\\slashed D^+&0\end{pmatrix}$ (R4.3.2); (c) twisting: for a principal $G$-bundle $Q$ with connection $A$ and a unitary representation $G\to U(k)$, $E = Q\times_G\mathbb C^k$, $\slashed S\otimes E$ is a Dirac bundle (exercise of §8.2) with Dirac operator $\slashed D_A$; (d) spin$^c$: for a spin$^c$ structure $P$ and a connection $A$ on $P_{\det}$ (equivalently a unitary connection on $L_{\det}$), $\tau^*(\omega+A)\in\Omega^1(P;\mathfrak{spin}^c(n))$ with $\mathfrak{spin}^c(n)\cong\mathfrak{so}(n)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$ is a connection on $P$; $\slashed S$ becomes a $Spin^c(n)$-module by $[g,z]\cdot s := z\rho(g)s$ (well-defined because $\rho(-1) = -1$); the spin$^c$ spinor bundle $\slashed S := P\times_{Spin^c(n)}\slashed S$ is a Dirac bundle and $\slashed D_A$ is the spin$^c$ Dirac operator; (e) R4.3.5: heuristically $\slashed S = \slashed S_0\otimes L_0$ with $L_{\det} = L_0^2$, so a connection $A_0$ on $L_0$ induces $A$ on $L_{\det}$ and $A_0+a_0$ corresponds to $A+2a_0$ — the origin of the $\tfrac12$ in `Thm - Variation of the Twisted and Spin-c Dirac Operators with the Connection` and of the $2g^{-1}dg$ in chapter XI; Convention callout: the vault's `Def - Spin Connection and the Dirac Operator` in frames; examples: $\mathbb R^3$, $\mathbb R^4$ with the trivial spin structure — the spinor bundle is the product bundle and $\slashed D$ has the explicit quaternionic form (proved in §8.4);
