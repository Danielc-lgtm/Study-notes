type: theorem (anchor; three parts); source items: A-T7.1.15 (Corollary 205), A-T7.1.16 (Theorem 206), A-T7.1.21 (Remark 216), A-X7.1.3, A-R7.1.4; prereqs: [Thm - Uniform Sobolev Bounds after Gauge Fixing, Thm - Rellich Compactness Theorem, Thm - Elliptic Regularity and the Elliptic Estimate, Def - Seiberg-Witten Moduli Space, Thm - The de Rham Complex is Elliptic and Harmonic Forms Represent de Rham Cohomology]; statement: (i) The Seiberg–Witten moduli space $\mathcal M = SW^{-1}(0)\cap\mathcal C^{5,2}/\mathcal G^{6,2}$ is compact. (ii) Every solution in $\mathcal C^{5,2}$ is gauge equivalent to a smooth solution, and $\mathcal M$ is homeomorphic to the $C^\infty$ moduli space $\mathcal M_\infty = \{$smooth solutions$\}/C^\infty(M;U(1))$, which is compact in the $C^\infty$ topology. (iii) The same holds for the perturbed equations $F_A^+ = \mu(\psi) + \eta$ with $\eta\in W^{4,2}(\Lambda^2_+T^*M\otimes i\mathbb R)$, with the $C^0$ bound $|\psi|^2\le\max\{0, -\min s_g\} + C\|\eta\|_{C^0}$-type; proof: (i) a sequence of solutions is gauge equivalent (via $\mathcal G^{6,2}$) to $(\psi_n, A_0 + \alpha_n)$ with $(\psi_n,\alpha_n)$ bounded in $W^{6,2}$ (`Thm - Uniform Sobolev Bounds`, $k = 6$); by `Thm - Rellich Compactness Theorem` a subsequence converges in $\mathcal C^{5,2}$ (compact embedding $W^{6,2}\hookrightarrow W^{5,2}$), and the limit solves the equations by continuity of $SW$; (ii) regularity: $F_A = F_{A_0} + d\alpha\in W^{k-1,2}$ for all $k$ (uniform bounds), so $d\alpha = (d^+ + d^*)\alpha$ (since $d^*\alpha = 0$) is smooth, hence $\alpha$ is smooth by elliptic regularity for the elliptic $(d^+ + d^*)$ (`Thm - Elliptic Regularity and the Elliptic Estimate`), so $A$ is smooth, and $\psi\in\ker\slashed D_A^+$ (smooth elliptic operator) is smooth; the $C^\infty$ compactness by a diagonal argument over $k$ (bounded in $W^{k,2}$ for every $k$ after gauge fixing, convergent subsequences in each $W^{k-1,2}$, diagonalise); **the level-compatibility of the gauge transformations (content-map gap) is handled by the uniqueness of Coulomb gauge modulo the finite $H^1(M;\mathbb Z)$**; (iii) the perturbed $C^0$ bound and the same bootstrap (Haydys's Exercise X7.1.3, "cosmetic changes"), written out; spec: mechanism: **uniform bounds in every Sobolev norm after gauge fixing plus Rellich give sequential compactness, and elliptic regularity upgrades $W^{5,2}$ limits to smooth ones**; targets: the moduli space is a compact manifold (§11.4), the invariant is a count/integral over a compact manifold (§11.5).
