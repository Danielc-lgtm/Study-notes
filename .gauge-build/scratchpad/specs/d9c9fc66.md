statement: For the spin$^c$ Dirac operator with connection $A$ on $L_{\det}$: $\slashed D_A^2\psi = \nabla_A^*\nabla_A\psi + \tfrac14s_g\psi + \tfrac12F_A\cdot\psi$, where $s_g$ is the scalar curvature and $F_A\cdot$ is Clifford multiplication by the 2-form $F_A$ ($F_A\cdot\psi = \tfrac12\sum_{i,j}F_A(e_i,e_j)e_ie_j\cdot\psi$ — normalisation stated); Lemma 1: $\sum_{j,k,l}R_{ijkl}e_je_ke_l = 2\sum_l\operatorname{Ric}_{il}e_l$ up to the sign fixed by the curvature convention — the writer carries out the standard reduction: split the sum over $(j,k,l)$ into the totally antisymmetric part (which vanishes by the first Bianchi identity, `Thm - First and Second Bianchi Identities`) and the remainder (which contracts to the Ricci tensor via $e_je_k + e_ke_j = -2\delta_{jk}$ and the symmetries $R_{ijkl} = -R_{jikl} = R_{klij}$, `Thm - Symmetries of the Riemann Tensor`), obtaining $\tfrac18\sum R_{ijkl}e_ie_je_ke_l = \tfrac14s_g$ (with the sign convention for $R$ fixed so that $s_g>0$ on spheres — the writer states the convention of RG III and checks on $S^2$); the $F_A$ term is $\tfrac12F_A\cdot$ by the normalisation; spec: replaces the flagged `Thm - Lichnerowicz Formula`; mechanism: **the Riemann tensor contracted against three Clifford generators collapses to Ricci by the Bianchi identity, and Ricci contracted against the fourth collapses to scalar curvature**.
