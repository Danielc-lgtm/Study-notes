type: theorem; source items: T4.1.19, T4.1.20, I4.1.3 (the "⇐" direction and uniqueness of lifts); prereqs: [Def - Covering and the Universal Covering, Def - Homotopy Groups, Thm - Continuous Maps Induce Homomorphisms of Homotopy Groups, Thm - Path Lifting and Homotopy Lifting, Thm - Lebesgue Number Lemma for Compact Metric Spaces]; statement: Let $p : \tilde Y \to Y$ be a covering, $\tilde y \in \tilde Y$, $y = p(\tilde y)$, $X$ path connected and locally path connected, $x \in X$, $f \in C(X,Y)$ with $f(x) = y$. (i) A lift $\tilde f$ of $f$ through $\tilde y$ exists if and only if $f_\sharp(\pi_1(X,x)) \subset p_\sharp(\pi_1(\tilde Y, \tilde y))$. (ii) (Uniqueness) Two lifts of $f$ that agree at one point of the connected space $X$ agree everywhere.
