## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-I3.1.2, A-T2.4.1 (the cases $G=U(1)$ and $G=Sp(1)$; the general statement is a scope remark), A-I2.4.2 (same), A-R2.4.1; prereqs: [Def - Classifying Bundle and Classifying Map, Def - The Hopf Bundle, Thm - Vector Bundles are Associated to Their Frame Bundles, Thm - Euclidean and Hermitian Structures are Reductions of the Structure Group, Thm - Homotopic Maps Pull Back Isomorphic Principal Bundles, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact manifold. (a) For every complex line bundle $L\to M$ there are $N$ and a smooth $f\colon M\to\mathbb{CP}^N$ with $f^*\mathcal O(-1)\cong L$; equivalently every principal $U(1)$-bundle is $f^*(S^{2N+1}\to\mathbb{CP}^N)$. (b) Two such maps $f\colon M\to\mathbb{CP}^N$, $f'\colon M\to\mathbb{CP}^{N'}$ classify isomorphic bundles iff they become homotopic in $\mathbb{CP}^{N+N'+1}$ (after the standard inclusions); hence $P\mapsto[f_P]$ is a bijection from isomorphism classes of principal $U(1)$-bundles over $M$ onto $[M;\mathbb{CP}^\infty]$. (c) The same holds for principal $Sp(1)$-bundles (equivalently quaternionic line bundles) with $\mathbb{HP}^N$ and $\mathbb{HP}^\infty$; proof: A: "not too hard to show", "cited"; write Hatcher VBKT Thm 1.16's argument for rank one: (a) choose finitely many sections $s_0,\dots,s_N$ of $L$ spanning each fibre (partition of unity) and a Hermitian structure; the map $m\mapsto$ the line $\{(\langle s_0(m),v\rangle,\dots,\langle s_N(m),v\rangle):v\in L_m\}\subset\mathbb C^{N+1}$ is smooth into $\mathbb{CP}^N$ (its lift to $S^{2N+1}$ is a unitary frame of $L$) and $f^*\mathcal O(-1)\cong L$; (b) "⇐" is the homotopy-invariance theorem; "⇒": two embeddings $L\hookrightarrow\underline{\mathbb C^{N+1}}$, $L\hookrightarrow\underline{\mathbb C^{N'+1}}$ are joined, after the "even/odd coordinate" trick, by a linear homotopy through injective bundle maps into $\underline{\mathbb C^{N+N'+2}}$, whose induced maps to projective space give the homotopy; (c) identical with $\mathbb H$ (right $\mathbb H$-lines; $Sp(1)$ acting by right multiplication; state the conventions); reference: Hatcher VBKT Thm 1.16 and Prop 1.17, Milnor–Stasheff Lemma 5.3 and Thm 5.6; spec: Convention callout: A states the theorem for every compact $G$ with a general classifying bundle; the series proves it only for $U(1)$ and $Sp(1)$ (and remarks that the identical argument gives $U(n)$ with Grassmannians), and uses nothing else. Targets: definition of the topological first Chern class; the classification pages.
