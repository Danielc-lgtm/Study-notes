type: theorem (anchor); source items: B-T3.3.12, B-R3.3.3, B-I3.3.4 (→ inside, Lemma 1), (3.18); prereqs: [Def - Yang-Mills Lagrangian and Action Functional, Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Connections, Def - Instanton, Thm - Self-Dual Decomposition of 2-Forms in Four Dimensions, Thm - The Killing Form of su(N) is 2N Times the Trace Form, Def - Pontryagin Classes, Thm - Chern-Weil Theorem, Thm - Principal Connections Induce Covariant Derivatives on Associated Bundles, Thm - Top de Rham Cohomology of a Closed Connected Oriented Manifold, Thm - The Trace Metric on the Adjoint Bundle is Parallel]; statement: Let $P\to M$ be an $SU(N)$-bundle ($N\ge2$) over a closed oriented Riemannian 4-manifold, $p_1 := p_1(\operatorname{ad}P) = \int_M\frac{1}{8\pi^2}(\operatorname{tr}\bar\Phi\wedge\operatorname{tr}\bar\Phi - \operatorname{tr}(\bar\Phi\wedge\bar\Phi))\in\mathbb R$ (with $\bar\Phi$ the curvature of any connection on the frame bundle of $\operatorname{ad}P$; independent of the choice by Chern–Weil). (i) (Energy identity) For every $\omega\in\mathcal C(P)$: $\|\bar\Omega\|^2_{L^2} = \|\bar\Omega^+\|^2 + \|\bar\Omega^-\|^2$ and $\frac{N}{4\pi^2}\big(\|\bar\Omega^+\|^2 - \|\bar\Omega^-\|^2\big) = p_1$, i.e. $p_1 = \frac{N}{4\pi^2}\int_M\lambda(\bar\Omega\wedge\bar\Omega) = \frac{N}{4\pi^2}\langle\bar\Omega,\star\bar\Omega\rangle_{L^2}$. (ii) (Bound (3.18)) $\int_ML_{YM}(\omega)\ge\frac{2\pi^2}{N}|p_1|$, with equality if and only if $\bar\Omega = \pm\star\bar\Omega$ (sign $= \operatorname{sign}p_1$). (iii) If $p_1<0$ there is no self-dual connection on $P$, $\int L_{YM}\ge-\frac{2\pi^2}{N}p_1$ with equality iff $\omega$ is anti-self-dual; if $p_1>0$ there is no anti-self-dual connection, $\int L_{YM}\ge\frac{2\pi^2}{N}p_1$ with equality iff $\omega$ is self-dual; if $p_1 = 0$, $\omega$ is (anti-)self-dual iff $\bar\Omega\equiv0$; proof: Bär's six steps, gaps filled: Lemma 1 ($\bar\Phi = \operatorname{ad}\circ\bar\Omega$ — from the associated-bundle curvature anchor with $\rho = \operatorname{Ad}$, $\rho_* = \operatorname{ad}$; if the anchor lacks the curvature statement, prove it here from the local formula $\nabla^\omega = d + \operatorname{ad}(A)$ and $F = dA + \tfrac12[A\wedge A]$ with $\operatorname{ad}$ a Lie algebra homomorphism); Lemma 2 ($\operatorname{ad}X$ is $\lambda$-skew, hence traceless, so $\operatorname{tr}\bar\Phi = 0$); Lemma 3 (the Killing-form page); Lemma 4 ($\int\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}\bar\Omega\wedge\operatorname{ad}\bar\Omega) = -2N\int\lambda(\bar\Omega\wedge\bar\Omega)$ using the metric pairing page); Lemma 5 ($\lambda(\bar\Omega\wedge\bar\Omega) = \langle\bar\Omega,\star\bar\Omega\rangle\mathrm{vol}$ by property (4) with $p = 0$ and $\star\star = 1$; then $\langle\bar\Omega,\star\bar\Omega\rangle = |\bar\Omega^+|^2-|\bar\Omega^-|^2$ from the self-dual decomposition page); then $0\le\|\bar\Omega\mp\star\bar\Omega\|^2 = 2(\|\bar\Omega\|^2\mp\langle\bar\Omega,\star\bar\Omega\rangle) = 2(2\int L_{YM}\mp\frac{4\pi^2}{N}p_1)$; cases as Bär; spec: this is where Haydys's silent identities of Corollary 199 (A-T7.1.11) live in the series — the $U(1)$ version with $c_1$ is `Ex - Energy Identity for U(1) Connections on a Four-Manifold`; mechanism: **the Yang–Mills energy splits into self-dual and anti-self-dual halves whose difference is a characteristic number; minimising the sum with fixed difference forces one half to vanish**; Bridges: Donaldson theory (XIII), Seiberg–Witten curvature bounds (XI).
