## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor); source items: A-D4.3.9, A-T4.3.2, A-I4.3.5, A-E4.3.3 (→ the spin-manifold case as Corollary 1); prereqs: [Def - Spin Structure and Spin-c Structure, Thm - Classification of Principal U(1)-Bundles by the First Chern Class, Def - Transition Functions and the Cocycle Condition, Thm - Principal Bundles are Classified by Cocycles]; statement: Let $M$ be an oriented Riemannian manifold admitting a spin$^c$ structure. (i) For a spin$^c$ structure $P$ and a principal $U(1)$-bundle $Q$, $P\cdot Q := (P\times_MQ)/U(1)$ where $U(1)$ acts freely by $(p,q)\cdot w := (p\cdot[1,w]^{-1}, q\cdot w)$, with the residual $Spin^c(n)$-action on the first factor (well-definedness and freeness proved on the page) is a spin$^c$ structure with spinor bundle $\slashed S_P\otimes L_Q$ and $L_{\det}(P\cdot Q) = L_{\det}(P)\otimes L_Q^2$; this defines an action of the group of isomorphism classes of $U(1)$-bundles, i.e. of $H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$ (by the chapter III anchor), on $\mathcal S(M)$. (ii) The action is free and transitive: $\mathcal S(M)$ is an $H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$-torsor; in particular $\mathcal S(M)\cong H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$ non-canonically, and canonically once a base point is chosen. (iii) If $M$ is spin with spin structure $P_{Spin}$, then $P_{Spin}\times_M(M\times U(1))/\{\pm1\}$ is a spin$^c$ structure with $L_{\det}$ trivial (the origin), and every spin$^c$ structure is $P_{Spin}\times_MQ/\{\pm1\}$ for a $U(1)$-bundle $Q$ (E4.3.3), with $c_1(L_{\det}) = 2c_1(Q)$; proof: Haydys: "can be shown"; follow Gompf–Stipsicz, *4-Manifolds and Kirby Calculus*, Prop. 2.4.16 / Morgan, *The Seiberg–Witten Equations*, §3.1: Lemma 1 (the homomorphism $\phi:Spin^c(n)\times_{SO(n)}Spin^c(n)\to U(1)$, $\phi([g,z],[\epsilon g,z']) := \epsilon z^{-1}z'$, is well defined with kernel the diagonal); Lemma 2 (transitivity: for spin$^c$ structures $P_1,P_2$ over the same $\operatorname{Fr}_{SO}$, $Q := (P_1\times_{\operatorname{Fr}_{SO}}P_2)\times_\phi U(1)$ is a $U(1)$-bundle and $P_1\cdot Q\cong P_2$ — constructed explicitly); Lemma 3 (freeness: $P\cdot Q\cong P$ implies $Q$ has a section, hence is trivial, using `Thm - Sections of a Principal Bundle and Triviality`); (iii) direct; the identification with $H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$ via the chapter III anchor; spec: mechanism: **two lifts of the same $SO(n)$-bundle to $Spin^c(n)$ differ by a $U(1)$-bundle because $Spin^c(n)\times_{SO(n)}Spin^c(n)$ is $Spin^c(n)\times U(1)$**; targets: the Seiberg–Witten invariant as a function on $H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$; finiteness (XI).
