## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: R5.3.4, D5.3.3, I5.3.7, (Bär's step (c)); prereqs: [Thm - De Rham Theorem via Smooth Singular Chains, Thm - Intersection Form is Unimodular, Thm - Poincare Duality via Hodge Star, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds, Thm - Tubular Neighbourhood Theorem, Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Forms, Thm - Hodge Decomposition Theorem, Thm - Harmonic Forms Represent de Rham Cohomology, Thm - Homology of a Simply Connected Closed Four-Manifold]; statement: Let $X$ be a compact oriented simply connected smooth four-manifold. (i) (Poincaré dual forms) For every closed oriented embedded surface $S \subset X$ there is a closed $2$-form $\eta_S$ supported in any prescribed tubular neighbourhood of $S$ with $\int_S \omega|_S = \int_X \omega \wedge \eta_S$ for all closed $2$-forms $\omega$; its class $[\eta_S] \in H^2_{dR}(X)$ is unique. (ii) For transverse $S_1, S_2$: $\int_X \eta_{S_1} \wedge \eta_{S_2} = S_1 \cdot S_2$. (iii) The map $H_2(X;\mathbb{Z}) \to H^2_{dR}(X)$, $[S] \mapsto [\eta_S]$, is injective with image the lattice $H^2_{dR}(X;\mathbb{Z}) := \{[\alpha] \mid \int_c \alpha \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ for all } c \in Z_{2,\text{smooth}}(X;\mathbb{Z})\}$ of classes with integral periods, and under it $Q_X([S_1], [S_2]) = \int_X \eta_{S_1} \wedge \eta_{S_2}$; so, as Bär says, $Q_X$ "can be realised on $H^2_{dR}(X;\mathbb{Z})$ by $\int_X \alpha \wedge \beta$". (iv) (Signature via harmonic forms) For any Riemannian metric on $X$, $\operatorname{sign}(Q_X) = b_2^+ - b_2^-$ where $b_2^\pm := \dim \mathcal{H}^\pm$ are the dimensions of the spaces of self-dual and anti-self-dual harmonic $2$-forms; $Q_X$ is positive definite iff $b_2^- = 0$; proof: source: (c) stated, fn. 2 asserted; the page follows Bott–Tu §6 for (i)–(ii): $\eta_S$ := the Thom form of $\nu_S$ (an angular form construction: choose a closed compactly supported form on the fibres $\mathbb{R}^2$ of total integral $1$, globalised by a connection or by a partition of unity — Bott–Tu Prop. 6.24 / Thm 6.17 with the explicit "compact vertical cohomology" argument for a rank-two bundle done by hand on the page), the identity $\int_S \omega = \int_X \omega \wedge \eta_S$ via the tubular neighbourhood and the homotopy $\omega|_U \simeq \pi^* (\omega|_S)$ (Poincaré lemma along fibres, `Thm - Homotopy Invariance of de Rham Cohomology`) plus Stokes; (ii) by Bott–Tu (6.31): $\eta_{S_1} \wedge \eta_{S_2}$ is supported near $S_1 \cap S_2$ and integrates, near each point $p$, to $\varepsilon(p)$ (the local computation of the product of two Thom forms in the straightened chart of `Ex - Straightening Two Transverse Planes by a Chart`); (iii) from `Thm - De Rham Theorem via Smooth Singular Chains` (iii) ($H^2_{dR} \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X;\mathbb{Z}), \mathbb{R})$ via periods, integral periods $\leftrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(H_2, \mathbb{Z})$), the period computation $\int_{S'} \eta_S = S' \cdot S$ (from (i)–(ii) with $\omega = \eta_{S'}$, or directly), and unimodularity (`Thm - Intersection Form is Unimodular`) which says $[S] \mapsto Q_X(\cdot, S)$ is an isomorphism onto $\operatorname{Hom}(H_2, \mathbb{Z})$; (iv) from `Thm - Poincare Duality via Hodge Star` and the Hodge theorem: $\mathcal{H}^2 = \mathcal{H}^+ \oplus \mathcal{H}^-$ orthogonally, $\int \alpha \wedge \alpha = \pm \lVert \alpha \rVert^2$ on $\mathcal{H}^\pm$ (`[NEEDED FROM I–XI: $\alpha \wedge \star\alpha = |\alpha|^2 \mathrm{vol}$ in the series' Hodge convention]` — with Bär's convention $\omega \wedge \eta = \langle \star \omega, \eta \rangle \mathrm{vol}$ this is $\alpha \wedge \alpha = \langle \star\alpha, \alpha \rangle \mathrm{vol} = \pm |\alpha|^2 \mathrm{vol}$ on $\mathcal{H}^\pm$, verified on the page), so by Sylvester's law (`Thm - Sylvester's Law of Inertia`) the signature of the real form $\int \alpha \wedge \beta$ on $H^2_{dR}(X)$ is $b_2^+ - b_2^-$, and this real form is $Q_X \otimes \mathbb{R}$ under (iii). Reference: Bott–Tu §6 (Prop. 6.24, eq. (6.31)); Lee Thm 18.14; Donaldson–Kronheimer §1.1 for the signature statement. spec: Why-true: "a surface is Poincaré dual to a bump form on its normal directions, and two bump forms multiply to a bump at each intersection point with the sign of the orientation"; Targets: Bär's steps (c)–(d) in `Thm - Donaldson Diagonalisation Theorem`; `Thm - Reducible Instantons Correspond to Classes of Self-Intersection One` (self-dual harmonic representatives when $b_2^- = 0$).
