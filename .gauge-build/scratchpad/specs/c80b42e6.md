## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: D5.1.1; prereqs: [Def - Immersion, Submersion, and Embedding, Def - Immersed Submanifold, Def - Tangent Space of a Submanifold, Def - Smooth Manifold]; spec: An immersion $f : S \to X$ of a closed surface into a four-manifold has *finitely many transverse double points* if there are finitely many points $x_1, \dots, x_N \in X$, each with exactly two preimages $p_i \ne q_i$, $f$ is injective away from $\{p_i, q_i\}$, and $df(T_{p_i} S) \oplus df(T_{q_i} S) = T_{x_i} X$ ("$f$ is transversal", Bär Step 3); the page records that transversality at a double point is an open condition and equivalent to $df(T_{p_i}S) \cap df(T_{q_i}S) = 0$ by dimension count (proved). Examples verified: two transverse planes in $\mathbb{R}^4 = \mathbb{C}^2$ meeting at $0$ (the local model $\mathbb{R}^2 \times 0$ and $0 \times \mathbb{R}^2$); an embedding (no double points); non-example: the immersion of $S^2$ into $\mathbb{R}^4$ whose two sheets meet along a curve (not transverse: the intersection of tangent planes is a line). Calibration check: check the dimension count $2 + 2 = 4$; state what "transverse" would mean for a curve and a surface in $X^4$ (they generically miss each other); explain why a double point of an immersed surface in a four-manifold is isolated once transverse.
