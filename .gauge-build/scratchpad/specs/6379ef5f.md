## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-T6.6.1 (Proposition 179); prereqs: [Def - Infinitesimal Action and Local Slice, Def - Quotient Topology and Identification Map, Thm - Universal Property of the Quotient]; statement: Let a Banach Lie group $G$ act freely on a Hilbert manifold $X$ and admit a local slice at every point. Then the quotient $X/G$ is a Hilbert manifold and $\pi:X\to X/G$ is a smooth submersion admitting local sections; proof: **Haydys says "clear"; the writer supplies it in full**: a slice $S$ at $x$ maps homeomorphically to an open neighbourhood of $[x]$ in $X/G$ (freeness makes $G\times S\to GS$ a diffeomorphism, so $GS/G\cong S$); the transition maps between two slice charts $S_1,S_2$ are smooth (composition of the diffeomorphism $G\times S_i\to GS_i$ and the projection); **Hausdorffness of $X/G$ (which Haydys omits) is proved from the slice property: two orbits $G\cdot x\ne G\cdot x'$ are separated because $GS$ is open and $G\times S\to GS$ is a diffeomorphism, so a small slice neighbourhood of one misses the other orbit** (the writer gives the argument; in the applications $X/G$ is metrisable, which also gives Hausdorffness); second countability from that of $X$; spec: mechanism: **a slice is a chart for the orbit space, and freeness plus the slice diffeomorphism makes the charts compatible**; targets: the configuration space modulo gauge $\mathcal B = \mathcal C_{\mathrm{irr}}/\mathcal G$ is a Hilbert manifold (XI).
