**[anchor]** — type: definition (compound: representation, faithful, trivial, standard, equivalence); source items: B-D1.3.1, B-D1.3.2, B-D1.3.3, B-D1.3.5, B-D1.3.12, B-R1.3.1, B-E1.3.4, B-E1.3.5; prereqs: [Def - Lie Group Homomorphism, Def - Classical Matrix Groups]; spec: A representation is a Lie group homomorphism $\varrho\colon G\to\operatorname{Aut}(V)=GL(V)$, $V$ finite-dimensional over $\mathbb K$; faithful means injective; equivalence is an intertwining isomorphism $T$. Verified examples: trivial representation (faithful iff $G=\{e\}$), the standard representations of the classical groups (E1.3.4), change of basis gives equivalent matrix representations (E1.3.5); non-example: $z\mapsto\bar z$ on $\mathbb C$ is a representation of $U(1)$ but $z\mapsto z^{1/2}$ is not well defined. Categorical: a $G$-module / a functor from $G$ to vector spaces. True name: "a linear $G$-action". Calibration: verify that the determinant $\det\colon GL(n;\mathbb K)\to GL(1;\mathbb K)$ is a one-dimensional representation and compute its kernel.
