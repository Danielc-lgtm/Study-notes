## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-R5.2.2 ((145)), A-I7.1.6 (partial); prereqs: [Def - Elliptic Differential Operator and Principal Symbol, Thm - Sobolev Spaces of Sections on a Compact Manifold via Charts, Def - Formal Adjoint of a Differential Operator]; statement: Let $M$ be compact and $L:\Gamma(E)\to\Gamma(F)$ a differential operator of order $\ell$. For every $k\in\mathbb Z$, $L$ extends uniquely to a bounded linear map $L:H_{k+\ell}(M;E)\to H_k(M;F)$, and the extensions are compatible with the inclusions; for $u\in H_{k+\ell}$ and $\phi\in\Gamma(F)$: $\langle Lu,\phi\rangle = \langle u,L^*\phi\rangle$ (the extension is the weak/distributional action); in particular for $u\in H_m$ (any $m$) the element $Lu\in H_{m-\ell}$ is characterised by $\langle Lu,\phi\rangle = \langle u,L^*\phi\rangle$ for all smooth $\phi$; proof: on the torus, $\partial^\alpha:H_{k+|\alpha|}\to H_k$ has norm $\le1$ (Fourier), multiplication by smooth coefficients is bounded on every $H_k$ (Lemma 1 of the charts page); patch; the pairing identity for smooth $u$ is the definition of $L^*$ and extends by density and continuity of the pairing (duality (iv) of the charts page); spec: mechanism: differentiation costs exactly one order on the Fourier side.
