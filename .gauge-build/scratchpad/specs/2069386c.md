**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: A-D2.2.16, B-D2.3.3, B-R2.3.2, B-D2.2.9 (gauge); prereqs: [Def - Connection on a Principal Bundle, Thm - Sections of a Principal Bundle and Triviality, Def - Pullback of a Differential Form on a Manifold]; spec: $A_s:=s^*\omega\in\Omega^1(U;\mathfrak g)$ for a local section $s$ (= local gauge); the physicists' gauge potential $A=A_\mu dx^\mu$; for the frame bundle and a local frame $e$, $e^*\omega$ is the connection matrix $A(\nabla,e)$ of II (this identification is the theorem in §4.4; here stated with a forward link); reconstruction of $\omega$ over $U$ from $A_s$: $\omega_{s(x)g}(dR_g\,ds(v)+\xi_P)=\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}A_s(v)+\xi$ (proved: this is B-T2.3.7's uniqueness half, written as a corollary). Verified examples: product connection has $A_s=0$ for the identity section and $A_{s\cdot g}=g^*\theta$ (pure gauge); the Hopf connection in B's two sections (exercise). Convention callout: A switches between $a$ and $A$; B writes $\omega_\alpha$. Calibration: $A_s$ is $\mathfrak g$-valued, not $\operatorname{ad}P$-valued; the family $(A_{s_\alpha})$ is not a global form.
