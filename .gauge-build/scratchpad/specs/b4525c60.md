## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor; compound: the gauge group, its action, reducible/irreducible configurations, the moduli space); source items: A-D7.1.3 (equation (189)), A-D7.1.4, A-D7.1.10 (Corollary 205), A-D7.1.11 (Theorem 206); prereqs: [Def - Seiberg-Witten Equations, Def - Gauge Transformation, Def - The Equivariant Deformation Complex and its Folded Operator, Thm - A Free Action with Slices has a Manifold Quotient]; spec: the *gauge group* $\mathcal G := \mathcal G(P_{\det})\cong C^\infty(M;U(1))$ acts on $\mathcal C$ on the right by $(\psi,A)\cdot g := (\bar g\psi, A + 2g^{-1}dg)$ (the factor $2$ as above; `Def - Gauge Transformation` is the principal-bundle gauge group specialised to $U(1)$); a configuration is *reducible* if $\psi\equiv0$ (stabiliser the constant maps $U(1)$) and *irreducible* if $\psi\not\equiv0$ (trivial stabiliser); the *Seiberg–Witten moduli space* $\mathcal M_{SW} := SW^{-1}(0)/\mathcal G$, its irreducible part $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{irr}}_{SW} := \{$irreducible solutions$\}/\mathcal G$; the Sobolev model $\mathcal M := \{(\psi,A)\in\mathcal C^{5,2} : SW(\psi,A) = 0\}/\mathcal G^{6,2}$ (the smooth structure and the identification with the $C^\infty$ moduli space $\mathcal M_\infty$ are `Thm - Compactness of the Seiberg-Witten Moduli Space`); topologised as a subspace of $\mathcal B := \mathcal C^{5,2}_{\mathrm{irr}}/\mathcal G^{6,2}$ (a Hilbert manifold by `Thm - A Free Action with Slices has a Manifold Quotient` once the Coulomb slice is in hand, §11.4); examples: for a metric of positive scalar curvature the moduli space of the trivial spin$^c$ structure is a single reducible point (§11.5); non-example: at a reducible configuration the action is not free, so $\mathcal M_{SW}$ can be singular there (avoided generically, §11.4).
