**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: A-D2.2.1, B-D2.2.1, B-R2.2.1, B-T2.2.1, B-D2.2.9, B-R2.2.3, B-E2.2.1 (statement; the Hopf bundle is `Def - The Hopf Bundle`), A §2.2 conventions (motivation "all frames at once"); prereqs: [Def - Fibre Bundle, Def - Free, Transitive, Effective, and Proper Group Actions, Def - Fundamental Vector Field of a Group Action]; spec: A principal $G$-bundle is a fibre bundle $\pi\colon P\to M$ with a smooth free right $G$-action preserving fibres and transitive on them, admitting $G$-equivariant local trivialisations $\psi_U(pg)=\psi_U(p)\cdot g$; both sources' four-clause definitions are shown equivalent on the page (A's "surjective submersion + free transitive on fibres + equivariant trivialisations" and B's "fibre bundle + free + transitive + equivariant trivialisations"). Corollaries proved: the orbit map $\ell_p\colon G\to P_{\pi(p)}$ is a diffeomorphism (B-T2.2.1, with the injectivity of $d_e\ell_p$ from the freeness argument via fundamental vector fields); the fibre carries no natural group structure; local sections are exactly local gauges (B-D2.9, R2.2.3 in Relate to Other Fields: "a choice of units"). Per-clause failure analysis: drop freeness — $S^2\times SO(2)\to S^2$ with the rotation action; drop equivariance of trivialisations — a bundle with fibre $G$ but no structure (mapping torus of an inner automorphism, which is still principal, versus of an outer one); drop transitivity — a subbundle. Verified examples: $M\times G$; $G\to G/H$ (link DG XI theorem, restated); the Hopf bundle (link). Categorical: a $G$-torsor bundle; a principal bundle is a functor to $G$-torsors. Convention callout: right action throughout; B's occasional $G\backslash P$ notation. Calibration: a principal bundle is trivial iff it has a section (forward to the theorem below); the structure group of $\operatorname{Fr}(E)$ is $GL_k$.
