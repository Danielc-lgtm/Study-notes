## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: I5.1.4, I5.2.1 (the geometric-to-cohomological bridge); prereqs: [Thm - Poincare Dual of an Embedded Submanifold is the Thom Class of its Normal Bundle, Thm - Properties of the Cup Product, Def - Cup Product, Def - Intersection Form of a Four-Manifold]; statement: Let $X$ be a closed oriented smooth $n$-manifold, $S_1, S_2 \subset X$ closed oriented embedded submanifolds of dimensions $k_1, k_2$ meeting transversally, so that $S_1 \cap S_2$ is a closed submanifold of dimension $k_1 + k_2 - n$, oriented by the convention that $\nu_{S_1 \cap S_2} = \nu_{S_1}|_{S_1 \cap S_2} \oplus \nu_{S_2}|_{S_1 \cap S_2}$ as oriented bundles. Then $u_{S_1} \smile u_{S_2} = u_{S_1 \cap S_2}$ in $H^{2n - k_1 - k_2}(X;\mathbb{Z})$. In particular, for $n = 4$, $k_1 = k_2 = 2$: $\langle u_{S_1} \smile u_{S_2}, [X] \rangle = S_1 \cdot S_2$ with the sign convention $\varepsilon(p)$ of `Def - Intersection Form of a Four-Manifold` (the page verifies that the orientation convention on $S_1 \cap S_2$ = finite set of points gives exactly $\varepsilon(p)$); proof: source: none; the page follows Bredon VI §11 (Thm 11.9) and Milnor–Stasheff Problem 11-C, in the following form: the Thom class of $\nu_{S_1} \oplus \nu_{S_2}$ (over $S_1 \cap S_2$) is the cup product of the pulled-back Thom classes (Milnor–Stasheff Property 9.6 for the Thom class of a Whitney sum, proved from uniqueness and the fibrewise computation), the tubular neighbourhood of $S_1 \cap S_2$ can be taken as the intersection of tubular neighbourhoods of $S_1$ and $S_2$ compatible with the transverse structure (a local-coordinate lemma: near a point of $S_1 \cap S_2$ there are coordinates in which $S_1 = \mathbb{R}^{k_1} \times 0$, $S_2 = \ldots$, proved from transversality and the inverse function theorem as in `Ex - Straightening Two Transverse Planes by a Chart` generalised), and the relative cup product $H^{n-k_1}(X, X \setminus S_1) \times H^{n-k_2}(X, X \setminus S_2) \to H^{2n-k_1-k_2}(X, X \setminus (S_1 \cap S_2))$ (relative cup product for open complements, from `Def - Cup Product`) carries $u_{\nu_1} \times u_{\nu_2}$ to $u_{\nu_1 \oplus \nu_2}$ by the local computation and uniqueness of Thom classes. Reference: Bredon VI.11 (Thm 11.9, "Poincaré duality takes intersection to cup product", and the surrounding lemmas); Milnor–Stasheff Problem 11-C; Bott–Tu (6.31) for the de Rham version. spec: Why-true: "Thom classes multiply fibrewise, and a transverse intersection has normal bundle the sum of the two normal bundles"; Targets: `Thm - Intersection Form Equals the Cup Product Form`.
