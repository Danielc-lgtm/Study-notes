type: theorem; source items: A-I3.3.2 (bundles over an interval are trivial — corollary), B-D2.1.7 (triviality, used), needed for A-T2.4.1, A-E2.4.1, A-D2.4.2 ("depend only on the isomorphism class"), B-T2.1.1 ("only if"); prereqs: [Def - Principal G-Bundle, Thm - The Pull-Back of a Fibre Bundle is a Fibre Bundle, Thm - Sections of a Principal Bundle and Triviality, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity, Def - Smooth Homotopy of Maps]; statement: (a) Let $P\to M\times[0,1]$ be a principal $G$-bundle over a compact manifold $M$ (more generally over any manifold $M$). Then $P\cong\operatorname{pr}^*(P|_{M\times\{0\}})$; in particular the restrictions to $M\times\{0\}$ and $M\times\{1\}$ are isomorphic. (b) If $f_0,f_1\colon M'\to M$ are smoothly homotopic then $f_0^*P\cong f_1^*P$ for every principal bundle $P\to M$. (c) Every principal bundle over a contractible manifold, and every principal bundle over a closed disc $D^n$ or over $[0,1]$, is trivial. (d) If $h\colon M\to M$ is a diffeomorphism isotopic to the identity then $h^*P\cong P$. (e) The same statements hold for vector bundles and for fibre bundles with structure group $G$; proof: Hatcher, *Vector Bundles and K-Theory*, Thm 1.6 and Prop 1.7 (written for vector bundles; the principal case is identical using equivariant trivialisations): Lemma 1 (if $P$ over $M\times[a,b]$ is trivial over $M\times[a,c]$ and $M\times[c,b]$ then over $M\times[a,b]$ — glue the two trivialisations by adjusting the second with the transition function on $M\times\{c\}$ extended by the projection); Lemma 2 (for compact $M$, a finite cover by $U_i$ with $P$ trivial over $U_i\times[0,1]$ exists — Lebesgue number of the cover of $M\times[0,1]$ by trivialising sets); Lemma 3 (construct the isomorphism $P\to\operatorname{pr}^*P|_{M\times0}$ inductively over $U_1\cup\dots\cup U_i$ using a partition of unity to "slide" — Hatcher's argument with $\psi_i=\rho_1+\dots+\rho_i$); Lemma 4 (paracompact case: Hatcher's countable-union trick); (b) apply (a) to $H^*P$ over $M'\times[0,1]$; (c),(d) corollaries; reference: Hatcher VBKT Thm 1.6, Prop 1.7; Husemoller Ch. 4 Thm 9.9; Steenrod §11; spec: Mechanism: "a bundle over $M\times I$ can be slid along the $I$ direction because triviality over a strip lets us extend a trivialisation from one end." Targets: classification by homotopy classes of maps (§3.6); characteristic classes depend only on the isomorphism class (VI); the mapping torus of $\phi$ is trivial only if $\phi$ is isotopic to the identity (corollary via clutching).
