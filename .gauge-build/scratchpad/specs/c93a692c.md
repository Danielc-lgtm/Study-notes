Corollaries proved on the page: $g\mapsto\theta_g$ is a homomorphism into $\operatorname{Diff}(M)$ (T1.5.1); free implies effective (T1.5.2); a compact group acts properly (closed-map argument); $p*g:=g^{-1}\cdot p$ turns a left action into a right action and conversely (T1.5.9, both directions written). Verified examples: trivial action (effective iff $G=\{e\}$), the action induced by a representation (never transitive unless $V=0$), left multiplication (free and transitive), conjugation (effective iff $Z(G)=\{e\}$), rotation of $S^2$ about the $z$-axis (effective, not free, not transitive; orbit space $[-1,1]$ via the $z$-coordinate — E1.5.5), scalar $U(1)$-action on $S^{2n-1}$ (free; transitive iff $n=1$). Convention callout: B writes $G\backslash M$ even for right actions; the series writes $P/G$ for right actions on bundles. Categorical: an action is a functor from $G$ (one-object groupoid) to manifolds. Calibration: decide freeness/effectiveness for $SO(3)$ on $S^2$, for $\mathbb Z/2$ on $S^n$ by the antipodal map, for $\mathbb R$ on $T^2$ by an irrational line.
