## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: B-D3.3.2; prereqs: [Def - Connection on a Vector Bundle, Def - Exterior Covariant Derivative on a Vector Bundle, Def - Differential k-Form on a Manifold, Def - Tensor Product of Vector Spaces]; spec: for $E\to M$ a $\mathbb K$-vector bundle, $\eta\in\Omega^k(M;E)$, $\mu\in\Omega^l(M;E)$: $\eta\wedge\mu := \sum\eta_{i_1\dots i_k}\otimes\mu_{j_1\dots j_l}\otimes dx^{i_1}\wedge\dots\wedge dx^{j_l}\in\Omega^{k+l}(M;E\otimes E)$ (coordinate-free version: $(\eta\wedge\mu)(X_1,\dots,X_{k+l}) = \sum_{\text{shuffles}}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\eta(X_{\sigma(1)},\dots)\otimes\mu(\dots)$, well-definedness proved); with a fibre metric, $\langle\eta\wedge\mu\rangle := \sum\langle\eta_{I},\mu_{J}\rangle\,dx^I\wedge dx^J\in\Omega^{k+l}(M;\mathbb K)$; for $E = \operatorname{End}$-type bundles, $\operatorname{tr}(\eta\wedge\mu)$; symmetry $\langle\eta\wedge\mu\rangle = (-1)^{kl}\langle\mu\wedge\eta\rangle$ (proved); the special case $\lambda(\bar\Omega\wedge\star\bar\Omega)$ for $\operatorname{ad}P$-valued 2-forms; Convention callout: the bracket $[\alpha\wedge\beta]$ of `Def - Lie-Algebra-Valued Differential Forms and Their Bracket` (IV) is the composition of this wedge with the Lie bracket $\mathfrak g\otimes\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak g$; examples: $E$ trivial of rank 1 recovers the ordinary wedge; non-example: $\eta\wedge\eta$ need not vanish for $E$-valued 1-forms (compute a $2\times2$ matrix example).
