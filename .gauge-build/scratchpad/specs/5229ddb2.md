type: theorem; source items: A-T6.4.1, A-I6.4.1, A-I6.4.2; prereqs: [Def - Determinant Line Bundle of a Family of Fredholm Operators, Thm - Stabilisation of Fredholm Operators and Continuity of the Kernel, Thm - Determinant Lines and Short Exact Sequences]; statement: For a continuous family $\{T_p\}_{p\in P}$ of Fredholm operators, $\det T\to P$ is a locally trivial real line bundle; proof: near $p_0$ choose a finite-dimensional $V\subset Y$ with $\operatorname{Im}T_{p_0} + V = Y$; the stabilised operators $T_{p,V}:X\oplus V\to Y$, $(x,v)\mapsto T_px + v$, are surjective at $p_0$, hence on a neighbourhood $W$ (`Thm - Stabilisation of Fredholm Operators and Continuity of the Kernel` (ii)), with $\ker T_{p,V}$ of constant dimension forming a continuous (indeed locally trivial) finite-rank vector bundle over $W$ (`Thm - Stabilisation ...` (iii)); the exact sequence $0\to\ker T_p\to\ker T_{p,V}\to V\to\operatorname{coker}T_p\to0$ (170) and `Thm - Determinant Lines and Short Exact Sequences` give a canonical isomorphism $\det T_p\cong\Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}\ker T_{p,V}\otimes\Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}V^*$, natural in $p$, so $\det T|_W\cong\det(\ker T_{p,V})\otimes(\det V)^*$ is a line bundle trivialised by a local trivialisation of $\ker T_{p,V}$; **the two facts Haydys cites ([MS12] local triviality of $\ker T_{p,V}$; openness of surjectivity) are `Thm - Stabilisation ...` (ii),(iii); the compatibility of the trivialisations for two choices $V,V'$ (Haydys does not discuss) is checked via $V + V'$ and the naturality of (170)**; spec: mechanism: **stabilising by a finite-dimensional space makes the kernel a genuine vector bundle, and the determinant line is that bundle's top power twisted by a constant line**.
