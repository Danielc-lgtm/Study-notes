## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor); source items: A-T6.1.1 (Theorem 162), A-I6.1.1; prereqs: [Def - Fredholm Map and Its Index, Thm - Inverse Function Theorem on Banach Spaces, Def - Fredholm Operator and Index, Thm - Closed Range is Automatic for Finite-Dimensional Cokernel]; statement: Let $X,Y$ be Banach spaces, $F:X\to Y$ a smooth Fredholm map, $p\in F^{-1}(0)$, $X_0 := \ker d_pF$, $Y_0 := \operatorname{Im}d_pF$, and choose closed complements $X = X_0\oplus X_1$, $Y = Y_0\oplus Y_1$ ($\dim X_0<\infty$, $\dim Y_1<\infty$). Then there are: a diffeomorphism $\phi$ from a neighbourhood of $0\in X$ onto a neighbourhood of $p$ with $\phi(0) = p$; the linear isomorphism $T := d_pF|_{X_1}:X_1\to Y_0$; and a smooth map $f:X\to Y_1$ (defined near $0$), such that $F(\phi(x_0,x_1)) = Tx_1 + f(x_0,x_1)$ for $(x_0,x_1)$ near $0$, with $f(0) = 0$ and $d_0f = 0$. In particular, with $f_0(x_0) := f(x_0,0)$, $\phi$ restricts to a homeomorphism from a neighbourhood of $0$ in $f_0^{-1}(0)\subset X_0$ onto a neighbourhood of $p$ in $F^{-1}(0)$; proof: Haydys omits; Lyapunov–Schmidt: $\Phi(x) := (x_0, \pi_{Y_0}(F(p+x)))\in X_0\oplus Y_0$ has $d_0\Phi = (x_0,x_1)\mapsto(x_0,Tx_1)$, an isomorphism (closed range of $d_pF$ and the open mapping theorem make $T$ an isomorphism onto $Y_0$); the inverse function theorem gives a local inverse $\Psi$; set $\phi(x_0,x_1) := p + \Psi(x_0,Tx_1)$ and $f := \pi_{Y_1}\circ F\circ\phi$; then $\pi_{Y_0}F\phi(x_0,x_1) = Tx_1$ by construction; the "in particular": $F\phi(x_0,x_1) = 0$ iff $Tx_1 = 0$ and $f(x_0,x_1) = 0$ iff $x_1 = 0$ and $f_0(x_0) = 0$; $d_0f = 0$ since $d_pF(X_0) = 0$ and $d_pF(X_1) = Y_0$; spec: Donaldson–Kronheimer §4.2.5; mechanism: **solve the equation in the directions where the linearisation is invertible by the inverse function theorem; what remains is a finite-dimensional equation on the kernel with values in the cokernel**; targets: local structure of moduli spaces near non-regular points (XI, XIII).
