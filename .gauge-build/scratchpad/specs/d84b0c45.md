## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: the Atiyah complex; the Seiberg–Witten deformation complex (191)); source items: A-D7.1.5 (equation (191)), A-D7.1.6, A-R7.1.3; prereqs: [Def - Seiberg-Witten Equations, Def - Seiberg-Witten Moduli Space, Def - The Equivariant Deformation Complex and its Folded Operator, Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Forms, Def - The Codifferential]; spec: the *Atiyah complex* on an oriented Riemannian 4-manifold is $0\to\Omega^0(M;i\mathbb R)\xrightarrow{d}\Omega^1(M;i\mathbb R)\xrightarrow{d^+}\Omega^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)\to0$, where $d^+ := \tfrac12(1+\star)d$ is the self-dual part of $d$; the *Seiberg–Witten deformation complex* at a solution $(\psi,A)$ is $0\to\Omega^0(M;i\mathbb R)\xrightarrow{R_{(\psi,A)}}\Gamma(\slashed S^+)\oplus\Omega^1(M;i\mathbb R)\xrightarrow{d_{(\psi,A)}SW}\Gamma(\slashed S^-)\oplus\Omega^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)\to0$ with $R_{(\psi,A)}\xi = (-\xi\psi, 2d\xi)$ (the infinitesimal gauge action, R7.1.3) and $d_{(\psi,A)}SW$ the linearised map (next page); it is a complex at a solution ($d_{(\psi,A)}SW\circ R_{(\psi,A)} = 0$) by `Thm - Equivariance of the Seiberg-Witten Map`; its cohomology $H^0,H^1,H^2$ is the stabiliser algebra, the moduli tangent space, and the obstruction, in the sense of chapter X's `Def - The Equivariant Deformation Complex and its Folded Operator`; examples: at an irreducible solution $H^0 = 0$; the Atiyah complex is the "gauge + curvature" half with the spinor set to zero; non-example: at a reducible solution $H^0 = i\mathbb R\ne0$.
