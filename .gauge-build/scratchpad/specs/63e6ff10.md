type: theorem; source items: B-R3.2.12, B-R3.2.13 ((3.16)), B-I3.2.4 (→ wikilink `Thm - Cartan's Magic Formula`, DG VIII, proof-complete), B-I3.2.5 (replaced by the horizontal-lift construction below); prereqs: [Def - Energy-Momentum Tensor of a U(1) Field, Def - Symmetrized Covariant Derivative and Divergence of a 2-Tensor, Thm - Metric Variation of the Electromagnetic Lagrangian, Thm - Cartan's Magic Formula, Def - Horizontal Subspace and Horizontal Lift, Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of Horizontal Lifts, Def - Flow of a Vector Field, Thm - Fundamental Theorem on Flows, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]; statement: (i) (Invariance) For a diffeomorphism $\phi$ of $M$ supported in $U\Subset M$ and any bundle isomorphism $\Phi:\phi^*P\to P$ covering $\phi$: $\int_UL_1(\Phi^*\omega,\phi^*g) = \int_UL_1(\omega,g)$. (ii) (Integration by parts) For every compactly supported vector field $X$ and symmetric $T$: $\int_MT\cdot\nabla^{sym}X\,\mathrm{vol}_g = -\int_M\langle\operatorname{div}T,X\rangle\mathrm{vol}_g$. (iii) (Divergence identity, (3.16)) For every $\omega\in\mathcal C(P)$: $-\langle\operatorname{div}T,X\rangle = \langle\iota_XF,\star d\star F\rangle$ for all vector fields $X$; if $d\star F + J = 0$ then $-\langle\operatorname{div}T,X\rangle = -\langle\iota_XF,\star J\rangle$ (source typo: Bär p. 95 writes "$d\star F = J$", inconsistent with the Euler–Lagrange equation $d\star F + J = 0$ of p. 86; the series uses the latter, so the sign here is as stated and Poynting's theorem below is re-derived with it); in particular $\operatorname{div}T = 0$ in vacuum; proof: (i) is the naturality computation $\int_U\langle\phi^*F,\phi^*F\rangle_{\phi^*g}\phi^*\mathrm{vol}_g = \int_U(\langle F,F\rangle_g\circ\phi)\,\phi^*\mathrm{vol}_g = \int_U\langle F,F\rangle_g\mathrm{vol}_g$ (change of variables, `Thm - Change of Variables for Integration on Manifolds`, DG IX); (ii) Stokes: $T\cdot\nabla^{sym}X = \operatorname{div}(T(\cdot,X^\flat)) - \langle\operatorname{div}T,X\rangle$ via metric compatibility and symmetry of $T$, and $\int\operatorname{div}Y\,\mathrm{vol} = \int d(\iota_Y\mathrm{vol}) = 0$ (prove $\operatorname{div}Y\,\mathrm{vol} = d(\iota_Y\mathrm{vol}) = \mathcal L_Y\mathrm{vol}$ as Lemma 1 via Cartan); (iii) let $\phi_t$ be the flow of $X$ (complete since compactly supported), and let $\tilde X$ be the horizontal lift of $X$ to $P$ with respect to $\omega$ (a $G$-invariant vector field, `Def - Horizontal Subspace and Horizontal Lift`), with flow $\tilde\phi_t$ consisting of bundle automorphisms covering $\phi_t$ (complete because its integral curves are horizontal lifts of those of $X$, `Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of Horizontal Lifts`); then $\omega_t := \tilde\phi_t^*\omega$ is a connection on $P$, $g_t := \phi_t^*g$, and $L_1(\omega_t,g_t) = \phi_t^*L_1(\omega,g)$ (naturality), so $\frac{d}{dt}\big|_0\int_UL_1(\omega_t,g_t) = 0$; on the other hand this derivative equals $\int_U\eta\wedge d\star F - \tfrac12\int_UT\cdot h\,\mathrm{vol}_g$ with $h = \mathcal L_Xg = 2\nabla^{sym}X$ and $i\eta := \frac{d}{dt}\big|_0 s^*\omega_t = s^*\mathcal L_{\tilde X}\omega = s^*(\iota_{\tilde X}d\omega + d\iota_{\tilde X}\omega) = s^*\iota_{\tilde X}\Omega = i\,\iota_XF$ (Cartan on $P$; $\omega(\tilde X) = 0$ since $\tilde X$ is horizontal; $d\omega = \Omega$ for abelian $G$ by the structure equation anchor; $s^*\iota_{\tilde X}\Omega = \iota_X s^*\Omega$ because $ds(X)$ and $\tilde X$ differ by a vertical vector on which $\Omega$ vanishes) — this replaces Bär's "$\iota_XF + df$" (his $f$ is zero for the horizontal lift; both routes agree since $\int df\wedge d\star F = 0$); combine with the Euler–Lagrange computation (`Thm - Euler-Lagrange Equation of the Electromagnetic Action`) and (ii), then the fundamental lemma; the identity $\int(\iota_XF)\wedge d\star F = \int\langle\iota_XF,\star d\star F\rangle\mathrm{vol}$ by property (5)/(4) with the Lorentzian sign tracked; spec: this page is the longest of §7.3; mechanism: **diffeomorphism invariance of the action, differentiated along a flow, is the covariant conservation law for $T$ (Noether's second theorem in its simplest instance)**;
