## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor; compound: the determinant line of one operator; continuous families; the line bundle; homotopy of families); source items: A-D6.4.1, A-D6.4.2, A-D6.4.3 (Definition 172), A-R6.4.1, A-R6.4.2; prereqs: [Def - Fredholm Operator and Index, Thm - Determinant Lines and Short Exact Sequences, Thm - Stabilisation of Fredholm Operators and Continuity of the Kernel]; spec: for a Fredholm operator $T:X\to Y$ of Hilbert spaces, $\det T := \Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}\ker T\otimes\Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}(\operatorname{coker}T)^*$, a real line; a *continuous family* is a norm-continuous $P\to\operatorname{Fred}(X;Y)$, $p\mapsto T_p$; the *determinant line bundle* $\det T\to P$ has fibres $\det T_p$; two families are *homotopic* if they extend to a continuous family over $P\times[0,1]$; Axiom Motivation (R6.4.1): the integer degree needs orientations, and in infinite dimensions the finite-dimensional orientation line $\Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}$ is replaced by the determinant line, which does not jump even though $\dim\ker$ and $\dim\operatorname{coker}$ do (R6.4.2, via the stabilisation sequence (170)); local triviality is the next page; examples: a family of isomorphisms ($\det T_p = \mathbb R$ canonically, trivial); the family $t\mapsto t\cdot\mathrm{id}$ on $\mathbb R$ ($\det$ jumps in $\ker$ at $t = 0$ but the line bundle is trivial — computed); non-example: the naive bundle $\Lambda^{\mathrm{top}}\ker T_p$ alone is not locally trivial (its rank jumps).
