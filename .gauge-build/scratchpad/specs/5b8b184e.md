## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: linking number of two disjoint oriented knots in $S^3$ via the homological degree of the Gauss map, self-linking number of an embedded solid torus, linking matrix of a family of disjoint embedded solid tori); source items: D5.2.1, E5.2.1; prereqs: [Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class, Thm - Homology of Spheres, Ex - Degree of a Self-Map of the Sphere via Hurewicz, Def - Embedded Submanifold]; spec: For disjoint oriented smooth embeddings $K_1, K_2 : S^1 \to \mathbb{R}^3 = S^3 \setminus \{\infty\}$ define $\operatorname{lk}(K_1, K_2) := \deg\big(G : S^1 \times S^1 \to S^2\big)$, $G(s,t) := (K_1(s) - K_2(t))/\lVert K_1(s) - K_2(t) \rVert$, the homological degree ($G_*[S^1 \times S^1] = \operatorname{lk} \cdot [S^2]$, with the fundamental class of the torus from `Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class` and the product orientation); for an embedding $f : \bar D^2 \times S^1 \to S^3$ the self-linking number $\operatorname{lk}(f,f) := \operatorname{lk}(f(0, \cdot), f(1, \cdot))$ and, for two such with disjoint images, $\operatorname{lk}(f_1, f_2) := \operatorname{lk}(f_1(0,\cdot), f_2(0,\cdot))$; the linking matrix $(\operatorname{lk}(f_i, f_j))_{ij}$. Proved on the page: symmetry $\operatorname{lk}(K_1, K_2) = \operatorname{lk}(K_2, K_1)$ (the Gauss map for the swapped pair is the antipode of $G$ precomposed with the factor-swap $(s,t) \mapsto (t,s)$ of $S^1 \times S^1$; that swap reverses the product orientation and so has degree $-1$, the antipodal map of $S^2$ has degree $(-1)^3 = -1$, and the two sign factors cancel, giving equality of the degrees), invariance under isotopy through disjoint pairs (homotopy invariance of the degree), and independence from the choice of point $\infty \notin K_1 \cup K_2$ (stated for orientation; proved via isotopy). Examples verified (E5.2.1): the standard unknotted solid torus $f_k(z, e^{i\theta}) := $ (the tubular neighbourhood of the unit circle in the $xy$-plane, with the boundary curve $f_k(1, \cdot)$ winding $k$ times around the core as it goes once along it — explicit parametrisation with a radius $\rho < 1$), has self-linking number $k$ (computed: the Gauss map degree for the core circle and the $(1,k)$-curve on the torus equals $k$ — by an explicit computation of the degree via a regular value, `Thm - Regular Value Theorem on Manifolds`, or via homotoping the $(1,k)$-curve to $k$ small meridians each linking once); Bär's three pictured embeddings realise $0$, $1$, $4$. Non-example: two disjoint circles in different half-spaces have $\operatorname{lk} = 0$ (the Gauss map misses a direction). Calibration check: compute $\operatorname{lk}$ of the Hopf link as $\pm 1$; explain why $\operatorname{lk}(f,f)$ depends on the framing $f$ and not only on the core; explain why a linking matrix is symmetric.
