## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: the infinitesimal action $R_x$; local slice of a group action); source items: A-D6.6.1 (equation (177)), A-D6.6.2 (Definition 178), A-I6.6.1 (→ inside, Notation: slices are a hypothesis, not proved in general); prereqs: [Def - Banach Manifold and Smooth Maps between Banach Spaces, Def - Smooth Action of a Lie Group, Def - Fundamental Vector Field of a Group Action, Def - Embedded Submanifold]; spec: from §10.5 on, $X$ and $G$ are **Hilbert** manifolds (design note (1)); for a smooth action of a Banach Lie group $G$ on $X$, the *infinitesimal action* at $x$ is $R_x:\operatorname{Lie}(G)\to T_xX$, $R_x\xi := \frac{d}{dt}\big|_0(\exp(t\xi)\cdot x)$, with image the tangent space to the orbit $G\cdot x$ (the Banach-manifold analogue of `Def - Fundamental Vector Field of a Group Action`); a *local slice* at $x$ is a Hilbert submanifold $S\ni x$ such that $GS := \{g\cdot s\}$ is open in $X$ and $G\times S\to GS$, $(g,s)\mapsto g\cdot s$, is a diffeomorphism; when the action is free, a slice gives a chart for the orbit space; the *orthogonal slice* used in practice is $S = x + \ker R_x^*$ (the $L^2$-orthogonal complement of the orbit direction), whose tangent space $\ker R_x^*$ is transverse to $\operatorname{Im}R_x$; examples: E6.6.1 (the rotation action of $U(1)$ on $S^2$ minus the poles, global slice $\{(x,0,z) : x>0\}$, quotient an interval — made an exercise); the Coulomb slice $\ker R_{(\psi,A)}^*$ of the Seiberg–Witten gauge action (XI); non-example: at a point with non-trivial stabiliser (a reducible Seiberg–Witten configuration) the action is not free and no slice of this form exists — the reason XI restricts to irreducibles.
