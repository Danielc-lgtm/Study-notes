**[anchor]** — type: theorem; source items: A-T3.3.1, A-D3.3.5, A-D3.3.6, A-D3.3.7, A-X3.3.2 (as Lemma), A-R3.3.6, A §3.3 conventions ($\mathcal R(M;G)$); prereqs: [Thm - Holonomy of a Flat Connection Depends Only on the Homotopy Class of the Loop, Thm - Existence of the Universal Cover and the Deck Action of the Fundamental Group, Def - Associated Bundle, Thm - Principal Connections Induce Covariant Derivatives on Associated Bundles, Thm - Local Triviality of Flat Connections, Thm - Gauge Transformations Act on Connections and Curvature, Def - Representation of a Lie Group]; statement: Let $M$ be a connected manifold with base point $m$, $G$ a Lie group, $\tilde M\to M$ the universal cover with the right action of $\Gamma:=\pi_1(M,m)$. (a) For every homomorphism $\rho\colon\Gamma\to G$ the associated bundle $P_\rho:=\tilde M\times_\rho G$ (extension of the structure group of the principal $\Gamma$-bundle $\tilde M$ along $\rho$) is a principal $G$-bundle over $M$ carrying a canonical flat connection $\omega_\rho$ (the one whose horizontal lifts are the images of paths in $\tilde M$; equivalently, in the induced vector bundle $\tilde M\times_\rho V$ for a representation $V$, $\pi^*\nabla s=d\hat s$ for $\Gamma$-equivariant $\hat s\colon\tilde M\to V$ — A's (47) with $a=0$), whose monodromy representation at the base point is $\rho$. (b) Conversely a flat connection $\omega$ on a principal $G$-bundle $P$ with monodromy $\rho_\omega$ at $p\in P_m$ is gauge equivalent to $(P_{\rho_\omega},\omega_{\rho_\omega})$ via an isomorphism of bundles with connection. (c) Gauge equivalent flat connections have conjugate monodromy representations (A-X3.3.2), and conjugate representations give isomorphic flat bundles. (d) Hence the assignment $\omega\mapsto[\rho_\omega]$ induces a bijection $\mathcal M^\flat_G(M):=\{(P,\omega):P$ a principal $G$-bundle over $M$, $\omega$ flat$\}/\text{isomorphism}\;\longrightarrow\;\mathcal R(M;G):=\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(M,m),G)/\text{conjugation}$; in particular for $G=GL_k(\mathbb R)$ this is A's correspondence between flat rank-$k$ vector bundles (all bundles at once) and $\mathcal R(M;GL_k(\mathbb R))$; and for a fixed bundle $P$, $\mathcal M^\flat(P)=\mathcal A^\flat(P)/\mathcal G(P)$ is the subset of $\mathcal R(M;G)$ of representations whose associated bundle is isomorphic to $P$ (with the gauge group $\mathcal G(P)$, versus $\operatorname{Aut}(P)$ over the identity: both give the same quotient — proved). The variants for $O(k)$, $U(k)$, $SO(k)$, $SU(k)$ (A-R3.3.6) are the cases of general $G$; proof: A gives the two constructions only; write: (a) $P_\rho$ is a principal $G$-bundle (III's extension theorem, with the discrete-group principal bundle $\tilde M$); the flat connection: on $\tilde M\times G$ take the product connection, which is invariant under the diagonal $\Gamma$-action (Lemma) and descends to $P_\rho$; flatness by local triviality; monodromy computed by lifting a loop to $\tilde M$; (b) the developing map: define $\Phi\colon\tilde M\times G\to P$, $\Phi(\tilde x,g):=\Gamma(\tilde c)(p)\cdot g$ where $\tilde c$ is any path from $\tilde m$ to $\tilde x$ projected to $M$ and $\Gamma$ its parallel transport — well defined by the homotopy-invariance theorem, $\Gamma$-invariant by definition of the monodromy, descends to an isomorphism $P_{\rho_\omega}\to P$ carrying $\omega_{\rho_\omega}$ to $\omega$ (horizontal lifts correspond); (c) both directions written; (d) assemble; reference: Kobayashi–Nomizu I Ch. II §9 (Prop 9.3), Taubes Ch. 13, Morita *Geometry of Characteristic Classes* §2.? ; spec: Mechanism summary: "a flat connection is the same as a way of transporting fibres along paths that only sees homotopy classes; the universal cover is the space of homotopy classes of paths, so a representation of $\pi_1$ is exactly the transport data."
