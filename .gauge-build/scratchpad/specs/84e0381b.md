## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: B-I2.5.2 (the Pfaffian is an invariant polynomial on $\mathfrak{so}(2m)$), B-E2.5.4; prereqs: [Def - Pfaffian, Thm - Determinant is Multiplicative, Def - Ad-Invariant Polynomial]; statement: For $A\in\mathfrak{so}(2m)$ and $B\in\operatorname{Mat}(2m\times2m;\mathbb R)$: (a) $\operatorname{Pf}(B^tAB)=\det(B)\operatorname{Pf}(A)$; (b) $\operatorname{Pf}(A)^2=\det A$; (c) $\operatorname{Pf}$ is $\operatorname{Ad}(SO(2m))$-invariant and homogeneous of degree $m$, hence in $I(SO(2m))$, but changes sign under $\operatorname{Ad}$ of $O(2m)\setminus SO(2m)$; (d) $\operatorname{Pf}$ of a block-diagonal matrix is the product of the Pfaffians of the blocks; (e) the polynomial $\det$ on $\mathfrak{so}(2m)$ equals $\operatorname{Pf}^2$, so $\operatorname{Pf}$ is not in the subring generated by the coefficients of the characteristic polynomial; proof: (a) via the $\Lambda^{2m}$ description: $B^t$ acts on $\sigma_A$ by $\Lambda^2(B^t)$ and on $\Lambda^{2m}$ by $\det B$; (b) every skew matrix is $B^tJB$ with $J$ block-diagonal standard (spectral theorem for skew-symmetric matrices — write the elementary reduction), then (a) and (d); (c) from (a) with $B=g^{-1}=g^t$; (d) direct; reference: Milnor–Stasheff Appendix C (Lemma C.? and the discussion of the Pfaffian), Kobayashi–Nomizu II Ch. XII §5 ; spec: Targets: the Euler class; $e^2=p_m$ (corollary of (b), proved in the Euler-class page).
