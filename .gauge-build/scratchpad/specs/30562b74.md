## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A §3.3 conventions ($\tilde M$, $\pi_1(M)$ acting by deck transformations, $\tilde M$ as a principal $\pi_1(M)$-bundle), A-D3.3.6 (prerequisite); prereqs: [Def - Covering Space, Def - Universal Cover, Def - Deck Transformation Group, Thm - Path Lifting and Homotopy Lifting, Thm - Lifting Criterion for Continuous Maps, Thm - Quotient by a Properly Discontinuous Action is a Manifold and a Covering Map, Def - Discrete Group and Properly Discontinuous Action, Def - Principal G-Bundle]; statement: Let $M$ be a connected manifold with base point $m$. (a) There is a simply connected covering $q\colon\tilde M\to M$ (the universal cover), unique up to isomorphism of coverings over $M$; $\tilde M$ carries a unique smooth structure making $q$ a smooth covering map (a local diffeomorphism), and $\tilde M$ is second countable. (b) The deck group $\operatorname{Deck}(\tilde M/M)$ acts smoothly, freely and properly discontinuously on $\tilde M$, transitively on each fibre, and $\tilde M/\operatorname{Deck}\cong M$. (c) There is an isomorphism $\pi_1(M,m)\cong\operatorname{Deck}(\tilde M/M)$ (after choosing $\tilde m\in q^{-1}(m)$), so $q\colon\tilde M\to M$ is a principal $\pi_1(M,m)$-bundle with the discrete group $\pi_1(M,m)$ acting on the right (by $\tilde x\cdot[\gamma]:=\varphi_{[\gamma]}^{-1}(\tilde x)$, or with the convention fixed on the page). (d) $\pi_1(M,m)$ is countable; proof: (a) Hatcher §1.3 construction (points are homotopy classes of paths from $m$; manifolds are locally path-connected and semi-locally simply connected since locally contractible), with the smooth structure lifted through the local homeomorphisms (Lee ISM 2e Prop 4.40 argument); second countability from countability of $\pi_1$ (d) and second countability of $M$; (b) AT II's Def page states these; prove them here: freeness and proper discontinuity via the unique-lifting property (linked AT II theorems), transitivity via the lifting criterion; (c) the standard bijection via endpoints of lifts, homomorphism property checked; (d) Lee, *Introduction to Topological Manifolds* 2e, Thm 7.21 (countable cover by coordinate balls, countably many components of intersections, loops generated by finitely many "junction" segments) — written in full; reference: Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.3 (pp. 63–65, Prop 1.39, Prop 1.40); Lee ISM 2e Prop 4.40; Lee TM 2e Thm 7.21; spec: NOTE for the coordinator: chapter XII (group 3) owns `Def - Covering and the Universal Covering` and `Thm - Lifting Criterion for Coverings`; this page proves the smooth-manifold existence statement because chapter V cannot depend on XII; XII should link this page for the manifold case rather than re-prove it, or the coordinator merges.
