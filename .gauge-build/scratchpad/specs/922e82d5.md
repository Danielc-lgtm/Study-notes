Then the EQUIVALENCE theorem, proved on the page: an $R$-orientation in Bär's sense is the same as a continuous family of local $R$-orientations $(\mu_x)$ in the sense of `Def - Local Homology and Local Orientation` (Hatcher's definition): a chart $\Phi$ at $x$ transports the standard generator of $H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus 0)$ (fixed once for all via the fixed generator $[c_{n-1}]$ of $H_{n-1}(S^{n-1})$ and the radial identification) to $\mu_x$; chart changes are $R$-orientation preserving at $x$ iff they induce the identity on $H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus 0) \cong R$, i.e. the two transported generators agree; continuity of the family is local constancy of the chart-transported generator. The fundamental class (Definition 4.3.10): for $M$ compact, connected, $R$-oriented, $[M] \in H_n(M;R)$ is the unique class whose image in $H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R)$ is $\mu_x$ for every $x$ (existence and uniqueness: forward link to `Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class`); Bär's description via the distinguished isomorphism $H_n(M;R) \xrightarrow{\partial} H_{n-1}(\dot B; R) \cong H_{n-1}(S^{n-1}; R) \cong R$ of Rem. 4.3.9 is proved equivalent on that theorem page. A class $\alpha \in H_k(X;R)$ is represented by $f : M \to X$ ($M$ a compact connected $R$-oriented $k$-manifold) iff $\alpha = f_*[M]$ (Definition 4.3.12). The degree of a map $f : M \to N$ between closed connected oriented $n$-manifolds: the integer $d$ with $f_*[M] = d [N]$ (this is the vault's homological degree; the RG IV page `Thm - Brouwer Degree is a Homotopy Invariant` is flagged and not used; homotopy invariance of this degree is immediate from `Thm - Homotopy Invariance of Singular Homology (Gauge Theory)` and is proved here). Examples verified: E4.3.1 (the picture for $n = 2$, $R = \mathbb{Z}$: a reflection of $\mathbb{R}^2$ reverses the generator — computed via the antipodal/reflection degree of `Ex - Degree of a Self-Map of the Sphere via Hurewicz`); E4.3.2 ($R = \mathbb{Z}/2$); E4.3.3 ($\mathrm{id}_M$ represents $[M]$); E4.3.4 (any $f : \{p\} \to X$ represents a generator of $H_0(X;R) \cong R$ for path connected $X$, from `Thm - Homology of a Point and of a Path-Connected Space`); non-example: the Möbius strip / $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is not $\mathbb{Z}$-orientable (forward link to `Ex - Top Homology of a Non-Orientable Closed Manifold Vanishes` for the proof).
