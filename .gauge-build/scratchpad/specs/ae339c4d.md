## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor; two parts); source items: A-T7.1.26, A-T7.1.27 (Remark 221); prereqs: [Thm - Dimension Formula for the Seiberg-Witten Moduli Space, Thm - Generic Absence of Reducible Solutions, Def - Determinant Line Bundle of a Family of Fredholm Operators, Thm - Homotopy Invariance of the Determinant Line Bundle, Thm - Orientation and Bordism Invariance of the Equivariant Moduli Space, Thm - Hodge Theorem for Elliptic Complexes]; statement: For $b_2^+\ge1$ and a generic $\eta$, $\mathcal M_\eta = \mathcal M^{\mathrm{irr}}_\eta$ is a smooth compact manifold of dimension $d$ (from the dimension-formula and reducible-avoidance and compactness pages), and it is orientable; a choice of orientation of the real lines $\det H^0(M;\mathbb R)$, $\det H^1(M;i\mathbb R)$ and $\det H^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)$ (equivalently of $H^0(M;\mathbb R)$, $H^1(M;\mathbb R)$ and a maximal positive subspace of $H^2(M;\mathbb R)$) orients it; proof: over the configuration space $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{irr}}$ the family $(\psi,A)\mapsto D_{(\psi,A)}$ of Fredholm operators has a determinant line bundle $\det D$ (`Def - Determinant Line Bundle of a Family of Fredholm Operators`); by homotopy through $D_0 + tB$, $\det D\cong\det D_0 = \det\slashed D_A^+\otimes\det(d^+ + d^*)$ (`Thm - Homotopy Invariance of the Determinant Line Bundle`); $\slashed D_A^+$ is complex-linear, so $\ker\slashed D_A^+$ and $\operatorname{coker}\slashed D_A^+\cong\ker\slashed D_A^-$ are complex vector spaces, hence canonically oriented, trivialising $\det\slashed D_A^+$; $\ker(d^+ + d^*) = H^1(M;i\mathbb R)$ and $\operatorname{coker}(d^+ + d^*) = H^0(M;i\mathbb R)\oplus H^2_+(M;i\mathbb R)$ are metric-independent up to the maximal-positive-subspace choice (all such being homotopic), so orientations of these cohomology groups trivialise $\det(d^+ + d^*)$; the resulting trivialisation of $\det D$ is $\mathcal G$-invariant (**the content-map gap that the complex orientation varies continuously and is gauge-invariant is filled by noting $\slashed D_A^+$ depends continuously on $(\psi,A)$ and the complex structure is gauge-natural**), so it orients $\mathcal M_\eta$ by `Thm - Orientation and Bordism Invariance of the Equivariant Moduli Space`; the non-canonicity (T7.1.27, Remark 221): the two conventions for orienting a complex space differ by $(-1)^{k(k-1)/2}$, and the cohomology orientations are a topological choice — recorded; spec: mechanism: **the deformation operator is homotopic to a decoupled operator whose Dirac half is complex (hence oriented) and whose Atiyah half has constant cohomology kernel and cokernel, so the determinant line is trivial and orients the moduli space**; targets: the signed count / fundamental class defining the invariant (§11.5).
