type: theorem (three parts); source items: A-E5.2.1, A-E5.2.2, A-E5.2.3 (source typo: Haydys Example 144 writes the inverse of Clifford multiplication by $\xi$ as $|\xi|^{-2}\xi$; with $u\cdot u = -|u|^2$ it is $-|\xi|^{-2}\xi$), A-T7.1.5 (partial: ellipticity of $\slashed D^\pm$ and of $d^++d^*$ as a Dirac-type operator is recorded here; the Atiyah complex itself is in XI); prereqs: [Def - Elliptic Differential Operator and Principal Symbol, Thm - The Principal Symbol is Well-Defined and Multiplicative, Def - Spinor Bundle and Dirac Operator, Def - Hodge Laplacian, Def - Hodge Star in Arbitrary Signature, Thm - Local Formula and Self-Adjointness of the Connection Laplacian]; statement: (i) The Laplacian $\Delta = -\sum\partial_i^2$ on $\mathbb R^n$ has $\sigma_\Delta(\xi) = -|\xi|^2$ and is elliptic. (ii) The Laplace–Beltrami operator $\Delta f = -\star d\star df$ on an oriented Riemannian manifold has coordinate expression $\Delta f = -|g|^{-1/2}\sum_{i,j}\partial_i(|g|^{1/2}g^{ij}\partial_jf)$ and symbol $\sigma_\Delta(\xi) = -|\xi|_g^2$; more generally every connection Laplacian $\nabla^*\nabla$ and the Hodge Laplacian $dd^*+d^*d$ have symbol $-|\xi|^2\,\mathrm{id}$ and are elliptic. (iii) The Dirac operator of any Dirac bundle has $\sigma_D(\xi) = \xi^\sharp\cdot$ (Clifford multiplication), invertible with inverse $-|\xi|^{-2}\xi^\sharp\cdot$, hence elliptic; in even dimensions the chiral operators $D^\pm:\Gamma(E^\pm)\to\Gamma(E^\mp)$ are elliptic; $d + d^*$ on $\Lambda T^*M$ is elliptic, and in dimension four $d^++d^*:\Omega^1\to\Omega^2_+\oplus\Omega^0$ is elliptic (symbol $\xi\mapsto(\xi\wedge\cdot)^+ - \iota_\xi$, injective on the 4-dimensional $\Lambda^1$ into the 4-dimensional $\Lambda^2_+\oplus\Lambda^0$, hence invertible — computed); proof: (i) direct; (ii) compute $\star d\star df$ in coordinates using $\star dx^j = \sum_i|g|^{1/2}g^{ij}\iota_{\partial_i}(dx^1\wedge\dots\wedge dx^n)$ (from `Thm - Properties of the Hodge Star in Arbitrary Signature` (1)), then read off the top-order term; the connection-Laplacian symbol from its local formula; (iii) $D = \sum e_i\cdot\nabla_{e_i}$, the symbol is $\sum\xi(e_i)e_i\cdot = \xi^\sharp\cdot$ by (i) of the symbol page; $d^++d^*$: explicit symbol computation with the bases of $\Lambda^2_+$; spec: mechanism: **ellipticity of the Laplacian is the positive-definiteness of the metric; ellipticity of the Dirac operator is the Clifford relation**.
