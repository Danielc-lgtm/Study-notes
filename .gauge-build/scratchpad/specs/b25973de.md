## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: local homology $H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R)$ of a topological $n$-manifold, the radial identifications of Bär's setup, local $R$-orientation $\mu_x$ as a generator, continuity of a family of local orientations); source items: R4.3.1, T4.3.1, (Hatcher §3.3 pp. 233–234); prereqs: [Def - Topological Manifold, Thm - Excision Theorem for Singular Homology, Thm - Long Exact Sequence of a Pair in Singular Homology, Thm - Homology of Spheres, Thm - Homotopy Invariance of Singular Homology (Gauge Theory)]; spec: For $x$ in a topological $n$-manifold $M$ and a chart ball $B \ni x$: $H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R) \cong H_n(B, B \setminus \{x\}; R) \cong \tilde H_{n-1}(B \setminus \{x\}; R) \cong \tilde H_{n-1}(S^{n-1}; R) \cong R$ (excision, the pair sequence, homotopy invariance, spheres — each isomorphism written out); Bär's setup: $\dot B(x,r) := B(x,r) \setminus \{x\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$, $F_{x,r} : S^{n-1} \to \dot B(x,r)$, $y \mapsto x + \tfrac{r}{2} y$, a homotopy equivalence, and T4.3.1 proved: for $r_1 < r_2$, $(F_{x,r_2})_* = (\iota_{r_1, r_2})_* (F_{x,r_1})_*$ because $\iota_{r_1,r_2} \circ F_{x,r_1} \simeq F_{x,r_2}$ by the radial homotopy $y \mapsto x + \tfrac{1}{2}((1-t) r_1 + t r_2) y$; the identification $H_{n-1}(\dot B(x,r); R) \cong H_n(B(x,r), \dot B(x,r); R) \cong H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus \{x\}; R)$ relating Bär's and Hatcher's pictures (proved). A local $R$-orientation at $x$ is a generator $\mu_x$ of the $R$-module $H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\}; R) \cong R$ (for $R = \mathbb{Z}$: one of two; for $R = \mathbb{Z}/2$: unique); for a chart ball $B \ni x, y$, the isomorphisms $H_n(M, M \setminus B) \to H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\})$ (the map $H_n(M, M \setminus B) \to H_n(M, M \setminus \{x\})$ is an isomorphism for a ball $B$ in a chart, proved via excision to $(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus B) \to (\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus \{x\})$ and the deformation retraction of $\mathbb{R}^n \setminus \{x\}$ onto $\mathbb{R}^n \setminus B$ for a closed ball $B$ centred at $x$, and then for $y \in B$ by a further deformation) let one transport $\mu_x$ to $\mu_y$; a family $(\mu_x)_{x \in M}$ is continuous (locally consistent) if every $x$ has a chart ball $B$ and a class $\mu_B \in H_n(M, M \setminus B; R)$ restricting to $\mu_y$ for all $y \in B$. Examples verified: $\mathbb{R}^n$ with $\mu_x$ the image of a fixed generator under translation; $S^{n-1} \cong$ boundary picture; non-example: on the Möbius strip no continuous $\mathbb{Z}$-family exists (forward link to `Def - The R-Orientation Covering`, where it is proved). Calibration check: identify $H_1(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}; \mathbb{Z})$ with $\mathbb{Z}$ and the two generators with the two "directions"; explain why $\mathbb{Z}/2$ has a unique generator.
