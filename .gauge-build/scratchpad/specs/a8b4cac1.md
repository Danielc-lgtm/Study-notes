type: theorem; source items: T4.3.6; prereqs: [Def - The R-Orientation Covering, Thm - Lifting Criterion for Coverings]; statement: If $M$ is a simply connected (hence connected, locally path connected) topological manifold then $M$ is $R$-orientable for every $R$; more precisely the page states: (i) simply connected implies $R$-orientable; (ii) for connected $M$, $\mathbb{Z}$-orientable iff the orientation double cover is disconnected (Hatcher Prop. 3.25(a)); proof: source sketch (lift $\mathrm{id}_M$ through the covering by Lemma 4.1.31, the lift is a section, a section is an orientation); the page writes it: by `Thm - Lifting Criterion for Coverings` applied to $\mathrm{id}_M : M \to M$ and the covering $\tilde M \to M$ with $M$ simply connected, a lift $\tilde{\mathrm{id}}_M$ through any point $(x, \mu_x)$ exists; it is a continuous section, hence an $R$-orientation by the section–orientation correspondence proved on the Def page; (ii) by the same correspondence and the fact that a two-sheeted covering of a connected space admits a section iff it is disconnected. spec: Targets: chapter XIII standing hypotheses (simply connected $4$-manifolds are orientable, so "oriented" there is a choice of one of two orientations, which the intersection form's sign tracks).
