## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: D4.3.3, I4.3.4 (topology on $\tilde M$); prereqs: [Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class, Def - Local Homology and Local Orientation, Def - Covering and the Universal Covering]; spec: $\tilde M := \{(x, \mu_x) \mid x \in M, \mu_x \text{ a local } R\text{-orientation at } x\}$ (equivalently, Bär's $\bigsqcup_x \mathcal{K}_x/\sim$ with $\Phi \sim \Psi$ iff $\Phi \circ \Psi^{-1}$ is $R$-orientation preserving at $0$ — the bijection with Hatcher's description proved as on the previous page; note $\tilde M$ has fibres in bijection with the generators of $R$, so with the units $R^\times$), topologised by the basis $U(\mu_B) := \{(y, \mu_B|_y) \mid y \in B\}$ for chart balls $B$ and $\mu_B \in H_n(M, M \setminus B; R)$ a generator (Hatcher p. 234); proved on the page: this is a basis, $p : \tilde M \to M$ is a covering (a fibre bundle with discrete fibre the set of generators of $R$; for $R = \mathbb{Z}$ a two-sheeted covering), and continuous sections of $p$ correspond bijectively to $R$-orientations of $M$ (Bär's claim in Rem. 4.3.8); for $R = \mathbb{Z}$ the subspace $\tilde M_{\pm} \subset \tilde M$ of the two generators is the orientable double cover and the page proves it is connected iff $M$ is connected and non-orientable (link `Def - Orientable Double Cover`, AT II, and check the definitions agree). Examples: $\tilde{S^n} \cong S^n \sqcup S^n$ (verified), the Möbius strip's double cover is the annulus (link `Ex - The Orientable Double Cover of the Möbius Strip`, verified against the definition here). Calibration check: describe the fibre over a point for $R = \mathbb{Z}/3$; explain why a section over a chart ball always exists; why the covering is trivial iff $M$ is orientable (for connected $M$, $R = \mathbb{Z}$).
