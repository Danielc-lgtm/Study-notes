## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: none of the sources (mechanism behind A-E2.4.1, A-T2.4.3's downstream use and R3.1.6/(94)); prereqs: [Thm - Principal Bundles are Classified by Cocycles, Thm - Homotopic Maps Pull Back Isomorphic Principal Bundles]; statement: Let $X$ be a closed connected $n$-manifold, $D\subset X$ a closed coordinate disc with interior $D^\circ$ and boundary $S=\partial D\cong S^{n-1}$, and $G$ a Lie group. (a) For every smooth $g\colon S\to G$ there is a principal $G$-bundle $P_g$, trivial over $X\setminus D^\circ$ and over $D$, with transition function (the restriction to a collar of $S$ of) $g$ — constructed as the cocycle bundle on the cover $\{X\setminus D^\circ$-thickened, $D$-thickened$\}$ using a collar. (b) Every principal $G$-bundle $P$ that is trivial over $X\setminus D^\circ$ and over $D$ is isomorphic to some $P_g$; if $G$ is connected and $P$ is trivial over $X\setminus\{x_0\}$ for a point $x_0\in D^\circ$, then $P$ is trivial over $X\setminus D^\circ$ and over $D$ (by (c) of the previous theorem), hence of the form $P_g$. (c) $P_g\cong P_{g'}$ iff there are smooth $a\colon X\setminus D^\circ\to G$ and $b\colon D\to G$ with $g'=(a|_S)\,g\,(b|_S)$; in particular $P_g\cong P_{g'}$ whenever $g$ and $g'$ are smoothly homotopic (a homotopy of clutching maps extends over a collar) ; (d) $P_g$ is trivial iff $g=(a|_S)(b|_S)$ as in (c) with $g'=e$; proof: (a),(b) from the cocycle theorem and the collar neighbourhood of $S$ (existence of a collar for $\partial D$ in a coordinate disc is explicit: $D$ is a round ball in a chart); (c) both directions from the cocycle theorem's cohomology statement, plus the homotopy clause via the previous theorem applied to $S\times[0,1]$; reference: Hatcher VBKT §1.2 "Clutching functions" (Prop 1.11 and the discussion after it), Atiyah *K-Theory* §1.4; spec: Targets: the degree of a line bundle over a surface (§3.6); the Chern number of an $SU(2)$-bundle over a 4-manifold (§3.6, VI); the mapping-torus "only if" clause: $E_\phi$ trivial iff $\phi\simeq\operatorname{id}$ through diffeomorphisms — stated and proved here as corollary for the structure-group version (record that for general fibre bundles the same proof works with $\operatorname{Diff}(F)$ as structure "group" — only the principal case is claimed).
