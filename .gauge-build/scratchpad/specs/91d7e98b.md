## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (the identification $\operatorname{Pic}(M) \cong H^2(M;\mathbb{Z})$ that chapters III–XI deferred to chapter XII, in the form chapter XIII needs; carries Haydys I2.4.6 and Bär I5.3.1/fn. 3 in the de Rham + Čech form); prereqs: [Def - Singular Cohomology with Coefficients and the Kronecker Pairing, Thm - Universal Coefficient Theorem for Cohomology, Thm - De Rham Theorem via Smooth Singular Chains, Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated, Def - Principal G-Bundle, Def - Transition Functions and the Cocycle Condition, Def - Fibre Bundle, Def - Partition of Unity on a Manifold]; statement: Let $M$ be a smooth manifold admitting a finite good cover (every compact manifold does, by `Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated` Lemma). (i) Isomorphism classes of Hermitian line bundles (equivalently principal $U(1)$-bundles) form an abelian group $\operatorname{Pic}(M)$ under tensor product, and $L \mapsto [\{g_{\alpha\beta}\}]$ identifies $\operatorname{Pic}(M)$ with the Čech cohomology $\check H^1(M; \underline{U(1)})$ of the sheaf of smooth $U(1)$-valued functions on a good cover. (ii) The connecting homomorphism $\delta$ of the coefficient sequence $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R} \xrightarrow{\exp(2\pi i \cdot)} U(1) \to 0$ is an isomorphism $\delta : \check H^1(M; \underline{U(1)}) \xrightarrow{\cong} \check H^2(M; \mathbb{Z})$, because $\check H^k(M; \underline{\mathbb{R}}) = 0$ for $k \ge 1$ on a good cover (the sheaf of smooth functions is fine: a partition of unity subordinate to the cover gives an explicit contracting homotopy of the Čech complex — proved on the page). (iii) On a good cover $\check H^\bullet(M; \mathbb{Z}) \cong H^\bullet(M; \mathbb{Z})$ (singular), the Čech–singular comparison, proved on the page by the same Mayer–Vietoris induction over the cover as `Thm - Homology of a Compact Smooth Manifold is Finitely Generated`, using that every non-empty finite intersection is contractible. Composing, $c_1 := \delta : \operatorname{Pic}(M) \xrightarrow{\cong} H^2(M; \mathbb{Z})$ is a group isomorphism, natural in $M$; and it is compatible with the de Rham first Chern class of chapter VI, sending $L$ under $H^2(M;\mathbb{Z}) \to H^2_{dR}(M)$ (`Thm - De Rham Theorem via Smooth Singular Chains`) to $[\tfrac{i}{2\pi} F_A]$; proof: (i) the bijection between line bundles and $U(1)$-cocycles from `Def - Transition Functions and the Cocycle Condition` (isomorphic bundles give cohomologous cocycles; every cocycle is realised — verified); (ii) fineness of $\underline{\mathbb{R}}$ and the long exact Čech sequence of the coefficient sequence; (iii) the Čech–singular comparison for good covers (Mayer–Vietoris induction / the nerve); compatibility by tracing $\tfrac{1}{2\pi i}\log g_{\alpha\beta}$ through the connecting map and its image in real cohomology, which is the curvature class (done in full). What is proved and what would be needed: the page PROVES $\operatorname{Pic}(M) \cong H^2(M;\mathbb{Z})$ for every manifold with a finite good cover (in particular every compact four-manifold of chapters XII–XIII), using only Čech machinery over a good cover and the singular tools of chapter XII; it does NOT use sheaf cohomology of the structure sheaf or the exponential sequence of holomorphic line bundles (which would need $\check H^\bullet(M;\mathcal{O})$). Reference: Bott–Tu, *Differential Forms in Algebraic Topology*, §I.5 and §II.10–11 (the Čech–de Rham and Čech–singular comparisons); Griffiths–Harris Ch. 1 §1 (the exponential sequence for line bundles, in the smooth category); Hatcher §3.1 (the singular side). spec: this is the page manifest-1's header defers to; it discharges the deferred identification and removes the standing restriction for chapter XIII on the pages that cite it. Why-true: "a line bundle is glued from $U(1)$ transition data, and the obstruction to taking a global logarithm of that data is exactly an integral second cohomology class". Targets: chapter XIII `Thm - Reducible Instantons Correspond to Classes of Self-Intersection One` and `Def - Fake Complex Projective Plane and the E8 Manifold` (each $\alpha \in H^2(X;\mathbb{Z})$ is $c_1$ of a unique Hermitian line bundle); chapter III's `Thm - Classification of Principal U(1)-Bundles by the First Chern Class` (retroactively identifies its $\operatorname{Pic}$ with $H^2(-;\mathbb{Z})$).
