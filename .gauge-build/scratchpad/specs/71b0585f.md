type: theorem (anchor); source items: A-T4.4.2 (Theorem 132), A-R4.4.2; prereqs: [Def - Spinor Bundle and Dirac Operator, Def - Curvature Endomorphism of a Dirac Bundle, Thm - Local Formula and Self-Adjointness of the Connection Laplacian, Thm - Existence of Synchronous Orthonormal Frames, Def - Curvature of a Vector-Bundle Connection]; statement: For a Dirac bundle $E$ with Dirac operator $D$: $D^2 = \nabla^*\nabla + \mathcal R$; proof: Haydys's proof with all four gaps filled: fix $m$ and a synchronous orthonormal frame at $m$ (previous theorem); at $m$: $D^2s = \sum_ie_i\cdot\nabla_{e_i}(\sum_je_j\cdot\nabla_{e_j}s) = \sum_{i,j}e_i\cdot e_j\cdot\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_j}s$ (condition (3) and $\nabla^{LC}_{e_i}e_j(m) = 0$) $= \sum_ie_i\cdot e_i\cdot\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}s + \tfrac12\sum_{i\ne j}e_i\cdot e_j\cdot(\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_j}-\nabla_{e_j}\nabla_{e_i})s$ (anticommutation; symmetrising the $i\ne j$ sum) $= -\sum_i\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}s + \tfrac12\sum_{i\ne j}e_ie_jR_{e_i,e_j}s$ ($e_i^2 = -1$; $[\nabla_{e_i},\nabla_{e_j}] = R_{e_i,e_j} + \nabla_{[e_i,e_j]}$ and $[e_i,e_j](m) = 0$) $= \nabla^*\nabla s + \mathcal Rs$ at $m$ (part (a) of the connection-Laplacian page in a synchronous frame; the $i = j$ terms of $\mathcal R$ vanish); both sides are frame-independent so the identity holds everywhere; spec: mechanism: **the Dirac operator squared is the connection Laplacian plus a zero-order curvature term because the Clifford relations turn the symmetric part of $\nabla^2$ into a trace and the antisymmetric part into curvature**; Bridges: Bochner's method (`Thm - Bochner's Theorem`, Hodge I, sketched — the exercise below gives a complete proof of the 1-form case); Cross-Field: Kato's inequality, Bochner vanishing theorems.
