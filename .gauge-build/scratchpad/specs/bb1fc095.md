## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (Hatcher Thm 3.2; needed for unimodularity over $\mathbb{Z}$, the de Rham identification, and the parity/spin argument); prereqs: [Def - Singular Cohomology with Coefficients and the Kronecker Pairing, Def - Hom and Ext of Abelian Groups, Thm - Zig-Zag Lemma for Short Exact Sequences of Chain Complexes]; statement: For any space $X$ (or pair), any abelian group $G$ and any $n$, there is a natural short exact sequence $0 \to \operatorname{Ext}(H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb{Z}), G) \to H^n(X;G) \xrightarrow{\text{ev}} \operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb{Z}), G) \to 0$ which splits (non-naturally). Corollaries: (a) if $H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb{Z})$ is free (in particular zero) then $H^n(X;G) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb{Z}), G)$; (b) for $G = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{Q}$, $H^n(X;G) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb{Z}), G)$ always; (c) if $H_*(X;\mathbb{Z})$ is finitely generated then $H^n(X;\mathbb{Z}) \cong F_n \oplus T_{n-1}$ where $F_n$ is the free part of $H_n$ and $T_{n-1}$ the torsion of $H_{n-1}$; (d) if $H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb{Z})$ is free then $H^n(X;\mathbb{Z}/2) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb{Z}), \mathbb{Z}/2)$; proof: source: none; the page follows Hatcher Thm 3.2 for free chain complexes: the short exact sequence $0 \to Z_n \to C_n \to B_{n-1} \to 0$ splits (since $B_{n-1}$ is free as a subgroup of a free abelian group; the singular chain groups are not finitely generated, so the page proves "every subgroup of a free abelian group is free" in full generality as Lemma 0, by transfinite induction over a well-ordered basis — Lang, *Algebra*, Appendix 2 §2 — with no restriction to countable complexes), dualises, and follows Hatcher's proof exactly: the sequence $0 \to \operatorname{Hom}(B_{n-1}, G) \to \operatorname{Hom}(C_n, G) \to \operatorname{Hom}(Z_n, G) \to 0$, its long exact sequence, and the identification of the connecting map with the map induced by $B_{n-1} \hookrightarrow Z_{n-1}$, whose cokernel is $\operatorname{Ext}(H_{n-1}, G)$ by the free resolution $0 \to B_{n-1} \to Z_{n-1} \to H_{n-1} \to 0$; splitting from the splitting of $C_n \to B_{n-1}$; naturality. Reference: Hatcher Thm 3.2, pp. 195–198. spec: Why-true: "cohomology is the dual of homology up to the torsion that duality cannot see, and that torsion is the $\operatorname{Ext}$ of the degree below"; Sources: simply connected spaces ($H_1 = 0$ so $H^2 \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_2, \mathbb{Z})$ — the chapter XIII input); real coefficients (de Rham); Targets: $H^2(X;\mathbb{Z})$ free for simply connected $X$; unimodularity of the intersection form; $H^2(X;\mathbb{Z}/2) = \operatorname{Hom}(H_2, \mathbb{Z}/2)$ for the spin criterion.
