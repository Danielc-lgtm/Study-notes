type: theorem; source items: A-X3.2.1(c), A-X3.2.1(d), A §3.2 conventions ($\deg g$ for $g\colon M\to SU(2)\cong S^3$); prereqs: [Thm - Transgression Formula and the Chern-Simons Form, Thm - Gauge Action on Connection Matrices, Thm - The Brouwer Degree is an Integer and a Homotopy Invariant, Thm - Classification of Principal SU(2)-Bundles over Four-Manifolds, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]; statement: For $A\in\Omega^1(M;\mathfrak{su}(2))$ on a closed oriented 3-manifold and $g\colon M\to SU(2)$ smooth, with $A^g=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg$: $\operatorname{cs}(A^g)=\operatorname{cs}(A)-\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}((g^{-1}dg)^3)-d\operatorname{tr}(dg\,g^{-1}\wedge A)$ (signs tracked), hence $\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\operatorname{cs}(A^g)=\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\operatorname{cs}(A)-\tfrac{1}{24\pi^2}\int_M\operatorname{tr}((g^{-1}dg)^3)=\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\operatorname{cs}(A)\mp\deg g$; consequently $\vartheta(A\cdot g)=\vartheta(A)+\deg g$ (mod the sign fixed by orientation conventions; the writer states the sign) in $\mathbb R$, and $\vartheta$ is well defined in $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ independently of the trivialisation; proof: the algebraic identity by direct expansion (every term displayed); Stokes kills the exact term; $W(g)=\deg g$ from III (restated with the value $24\pi^2$); reference: Freed, *Classical Chern–Simons theory I* Prop 2.? ; Donaldson–Kronheimer §2.1.4 ; spec: Mechanism: "the Chern–Simons form is not gauge invariant; its variation is an exact term plus the pull-back of the bi-invariant 3-form of $SU(2)$, whose integral counts the degree."
