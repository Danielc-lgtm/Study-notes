## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: I4.1.3 (fibre-bundle part), I4.1.4 (continuity of the lifted family); prereqs: [Def - Fibre Bundle, Thm - Lebesgue Number Lemma for Compact Metric Spaces, Def - Homotopy and Homotopy Equivalence]; statement: Let $p : E \to B$ be a fibre bundle (locally trivial, with any fibre $F$). (i) For every $n \ge 0$, every homotopy $G : I^n \times I \to B$ and every lift $g_0 : I^n \times \{0\} \to E$ of $G|_{I^n \times \{0\}}$ there is a homotopy $\tilde G : I^n \times I \to E$ with $p \circ \tilde G = G$ and $\tilde G|_{I^n \times \{0\}} = g_0$. (ii) (Relative form) The same holds with the lift prescribed on $I^n \times \{0\} \cup \partial I^n \times I$; equivalently, since $(I^n \times I, I^n \times \{0\} \cup \partial I^n \times I) \cong (I^n \times I, I^n \times \{0\})$, on $J^n$. (iii) Consequently $p$ has the homotopy lifting property with respect to all CW complexes — not needed and not proved; the page states only (i)–(ii); proof: source: asserted; the page follows Hatcher Prop. 4.48 (fibre bundles are Serre fibrations): Lebesgue-number subdivision of $I^n \times I$ into small cubes mapped into trivialising opens, and inductive lifting cube by cube in a fixed order using the trivialisation $p^{-1}(U) \cong U \times F$ where lifting is projection onto the second factor composed with a retraction $I^n \times I \to I^n \times \{0\} \cup \partial I^n \times I$ (explicit radial retraction formula); the tube lemma needed to ensure a subdivision exists is re-proved inline as Lemma 1 (the vault page is flagged); the homeomorphism of pairs in (ii) is written explicitly (Hatcher p. 376, "$J^n$" trick). Reference: Hatcher Prop. 4.48 and the paragraph following Thm 4.41; Bredon VII.6; Steenrod §11. spec: Why-true: "locally a bundle is a product and products lift trivially; compactness of the cube makes the local lifts finite in number and the retraction glues them". Sources: coverings (discrete fibre) — then the lift is unique; principal bundles; associated bundles. Targets: the boundary homomorphism, the long exact sequence, and — in chapter XIII — homotopy invariance of pull-back bundles is NOT derived here (that is chapter III's business).
