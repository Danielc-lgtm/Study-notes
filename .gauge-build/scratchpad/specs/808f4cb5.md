## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (compound: three interlocking notions); source items: B-D3.1.1, B-D3.1.2, B-D3.1.3; prereqs: [Def - Bilinear Form, Def - Symmetric and Alternating Bilinear Form, Thm - Sylvester's Law of Inertia, Def - Alternating Tensor and Lambda k V Dual, Def - Orientation of a Vector Space]; spec: $V$ real $n$-dimensional with a non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ (not necessarily definite); a *generalized orthonormal basis* $e_1,\dots,e_n$ has $\langle e_i,e_j\rangle = 0$ ($i\ne j$), $\langle e_j,e_j\rangle = \epsilon_j = \pm1$; the *index* $p$ is the number of $j$ with $\epsilon_j = -1$ (well-defined by Sylvester — wikilink); the *induced inner product* on $\Lambda^kV^*$ is $\langle\omega,\eta\rangle := \sum_{i_1<\dots<i_k}\epsilon_{i_1}\cdots\epsilon_{i_k}\,\omega(e_{i_1},\dots,e_{i_k})\,\eta(e_{i_1},\dots,e_{i_k})$; the *volume form* is $\mathrm{vol} := e_1^*\wedge\dots\wedge e_n^*$ for a positively oriented generalized ONB, with $\langle\mathrm{vol},\mathrm{vol}\rangle = (-1)^p$ and $\mathrm{vol}\mapsto-\mathrm{vol}$ under orientation reversal. Existence of a generalized ONB is proved in the Examples (Gram–Schmidt on a non-degenerate form: diagonalise via `Thm - Diagonalization of a Symmetric Bilinear Form`, Linear Algebra IX, then rescale). Examples: Euclidean $\mathbb R^n$ ($p = 0$); Minkowski $\mathbb R^{1,3}$ with $(-,+,+,+)$ ($p=1$), where $\langle dt\wedge dx, dt\wedge dx\rangle = -1$; non-example: a degenerate form (no generalized ONB). Convention callout: Bär's "index" is the number of negative signs; the vault's `Def - Semi-Riemannian Metric and Signature` writes signature $(n-s,s)$ with $s = p$; SR XXI uses $(+,-,-,-)$, i.e. $p = 3$ for the same physics. Calibration check: compute $\langle e_1^*\wedge e_2^*, e_1^*\wedge e_2^*\rangle$ on Minkowski for spacelike and mixed pairs; verify $\langle\mathrm{vol},\mathrm{vol}\rangle = (-1)^p$ on $\mathbb R^{1,3}$.
