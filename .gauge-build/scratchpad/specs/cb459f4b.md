## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor); source items: A-T5.1.1 (ii) ((137)), A-I5.1.2, A-I5.1.4 (Arzelà–Ascoli is *not* used — the Fourier route replaces it; recorded in the Motivation as the route of Haydys's Remark 138); prereqs: [Thm - Sobolev Spaces of Sections on a Compact Manifold via Charts, Def - Compact Operator, Thm - Basic Properties of Compact Operators, Thm - Convergence of the Lattice Sum]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact manifold, $E\to M$ a vector bundle, $k>m$ integers. The inclusion $H_k(M;E)\hookrightarrow H_m(M;E)$ is a compact operator: every sequence bounded in $H_k$ has a subsequence converging in $H_m$. **Convention callout:** Haydys's (ii) for $W^{k,p}\subset W^{m,q}$ with $k-\frac np>m-\frac nq$, $k>m$ (Rellich–Kondrachov) recorded as context (Evans 5.7.1); proof: on the torus, the inclusion is the norm limit of the finite-rank truncations $T_R u := \sum_{|\xi|\le R}\hat u(\xi)e^{i\langle\xi,x\rangle}$: $\|u - T_Ru\|_m^2 = \sum_{|\xi|>R}(1+|\xi|^2)^m|\hat u|^2\le(1+R^2)^{m-k}\|u\|_k^2$, so $\|\iota - T_R\|_{H_k\to H_m}\le(1+R^2)^{(m-k)/2}\to0$; compact by (i) of the basic-properties page; on $M$: the inclusion is $\sum_i$ (extension by zero) $\circ$ (torus inclusion) $\circ$ (localisation $u\mapsto(\phi_iu)\circ\kappa_i^{-1}$), each factor bounded, so compact by the ideal property and (iii); spec: mechanism: **a bounded set in $H_k$ has uniformly small Fourier tails in $H_m$, so it is totally bounded in $H_m$**; targets: finite-dimensionality of kernels of elliptic operators; compactness of the Seiberg–Witten moduli space.
