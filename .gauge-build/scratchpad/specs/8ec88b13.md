## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: none of the sources (needed for the clutching arguments); prereqs: [Thm - Fundamental Theorem on Flows, Ex - Compactly Supported Vector Fields are Complete, Thm - Existence of Smooth Bump Functions]; statement: Let $M$ be a connected manifold, $x,y\in M$ and $K\subset M\setminus\{x,y\}$ a closed set with $M\setminus K$ connected. Then there is a diffeomorphism $h\colon M\to M$, smoothly isotopic to the identity through diffeomorphisms fixing $K$ pointwise, with $h(x)=y$. Consequently any finite set of points of a connected manifold of dimension $\ge2$ can be moved into any prescribed coordinate disc by a diffeomorphism isotopic to the identity; proof: Milnor TDV §4 "Homogeneity Lemma": inside a coordinate ball, flow along a compactly supported vector field; the set of points reachable from $x$ by such diffeomorphisms is open and closed; for the finite-set clause use connectedness of $M$ minus finitely many points (proved: removing a finite set from a connected manifold of dimension $\ge2$ leaves it connected — a chart argument) and iterate; reference: Milnor, *Topology from the Differentiable Viewpoint*, §4, Lemma (Homogeneity) and its proof; spec: Targets: gathering the zeros of a section into one disc, so that a bundle is trivial off a disc.
