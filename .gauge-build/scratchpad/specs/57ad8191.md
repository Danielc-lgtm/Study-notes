## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: (Hatcher §3.2; needed for the cup-product form); prereqs: [Def - Singular Cohomology with Coefficients and the Kronecker Pairing]; spec: For a commutative ring $R$, $(\phi \smile \psi)(\sigma) := \phi(\sigma|_{[v_0, \dots, v_k]}) \, \psi(\sigma|_{[v_k, \dots, v_{k+l}]})$ with the front and back faces written as explicit compositions with affine maps $\Delta^k \to \Delta^{k+l}$, $\Delta^l \to \Delta^{k+l}$; the Leibniz formula $\delta(\phi \smile \psi) = \delta\phi \smile \psi + (-1)^k \phi \smile \delta\psi$ (proved on the page: Hatcher Lemma 3.6, the term-by-term cancellation written out); hence $\smile$ descends to $H^k(X;R) \times H^l(X;R) \to H^{k+l}(X;R)$; relative versions $H^k(X,A) \times H^l(X,B) \to H^{k+l}(X, A \cup B)$ for $A, B$ open (or $A$, $B$ with $\operatorname{int}$ covering $A \cup B$; the page proves the case needed later: $A$, $B$ open, via the small chains theorem, Hatcher p. 209). Examples verified: cup product with the unit $1 \in H^0$; the cup product of two $1$-cocycles on $S^1$ is zero for degree reasons; non-example: the cup product is not commutative at cochain level (an explicit computation on $\Delta^2$). Calibration check: check the Leibniz sign for $k = 1$, $l = 1$; write the front $1$-face of a $2$-simplex; verify naturality $f^*(\alpha \smile \beta) = f^*\alpha \smile f^*\beta$ at cochain level.
