type: definition (compound: intersection number of transverse oriented surfaces, the sign $\varepsilon(p)$, the intersection form $Q_X$, orientation reversal $\overline{X}$, the notation $k\mathbb{CP}^2 \# l\overline{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, the passage to $H_2/\text{torsion}$ in general); source items: D5.1.2, D5.1.3, T5.1.8, R5.1.2, T5.1.5 (statement; proof on the connected-sum theorem page); prereqs: [Thm - Every Class in the Second Homology of a Simply Connected Four-Manifold is Represented by an Embedded Surface, Def - Orientation of a Vector Space, Def - Orientation of a Smooth Manifold, Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class, Def - Connected Sum of Manifolds, Def - Bilinear Form]; spec: For embedded compact oriented surfaces $S_1, S_2 \subset X$ meeting transversally (or not at all): $S_1 \cdot S_2 := \sum_{p \in S_1 \cap S_2} \varepsilon(p)$, $\varepsilon(p) = +1$ iff the orientation of $T_pS_1 \oplus T_pS_2 = T_pX$ (in this order) agrees with that of $X$; $Q_X : H_2(X;\mathbb{Z}) \times H_2(X;\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathbb{Z}$, $Q_X([S_1], [S_2]) := S_1 \cdot S_2$ `(source typo 27: Def. 5.1.2 prints $S_2 \cdot S_2$; read $S_1 \cdot S_2$)`, with the representability theorem guaranteeing representatives and the transverse-pair corollary guaranteeing transverse pairs; well-definedness (independence of representatives), bilinearity and symmetry are proved on `Thm - Intersection Form Equals the Cup Product Form` (which carries T5.1.2 as its Corollary; forward link — the definition page states well-definedness, bilinearity and symmetry as the theorem to be proved there and does not assume them). $Q_{\overline{X}} = -Q_X$ (T5.1.5: reversing the orientation of $X$ flips every $\varepsilon(p)$; proved here as a corollary). T5.1.8 proved: a symmetric bilinear form $Q : H \times H \to \mathbb{Z}$ on a finitely generated abelian group kills torsion ($k a = 0 \Rightarrow k Q(a,b) = Q(ka, b) = 0 \Rightarrow Q(a,b) = 0$), so $Q$ descends to $H/\text{Torsion} \cong \mathbb{Z}^r$; the page remarks (R5.1.2) that for the simply connected $X$ of the standing hypothesis $H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$ is already free (forward link to `Thm - Homology of a Simply Connected Closed Four-Manifold`) and that the general definition for closed oriented four-manifolds is the cup-product form on $H^2/\text{torsion}$ (`Thm - Intersection Form Equals the Cup Product Form`). Notation $k\mathbb{CP}^2 \# l\overline{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ (D5.1.3) with $\overline{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ the orientation reversal of $\mathbb{CP}^2$. Examples: $S^2 \times \{p\}$ and $\{p\} \times S^2$ in $S^2 \times S^2$ meet transversally in one point with $\varepsilon = +1$ (verified with the product orientation); non-example: two surfaces meeting along a curve are not transverse and $S_1 \cdot S_2$ is not defined for them — one must push off first.
