## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (three parts; X4.4.1(a)–(c) are its content); source items: A-D4.4.1 (→ inside, Notation/Statement: $\nabla^*\nabla := \nabla^*\circ\nabla$ with $\nabla^* = -\star d^\nabla\star:\Omega^1(E)\to\Gamma(E)$), A-X4.4.1; prereqs: [Def - Exterior Covariant Derivative on a Vector Bundle, Def - Hodge Star in Arbitrary Signature, Thm - Existence of Synchronous Orthonormal Frames, Thm - Dirac Operators are Formally Self-Adjoint, Thm - Codifferential is the Adjoint of d]; statement: For a Euclidean bundle $E$ with metric connection $\nabla$ over an oriented Riemannian manifold, define $\nabla^* := -\star d^\nabla\star:\Omega^1(M;E)\to\Gamma(E)$ and $\nabla^*\nabla := \nabla^*\circ\nabla$. (a) In a local orthonormal frame $(e_i)$ of $TM$: $\nabla^*\nabla s = -\sum_i(\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}s - \nabla_{\nabla^{LC}_{e_i}e_i}s)$; in a synchronous frame at $m$, $(\nabla^*\nabla s)(m) = -\sum_i\nabla_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}s(m)$. (b) $\nabla^*$ is a formal adjoint of $\nabla$: $\int\langle\nabla s,\alpha\rangle = \int\langle s,\nabla^*\alpha\rangle$ for compactly supported $s$; hence $\nabla^*\nabla$ is formally self-adjoint and non-negative. (c) For $M$ closed, $\langle\nabla^*\nabla s,s\rangle_{L^2} = \|\nabla s\|_{L^2}^2$; proof: (b) first: $\star d^\nabla\star$ on $E$-valued 1-forms is computed as $-\sum\iota_{e_i}\nabla_{e_i}$ in a frame (Lemma 1, the $E$-valued version of Lemma 3 of `Thm - The Hodge-de Rham Operator is a Dirac Operator`, proved by the same divergence argument), giving (a) and (b) together; (c) from (b); the signs: $\Delta = \nabla^*\nabla\ge0$ (Haydys's non-negative convention); spec: load-bearing in XI (Bochner identity, $C^0$ bound); mechanism: **the connection Laplacian is minus the trace of the second covariant derivative, and integration by parts makes it the square of $\nabla$**.
