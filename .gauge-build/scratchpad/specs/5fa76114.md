## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor; backlink-map name, 11 external links); source items: B-D3.3.7; prereqs: [Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Connections, Def - Yang-Mills Lagrangian and Action Functional]; spec: Bär: an *instanton* on $P\to M$ ($M$ an oriented Riemannian 4-manifold) is a connection with self-dual curvature $\star\bar\Omega = \bar\Omega$; Convention callout (mandatory): Donaldson–Kronheimer, Freed–Uhlenbeck and Haydys use *anti*-self-dual connections as instantons; since reversing the orientation of $M$ swaps the two, the series' statements are made for "(anti-)self-dual" with the sign fixed per page, and `Def - Instanton` records both usages; instantons are absolute minimisers of $\mathcal{YM}$ in their topological class (proved on `Thm - Energy Identity and the Topological Bound for Yang-Mills`); on Minkowski space (Lorentzian) the notion is different ($\star\star = -1$) — remark; examples: the BPST instanton (exercise), flat connections, the Hopf-bundle connection over $S^4 = \mathbb{HP}^1$ (mentioned, bold); non-example: a Yang–Mills connection that is not (A)SD — e.g. a $U(1)$ connection on $S^2\times S^2$ with harmonic curvature $c_1\mathrm{vol}_1 + c_2\mathrm{vol}_2$, $c_1\ne\pm c_2$ (harmonic ⇒ Yang–Mills, but not (A)SD) — verified.
