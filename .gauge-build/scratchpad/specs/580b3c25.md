type: theorem (two parts; the exercises X4.3.1, X4.3.2 are its content); source items: A-X4.3.1, A-X4.3.2 ((127)); prereqs: [Def - Spin-c Dirac Operator Twisted by a Connection, Thm - The Space of Connections is an Affine Space, Thm - Principal Connections Induce Covariant Derivatives on Associated Bundles]; statement: (i) For a twisted Dirac operator $\slashed D_A$ on $\slashed S\otimes E$ and $a\in\Omega^1(M;\operatorname{ad}Q)$: $\slashed D_{A+a}\psi = \slashed D_A\psi + a\cdot\psi$, where $a\cdot\psi$ is the twisted Clifford multiplication $T^*M\otimes\operatorname{ad}Q\to\operatorname{End}(\slashed S)\otimes\operatorname{End}(E)$. (ii) For the spin$^c$ Dirac operator and $a\in\Omega^1(M;i\mathbb R)$: $\slashed D_{A+a}\psi = \slashed D_A\psi + \tfrac12a\cdot\psi$; proof: (i) the covariant derivative on $\slashed S\otimes E$ changes by $1\otimes a$ (associated-bundle anchor), and $\mathrm{Cl}\circ(1\otimes a) = a\cdot$; (ii) the connection $\tau^*(\omega+A+a)$ on $P$ differs from $\tau^*(\omega+A)$ by $\tau^*a$, and under $\mathfrak{spin}^c(n)\cong\mathfrak{so}(n)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$ the $\mathfrak u(1)$-summand acts on $\slashed S$ through $d\rho_{\det}$-compatible scaling: $[g,z]\cdot s = z\rho(g)s$ and $\rho_{\det}[g,z] = z^2$, so the infinitesimal generator $i\theta\in\mathfrak u(1)$ of $P_{\det}$ acts on $\slashed S$ as $\tfrac12i\theta$ — this is the whole content: the $U(1)$ of $P_{\det}$ is the square of the central $U(1)$ of $Spin^c$; spec: load-bearing for XI (`Thm - Differential of the Seiberg-Witten Map`, `Thm - Equivariance of the Seiberg-Witten Map`); mechanism: **the connection enters the Dirac operator linearly through the Clifford contraction, and the determinant line sees the central circle with weight two**.
