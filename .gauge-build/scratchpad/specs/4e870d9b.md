Convention callout: SR XXI's `Def - The Electromagnetic Field Tensor` uses $(+,-,-,-)$ and SI units — same 2-form $F$, same $\mathbf E,\mathbf B$, $g_{SR} = -g_B$, $\star_V^{SR} = -\star_B$ on Minkowski; SR XXII's `Def - The Four-Potential` $A$ with $F = dA$ is Bär's $A$ for the trivial background $\omega_0 = $ flat connection on the trivial bundle; also state Haydys's local form: on a trivialisation, $\omega = s^*\omega = iA_{\text{loc}}$, $F = dA_{\text{loc}}$. Categorical section: the $U(1)$ gauge field is a connection on a $U(1)$-torsor bundle; gauge potentials are its local trivialised forms.
