## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-I5.1.5, A-R5.1.3; prereqs: [Def - Sobolev Space of Sections, Thm - The Space of Connections is an Affine Space, Def - Compact Space]; statement: Let $M$ be compact, $E\to M$ a vector bundle. For any two choices $(g,\nabla^E,\nabla^M)$ and $(g',\nabla'^E,\nabla'^M)$ of Riemannian metric and connections, and any two fibre metrics on $E$, the norms $\|\cdot\|_{W^{k,2}}$ and $\|\cdot\|'_{W^{k,2}}$ on $\Gamma(E)$ are equivalent for every $k\ge0$; hence $W^{k,2}(M;E)$ is well defined as a topological vector space (and as a Hilbertable space); proof: induction on $k$: $\nabla' = \nabla + a$ with $a$ a smooth bundle-valued 1-form (affine-space anchor) on each of $E$ and $(T^*M)^{\otimes i}\otimes E$; then $\nabla'^{i}u - \nabla^iu$ is a linear combination of $\nabla^ju$, $j<i$, with smooth (hence bounded, by compactness) coefficients; the metrics are comparable by compactness ($c\,g\le g'\le C\,g$ pointwise, and likewise on $E$), and the volume forms differ by a bounded positive smooth factor; combine into $\|u\|'_{k}\le C\|u\|_k$ and symmetrically; spec: the same proof gives the $W^{k,p}$ statement for all $p$ — record; mechanism: **on a compact manifold every smooth object is bounded, and the difference of two connections is a smooth zero-order object**.
