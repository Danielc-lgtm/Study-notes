## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (Hatcher Thm 2.20; needed for local homology and Poincaré duality); prereqs: [Thm - Small Chains Theorem via Barycentric Subdivision, Def - Relative Singular Homology, Thm - Long Exact Sequence of a Pair in Singular Homology]; statement: (i) If $Z \subset A \subset X$ with $\overline{Z} \subset \operatorname{int} A$, the inclusion $(X \setminus Z, A \setminus Z) \hookrightarrow (X, A)$ induces isomorphisms $H_n(X \setminus Z, A \setminus Z; R) \cong H_n(X, A; R)$ for all $n$. (ii) Equivalently, for $A, B \subset X$ with $\operatorname{int} A \cup \operatorname{int} B = X$, the inclusion $(B, A \cap B) \hookrightarrow (X, A)$ induces isomorphisms on homology; proof: source: none; the page derives (ii) from the small chains theorem with $\mathcal{U} = \{A, B\}$ (the quotient $C^{\mathcal{U}}_n(X)/C_n(A) \cong C_n(B)/C_n(A \cap B)$ as complexes, and the chain equivalence $C^{\mathcal{U}}(X) \simeq C(X)$ descends to the quotients by $C(A)$ because $\rho$ and $D$ preserve $C(A)$ — Hatcher's argument via the five lemma is replaced by the direct chain-homotopy argument Hatcher gives on p. 124), and (i) from (ii) with $B = X \setminus Z$. Reference: Hatcher Thm 2.20 and the proof on p. 124. spec: Targets: local homology of a manifold; the Mayer–Vietoris sequence; Poincaré duality.
