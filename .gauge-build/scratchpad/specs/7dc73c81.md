type: theorem; source items: A-D3.2.1, A-X3.2.1(b), A-X3.2.1(a) (the $M\times\mathbb R$ special case with the corrected factor), A-R3.2.1, A-I3.2.2; prereqs: [Def - Chern-Simons Functional, Thm - Transgression Formula and the Chern-Simons Form, Thm - Second Chern Number of an SU(2)-Bundle over a Closed Four-Manifold is the Clutching Degree, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds, Def - Manifold with Boundary and Induced Orientation]; statement: (a) Let $X$ be a compact oriented 4-manifold with $\partial X=M$, $P_X\to X$ a principal $SU(2)$-bundle with $P_X|_M\cong P$ and $A_X$ a connection restricting to $A$. Then $\vartheta(A)\equiv\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\int_X\operatorname{tr}(F_{A_X}\wedge F_{A_X})\pmod{\mathbb Z}$ — A's (95). (b) For two such extensions $(X,A_X)$, $(X',A'_X)$, the two integrals differ by an integer (A's well-definedness argument via the closed manifold $X\cup_M\overline{X'}$ and the integrality theorem, written with the gluing of the bundles and connections made explicit — smooth gluing along a collar after making the connections agree on the collar by a homotopy). (c) For $X=M\times[t_0,t_1]$ and a connection $A$ on $X$ with slices $A_t$: $\vartheta(A_{t_1})-\vartheta(A_{t_0})=\tfrac1{8\pi^2}\int_{M\times[t_0,t_1]}\operatorname{tr}(F_A\wedge F_A)$ in $\mathbb R$ (A's Exercise 96(a) second display, with the missing $\tfrac1{8\pi^2}$ restored); proof: (a) $P_X$ over a compact 4-manifold with boundary need not be trivial; but $\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)$ is locally $d\operatorname{cs}$; the writer proves (a) by first treating the case where $P_X$ is trivial (Stokes directly) and then the general case via the clutching description of $P_X$ relative to the boundary (a generic section of the associated $\mathbb C^2$-bundle vanishing nowhere near $M$ — relative version of III's generic-sections theorem — trivialises $P_X$ off a disc in the interior, and the disc contributes the integer $k$ by the integrality theorem); (b) from (a) and the closed-manifold theorem; (c) Stokes with the two boundary components; reference: Freed, *Classical Chern–Simons theory I* §2; Donaldson–Kronheimer §2.1.4 and §5.? ; spec: Convention callout: the orientation of $\overline{X'}$ reversed so that $\partial X'=-M$; A-I3.2.2 (existence of an extension of the bundle and the connection over $X$) is never assumed: the theorem is conditional on an extension being given. Targets: Chern–Simons as the boundary term of the Yang–Mills action (VII), instanton Floer theory (bold outlook).
