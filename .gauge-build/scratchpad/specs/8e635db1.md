type: theorem (two parts); source items: A-T7.1.5 (Proposition 194), A-T7.1.6 (Proposition 193), A-X7.1.2; prereqs: [Def - The Seiberg-Witten Deformation Complex and the Atiyah Complex, Thm - A Short Complex is Elliptic iff its Folded Operator is Elliptic, Thm - Laplacians and Dirac Operators are Elliptic, Thm - Differential of the Seiberg-Witten Map, Def - Elliptic Complex and Its Laplacians]; statement: (i) The Atiyah complex $0\to\Omega^0\xrightarrow{d}\Omega^1\xrightarrow{d^+}\Omega^2_+\to0$ on an oriented Riemannian 4-manifold is elliptic; equivalently $(d^+, d^*):\Omega^1\to\Omega^2_+\oplus\Omega^0$ (its folded operator) is elliptic, and it is a Dirac-type operator. (ii) At any solution $(\psi,A)$, the Seiberg–Witten deformation complex (191) is elliptic; proof: (i) two routes, both recorded (Haydys's X7.1.2 asks for one): the symbol route computes $\sigma$ of $d$ and $d^+$ and checks exactness of $0\to\Lambda^0\xrightarrow{\xi\wedge}\Lambda^1\xrightarrow{\pi_+\circ\xi\wedge}\Lambda^2_+\to0$ for $\xi\ne0$ (the folding theorem `Thm - A Short Complex is Elliptic iff its Folded Operator is Elliptic` reduces it to ellipticity of $(d^+,d^*)$, whose symbol injectivity is the computation on `Thm - Laplacians and Dirac Operators are Elliptic`); the Dirac route identifies $d^+ + d^*:\Omega^1\to\Omega^2_+\oplus\Omega^0$ with a twisted Dirac operator (VIII), hence elliptic; (ii) modulo zero-order terms (immaterial for ellipticity, since ellipticity is a top-symbol condition — the writer proves this insensitivity as Lemma 1), (191) is the direct sum of the Atiyah complex and $0\to0\to\Gamma(\slashed S^+)\xrightarrow{\slashed D_A^+}\Gamma(\slashed S^-)\to0$, both elliptic ($\slashed D_A^+$ by `Thm - Laplacians and Dirac Operators are Elliptic`); spec: **fills Haydys's gap that "(191) is a complex" needs the equivariance lemma and that ellipticity ignores zero-order terms**; mechanism: **the Seiberg–Witten complex is the Atiyah complex plus the chiral Dirac operator up to lower order, and a direct sum of elliptic complexes is elliptic**; targets: Fredholmness of $D_{(\psi,A)}$ and the dimension formula (§11.4).
