type: theorem; source items: A-T7.1.4 (Lemma 192); prereqs: [Def - The Seiberg-Witten Deformation Complex and the Atiyah Complex, Def - Seiberg-Witten Equations, Thm - Variation of the Twisted and Spin-c Dirac Operators with the Connection, Def - The Quadratic Map on Positive Spinors]; statement: The differential of $SW$ at $(\psi,A)$ is $d_{(\psi,A)}SW(\dot\psi,\dot a) = \big(\slashed D_A^+\dot\psi + \tfrac12\dot a\cdot\psi,\ d^+\dot a - 2\mu(\psi,\dot\psi)\big)$, where $\dot a\cdot\psi$ is Clifford multiplication (of the imaginary $1$-form $\dot a$), $d^+\dot a$ the self-dual part of $d\dot a$, and $\mu(\psi,\dot\psi)$ the polarisation of $\mu$; proof: differentiate each component along $A + t\dot a$, $\psi + t\dot\psi$: the Dirac part from `Thm - Variation of the Twisted and Spin-c Dirac Operators with the Connection` ($\slashed D_{A+t\dot a}^+(\psi+t\dot\psi) = \slashed D_A^+\psi + t(\slashed D_A^+\dot\psi + \tfrac12\dot a\cdot\psi) + O(t^2)$) and the curvature part from $F_{A+t\dot a}^+ = F_A^+ + t\,d^+\dot a$ and the quadratic expansion $\mu(\psi + t\dot\psi) = \mu(\psi) + 2t\mu(\psi,\dot\psi) + O(t^2)$; spec: **Haydys omits it; the polarisation and the factor $\tfrac12$ are written out**; mechanism: **the Seiberg–Witten map is affine-linear in the connection through Clifford multiplication and quadratic in the spinor, so its differential is Dirac plus a Clifford term plus $d^+$ minus the polarised quadratic**; targets: the deformation operator $D_{(\psi,A)}$ and transversality (§11.4).
