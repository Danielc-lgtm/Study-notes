## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: none of the sources directly (replaces flagged RG IV theorem; needed for A-X3.2.1(d), the $SU(2)$ classification, the hairy-ball theorem, and B-D2.4.4's degree); prereqs: [Def - Brouwer Degree of a Map, Thm - The Top de Rham Cohomology of a Closed Connected Oriented Manifold is R, Thm - Sard's Theorem when the Source Dimension does not Exceed the Target Dimension, Thm - Change of Variables for Integration on Manifolds, Thm - Homotopy Invariance of de Rham Cohomology, Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]; statement: Let $M,N$ be closed oriented $n$-manifolds, $N$ connected, $f\colon M\to N$ smooth, and $\deg f:=\int_Mf^*\omega$ for any $\omega\in\Omega^n(N)$ with $\int_N\omega=1$ (RG IV's definition, linked). Then: (a) $\deg f$ is independent of $\omega$; (b) for every regular value $y$, $\deg f=\sum_{x\in f^{-1}(y)}\operatorname{sign}\det df_x$, hence $\deg f\in\mathbb Z$; (c) smoothly homotopic maps have equal degree; (d) if $\deg f\ne0$ then $f$ is surjective; (e) $\deg(g\circ f)=\deg g\cdot\deg f$; (f) if $M=\partial W$ for a compact oriented $(n+1)$-manifold $W$ and $f$ extends smoothly to $W$, then $\deg f=0$; (g) if $M$ is disconnected the degree is the sum over components; proof: (a) by the top-cohomology theorem; (b) choose $\omega$ supported in an evenly covered neighbourhood of the regular value $y$ (finitely many preimages by easy Sard (c)), apply change of variables on each sheet; (c) DG X's homotopy invariance (restated); (d) contrapositive: if $y\notin f(M)$ take $\omega$ supported near $y$; (e) pull-back functoriality; (f) Stokes; reference: Lee ISM 2e Thm 17.35 and Prop 17.36; Milnor TDV §5; spec: Convention: orientation conventions for $\partial W$ as in DG IX. Targets: winding numbers, the degree of $q\mapsto q^k$ on $S^3$, the Chern number of an $SU(2)$-bundle, the gauge variation of Chern–Simons (VI).
