## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (Milnor–Stasheff Thm 11.3; needed for I5.1.4 and I5.2.1); prereqs: [Thm - Thom Isomorphism for Oriented Vector Bundles, Thm - Tubular Neighbourhood Theorem, Thm - Poincare Duality, Thm - Excision Theorem for Singular Homology, Def - R-Orientation and the Fundamental Class]; statement: Let $X$ be a closed oriented smooth $n$-manifold and $S \subset X$ a closed oriented embedded submanifold of dimension $k$, with normal bundle $\nu_S$ oriented so that $TS \oplus \nu_S = TX|_S$ as oriented bundles, $U \cong \nu_S$ a tubular neighbourhood. Define $u_S \in H^{n-k}(X;\mathbb{Z})$ as the image of the Thom class $u_{\nu_S} \in H^{n-k}(\nu_S, \nu_S \setminus S) \cong H^{n-k}(U, U \setminus S) \cong H^{n-k}(X, X \setminus S)$ (excision) under $H^{n-k}(X, X \setminus S) \to H^{n-k}(X)$. Then $[X] \frown u_S = \iota_*[S] \in H_k(X;\mathbb{Z})$, i.e. $u_S$ is the Poincaré dual of the class represented by $S$; proof: source: none; the page follows Milnor–Stasheff Thm 11.3 / Bredon VI.11: reduce by naturality of the cap product to the pair $(U, U \setminus S)$; precisely: $[X] \frown u_S$ is the image of $\mu_{\bar S} \frown u_{\nu}$ where $\mu_{\bar S} \in H_n(X, X \setminus S)$ is the restriction of the fundamental class, and, using the relative cap product $H_n(U, U \setminus S) \times H^{n-k}(U, U \setminus S) \to H_k(U)$, the page proves the fibrewise statement: for each $x \in S$ the local computation in a trivialisation $U|_V \cong V \times \mathbb{R}^{n-k}$ shows $\mu \frown u$ restricts to the local orientation class of $S$ at $x$; by the uniqueness of the fundamental class of $S$ (`Thm - Existence and Uniqueness of the Fundamental Class`, characterisation by local restrictions), $\mu_{\bar S} \frown u_\nu = [S]$ in $H_k(U) \cong H_k(S)$. Reference: Milnor–Stasheff Thm 11.3 (and the discussion pp. 120–122); Bredon VI.11 ("the dual of a submanifold"); Bott–Tu §6 for the de Rham picture. spec: Targets: the cup-product formula for transverse intersections; the Euler class of $\nu_S$ and self-intersection.
