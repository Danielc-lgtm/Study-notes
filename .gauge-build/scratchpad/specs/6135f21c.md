type: theorem; source items: A-T2.2.3; prereqs: [Def - Equivariant and Basic Forms on a Principal Bundle, Def - Associated Bundle, Thm - Sections of an Associated Bundle are Equivariant Functions]; statement: For a principal $G$-bundle $P$, a representation $\rho$ on $V$ and $q\ge0$, the map $\Omega^q(M;P\times_\rho V)\to\Omega^q_{\mathrm{bas}}(P;V)^G$, $a\mapsto\hat a$ with $a(d\pi\hat v_1,\dots,d\pi\hat v_q)=[p,\hat a_p(\hat v_1,\dots,\hat v_q)]$, is a bijection (of $C^\infty(M)$-modules); for $\rho$ trivial it is $a\mapsto\pi^*a$ onto basic invariant forms; proof: source full modulo the equivariance computation and independence of choices — write: (⇒) $\hat a$ is well defined because $[p,\cdot]\colon V\to E_{\pi(p)}$ is an isomorphism; basic because $d\pi$ kills vertical vectors; equivariant because $[pg,\rho(g^{-1})v]=[p,v]$ and $d\pi\circ dR_g=d\pi$; smooth via local sections; (⇐) independence of the lift (difference of lifts is vertical, killed by basic-ness) and of $p$ (equivariance), smoothness; the two constructions are inverse; reference: Kobayashi–Nomizu I Ch. II §5 (tensorial forms), Baum *Eichfeldtheorie* Satz 3.5; spec: Mechanism: "basic kills the fibre directions, equivariance identifies the fibres, together they leave exactly a form on the base with values in the associated bundle." Targets: curvature of a principal connection lives on $M$ (IV), Chern–Weil forms descend (VI).
