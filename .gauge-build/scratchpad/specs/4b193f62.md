## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: (the notion used in A-T5.1.1 (ii) and A-D5.2.6); prereqs: [Def - Compact Space, Def - Totally Bounded Metric Space, Thm - Compactness in Metric Spaces (Three Equivalents)]; spec: a bounded linear map $K:X\to Y$ between Banach spaces is compact if the image of the unit ball is relatively compact, equivalently (in a complete space) totally bounded, equivalently every bounded sequence $(x_j)$ has a subsequence with $(Kx_j)$ convergent; examples: finite-rank operators; the inclusion $H_k(T^n)\subset H_m(T^n)$ for $k>m$ (proved on the Rellich page); diagonal operators on $\ell^2$ with $\lambda_j\to0$ (verified); non-example: the identity on an infinite-dimensional space (Riesz's lemma, proved on `Thm - Riesz-Schauder Theory for Compact Perturbations of the Identity`).
