## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-T7.1.8 (Lemma 196), A-T7.1.9 (Remark 197), A-R7.1.5; prereqs: [Def - Seiberg-Witten Equations, Thm - The Bochner Identity for the Spinor Norm, Thm - Clifford Action of 2-Forms and the Chiral Weitzenbock Formula in Dimension Four, Def - The Quadratic Map on Positive Spinors, Thm - Sobolev Embedding Theorem, Def - Scalar Curvature]; statement: There is a constant depending only on the metric such that every solution $(\psi,A)\in\mathcal C^{5,2}$ of the Seiberg–Witten equations satisfies the pointwise bound $|\psi|^2\le\max\{0, -\min_M s_g\}$; in particular $\|\psi\|_{C^0}^2\le\tfrac12\max_M(-s_g)^+$ where $s_g$ is the scalar curvature (the constant made precise on the page); proof: $\psi\in W^{5,2}\subset C^2$ (`Thm - Sobolev Embedding Theorem`, $5 - 2>2$); at a maximum $x_0$ of $|\psi|^2$, $\Delta|\psi|^2\ge0$ (non-negative Laplacian), so by `Thm - The Bochner Identity for the Spinor Norm` $\langle\nabla_A^*\nabla_A\psi,\psi\rangle\ge|\nabla_A\psi|^2\ge0$ at $x_0$; the chiral Weitzenböck (Lichnerowicz) formula `Thm - Clifford Action of 2-Forms and the Chiral Weitzenbock Formula in Dimension Four` with $\slashed D_A^+\psi = 0$ and $F_A^+ = \mu(\psi)$ gives $0 = \langle\nabla_A^*\nabla_A\psi,\psi\rangle + \tfrac14 s_g|\psi|^2 + \tfrac12\langle F_A^+\cdot\psi,\psi\rangle$ and $\langle F_A^+\cdot\psi,\psi\rangle = \langle\mu(\psi)\cdot\psi,\psi\rangle = \tfrac12|\psi|^4$ (from `Def - The Quadratic Map on Positive Spinors`); combining, at $x_0$: $\tfrac14 s_g|\psi|^2 + \tfrac14|\psi|^4\le -|\nabla_A\psi|^2\le0$, so $|\psi(x_0)|^2\le -s_g(x_0)\le\max_M(-s_g)$ (dividing by $|\psi(x_0)|^2$ if non-zero); **source typo (Haydys B17, p. 65): the printed constants show a factor-2 inconsistency between $\tfrac14|\psi|^4$ in the Weitzenböck identity and $\tfrac12|\psi|^4$ in the following inequality; the page uses $\langle\tfrac12 F_A^+\cdot\psi,\psi\rangle = \tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12|\psi|^4 = \tfrac14|\psi|^4$ consistently and states the resulting bound $|\psi|^2\le -s_g$**; the sign remark (T7.1.9, R7.1.6): for the equation $F_A^+ = -\mu(\psi)$ the $|\psi|^4$ term flips sign and the argument fails — recorded; spec: mechanism: **at the maximum of $|\psi|^2$ the connection Laplacian is non-negative, and the Lichnerowicz formula turns the equation into a pointwise inequality bounding $|\psi|^4$ by the scalar curvature**; targets: the vanishing theorem (§11.5), compactness.
