## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: none of the sources (needed for A-T2.4.3, A-E2.4.1 and the clutching arguments); prereqs: [Thm - Sard's Theorem when the Source Dimension does not Exceed the Target Dimension, Thm - Regular Value Theorem on Manifolds, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity, Def - Vector Bundle]; statement: Let $E\to M$ be a real vector bundle of rank $r$ over a compact manifold of dimension $m$. (a) There exist finitely many sections $s_1,\dots,s_N$ spanning every fibre. (b) For almost every $a\in\mathbb R^N$ the section $s_a=\sum a_is_i$ is transverse to the zero section: at each zero $x$ of $s_a$ the vertical derivative $D_xs_a\colon T_xM\to E_x$ is surjective. (c) Consequently, if $r>m$ then $E$ has a nowhere-vanishing section; if $r=m$ then $E$ has a section with finitely many zeros, each nondegenerate ($D_xs_a$ an isomorphism), and when $M$ and $E$ are oriented each zero carries a sign $\epsilon(x)=\operatorname{sign}\det D_xs_a$; (d) the same holds for sections prescribed on a neighbourhood of a closed set $K$ where a given section is already transverse (relative version, needed for the clutching arguments); proof: (a) local frames times bump functions; (b) $F\colon M\times\mathbb R^N\to E$, $F(x,a)=s_a(x)$, is a submersion (surjective on fibres by (a)), so $W=F^{-1}(0_E)$ is a submanifold of dimension $m+N-r$ (regular value theorem), and by the easy Sard theorem applied to $\operatorname{pr}_2|_W\colon W\to\mathbb R^N$ almost every $a$ is a regular value; unwind: $a$ regular for $\operatorname{pr}_2|_W$ iff $s_a$ transverse to $0_E$ (the standard parametric transversality argument, written in full with the tangent-space identification); (c) dimension count; (d) multiply the perturbation by a cutoff vanishing near $K$ and use openness of transversality on compact sets; reference: Guillemin–Pollack Ch. 2 §3 (Transversality Theorem, parametric form), Bott–Tu Prop 11.? (generic sections), Hirsch Ch. 3 Thm 2.5; spec: Targets: triviality of $SU(2)$-bundles in dimension $\le3$; sections of line bundles over surfaces with finitely many signed zeros; "every $SU(2)$-bundle over a 4-manifold is trivial off a point".
