**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: B-D1.5.10, B-E1.5.8, B-R1.5.2, B-T1.5.7 (flow statement), B-I2.4.2, A-D2.2.10 (the infinitesimal action $K_\xi$ and vertical subspace — the vertical-subspace part is restated in III); prereqs: [Def - Free, Transitive, Effective, and Proper Group Actions, Thm - One-Parameter Subgroups are the Integral Curves of Left-Invariant Vector Fields]; spec: For a left action, $\bar X(p):=\tfrac{d}{dt}\big|_0\exp(tX)\cdot p=d_e\ell_p(X)$ with $\ell_p(g)=g\cdot p$; for a right action (the series' convention on bundles), $\xi_P(p):=\tfrac{d}{dt}\big|_0p\cdot\exp(t\xi)$; smoothness proved (it is $d\theta$ applied to the field $(X,0)$ on $G\times M$). Corollaries proved on the page: the flow of $\bar X$ is $t\mapsto\theta_{\exp tX}$ (T1.5.7 first half); $\bar X(p)=0$ iff $\exp(tX)\cdot p=p$ for all $t$; for a free action $X\mapsto\bar X(p)$ is injective, so a free action of a group with $\dim G\ge1$ gives a nowhere-vanishing vector field on $M$. The consequence "$\chi(M)=0$, so no positive-dimensional Lie group acts freely on $S^{2n}$" (R1.5.2, I1.5.3) is stated in bold plain text as a forward reference to chapter III's `Thm - Hairy Ball Theorem`, where it is proved as a corollary. Verified example: E1.5.8 (fundamental fields of the $z$-axis rotation are tangent to latitude circles, $\bar X=\partial_\varphi$); non-example: the velocity field of a non-group flow. Convention callout: B's $\bar X$ (left) vs. A's $K_\xi$ and the series' $\xi_P$ (right).
