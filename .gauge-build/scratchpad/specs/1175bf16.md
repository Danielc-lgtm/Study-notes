## Spec (from the manifest)

**[anchor]** — type: definition; source items: B-R2.5.3 ($\phi_g=dL_{g^{-1}}$), A §2.2 conventions ($g^{-1}dg$ for matrix groups, $g^*\theta$ in the transformation law); prereqs: [Def - Left and Right Translations and Conjugation on a Lie Group, Def - Lie-Algebra-Valued Differential Forms and Their Bracket]; spec: $\theta\in\Omega^1(G;\mathfrak g)$, $\theta_g:=d_gL_{g^{-1}}\colon T_gG\to T_eG=\mathfrak g$; left-invariance $L_h^*\theta=\theta$ and $R_h^*\theta=\operatorname{Ad}_{h^{-1}}\theta$ (proved); $\theta(\tilde\xi)=\xi$ for the left-invariant field $\tilde\xi$; for matrix groups $\theta=g^{-1}dg$ (proved); the pull-back $g^*\theta$ for a map $g\colon U\to G$ and its product rule $(g_1g_2)^*\theta=\operatorname{Ad}_{g_2^{-1}}(g_1^*\theta)+g_2^*\theta$ (proved as corollary; it is the identity behind every local transformation law). Verified examples: $U(1)$: $\theta=z^{-1}dz=i\,d\varphi$; $SU(2)$ in quaternion coordinates. Non-example: $dg\,g^{-1}$ is the right-invariant form, not $\theta$. Calibration: $\theta$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is $dx$; $\theta$ evaluated on a curve $g(t)$ is $g^{-1}\dot g$.
