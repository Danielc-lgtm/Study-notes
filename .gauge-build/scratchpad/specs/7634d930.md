**[anchor]** — type: theorem; source items: A-E2.4.1, A-T2.4.2 (i),(iii),(iv) and the surface case of (ii), A-I2.4.7, A-X2.4.1 (statement; exercise), A-I2.4.4, A-I2.4.5 (replaced), A-R2.4.1; prereqs: [Def - First Chern Class via the Classifying Map, Thm - Generic Sections are Transverse to the Zero Section, Thm - Homogeneity Lemma for Connected Manifolds, Thm - Homotopic Maps Pull Back Isomorphic Principal Bundles, Thm - Clutching Construction for Bundles over a Closed Manifold, Thm - Winding Number of a Map from the Circle to U(1), Def - Constructions on Representations]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact manifold and $\operatorname{Pic}(M)$ the set of isomorphism classes of principal $U(1)$-bundles (= Hermitian line bundles) over $M$. (A) $\operatorname{Pic}(M)$ is an abelian group under tensor product of line bundles, with inverse the dual (= conjugate) bundle and identity the trivial bundle, and $P\mapsto[f_P]$ is a bijection $\operatorname{Pic}(M)\to[M;\mathbb{CP}^\infty]$. (B) $c_1^{top}\colon\operatorname{Pic}(M)\to H^2_{dR}(M)$ satisfies $c_1^{top}(\underline{\mathbb C})=0$, $c_1^{top}(L^\vee)=-c_1^{top}(L)$, $c_1^{top}(h^*L)=h^*c_1^{top}(L)$ for smooth $h\colon M'\to M$, and $c_1^{top}$ depends only on the isomorphism class. (C) If $M=\Sigma$ is a closed connected oriented surface, then $\deg\colon\operatorname{Pic}(\Sigma)\to\mathbb Z$ (the clutching winding number) is a group isomorphism: every $P$ over $\Sigma$ is trivial over the complement of a point and hence given by a clutching map; $\deg(P_1\otimes P_2)=\deg P_1+\deg P_2$; for every $d\in\mathbb Z$ there is a bundle of degree $d$; and $\deg P=0$ implies $P$ trivial. (D) [proved in chapter VI on `Thm - First Chern Class of a Line Bundle from Curvature`; stated here in bold as a forward reference, not as part of the theorem] $\deg P=\int_\Sigma c_1^{top}(P)$ and $c_1^{top}(L_1\otimes L_2)=c_1^{top}(L_1)+c_1^{top}(L_2)$ for all $M$. Scope remark (bold, not a theorem): the identification $\operatorname{Pic}(M)\cong H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$ via $[M;\mathbb{CP}^\infty]\cong H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$ requires singular cohomology and is neither proved nor used in this series; A's $H^2(M;\mathbb Z)$-valued $c_1$ is recovered only in the forms above; proof: (A) group axioms via associated bundles of $\varrho_k$ (chapter I) and the frame-bundle theorem; bijection = previous theorem; (B) from the definition (constant classifying map; conjugation on $\mathbb{CP}^N$ pulls $\omega_N$ back to $-\omega_N$ — verified in coordinates; $f_{h^*L}=f_L\circ h$; homotopy invariance); (C) Step 1: $L=P\times_{U(1)}\mathbb C$ is an oriented real rank-2 bundle over the 2-manifold $\Sigma$, so a generic section has finitely many nondegenerate zeros; Step 2: gather the zeros into a coordinate disc $D$ by the homogeneity lemma and the isotopy-invariance clause (d) of the homotopy theorem; Step 3: $L$ (hence $P$) is trivial over $\Sigma\setminus D^\circ$ (the nowhere-vanishing section normalised) and over $D$, so $P\cong P_g$; Step 4: additivity of $\deg$ from $w(g_1g_2)=w(g_1)+w(g_2)$ (tensor product of clutching bundles has clutching function the product — proved via cocycles); Step 5: existence for each $d$ by clutching with $z\mapsto z^d$; Step 6: $\deg P=0\Rightarrow w(g)=0\Rightarrow g$ extends over $D$ $\Rightarrow P_g$ trivial (clutching theorem (d)); Step 7: injectivity from additivity and Step 6; reference: Hatcher VBKT §1.2 (clutching over spheres) adapted to surfaces; Milnor TDV §4–5 for the isotopy tools; spec: Convention callout: A's route via $[\Sigma;\mathbb{CP}^\infty]=[\Sigma;\mathbb{CP}^1]=[\Sigma;S^2]\cong\mathbb Z$ (cellular approximation I2.4.4 and the Hopf degree theorem I2.4.5) is recorded as the classical route and replaced by the clutching proof; both give the same integer. Mechanism: "a line bundle over a surface is trivial off a disc, and a map from the boundary circle to $U(1)$ has just one invariant." Targets: degree of $\mathcal O(-1)$ is $-1$; the Chern–Weil identification (VI); flat $U(1)$-bundles over surfaces (V).
