## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem (anchor; two parts); source items: A-T5.2.1 (Theorem 146), A-I5.2.1, A-I7.1.6; prereqs: [Thm - Elliptic Estimate for Constant-Coefficient Operators on the Torus, Thm - Friedrichs Mollifier Lemma on the Torus, Thm - Differential Operators are Bounded between Sobolev Spaces, Thm - Sobolev Spaces of Sections on a Compact Manifold via Charts, Thm - Sobolev Embedding Theorem, Thm - Existence of Smooth Partitions of Unity]; statement: Let $M$ be a compact manifold and $L:\Gamma(E)\to\Gamma(F)$ an elliptic differential operator of order $\ell$. (i) (Elliptic estimate) For every $k\in\mathbb Z$ and every $m<k+\ell$ there is $C = C(L,k,m)$ with $\|u\|_{k+\ell}\le C(\|Lu\|_k + \|u\|_m)$ for all $u\in H_{k+\ell}(M;E)$; in particular, for $k\ge0$, $\|u\|_{W^{k+\ell,2}}\le C(\|Lu\|_{W^{k,2}} + \|u\|_{L^2})$ (Haydys's (146) with $p = 2$). (ii) (Regularity) If $u\in H_m(M;E)$ for some $m\in\mathbb Z$ and $Lu\in H_k(M;F)$ (in the weak sense of the boundedness page), then $u\in H_{k+\ell}(M;E)$ and the estimate (i) holds for it; in particular if $Lu$ is smooth then $u$ is smooth. **Convention callout:** Haydys states (146) for $W^{k,p}$, all $p>1$; the $L^p$ case is Calderón–Zygmund theory (Gilbarg–Trudinger Ch. 9; Taylor *PDE III* Ch. 13) and is not proved or used in the series; proof: **Step 1** (freezing): for $x_0$ in a chart, $P$ the local operator, $P_0$ its top-order part with coefficients frozen at $x_0$; for $u$ supported in the ball $B_\delta(x_0)$: $\|u\|_{k+\ell}\le C_0(\|P_0u\|_k + \|u\|_k)$ (constant-coefficient page) and $\|(P-P_0)u\|_k\le\eta(\delta)\|u\|_{k+\ell} + C_\delta\|u\|_{k+\ell-1}$ where $\eta(\delta)\to0$ as $\delta\to0$ (Lemma 1: the top-order part of $P-P_0$ has coefficients bounded by $\eta(\delta)$ on $B_\delta$; multiplication by a smooth function $b$ supported in $B_{2\delta}$ with $\|b\|_{C^0}\le\eta$ satisfies $\|b\,w\|_k\le\eta\|w\|_k + C\|w\|_{k-1}$ for $k\ge0$ by Leibniz, and for $k<0$ by duality; the lower-order part of $P-P_0$ is bounded $H_{k+\ell-1}\to H_k$); choose $\delta$ with $C_0\eta(\delta)\le\tfrac12$ and absorb: $\|u\|_{k+\ell}\le C(\|Pu\|_k + \|u\|_{k+\ell-1})$ for $u$ supported in $B_\delta(x_0)$. **Step 2** (patching): cover $M$ by finitely many such balls, take a subordinate partition of unity $(\phi_i)$; for $u\in\Gamma(E)$, $\|u\|_{k+\ell}\le C\sum_i\|\phi_iu\|_{k+\ell}\le C\sum_i(\|L(\phi_iu)\|_k + \|\phi_iu\|_{k+\ell-1})\le C(\|Lu\|_k + \|u\|_{k+\ell-1})$ using $L(\phi_iu) = \phi_iLu + [L,\phi_i]u$ with $[L,\phi_i]$ of order $\ell-1$ (Lemma 2, from the symbol page). **Step 3** (interpolation): $\|u\|_{k+\ell-1}\le\epsilon\|u\|_{k+\ell} + C_\epsilon\|u\|_m$ absorbs the middle term, giving (i) for smooth $u$, then for all $u\in H_{k+\ell}$ by density. **Step 4** (regularity): it suffices to show $u\in H_m$, $Lu\in H_k$, $m<k+\ell$ imply $u\in H_{m+1}$ (then iterate); localise: $\phi_iu\in H_m$ with $L(\phi_iu) = \phi_iLu + [L,\phi_i]u\in H_{\min(k,m-\ell+1)}$; transport to the torus and apply the estimate of Step 1–3 (valid on the torus for the localised operator, with the Step-1 constant) to the smooth sections $J_\epsilon(\phi_iu)$: $\|J_\epsilon(\phi_iu)\|_{m+1}\le C(\|PJ_\epsilon(\phi_iu)\|_{m+1-\ell} + \|J_\epsilon(\phi_iu)\|_m)\le C(\|J_\epsilon P(\phi_iu)\|_{m+1-\ell} + \|[P,J_\epsilon](\phi_iu)\|_{m+1-\ell} + \|\phi_iu\|_m)$, and each term is bounded uniformly in $\epsilon$: the first since $P(\phi_iu)\in H_{m+1-\ell}$ (as $m+1-\ell\le k$), the second by Friedrichs (iv) with $t = m+1-\ell$, $t+\ell-1 = m$; hence by Friedrichs (v), $\phi_iu\in H_{m+1}$; sum over $i$ (a technical point: the localised operator $P$ on the torus must be elliptic on all of $T^n$ — extend the coefficients from the chart image to the torus keeping ellipticity, e.g. by interpolating with the frozen operator $P_0$ via a cutoff, which is possible on a small enough ball since ellipticity is an open condition on the symbol — Lemma 3, written out); spec: references Lawson–Michelsohn Thm III.5.2 and the discussion before it; Wells IV Thm 4.? (elliptic regularity via Friedrichs); Taylor *PDE I* §5.11 (interior regularity by difference quotients — alternative recorded); mechanism: **an elliptic operator is invertible at the level of symbols, and the symbol calculus commutes with everything up to lower order; regularity follows because the a priori estimate applied to mollifications is uniform**; What Makes This Hard: the freezing constant must be uniform in the ball radius, and the estimate must be applied to *smooth approximants* of $u$, not to $u$ itself.
