## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: T5.1.2 (as a corollary), I5.1.4 (well-definedness), I5.2.1 (homological definition for topological manifolds), T5.2.1 (first half); prereqs: [Thm - Cup Product of Poincare Duals of Transverse Submanifolds, Thm - Every Class in the Second Homology of a Simply Connected Four-Manifold is Represented by an Embedded Surface, Thm - Poincare Duality, Thm - Homology of a Simply Connected Closed Four-Manifold, Def - Intersection Form of a Four-Manifold]; statement: (i) For any closed oriented topological four-manifold $X$ define $Q^{\smile}_X : H^2(X;\mathbb{Z}) \times H^2(X;\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathbb{Z}$, $Q^{\smile}_X(\alpha, \beta) := \langle \alpha \smile \beta, [X] \rangle$, and transport it to $H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$ by Poincaré duality $D_X^{-1}$; it is symmetric and bilinear and descends to $H_2/\text{torsion}$. (ii) For $X$ smooth, compact, oriented, simply connected: for all $a, b \in H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$ represented by transverse embedded oriented surfaces $S_1, S_2$, $S_1 \cdot S_2 = Q^{\smile}_X(D_X^{-1} a, D_X^{-1} b)$; hence the geometric intersection number depends only on the classes, and $Q_X = Q^{\smile}_X \circ (D_X^{-1} \times D_X^{-1})$; proof: (i) from `Thm - Properties of the Cup Product` (graded commutativity in degree $2 \cdot 2$) and bilinearity of cup and Kronecker pairing; (ii) from the previous page: $D_X^{-1}[S_i] = u_{S_i}$ and $\langle u_{S_1} \smile u_{S_2}, [X] \rangle = S_1 \cdot S_2$. Corollary (T5.1.2): $Q_X$ is well defined, bilinear and symmetric. spec: Why-true: "both sides count the same points: Poincaré duality converts 'meets $S_2$' into 'evaluate on $u_{S_2}$'"; Targets: unimodularity; homotopy invariance (§13.5); the topological definition used for Freedman's manifolds.
