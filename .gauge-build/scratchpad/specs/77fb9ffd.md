## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: A-T6.6.2 (Theorem 183), A-R6.6.1; prereqs: [Def - The Equivariant Deformation Complex and its Folded Operator, Thm - A Free Action with Slices has a Manifold Quotient, Thm - Regular Value and Transversality Theorems for Fredholm Maps, Thm - Fredholm Alternative for Elliptic Operators]; statement: Let $y$ be a fixed point of the $G$-action on $Y$; assume (i) $G$ acts freely on $X$; (ii) $y$ is a regular value of $F_w$; (iii) there is a local slice at each $x\in F_w^{-1}(y)$; (iv) $D_x = (R_x^*,d_xF_w)$ is Fredholm of index $d$. Then $\mathcal M_w = F_w^{-1}(y)/G$ is a smooth manifold of dimension $d$; proof: local statement at $x$; take the orthogonal slice $S = x + \ker R_x^*$ (so $T_xS = (\operatorname{Im}R_x)^\perp = \ker R_x^*$, transverse to the orbit); **the identification $\ker d_x(F_w|_S) = \ker D_x$ that Haydys's proof needs (content-map gap) is written out**: $\ker d_x(F_w|_S) = \ker d_xF_w\cap T_xS = \ker d_xF_w\cap\ker R_x^* = \ker(R_x^*,d_xF_w) = \ker D_x$; $y$ is a regular value of $F_w|_S$ because $\operatorname{Im}d_x(F_w|_S) = \operatorname{Im}d_xF_w$ (since $d_xF_w$ annihilates $\operatorname{Im}R_x$, adding the orbit directions does not enlarge the image) $= T_yY$ by (ii); so $F_w^{-1}(y)\cap S$ is a manifold of dimension $\dim\ker D_x$ (regular-value theorem), and $\operatorname{coker}D_x = 0$ (freeness ⇒ $R_x$ injective with closed range ⇒ $R_x^*$ surjective onto $\operatorname{Lie}(G)$; combined with (ii)) gives $\dim\ker D_x = \operatorname{index}D_x = d$; the slice identifies $F_w^{-1}(y)\cap S$ with a neighbourhood of $[x]$ in $\mathcal M_w$; spec: **the three gaps the content map flags (the orthogonal slice, surjectivity of $R_x^*$ on the complement, regularity of $y$ for $F_w|_S$) are all filled**; mechanism: **cutting the equation down to a slice removes the gauge directions, and the folded operator's kernel is the tangent space of the moduli space**; targets: `Thm - Generic Regularity and Orientability of the Seiberg-Witten Moduli Space` (XI).
