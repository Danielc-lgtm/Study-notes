## Spec (from the manifest)

type: theorem; source items: (needed for Steps 2–4, I5.1.2, I5.1.3, and for the Thom-class pages of §13.2); prereqs: [Def - Embedded Submanifold, Thm - The Inverse Function Theorem, Def - Riemannian Metric, Def - The Riemannian Exponential Map, Thm - Existence of Riemannian Metrics via Partitions of Unity]; statement: Let $S \subset X$ be a compact embedded submanifold of a smooth manifold $X$ (with $\partial X = \emptyset$), $\nu_S := TX|_S / TS$ its normal bundle (identified, after choosing a Riemannian metric, with the orthogonal complement $TS^\perp \subset TX|_S$). There is an open neighbourhood $U$ of $S$ in $X$ and a diffeomorphism $\Phi : \nu_S \to U$ restricting to the zero-section identification $S \to S$ and inducing on the normal bundle of the zero section the identity of $\nu_S$ (in particular $\Phi(v) = \exp_x(v)$ for $v \in (\nu_S)_x$ small works after rescaling); $U$ deformation retracts onto $S$; if $X$ and $S$ are oriented, $\nu_S$ is oriented by the rule "orientation of $TS$ followed by orientation of $\nu_S$ gives the orientation of $TX$"; proof: source: none (Bär does not state it); the page follows Lee (2nd ed.) Thm 6.24 (the normal exponential map $E : \nu_S \to X$ is a local diffeomorphism along the zero section by the inverse function theorem, and injective on a neighbourhood of the zero section by a compactness/sequence argument; the rescaling of $\nu_S$ onto the $\varepsilon$-neighbourhood by $v \mapsto \varepsilon v / \sqrt{1 + \lVert v \rVert^2}$ gives a global diffeomorphism onto $U$). Reference: Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds* (2nd ed.), Thm 6.24; Bredon II.11.4. spec: Why-true: "normal geodesics from a compact submanifold do not cross for short times"; Targets: smoothing, transversality perturbations, the local model at a double point, the Thom class of the normal bundle, push-offs of surfaces (self-intersection numbers).
