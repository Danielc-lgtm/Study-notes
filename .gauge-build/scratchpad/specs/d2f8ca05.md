## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: D4.2.7; prereqs: [Def - Topological Manifold, Def - Smooth Manifold, Def - Manifold with Boundary and Induced Orientation, Def - Orientation of a Smooth Manifold]; spec: For connected $n$-manifolds $M$, $N$ and embeddings $\phi_M : \bar B^n \to M$, $\phi_N : \bar B^n \to N$ (orientation-preserving and orientation-reversing respectively when $M, N$ are oriented, so that $M \# N$ is oriented compatibly with $M \setminus \phi_M(B^n)$ and $N \setminus \phi_N(B^n)$), $M \# N := (M \setminus \phi_M(B^n)) \cup_{\partial} (N \setminus \phi_N(B^n))$ glued along $\phi_M(S^{n-1}) \ni \phi_M(y) \sim \phi_N(y) \in \phi_N(S^{n-1})$; in the smooth case the gluing is smoothed with a collar $S^{n-1} \times (-1,1)$ (Bär's "small neck"), and the page gives the explicit open decomposition $M \# N = \dot M \cup \dot N$ with $\dot M \cap \dot N \cong S^{n-1} \times (0,1)$ used in the homology computation. Convention callout: the page states that independence of $M \# N$ from the choices (up to diffeomorphism) is a theorem (Palais / Cerf disc theorem) that the series does NOT prove and does NOT use — every statement in chapters XII–XIII is proved for the connected sum formed with any fixed choice of discs, and the notation $M \# N$ always refers to some such choice; the orientation convention for $\overline{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ summands is recorded here. Examples verified: $M \# S^n \cong M$ (explicit), $S^2 \# T^2$ is a torus (via the $\dot{S^2} \cong$ disc picture; as a homology computation only). Calibration check: identify $\dot M \cap \dot N$, explain the orientation reversal on one side, compute $\chi(M \# N) = \chi(M) + \chi(N) - 2$ for surfaces after the homology theorem.
