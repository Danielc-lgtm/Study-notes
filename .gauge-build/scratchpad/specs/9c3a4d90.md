## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition (anchor); source items: (the notion $\star\bar\Omega = \pm\bar\Omega$ from B p. 102; D3.3.7's "self-dual" clause); prereqs: [Def - The Yang-Mills Field Strength, Thm - Self-Dual Decomposition of 2-Forms in Four Dimensions, Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Forms]; spec: on an oriented Riemannian 4-manifold, $\omega$ is *self-dual* if $\star\bar\Omega = \bar\Omega$ and *anti-self-dual* if $\star\bar\Omega = -\bar\Omega$; equivalently $F^- = 0$, resp. $F^+ = 0$, where $F^\pm := \tfrac12(F\pm\star F)$ (the splitting $\Omega^2(M;\operatorname{ad}P) = \Omega^2_+\oplus\Omega^2_-$ tensored with $\operatorname{ad}P$); orientation reversal exchanges the two notions; the anti-self-duality *equations* $F_A^+ = 0$ as a first-order nonlinear PDE for $A$ (Haydys R1.2 mentions them); examples: flat connections (both); $U(1)$ over $S^2\times S^2$ with $F = c_1\mathrm{vol}_1 + c_2\mathrm{vol}_2$ is (A)SD iff $c_1 = \pm c_2$ (computed); non-example: a $U(1)$-connection with $F^+\ne0\ne F^-$.
