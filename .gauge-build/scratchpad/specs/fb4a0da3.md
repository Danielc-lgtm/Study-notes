## Spec (from the manifest)

type: definition; source items: D4.1.12 (→ link `Def - Exact Sequence of Groups`, AT III, for the definition of exactness; this page restates it), D4.1.13, R4.1.2; prereqs: [Def - Fibre Bundle, Def - Homotopy Groups, Def - Exact Sequence of Groups, Thm - Homotopy Lifting Property for Fibre Bundles]; spec: For a fibre bundle $p : E \to B$ (link `Def - Fibre Bundle`), $e_0 \in E$, $b_0 = p(e_0)$, $F = p^{-1}(b_0)$ with $J^{n-1} := \overline{\partial I^n \setminus (I^{n-1} \times \{0\})}$: given $u \in C(I^n, B)$ with $u(\partial I^n) = \{b_0\}$, the homotopy lifting property (forward link) applied to the homotopy $u : I^{n-1} \times I \to B$ (last coordinate as time, in the reversed direction so that the lift starts on $I^{n-1} \times \{1\}$) with initial lift the constant map $e_0$ on $I^{n-1} \times \{1\}$ — more precisely, the relative version with the lift prescribed on $J^{n-1}$ — yields $\tilde u \in C(I^n, E)$ with $p \circ \tilde u = u$ and $\tilde u(J^{n-1}) = \{e_0\}$; define $\partial [u] := [\tilde u|_{I^{n-1} \times \{0\}}] \in \pi_{n-1}(F, e_0)$. Bär's description (lifting the family of straight lines from $I^{n-1} \times \{1\}$ continuously in the initial point, R4.1.2 and figure) is given as the mental picture, and the page explains that continuity in the initial point is exactly the homotopy lifting property. The page proves: (a) $\tilde u|_{I^{n-1} \times \{0\}}$ maps $\partial I^{n-1}$ to $e_0$ and lands in $F$; (b) $\partial[u]$ is independent of the choice of lift $\tilde u$ and of the representative $u$ (both via the relative homotopy lifting property applied to $I^n \times I$ with the lift prescribed on $J^n$ — the standard argument, Hatcher p. 376 or Bredon VII.6); (c) $\partial$ is a homomorphism for $n \ge 2$ (lift $u_1 * u_2$ by concatenating lifts in the first coordinate). Examples: the trivial bundle has $\partial = 0$ (verified: lift $u$ as $(u, e_0)$); the covering $\exp : \mathbb{R} \to S^1$ has $\partial : \pi_1(S^1, 1) \to \pi_0(\mathbb{Z}, 0) = \mathbb{Z}$ sending the standard generator to $1$ (verified by lifting $t \mapsto e^{2\pi i t}$). Calibration check: compute $\partial$ for $z \mapsto z^k$; explain why $\partial$ lands in the fibre.
