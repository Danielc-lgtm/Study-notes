## §1.6 Finite Abelian Groups — Exercises

- [[Ex - Counting abelian groups of a given order]] — counts isomorphism types of abelian group of order $720$ by reducing each prime's exponent to a partition, giving $p(4)\cdot p(2)\cdot p(1) = 10$ [[[Def - Abelian Group]], [[Def - Direct Product]], [[Def - Isomorphism]], [[Thm - Classification of Finite Abelian Groups]], [[Thm - Chinese Remainder Theorem for Cyclic Groups]]]
- [[Ex - Invariant factors from elementary divisors]] — converts an abelian group from elementary divisor to invariant factor form by the grid-transpose-and-fuse algorithm: sort prime powers into descending columns, fuse across rows [[[Def - Abelian Group]], [[Def - Direct Product]], [[Def - Order of a Group and of an Element]], [[Thm - Classification of Finite Abelian Groups]], [[Thm - Chinese Remainder Theorem for Cyclic Groups]]]
- [[Ex - The Chinese remainder theorem and cyclic groups]] — proves $C_{mn}\cong C_m\times C_n$ for coprime $m,n$ by exhibiting an element of order $mn$, then splits $C_{60}$ into prime-power cyclic factors [[[Def - Abelian Group]], [[Def - Direct Product]], [[Def - Order of a Group and of an Element]], [[Def - Isomorphism]], [[Thm - Chinese Remainder Theorem for Cyclic Groups]]]
