## §1A The Spaces $F^n$ — Exercises

LADR §1A introduces complex numbers and the spaces $\mathbb{F}^n$ as the motivating example of a vector space, with coordinate-wise addition and scalar multiplication. The exercises drill verification of the field axioms for $\mathbb{C}$, manipulation of complex arithmetic, and properties of $\mathbb{F}^n$ anticipating the abstract vector-space axioms. The unifying skill is **manipulating axiom-level identities by direct computation** — checking commutativity, associativity, distributivity, and the existence of inverses, both for $\mathbb{C}$ as a field and for $\mathbb{F}^n$ as a vector space. These verifications are routine but worth doing once to internalize what the axioms force and what they leave free.

- [[Ex - Complex multiplicative inverse formula]] (⭐) — existence and uniqueness of multiplicative inverse in $\mathbb{C}$ via the conjugate trick $\alpha^{-1} = \overline{\alpha}/|\alpha|^2$; foundational field-axiom verification ([[Def - Field]]).
- [[Ex - Cube roots of unity]] (⭐) — verifying $\omega = \tfrac{-1 + \sqrt{3} i}{2}$ satisfies $\omega^3 = 1$ by direct computation, and listing all three cube roots; prototype of polar-form arithmetic in $\mathbb{C}$ ([[Def - Field]]).
- [[Ex - Solving a linear equation in F^n]] (⭐) — solve $v + \lambda x = w$ uniquely; drills cancellation in a vector space and the role of nonzero-scalar-inverses ([[Def - Vector Space]], [[Thm - Uniqueness of Additive Identity and Inverses]]).
