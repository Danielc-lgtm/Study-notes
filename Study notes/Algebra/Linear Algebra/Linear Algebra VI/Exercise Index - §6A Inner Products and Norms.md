## §6A Inner Products and Norms — Exercises

The exercises of §6A drill the foundational technology of inner-product spaces: the inner product itself, the induced norm, the basic inequalities (Cauchy-Schwarz, triangle, parallelogram law), and the polarization identity. The techniques practiced here recur throughout the rest of the chapter and (for those moving into functional analysis) throughout Hilbert-space theory. Most exercises involve **squared-norm expansion via sesquilinearity** (operation 1) or **applying Cauchy-Schwarz** (operation 5), with the recurring move being "expand $\|u + tv\|^2$ as a polynomial in $t$ and exploit its non-negativity".

- [[Ex - Cauchy-Schwarz attained iff one vector is a scalar multiple of the other]] (⭐⭐) — characterize the equality case in Cauchy-Schwarz by tracing through the orthogonal-projection proof and identifying the residual norm that must vanish ([[Def - Inner Product Space]], [[Def - Norm Induced by an Inner Product]], [[Thm - Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality]], [[Thm - Pythagorean Theorem]])
- [[Ex - Inner product determined by norm via the polarization identity]] (⭐⭐) — recover the inner product from the norm using the polarization identity, deriving the real-case two-term and complex-case four-term formulas via squared-norm expansion ([[Def - Inner Product Space]], [[Def - Norm Induced by an Inner Product]], [[Thm - Parallelogram Law]])
- [[Ex - Orthonormal lists are linearly independent]] (⭐) — extract coefficients by taking inner products with basis vectors; the orthonormal structure makes a vanishing linear combination force each coefficient to be zero ([[Def - Orthogonal and Orthonormal Vectors]], [[Def - Inner Product Space]], [[Thm - Pythagorean Theorem]])
