## §2.1 Definitions and Examples — Exercises

- [[Ex - Units and zero divisors of the integers mod n]] — translate the equation $\overline{a}\,\overline{x} = \overline{1}$ into a Bézout linear combination to find the units of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, then use a finiteness/pigeonhole argument to show every nonzero non-unit is a zero divisor, deducing the field criterion ([[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Unit and Field]], [[Def - Characteristic of a Ring]])
- [[Ex - A power series is a unit if and only if its constant term is]] — solve the unit equation $fg = 1$ coefficient by coefficient, exploiting that multiplication by $X$ raises degree to make the system triangular and recursively invert in $R[[X]]$ ([[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Unit and Field]], [[Def - Polynomial Ring]])
- [[Ex - Boolean rings are commutative]] — instantiate the universal idempotent identity $x^2 = x$ at compound elements $x + x$ and $x + y$, expanding squares without assuming commutativity, to force characteristic two and then commutativity ([[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Characteristic of a Ring]])
- [[Ex - Subrings generated by an element]] — apply the subring test (closure plus inherited axioms) to $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ and $\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12]$, then characterise the smallest subring containing an element by a double inclusion as the image of evaluation $\mathbb{Z}[X] \to R$ ([[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Subring]], [[Def - Polynomial Ring]])
