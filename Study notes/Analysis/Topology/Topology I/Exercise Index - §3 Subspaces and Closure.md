## §3 Subspaces, Closure, and Interior — Exercises

- [[Ex - Closure of the rationals in different topologies]] (⭐) — computing $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ in four topologies on $\mathbb{R}$ (standard, discrete, cofinite, Sorgenfrey) and reading each answer off the closed-set description, showing closure is a purely topological operation ([[Def - Closure, Interior, and Boundary]], [[Def - Dense Subset]], [[Thm - Characterizations of the Closure]], [[Def - Topological Space]]).

- [[Ex - Constructing a continuous map by pasting]] (⭐⭐) — building a continuous piecewise-linear map via the pasting lemma on a finite closed cover, then exhibiting the role of the closedness hypothesis through counterexamples ([[Def - Continuous Map]], [[Thm - The Pasting Lemma]], [[Def - Subspace Topology]], [[Def - Homeomorphism]]).

- [[Ex - A continuous bijection that is not a homeomorphism]] (⭐⭐) — showing the winding map $[0, 2\pi) \to S^1$ is a continuous bijection but not open, identifying the failure at $(1, 0)$, and recovering $S^1$ as the quotient $[0, 2\pi]/(0 \sim 2\pi)$ ([[Def - Continuous Map]], [[Def - Homeomorphism]], [[Def - Subspace Topology]], [[Def - Open and Closed Sets in a Metric Space]]).
