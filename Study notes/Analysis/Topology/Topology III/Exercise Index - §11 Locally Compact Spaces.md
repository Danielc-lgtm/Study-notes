## §11 Locally Compact Spaces — Exercises

- [[Ex - The one-point compactification of Rn is the sphere]] — explicit stereographic homeomorphism $S^n \to (\mathbb{R}^n)^+$ via $(x_1, \dots, x_{n+1}) \mapsto (x_1, \dots, x_n)/(1 - x_{n+1})$, finished by the compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection trick ([[Thm - One-Point Compactification]], [[Def - Locally Compact Space]])
- [[Ex - LC + Hausdorff is needed for closure-of-open-bounded properties]] — closed unit ball in $\ell^2$ is bounded and closed but not compact (orthonormal basis $e_n$ has pairwise distances $\sqrt 2$), so $\ell^2$ is not locally compact ([[Def - Locally Compact Space]], [[Def - Cauchy Sequence and Complete Metric Space]], [[Thm - Compactness in Metric Spaces (Three Equivalents)]])
- [[Ex - Stone–Čech compactification of N]] — $\beta\mathbb{N}$ via embedding in $[0, 1]^{\ell^\infty}$, ultrafilter correspondence, cardinality $2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$, principal vs free ultrafilters ([[Thm - Stone–Čech Compactification]], [[Def - Completely Regular Space]], [[Def - Locally Compact Space]])
