## §8 Products — Exercises

The exercises of §8 drill the product topology and its distinguishing features. Hausdorff passes to products (by projecting to a coordinate where points differ); Hausdorffness is equivalent to closedness of the diagonal $\Delta = \{(x, x)\}$ in $X \times X$ (a useful reformulation); and for countable products, the box topology is *strictly finer* than the product topology — illustrated by the diagonal sequence $(1/k)_k$ that converges coordinatewise but not in the box.

- [[Ex - Product of Hausdorff is Hausdorff]] (⭐) — separation of points in a product via projection to a single coordinate where they differ ([[Def - Product Topology]], [[Def - Separation Axioms|Def - Hausdorff Space]])
- [[Ex - The diagonal is closed iff Hausdorff]] (⭐⭐) — reformulation of Hausdorffness as a topological property of a specific subset of $X \times X$ ([[Def - Separation Axioms|Def - Hausdorff Space]], [[Def - Product Topology]])
- [[Ex - Box vs product topology on a countable product]] (⭐⭐⭐) — strict-finer comparison and the diagonal sequence $x_n^{(k)} = 1/k$ converging in product but not in box ([[Def - Product Topology]], [[Thm - Net Convergence in Product is Coordinatewise]])
