## §9 Metric Spaces Revisited — Exercises

The exercises of §9 drill the deeper structure of metric spaces: completion via equivalence classes of Cauchy sequences, bounded-metric truncation that preserves topology, and the three equivalent characterisations of compactness (compact = sequentially compact = complete + totally bounded). The unifying observation: metric concepts (completeness, boundedness, total boundedness) are *not* topological, but their *combinations* recover the topological notion of compactness.
