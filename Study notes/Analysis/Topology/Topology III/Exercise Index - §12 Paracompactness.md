## §12 Paracompactness — Exercises

The exercises of §12 drill paracompactness and its application to partitions of unity. An explicit smooth partition of unity on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is built from the bump $\exp(-1/t)$ and $\mathbb{Z}^n$-translates; the long line shows local Euclidean-ness *without* paracompactness (uncountable cofinality breaks countable exhaustions); and smooth manifolds, being LC + $\sigma$-compact + Hausdorff, are automatically paracompact and so support partitions of unity — the bedrock that makes Riemannian metrics and global integration on manifolds possible.

- [[Ex - Partitions of unity on Rn]] (⭐⭐) — smooth bump function via $\exp(-1/t)$, translates by $\mathbb{Z}^n$, normalize by the locally finite sum ([[Def - Partition of Unity]], [[Def - Locally Finite Family and Refinement]])
- [[Ex - A non-paracompact space]] (⭐⭐⭐) — the long line $\omega_1 \times [0, 1)$ with lexicographic order is locally Euclidean but not paracompact, by uncountable cofinality of $\omega_1$ ([[Def - Paracompact Space]], [[Def - Locally Finite Family and Refinement]])
- [[Ex - Partition of unity for a smooth manifold]] (⭐⭐⭐) — smooth manifolds are LC + $\sigma$-compact + Hausdorff ⇒ paracompact ⇒ smooth partitions of unity exist; applications to Riemannian metrics and integration of top-forms ([[Def - Partition of Unity]], [[Def - Paracompact Space]], [[Thm - Locally Compact σ-Compact Hausdorff is Paracompact]], [[Thm - Paracompact Has Partitions of Unity]])
