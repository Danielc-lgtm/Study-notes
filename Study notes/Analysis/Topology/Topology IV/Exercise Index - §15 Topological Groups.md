## §15 Topological Groups — Exercises

- [[Ex - SO(n) is connected]] — Prove path-connectedness of $\operatorname{SO}(n)$ by induction using the homogeneous space $\operatorname{SO}(n)/\operatorname{SO}(n-1) \cong S^{n-1}$, or constructively via block plane rotations ([[Def - Topological Group]], [[Def - Connected Space]], [[Def - Path-Connected Space]], [[Thm - Closure of a Subgroup is a Subgroup]]).

- [[Ex - The orthogonal group has two components]] — Show $\operatorname{O}(n) = \operatorname{SO}(n) \sqcup \operatorname{SO}(n) \cdot D$ via the continuous determinant $\det : \operatorname{O}(n) \to \{\pm 1\}$ ([[Def - Topological Group]], [[Def - Connected Space]], [[Def - Continuous Map]], [[Thm - Translations are Homeomorphisms]]).

- [[Ex - S1 and SO(2) are homeomorphic as topological groups]] — Construct the explicit angle-parametrization isomorphism $e^{i\theta} \mapsto R(\theta)$, upgrade via compact-Hausdorff ([[Def - Topological Group]], [[Def - Topological Subgroup, Homomorphism, Action]], [[Thm - Compact to Hausdorff Continuous Bijection is Homeomorphism]]).
