## §14 Homotopy — Exercises

The exercises of §14 drill the construction of explicit homotopies and deformation retracts. Convex sets contract by linear interpolation, punctured Euclidean space deformation-retracts to its boundary sphere via radial projection, and contractibility passes to retracts by composing with the retraction. The unifying technique: write down an explicit one-parameter family of continuous maps from the source to itself, interpolating between two desired endpoints, and check the endpoint and codomain conditions.

- [[Ex - Rn is contractible]] (⭐) — Show $\mathbb{R}^n$ is contractible via the linear homotopy $F(x, t) = (1-t)x$ ([[Def - Homotopy]], [[Def - Homotopy Equivalence and Contractible Space]]).

- [[Ex - Sphere is a deformation retract of punctured Euclidean space]] (⭐⭐) — Show $S^{n-1}$ is a strong deformation retract of $\mathbb{R}^n \setminus \{0\}$ via $F(x, t) = (1-t)x + t \cdot x/\|x\|$ ([[Def - Deformation Retract]], [[Def - Homotopy]], [[Def - Homotopy Equivalence and Contractible Space]]).

- [[Ex - A retract of a contractible space is contractible]] (⭐⭐) — Show contractibility is inherited by retracts: compose the ambient contraction with the retraction ([[Def - Homotopy Equivalence and Contractible Space]], [[Def - Deformation Retract]], [[Def - Homotopy]]).
