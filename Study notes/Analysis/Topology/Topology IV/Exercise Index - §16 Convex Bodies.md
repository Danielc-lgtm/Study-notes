## §16 Convex Bodies — Exercises

- [[Ex - Every n-simplex is homeomorphic to the disk]] — Identify $\Delta^n \cong D^n$ via the affine projection from the simplex's hyperplane to $\mathbb{R}^n$ and applying the convex body theorem ([[Def - Convex Body]], [[Thm - Compact Convex Body is Homeomorphic to a Disk]], [[Def - Compact Space]]).

- [[Ex - The cube and the ball]] — Construct the explicit norm-swapping radial homeomorphism $D^n \cong [-1, 1]^n$ via $f(x) = x \cdot \|x\|/\|x\|_\infty$ ([[Def - Convex Body]], [[Thm - Compact Convex Body is Homeomorphic to a Disk]]).

- [[Ex - The Brouwer fixed-point theorem]] — Prove every continuous $f : D^n \to D^n$ has a fixed point, via IVT ($n=1$) and the no-retraction argument ($n \geq 2$) ([[Def - Convex Body]], [[Thm - Compact Convex Body is Homeomorphic to a Disk]], [[Def - Continuous Map]]).
