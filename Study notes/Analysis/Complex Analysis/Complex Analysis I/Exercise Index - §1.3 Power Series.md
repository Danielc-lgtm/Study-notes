## §1.3 Power Series — Exercises

The exercises of §1.3 drill the toolkit for power series: computing radii via root/ratio tests, termwise differentiation (legal inside the radius), and constructing series with arbitrary prescribed radius. The unifying observation: every formal power series has a radius of convergence in $[0, \infty]$, and inside that radius the series is a holomorphic function whose derivative is the termwise derivative — the foundation of analytic-function-as-power-series identification.

- [[Ex - Computing radii of convergence]] (⭐) — mechanical computation of radii via root/ratio test, with boundary analysis ([[Def - Power Series and Radius of Convergence]], [[Thm - Radius of Convergence Formula and Uniform Convergence on Sub-Discs]])
- [[Ex - Termwise differentiation of a power series]] (⭐⭐) — verifying termwise vs. closed-form differentiation for the geometric series, iteration to higher derivatives ([[Thm - Power Series is Holomorphic with Termwise Derivative]], [[Def - Power Series and Radius of Convergence]])
- [[Ex - Power series with prescribed radius of convergence]] (⭐⭐) — constructing explicit series with any specified $R \in [0, \infty]$ ([[Def - Power Series and Radius of Convergence]], [[Thm - Radius of Convergence Formula and Uniform Convergence on Sub-Discs]])
