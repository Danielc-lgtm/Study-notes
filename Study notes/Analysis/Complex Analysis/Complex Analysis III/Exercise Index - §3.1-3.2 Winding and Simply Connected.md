## §3.1–3.2 Winding and Simply Connected — Exercises

The exercises of §3.1-3.2 drill the integral and additivity properties of the winding number. Each exercise computes a winding number directly from the integral formula $I(\gamma; w) = (2\pi i)^{-1} \int dz/(z - w)$, using additivity to handle concatenated curves and the unbounded-component property to handle exterior points. The unifying observation: winding numbers are integer-valued topological invariants that classify how curves loop around obstructions.

- [[Ex - Winding number of a figure eight]] (⭐⭐) — Decompose a curve into pieces and use additivity ([[Def - Winding Number]], [[Thm - Existence and Properties of the Winding Number]]).
