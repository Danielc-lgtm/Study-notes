## §3.3 Laurent Series and Residues — Exercises

The exercises of §3.3 drill the Laurent expansion and the residue calculus. Each exercise practices a different technique for extracting the residue: the simple-pole formula $g(a)/h'(a)$ for rational functions; the $(k-1)$-th derivative formula for higher-order poles; the Laurent expansion read-off for transcendental functions; and the trichotomy "removable / pole / essential" classified by the principal part. The unifying skill is matching the right tool to the singularity's structure.

- [[Ex - Computing residues at simple poles]] (⭐) — Apply the simple-pole formula to a rational function ([[Def - Residue]], [[Thm - Computing Residues]]).
- [[Ex - Residue at a higher-order pole]] (⭐⭐) — Apply the $(k-1)$-th derivative formula ([[Def - Residue]], [[Thm - Computing Residues]]).
- [[Ex - Classifying singularities of explicit functions]] (⭐⭐) — Classify removable, pole, essential by Laurent expansion ([[Def - Removable Singularity, Pole, Essential Singularity]], [[Thm - Riemann's Removable Singularity Theorem]], [[Thm - Pole Characterization]], [[Thm - Casorati–Weierstrass]]).
