## §3.5b Möbius and Conformal Maps — Exercises

The exercises of §3.5b drill the construction and use of Möbius transformations and other conformal maps. The triple-transitivity of the Möbius group lets one specify a transformation by its action on three points; the Cayley transform $(z - i)/(z + i)$ maps $\mathbb{H} \to \mathbb{D}$, the canonical isomorphism between the two standard simply connected domains; and the Joukowski map sends circles to aerofoils, the bridge between cylinder flow and aerodynamic lift.
