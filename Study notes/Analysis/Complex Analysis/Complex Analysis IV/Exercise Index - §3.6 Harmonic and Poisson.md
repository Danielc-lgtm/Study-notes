## §3.6 Harmonic Functions and Poisson — Exercises

The exercises of §3.6 drill the harmonic-function toolkit and the Dirichlet problem. Hurwitz's theorem controls zeros of locally uniform limits; the Poisson integral formula solves the Dirichlet problem on the disc, with the kernel encoding harmonic-measure densities; and conformal mapping (Cayley transform) transports solutions between the disc and the half-plane. The unifying frame: every harmonic-function problem on a simply connected domain reduces to the disc (or half-plane) via conformal pullback.

- [[Ex - Solving Laplace's equation on a disc]] (⭐⭐) — Dirichlet problem on $\mathbb{D}$ with $\cos(2\theta)$ boundary ([[Def - Harmonic Function]], [[Thm - Poisson Integral Formula]], [[Thm - Harmonic ↔ Real Part of Holomorphic (on Simply Connected)]]).
