## §3.5 Argument Principle and Mapping — Exercises

The exercises of §3.5 drill the argument principle and its corollaries Rouché and the open mapping theorem. Each exercise practices a different application: counting zeros by comparing a polynomial to its dominant term on a circle (Rouché), recovering constancy from a maximum-modulus equality (open mapping), and deriving the fundamental theorem of algebra by asymptotic analysis of $p'/p$ on large circles. The unifying technique: zeros and poles of holomorphic functions are accessible *globally* via contour integrals of logarithmic derivatives.

- [[Ex - Open mapping in action]] (⭐⭐) — Constancy from constant $|f|$ via open mapping or Cauchy-Riemann ([[Thm - Open Mapping Theorem]], [[Thm - Cauchy–Riemann Equations]]).
