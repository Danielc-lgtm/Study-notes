## §2.2 Cauchy's Theorem — Exercises

The exercises of §2.2 drill the strategic use of Cauchy's theorem on non-trivial domains: even when the integrand has singularities (here $1/z$ on $\mathbb{C}^\times$), one can apply Cauchy by finding a star-shaped subdomain containing the contour and avoiding the singular set. The recurring move is "find a star-shaped slit-plane neighbourhood; apply Goursat or star-shaped Cauchy there."
