## §2.4 Liouville and Max Modulus — Exercises

This section drills the *rigidity* arsenal of §2: [[Thm - Liouville's Theorem|Liouville]], [[Thm - Cauchy Estimates|Cauchy estimates]], and the [[Thm - (Global) Maximum Modulus Principle|maximum modulus principle]]. The unifying theme is that *quantitative* size information about a holomorphic function (a bound on the modulus, a growth rate at infinity, an equality on a boundary) converts into *structural* conclusions: constancy, polynomial truncation, identification with a specific function. Each exercise drills a different signature pattern of this conversion: bounded-entire-implies-constant lifted to harmonic; polynomial growth lifted to polynomial structure; boundary modulus equality lifted to a pin on the function via interior maximum.

- [[Ex - Liouville for harmonic functions]] (⭐⭐) — bounded harmonic on $\mathbb{R}^2$ is constant, via harmonic conjugate + exponentiation + Liouville ([[Thm - Liouville's Theorem]], [[Thm - Real and Imaginary Parts of a Holomorphic Function are Harmonic]], [[Thm - Existence of a Logarithm on Simply Connected Domains]])
- [[Ex - Cauchy estimates bound the growth of an entire function]] (⭐⭐) — entire with $|f(z)| \leq A|z|^n$ at infinity is a polynomial of degree $\leq n$; Cauchy estimate at $0$ with growth-bounded $M(R)$ and $R \to \infty$ kills high Taylor coefficients ([[Thm - Cauchy Estimates]], [[Thm - Higher Derivatives via CIF]], [[Thm - Holomorphic Functions are Analytic (Local Power Series Expansion)]], [[Thm - Liouville's Theorem]])
- [[Ex - Maximum modulus pins a function via boundary values]] (⭐⭐) — interior-attained maximum forces constancy via the strong max modulus principle; alternative two-sided squeeze via $1/f$ gives the same conclusion ([[Thm - (Global) Maximum Modulus Principle]], [[Thm - Local Maximum Modulus Principle]], [[Def - Holomorphic Function]])
