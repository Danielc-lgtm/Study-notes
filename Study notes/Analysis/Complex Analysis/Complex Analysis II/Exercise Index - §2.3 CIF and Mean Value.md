## §2.3 CIF and Mean Value — Exercises

The exercises of §2.3 drill the Cauchy integral formula in two registers: as a direct evaluator of contour integrals of the form $f(z)/(z - w)$, and as the parent of the mean value property — both for holomorphic functions and (by harmonic conjugate) for harmonic ones. The trigger-reaction pattern: see $f(z)/(z - w)$ with $f$ holomorphic, $w$ inside contour — reach for CIF and read off $2\pi i \cdot f(w)$.

- [[Ex - Computing an integral via CIF]] (⭐) — direct CIF application to $\int e^z/(z - 1)\,dz$ on $|z|=2$ ([[Thm - Cauchy Integral Formula]])
- [[Ex - Gauss's mean value identity for harmonic functions]] (⭐⭐) — lifting MVP for holomorphic to harmonic via harmonic conjugate ([[Thm - Mean Value Property for Holomorphic Functions]], [[Thm - Real and Imaginary Parts of a Holomorphic Function are Harmonic]])
