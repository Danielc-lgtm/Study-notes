## §2.3 Submanifolds of Euclidean Space — Exercises

- [[Ex - The sphere as a regular level set]] — prove the unit sphere is a smooth $(n-1)$-dimensional submanifold by checking that $1$ is a regular value of $x\mapsto|x|^2$ (gradient $2x$, nonzero on the sphere), read off the tangent space as the hyperplane orthogonal to the radius, and contrast with the critical value $0$ where the level set degenerates to a point ([[Thm - The Regular Value Theorem]], [[Def - Submanifold of Euclidean Space]], [[Def - The Tangent Space to a Submanifold]]).

- [[Ex - The orthogonal group as a submanifold]] — prove $O(n) = \{A^TA = I\}$ is a submanifold of the matrix space by taking the defining map $A\mapsto A^TA - I$ into the *symmetric* matrices (the non-obvious codomain correction), showing its derivative is surjective at every orthogonal matrix, computing the dimension $n(n-1)/2$, and identifying the tangent space at the identity as the skew-symmetric matrices — the Lie algebra ([[Thm - The Regular Value Theorem]], [[Def - Submanifold of Euclidean Space]], [[Def - The Tangent Space to a Submanifold]], [[Def - Group]]).

- [[Ex - Computing a tangent space]] — compute the tangent space to a paraboloid at a point by all three routes, kernel of the implicit derivative, image of a parametrization's derivative, and graph of the linearization of the defining function, then verify the three answers coincide and extract the tangent plane and normal vector ([[Def - The Tangent Space to a Submanifold]], [[Def - Submanifold of Euclidean Space]], [[Thm - The Regular Value Theorem]], [[Thm - The Chain Rule]]).
