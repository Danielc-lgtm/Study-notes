## §H.5 Homotopy-Coherent Composition — Exercises

These exercises close the chapter by explaining *why* composition should be homotopy-coherent and where the machinery pays off. The first proves the foundational fact that the singular complex of a space is a Kan complex — the fundamental ∞-groupoid — using that a geometric horn is a retract of the solid simplex; this is the combinatorial form of "spaces are ∞-groupoids". The second makes precise the slogan "inner-horn fillers are composites", showing the composite is a contractible space of choices and that the higher horns enforce its contractibility — the conceptual heart of the chapter. The third is the motivational algebraic-geometry payoff: the derived category of coherent sheaves is non-functorial precisely because it is a homotopy category, and the ∞-categorical enhancement repairs it. Together they answer "why bother with all of this?".
