## §H.1 2-Categories and Bicategories — Exercises

This section's exercises drill the move from one-dimensional to two-dimensional category theory: recognising a familiar structure as a higher category, and computing with the two compositions of $2$-cells. The first exercise verifies the motivating example $\mathbf{Cat}$ and isolates the interchange law as the naturality of natural transformations. The second establishes the dimension-shift dictionary "monoidal category = one-object bicategory", the prototype of degeneracy moving structure up a dimension. The third makes pasting concrete, showing interchange is what gives a grid of $2$-cells a single value. Together they install the two reflexes of $2$-category theory: read $2$-cells as enrichment in $\mathbf{Cat}$, and use interchange to make pasting unambiguous.
