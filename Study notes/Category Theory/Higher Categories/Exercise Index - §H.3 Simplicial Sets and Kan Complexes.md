## §H.3 Simplicial Sets and Kan Complexes — Exercises

These exercises build fluency with the combinatorial substrate of $\infty$-category theory: the simplex category $\Delta$, the standard simplices, and the presheaf structure of $\mathbf{sSet}$. The first works out the cosimplicial identities and computes the simplices of $\Delta^n$ via Yoneda, installing the reflex "never enumerate simplices by hand — read them off the representable description". The second shows the standard simplices are nerves of finite ordinals, linking $\Delta$ to $\mathbf{Cat}$ and exhibiting the nerve as an extension of the Yoneda embedding. The third recognises $\mathbf{sSet} = [\Delta^{op},\mathbf{Set}]$ as a presheaf category, inheriting completeness and cocompleteness pointwise — the silent engine behind every gluing construction (boundaries, horns, realisation) in the chapter.
