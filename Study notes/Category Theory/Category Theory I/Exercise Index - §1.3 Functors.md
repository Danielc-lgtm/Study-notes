## §1.3 Functors — Exercises

These exercises drill [[Def - Functor|functoriality]] from three angles: variance, the full/faithful checklist, and functoriality as a transfer principle. The first verifies the two power-set functors and extracts the universal heuristic "pushforward is covariant, pullback is contravariant". The second runs the [[Def - Functor|forgetful functor]] $\mathbf{Grp} \to \mathbf{Set}$ against the [[Def - Full, Faithful, and Essentially Surjective Functor|full/faithful/essentially-surjective]] checklist, pinning down the canonical "faithful, not full" profile of every algebraic-structure-forgetting functor. The third proves $\pi_1$ is a functor and harvests the consequence — functors preserve isomorphisms — to distinguish the plane from the punctured plane, the prototype of algebraic-topological invariants.

- [[Ex - The covariant and contravariant power-set functors]] (⭐) — checking functoriality and diagnosing variance; image is covariant, preimage is contravariant ([[Def - Functor]], [[Def - Opposite Category and Duality]])
- [[Ex - Forgetful functors and faithfulness]] (⭐⭐) — running a functor against the full/faithful/essentially-surjective checklist; forgetful = faithful, not full ([[Def - Functor]], [[Def - Full, Faithful, and Essentially Surjective Functor]], [[Def - Group]])
- [[Ex - The fundamental group is a functor]] (⭐⭐) — verifying functoriality of $\pi_1$ and transporting non-isomorphism through it ([[Def - Functor]], [[Def - Path-Product and the Fundamental Group]], [[Thm - The Fundamental Group is a Group]], [[Thm - Functors Preserve Isomorphisms]])
