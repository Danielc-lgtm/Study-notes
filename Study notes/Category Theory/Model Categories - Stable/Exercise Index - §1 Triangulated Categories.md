## §1 Triangulated Categories — Exercises

These exercises drill the *internal* mechanics of a [[Def - Triangulated Category|triangulated category]] — the apparatus that makes it usable independently of where it came from. The throughline is that everything operational flows from one move: complete a map to a distinguished triangle and apply a hom-functor. The first exercise establishes the long exact sequence (the single most-used fact); the second shows that rotation, with its mysterious sign, is exactly the device that propagates exactness around the triangle; the third decodes the octahedral axiom — the most opaque of TR1–TR4 — as the familiar third isomorphism theorem, by specializing the [[Def - Chain Map and Chain Homotopy|derived category]] to modules in degree $0$. Together they convert the axiom list into working intuition.

- [[Ex - The long exact sequence induced by a distinguished triangle]] (⭐⭐) — exactness from the axioms via rotation and TR3-comparison; the foundational computational fact ([[Def - Triangulated Category]], [[Def - Abelian Group]])
- [[Ex - Rotation of a triangle is forced by the axioms]] (⭐⭐) — rotation propagates exactness, and the sign $-\Sigma u$ is forced by the threefold-rotation constraint and boundary-sign consistency ([[Def - Triangulated Category]])
- [[Ex - The octahedral axiom as the third isomorphism theorem]] (⭐⭐⭐) — decoding TR4 in $D(R)$ on degree-$0$ injections recovers $(Z/X)/(Y/X) \cong Z/Y$ ([[Def - Triangulated Category]], [[Def - Chain Map and Chain Homotopy]], [[Def - Module]])
