## §3 Contractions and Batanin–Leinster Weak ω-Categories — Exercises

This is the culminating section, where weakness and coherence enter through the contraction and the definition "weak $\omega$-category $=$ $L$-algebra" is assembled and validated. The six exercises drill the conceptual core and its consequences: seeing every coherence cell (associator, unitor, pentagon) as a *contraction lift of a parallel pair*; producing weak $\omega$-categories canonically from contractible globular sets via initiality of $L$ (the homotopy-hypothesis shadow); embedding the strict theory fully and faithfully into the weak one; and understanding the one subtlety of truncation — why finite-dimensional contractions need *tameness*. Start with the associator/unitor exercise to make the contraction concrete, then the contractible-globular-set and strict-embedding exercises (both run on initiality of $L$), and finish with the tameness exercise, the deepest.

- [[Ex - A contraction supplies the associator and unitor cells]] (⭐⭐) — exhibit the associator, unitors, and pentagonator as contraction lifts of parallel pairs, seeing coherence and weak composition as one mechanism ([[Def - Contraction on a Globular Operad]], [[Def - Globular Operad]], [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory]])
- [[Ex - Any contractible globular set is a weak omega-category]] (⭐⭐⭐) — build a contraction on $\mathrm{End}(X)$ from contractibility of $X$ and use initiality of $L$ to make $X$ a weak $\omega$-groupoid, the algebraic mirror of "a contractible space is an $\infty$-groupoid" ([[Def - Weak ω-Category and Weak n-Category (Batanin-Leinster)]], [[Def - Contraction on a Globular Operad]], [[Def - Globular Operad]])
- [[Ex - Strict omega-categories are weak omega-categories]] (⭐⭐) — show $1$ is terminal in $\mathbf{OC}$ and the induced functor $\mathbf{Str\text{-}\omega\text{-}Cat} \to \mathbf{Wk\text{-}\omega\text{-}Cat}$ is full and faithful via non-empty fibres of $L$ ([[Def - Weak ω-Category and Weak n-Category (Batanin-Leinster)]], [[Def - Globular Operad]], [[Def - Contraction on a Globular Operad]], [[Thm - The Initial Contractible Globular Operad Exists]])
- [[Ex - Why finite-dimensional contractions need tameness]] (⭐⭐⭐) — recast precontraction/tameness as surjectivity/injectivity of the source-target pairing and exhibit a precontractible non-contractible operad that no higher dimension can repair ([[Def - Contraction on a Globular Operad]], [[Def - Weak ω-Category and Weak n-Category (Batanin-Leinster)]], [[Def - Globular Operad]])
