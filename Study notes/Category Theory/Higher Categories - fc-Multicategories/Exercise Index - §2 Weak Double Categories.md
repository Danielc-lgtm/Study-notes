## §2 Weak Double Categories — Exercises

These exercises develop the [[Def - Weak Double Category|weak double category]] as "an [[Def - fc-Multicategory|fc-multicategory]] in which every string has a universal composite", and they drill the chapter's signature lesson that *weakness is the shadow of a universal property*. The two concrete examples — rings with bimodule tensor, and spans with pullback — are the colimit and limit prototypes; in each the horizontal composition is a universal construction, so it associates only up to a canonical isomorphism that genuinely moves data. The structural exercises then show that turning off the vertical dial yields a [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory|bicategory]] (and, with one object, a [[Def - Monoidal Category|monoidal category]]), and that representability is exactly the condition upgrading an fc-multicategory to a weak double category, with all coherence derived from uniqueness of representing objects.

- [[Ex - The double category of rings and bimodules is weak not strict]] (⭐⭐) — horizontal composition by $\otimes_R$; refuting strictness by an explicit non-identity associator on constructed tensor objects ([[Def - Weak Double Category]], [[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Tensor Product of Modules]], [[Thm - Universal Property of the Tensor Product of Modules]])
- [[Ex - The double category of spans]] (⭐⭐) — horizontal composition by pullback; interchange from uniqueness of maps into a pullback, weakness from associativity of limits ([[Def - Weak Double Category]], [[Def - Pullback and Pushout]], [[Def - Category]])
- [[Ex - A bicategory is a one-object weak double category]] (⭐⭐) — turning off the vertical dial collapses squares to globular $2$-cells; coherence transports verbatim, and delooping gives the monoidal corollary ([[Def - Weak Double Category]], [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]])
- [[Ex - Representable fc-multicategories are weak double categories]] (⭐⭐⭐) — representability defines $\odot$; associator, unitors, pentagon, and triangle all from uniqueness of representing objects ([[Def - fc-Multicategory]], [[Def - Weak Double Category]], [[Thm - fc-Multicategories Subsume Bicategories and Monoidal Categories]])
