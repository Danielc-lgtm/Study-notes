## §1 fc-Multicategories — Exercises

These exercises drill the central recognition skill of the chapter: locating a concrete higher structure inside the single framework of the [[Def - fc-Multicategory|fc-multicategory]] by setting the four dials (number of objects, vertical structure trivial or not, one-object or many, representable or not). They run from the simplest collapse — one object, trivial vertical structure, giving a plain [[Def - Multicategory|multicategory]] — through the representable one-object case (a [[Def - Monoidal Category|monoidal category]]) to the richest four-layer example (rings and bimodules), which inhabits the corner no simpler structure can. Across all three, the reusable principle is that the classical coherence is inherited from the strict associativity of string concatenation, so identification is mostly a matter of reading off dials rather than verifying axioms by hand.

- [[Ex - A multicategory is a one-object fc-multicategory]] (⭐) — collapsing the fc-multicategory along the "one object, trivial vertical" dials; matching string-topped $2$-cells with multimaps and substitution with composition, including the nullary ($n=0$) layer ([[Def - fc-Multicategory]], [[Def - Multicategory]])
- [[Ex - A monoidal category as a one-object fc-multicategory]] (⭐⭐) — representability via the tensor; recovering the associator from uniqueness of representing objects and the unit from the empty string ([[Def - fc-Multicategory]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]], [[Thm - fc-Multicategories Subsume Bicategories and Monoidal Categories]])
- [[Ex - The fc-multicategory of rings bimodules and maps]] (⭐⭐⭐) — building the full four-layer example; $2$-cells over a string as maps out of the iterated tensor, substitution associativity from $\otimes$-associativity, and non-triviality of both dials ([[Def - fc-Multicategory]], [[Def - Ring]], [[Def - Tensor Product of Modules]], [[Thm - Universal Property of the Tensor Product of Modules]])
