## §1 Globular Sets and Strict n-Categories — Exercises

This section builds the cellular foundations of higher category theory. The exercises drill the two foundational moves: recognising hand-given cellular data as a [[Def - Presheaf|presheaf]] on a shape category (and inheriting limits, colimits, and free objects for free), and imposing strict composition correctly — which means, above all, getting the [[Thm - The Interchange Law|interchange law]] right. Together they teach why globular sets are the natural shape for *strict* higher categories (unique source, unique target, unique composites) and why the interchange law, not associativity, is the subtle axiom that makes multi-dimensional pasting well-defined. The third exercise is the strict shadow of the coherence machinery developed in §3.

- [[Ex - The globe category and its presheaves]] (⭐⭐) — recognising a globular set as a presheaf on $\mathbb{G}$; computing the standard globes and using Yoneda to identify maps out of them with cells ([[Def - Globular Set]], [[Def - Presheaf]], [[Thm - The Yoneda Lemma]])
- [[Ex - A strict 2-category is a Cat-enriched category]] (⭐⭐) — the interchange law is exactly functoriality of the enriched composition; iterated enrichment as the inductive definition ([[Def - Strict n-Category and Strict ω-Category]], [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory]], [[Def - Enriched Category]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]])
- [[Ex - Pasting and unique composites in a strict omega-category]] (⭐⭐⭐) — unique pasting composites from interchange (strict) versus interchange + coherence (weak); a non-example violating interchange ([[Def - Strict n-Category and Strict ω-Category]], [[Thm - The Interchange Law]], [[Thm - Coherence for Unbiased Monoidal Categories]], [[Def - Globular Set]])
