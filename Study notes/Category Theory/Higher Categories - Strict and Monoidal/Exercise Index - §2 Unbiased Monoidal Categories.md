## §2 Unbiased Monoidal Categories — Exercises

This section reformulates monoidal categories so that coherence becomes transparent: take the $n$-ary tensor as primitive for every arity rather than singling out the binary one. The exercises drill the three core skills: recognising algebraic structure as a [[Def - Weak and Lax Monoidal Functor|lax monoidal functor]] (a monoid is a lax functor from a point), moving fluently between the [[Def - Monoidal Category|biased]] and [[Def - Unbiased Monoidal Category|unbiased]] presentations and seeing exactly where [[Thm - Mac Lane Coherence Theorem|coherence]] enters, and transporting monoids along a strong monoidal functor. The running lesson is that the four flavours of monoidal functor (lax/oplax/weak/strict) are distinguished by the invertibility and direction of one comparison map, and choosing the wrong level silently discards your examples.

- [[Ex - A monoid is a lax monoidal functor from a point]] (⭐⭐) — monoids as lax functors $\mathbf{1}\to\mathcal{D}$; comonoids as oplax functors; why lax (not weak) is the correct level ([[Def - Weak and Lax Monoidal Functor]], [[Def - Monoid in a Monoidal Category]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]])
- [[Ex - From biased to unbiased and back]] (⭐⭐) — building the associator from the composition isomorphisms; defining $\gamma$ by re-bracketing needs Mac Lane coherence; the pentagon as the length-four associativity axiom ([[Def - Unbiased Monoidal Category]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]], [[Thm - Biased and Unbiased Monoidal Categories Coincide]], [[Thm - Mac Lane Coherence Theorem]])
- [[Ex - The free vector space functor is strong monoidal]] (⭐) — the free functor as a strong monoidal functor sending monoids to monoid algebras; why the forgetful functor is only lax ([[Def - Weak and Lax Monoidal Functor]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]], [[Def - Monoid in a Monoidal Category]], [[Def - Vector Space]])
