## §1 Reedy Categories and Diagram Model Structures — Exercises

This section installs the combinatorial foundation of the chapter: the [[Def - Reedy Category and the Reedy Model Structure|Reedy category]], the structure on an indexing category $\mathcal{R}$ that lets diagrams $\mathcal{M}^{\mathcal{R}}$ carry a model structure for *any* model category $\mathcal{M}$. The exercises drill the three skills you will use constantly: recognizing a category as Reedy by exhibiting its degree function and direct/inverse split (the prototype is $\Delta$, the simplex category); computing latching and matching objects as concrete colimits and limits, and seeing that for simplicial sets the latching object is the degenerate simplices; and reading off, for towers and cubes, when the ordinary limit or colimit already computes the [[Def - Homotopy Limit and Colimit|homotopy limit or colimit]]. Together they make [[Thm - Diagrams over a Reedy Category Form a Model Category]] usable in practice and prepare the ground for framings in §2.

- [[Ex - The simplex category is a Reedy category]] (⭐) — verifying the Reedy axioms via epi-mono factorization; the prototype recognition pattern ([[Def - Reedy Category and the Reedy Model Structure]], [[Def - Simplicial Set]])
- [[Ex - Latching and matching objects for cosimplicial and simplicial objects]] (⭐⭐) — computing $L_n$ and $M_n$ over $\Delta$ and $\Delta^{op}$; latching = degenerate simplices, matching = compatible boundaries ([[Def - Reedy Category and the Reedy Model Structure]], [[Def - Simplicial Set]], [[Def - Limit and Colimit]])
- [[Ex - Towers and cubes as Reedy diagram model structures]] (⭐⭐) — posets as Reedy categories and the criterion for strict (co)limit = homotopy (co)limit ([[Def - Reedy Category and the Reedy Model Structure]], [[Def - Pullback and Pushout]], [[Def - Homotopy Limit and Colimit]])
