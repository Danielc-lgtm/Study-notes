## §3.1 Products and Coproducts — Exercises

These exercises drill the two simplest [[Def - Limit and Colimit|limits and colimits]] — the [[Def - Product and Coproduct|product and coproduct]] — and the technique that runs through the entire chapter: identify a familiar set-level construction by verifying a universal property, then watch the same construction behave differently across $\mathbf{Set}$, $\mathbf{Grp}$, $\mathbf{Ab}$, and $\mathbf{Top}$. The decisive lesson is the product/coproduct asymmetry (products are computed on underlying sets, coproducts freely generate) and the degeneracy of the nullary case into terminal and initial objects. Master these and the verification routine transfers verbatim to equalizers, pullbacks, and general limits.

- [[Ex - Products in Set Grp and Top]] (⭐) — verifying the product universal property in three categories; the product topology is *forced* by universality ([[Def - Product and Coproduct]], [[Def - Direct Product]], [[Def - Product Topology]], [[Thm - Limits are Unique up to Unique Isomorphism]])
- [[Ex - Coproducts are disjoint unions free products and direct sums]] (⭐⭐) — the coproduct depends on the category; commutativity of the target decides whether it collapses to the direct sum or stays a free product ([[Def - Product and Coproduct]], [[Def - Free Group and Free Product]], [[Def - Direct Sum of Modules]], [[Thm - Limits are Unique up to Unique Isomorphism]])
- [[Ex - The empty product is a terminal object]] (⭐) — nullary universal constructions force unit objects; the empty product is terminal, the empty coproduct initial ([[Def - Product and Coproduct]], [[Def - Initial and Terminal Object]], [[Def - Limit and Colimit]])
