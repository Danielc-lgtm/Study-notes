## §2 Classical Operads — Exercises

These exercises drill the central objects of §2: an [[Def - Operad|operad]] is a one-object [[Def - Multicategory|multicategory]], a presentation-free description of a type of algebra, and its [[Def - Algebra for an Operad|algebras]] are the structures of that type. The first computes the two foundational examples — the associative and commutative operads — and shows how the symmetric-group action is the dial between "order matters" and "order does not", recovering monoids and commutative monoids as algebras. The second separates the symmetric and plain (non-symmetric) flavours, showing symmetrisation is a free functor and that commutativity is irreducibly symmetric. The third verifies from first principles that the [[Def - Endomorphism Operad|endomorphism operad]] is an operad and grounds the definition of "algebra over an operad" as a morphism into it. The recurring techniques: reading algebras off the binary generator and arity-three relations, using free-action invariants, and translating operad axioms into equations of functions.

- [[Ex - The associative and commutative operads]] (⭐⭐) — $\mathrm{Assoc}(n) = S_n$ and $\mathrm{Comm}(n) = *$; algebras are monoids and commutative monoids; the $S_n$-action as the commutativity dial ([[Def - Operad]], [[Def - Algebra for an Operad]], [[Def - Monoid in a Monoidal Category]])
- [[Ex - Symmetric versus plain operads]] (⭐⭐) — symmetrisation $\mathrm{Sym}(Q)(n) = Q(n)\times S_n$; free-action invariant; commutativity is not a symmetrised plain phenomenon ([[Def - Operad]], [[Def - Algebra for an Operad]])
- [[Ex - Verifying the endomorphism operad axioms]] (⭐⭐) — $\mathrm{End}_X$ from first principles; algebras as operad maps $P \to \mathrm{End}_X$; low-arity reading ([[Def - Endomorphism Operad]], [[Def - Operad]], [[Def - Algebra for an Operad]])
