## §1 Classical Multicategories — Exercises

These exercises drill the core move of §1: a [[Def - Multicategory|multicategory]] is the right home for *multi-input* operations, generalising a category exactly as multilinear maps generalise linear ones. The first exercise establishes that ordinary categories are the arity-one slice, so multicategory theory genuinely extends category theory. The second computes the most important concrete example — multilinear maps — and identifies the tensor product as a representability phenomenon. The third proves the structural punchline, that monoidal categories and representable multicategories are the same notion, which is the conceptual bridge from §1 into the operad theory of §2. Across all three, the recurring techniques are: matching two definitions piece by piece, checking substitution-closure by an arity count, and recognising universal properties as representability.

- [[Ex - A category is a multicategory with unary maps only]] (⭐⭐) — categories as the arity-one slice; vacuous-axiom discharge and arity-count closure ([[Def - Multicategory]], [[Def - Category]])
- [[Ex - The multicategory of multilinear maps]] (⭐⭐) — the multicategory $\mathbf{Vect}_k^{\otimes}$ of multilinear maps; tensor product as representability ([[Def - Multicategory]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]])
- [[Ex - Every symmetric monoidal category has an underlying multicategory]] (⭐⭐⭐) — monoidal category = representable multicategory; coherence suppression and universal-property coherence ([[Def - Multicategory]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]], [[Thm - Mac Lane Coherence Theorem]])
