## §6.3 Pre-Triangulated Categories — Exercises

This section drills the structural payoff of the chapter: the axiomatization of $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{C})$ as a pre-triangulated category and its relationship to triangulated categories. The first exercise verifies the axioms for pointed spaces and refutes triangulation by showing $\Sigma$ is not invertible — locating the single discriminating hypothesis. The second shows triangulated categories are the special invertible case, so pre-triangulated is genuinely weaker, with the number of distinguished-sequence classes as the diagnostic. The third uses the opposite category to reveal cofiber/fiber duality as one self-duality, the formal source of every "and dually" in the chapter. The techniques drilled are: check the four pre-triangulated ingredients, apply the diagnostic "is $\Omega\Sigma X \simeq X$?", and dualize via the opposite category.

- [[Ex - The homotopy category of pointed spaces is pre-triangulated but not triangulated]] (⭐⭐) — verify the pre-triangulated axioms for $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathbf{Top}_*)$; refute triangulation via $\Omega\Sigma S^0 \ne S^0$ and $\mathbb{C}P^2$ not a suspension; Freudenthal ([[Def - Pre-Triangulated Category]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]], [[Thm - The Puppe Cofiber and Fiber Sequences Agree]], [[Def - Higher Homotopy Group]])
- [[Ex - Every triangulated category is pre-triangulated]] (⭐⭐) — build the pre-triangulated data from $\Sigma$ an equivalence ($\Omega = \Sigma^{-1}$); the merge of two sequence classes into distinguished triangles; invertibility as the extra hypothesis ([[Def - Pre-Triangulated Category]], [[Def - Cofiber and Fiber Sequence]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]])
- [[Ex - The opposite of a pre-triangulated category is pre-triangulated]] (⭐⭐⭐) — the opposite swaps $\Sigma \leftrightarrow \Omega$ and cofiber $\leftrightarrow$ fiber sequences; cofiber/fiber duality as one self-duality; opposite model structures ([[Def - Pre-Triangulated Category]], [[Def - Opposite Category and Duality]], [[Def - Cofiber and Fiber Sequence]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]])
