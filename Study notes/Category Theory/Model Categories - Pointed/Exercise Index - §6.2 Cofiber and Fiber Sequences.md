## §6.2 Cofiber and Fiber Sequences — Exercises

This section drills the construction and use of the Puppe (co)fiber sequences and the long exact sequences they generate. The first exercise establishes the structural identity behind the whole sequence — the cofiber of the cofiber is the suspension — by pasting [[Def - Homotopy|homotopy]]-pushout squares, which is the universal technique for extending exact sequences. The second and third produce the two most-used long exact sequences in topology — of a [[Def - Fibration|fibration]] (fiber sequence, $[Z, -]$, homotopy) and of a pair (cofiber sequence, $[-, Z]$, cohomology) — as single instances of the abstract machinery, training the variance decision (maps in versus maps out) and showing every degree shift is a suspension or loop in disguise.

- [[Ex - The cofiber of the cofiber is the suspension]] (⭐⭐) — pasting homotopy-pushout squares to prove $C_i \simeq \Sigma X$, the connecting map, and the infinite Puppe sequence with signs ([[Def - Cofiber and Fiber Sequence]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]], [[Def - Pullback and Pushout]])
- [[Ex - The long exact sequence of a fibration from a fiber sequence]] (⭐⭐) — apply $[S^n, -]$ to a fiber sequence; the connecting map's degree shift from $[S^n, \Omega B] = \pi_{n+1}(B)$; path–loop fibration ([[Def - Cofiber and Fiber Sequence]], [[Def - Higher Homotopy Group]], [[Thm - The Suspension-Loop Adjunction]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]])
- [[Ex - The cofiber sequence computes the long exact sequence of a pair]] (⭐⭐) — cofiber of a cofibration is the quotient; apply a cohomology theory to recover the long exact sequence of a pair; suspension isomorphism as the boundary shift ([[Def - Cofiber and Fiber Sequence]], [[Def - Cofibrant and Fibrant Objects]], [[Def - Singular Homology]], [[Def - Pointed Model Category Suspension and Loop]])
