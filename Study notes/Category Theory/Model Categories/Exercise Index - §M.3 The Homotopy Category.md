## §M.3 The Homotopy Category — Exercises

These exercises drill the construction of the homotopy category through the homotopy relation. The first proves that left homotopy is an equivalence relation on cofibrant objects — the technical heart that the fundamental theorem depends on — and pinpoints where cofibrancy and two-out-of-three are used, in particular how transitivity is gluing cylinders. The second confirms the abstract cylinder/homotopy machinery recovers the classical notion in $\mathbf{Top}$, so the axiomatization is seen to capture the right idea. The third carries out the headline identification of the chapter, recognizing $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathbf{Ch}(R))$ as the derived category $D(R)$, with cofibrant replacement as projective resolution and model homotopy as chain homotopy. The thread is that the localization, opaque in general, becomes concrete homotopy classes between bifibrant objects.

- [[Ex - Left homotopy is an equivalence relation on cofibrant objects]] (⭐⭐⭐) — reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity of left homotopy via the cylinder, with the key lemma that end-inclusions are trivial cofibrations ([[Def - Cylinder Object, Path Object, and Homotopy]], [[Def - Cofibrant and Fibrant Objects]], [[Def - Model Category]])
- [[Ex - Homotopy in spaces recovers the usual notion]] (⭐⭐) — showing $A \times [0,1]$ is a cylinder object so that model homotopy is classical homotopy, with the dual path-space picture ([[Def - Cylinder Object, Path Object, and Homotopy]], [[Def - Homotopy]], [[Def - Model Category]], [[Def - Topological Space]])
- [[Ex - The homotopy category of chain complexes is the derived category]] (⭐⭐⭐) — identifying $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathbf{Ch}(R))$ with $D(R)$ via cofibrant = projective and model homotopy = chain homotopy ([[Thm - The Homotopy Category of a Model Category]], [[Def - Cylinder Object, Path Object, and Homotopy]], [[Def - Chain Map and Chain Homotopy]], [[Def - Module]])
