## §M.2 Lifting, Factorization, and the Retract Argument — Exercises

These exercises drill the combinatorial engine of the theory: lifting properties, factorizations, and the retract argument that links them. The first executes the retract argument in full diagrammatic detail, both forms, so the one diagram chase underlying every closure statement becomes automatic. The second proves the substantial half of the closure theorem — that cofibrations are exactly the maps lifting against trivial fibrations — and derives retract, pushout, and coproduct closure as formal corollaries, establishing the "factor-lift-retract" template. The third sketches the small object argument, the transfinite construction that actually produces the factorizations axiom MC5 demands, with smallness as the termination condition. The thread is that lifting plus factorization, glued by the retract argument, determine the entire model structure.
