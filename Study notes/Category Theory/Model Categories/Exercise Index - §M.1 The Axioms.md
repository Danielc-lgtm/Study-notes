## §M.1 The Axioms — Exercises

These exercises drill the five Quillen axioms themselves: what they assert, which consequences are immediate, and how they behave under the most basic operations. The first builds a complete (if degenerate) model structure from scratch and isolates the *shape* of an axiom check — cheap axioms first, lifting and factorization last — so that the hard examples later are approached with the right reflexes. The second extracts the elementary consequences of two-out-of-three and the retract axiom, establishing that weak equivalences behave like a would-be class of isomorphisms. The third proves the axiom list is self-dual, the structural fact that halves the labour in the rest of the chapter. Together they install the working vocabulary of the subject before any deep theorem is invoked.

- [[Ex - The trivial model structures on a category]] (⭐⭐) — verifying all five axioms on a degenerate model structure and identifying its homotopy category, isolating the axiom-check shape ([[Def - Model Category]], [[Def - Lifting Property and the Retract Argument]], [[Thm - The Homotopy Category of a Model Category]])
- [[Ex - Two-out-of-three and isomorphisms]] (⭐) — deducing that identities and isomorphisms are weak equivalences and proving the cancellation property, from MC2 and MC3 alone ([[Def - Model Category]], [[Def - Isomorphism, Monomorphism, Epimorphism]])
- [[Ex - The opposite of a model category]] (⭐⭐) — proving $\mathcal{M}^{op}$ is a model category with cofibrations and fibrations swapped, and reading off the cylinder/path duality ([[Def - Model Category]], [[Def - Opposite Category and Duality]], [[Def - Lifting Property and the Retract Argument]])
