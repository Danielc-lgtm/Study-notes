## §1 Algebraic Definitions — Exercises

The algebraic definitions treat composition as *chosen structure* — an operad action, a monad algebra, an operadic enrichment. The exercises in this section drill the two reflexes that this stance demands. The first is exhibiting an *algebra structure*: to show something is a weak higher category in an algebraic sense, you produce the action map and verify the algebra axioms, usually by leaning on a universal property rather than computing. The second is the *truncation / sanity check*: a weak higher category, restricted to its top dimension, must behave rigidly, because the coherence cells that would weaken it live one dimension higher and are killed by truncation. Across the three exercises you will see how strictness collapses the contraction to identities, how truncation strictifies the top-dimensional laws, and how Trimble's operadic enrichment interpolates between the algebraic and geometric camps via contractibility.

- [[Ex - Every strict omega-category is a Penon weak omega-category]] (⭐) — exhibit an $H$-algebra structure via the universal property of the stretching; strictness collapses the contraction cells to identities ([[Def - Penon Weak ω-Category]], [[Def - Monad and Comonad]])
- [[Ex - Penon weak 1-categories are ordinary categories]] (⭐⭐) — level-$1$ truncation: absence of nondegenerate $2$-cells forces strict associativity, recovering ordinary categories ([[Def - Penon Weak ω-Category]], [[Def - Category]], [[Def - Monad and Comonad]])
- [[Ex - Trimble enrichment and the E-infinity operad]] (⭐⭐⭐) — coherence-by-contractibility: a contractible $E_\infty$-operad supplies composition and all higher coherences with no obstruction ([[Def - Enriched Category]], [[Def - Penon Weak ω-Category]], [[Def - Monoidal Category]])
