## §3 Stable Module Categories and Comodules — Exercises

These exercises construct the [[Def - Stable Module Category over a Frobenius Ring|stable module category]] of a Frobenius ring and equip it with its triangulated structure. The first verifies that the quotient by maps-through-projectives is a genuine category (the ideal axioms). The second characterises the zero objects of that quotient — exactly the projective modules — so the stable category is "representation theory modulo the free part". The third builds the suspension as the cosyzygy and shows, using the Frobenius condition (projective = injective), that it is an invertible autoequivalence, making the category triangulated. The unifying skill is reasoning in a morphism-ideal quotient: translating "$X \cong 0$" into "identity in the ideal", recognising factorizations of identities as retracts, and exploiting projective = injective to cancel syzygy against cosyzygy.

- [[Ex - Stable equivalence and maps through projectives]] (⭐⭐) — verifying $\operatorname{PHom}$ is a two-sided ideal (subgroup via the direct sum of projectives, absorption on both sides), so $\underline{\mathbf{Mod}}_R$ is a category ([[Def - Stable Module Category over a Frobenius Ring]], [[Def - Projective Module]], [[Def - Module]])
- [[Ex - Projective objects become zero in the stable category]] (⭐⭐) — an object is zero iff its identity lies in the ideal; identity-through-a-projective means retract-of-a-projective means projective ([[Def - Stable Module Category over a Frobenius Ring]], [[Def - Projective Module]], [[Def - Module]])
- [[Ex - The syzygy is the loop functor on the stable category]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Schanuel's lemma for well-definedness, the Frobenius double-reading of one short exact sequence to cancel $\Sigma\Omega$, and the distinguished triangles giving the triangulated structure ([[Def - Stable Module Category over a Frobenius Ring]], [[Def - Projective Module]], [[Def - Module]])
