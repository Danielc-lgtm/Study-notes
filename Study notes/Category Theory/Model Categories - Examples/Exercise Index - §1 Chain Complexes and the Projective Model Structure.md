## §1 Chain Complexes and the Projective Model Structure — Exercises

These exercises drill the [[Def - Projective Model Structure on Chain Complexes|projective model structure]] on $\mathbf{Ch}(R)$ from three angles: identifying the cofibrant objects (complexes of projectives) and seeing that cofibrant replacement is projective resolution; computing maps out of the sphere and disk complexes to verify the lifting axiom by hand; and cashing out the payoff — that the homotopy category is the derived category $D(R)$ and that the derived tensor product computes Tor. Together they take you from the raw definition of the three classes to the identification with classical homological algebra. The unifying skill is the translation between homotopy-theoretic language (cofibrant, fibrant, weak equivalence) and algebraic language (projective, surjective, quasi-isomorphism).

- [[Ex - Identifying the cofibrant objects in chain complexes]] (⭐⭐) — unwinding cofibrancy over the initial object into degreewise projectivity, and identifying cofibrant replacement with projective resolution ([[Def - Projective Model Structure on Chain Complexes]], [[Def - Projective Module]], [[Def - Cofibrant and Fibrant Objects]])
- [[Ex - Sphere and disk complexes and the lifting axiom]] (⭐⭐) — representability $\mathbf{Ch}(R)(S^n, C) = Z_n(C)$ and $\mathbf{Ch}(R)(D^n, C) = C_n$, then characterising fibrations and trivial fibrations by lifting against the generators ([[Def - Projective Model Structure on Chain Complexes]], [[Def - Chain Map and Chain Homotopy]], [[Def - Model Category]])
- [[Ex - The homotopy category of chain complexes is the derived category]] (⭐⭐⭐) — applying the fundamental theorem, identifying abstract homotopy with chain homotopy via the cylinder object, concluding $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathbf{Ch}(R)) \simeq D(R)$ ([[Thm - Chain Complexes of Modules Form a Model Category]], [[Thm - The Homotopy Category of a Model Category]], [[Def - Chain Map and Chain Homotopy]])
- [[Ex - The derived tensor product computes Tor on chain complexes]] (⭐⭐⭐) — the "resolve, apply, take homology" recipe for total derived functors, verified numerically on $\mathrm{Tor}^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/m, \mathbb{Z}/n)$ ([[Thm - Chain Complexes of Modules Form a Model Category]], [[Def - Tensor Product of Modules]], [[Def - Projective Module]])
