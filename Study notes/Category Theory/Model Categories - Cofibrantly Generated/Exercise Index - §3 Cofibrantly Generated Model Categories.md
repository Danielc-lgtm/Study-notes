## §3 Cofibrantly Generated Model Categories — Exercises

These exercises drill the capstone of the chapter: exhibiting generating sets for the standard examples and running Kan's recognition theorem as a checklist. The first identifies the generators $I = \{S^{n-1}\hookrightarrow D^n\}$, $J = \{D^n\hookrightarrow D^n\times[0,1]\}$ for $\mathbf{Top}$ and shows the standard topological classes are lifting classes in disguise, with smallness from compactness. The second does the same for $\mathbf{sSet}$ with boundary and horn inclusions, where the skeletal filtration realizes every monomorphism as a cell complex and smallness is automatic in a presheaf category. The third works through the recognition theorem's five conditions as an axiom-by-axiom checklist, locating the two non-formal conditions and the four-move reverse-inclusion argument that is the signature proof of cofibrant generation. Together they teach the workflow "name $I, J$, read off the four classes by lifting, certify smallness, check Kan's conditions," the standard route by which any new model category is built.
