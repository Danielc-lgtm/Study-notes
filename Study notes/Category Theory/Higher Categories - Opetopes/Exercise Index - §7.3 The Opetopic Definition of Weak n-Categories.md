## §7.3 The Opetopic Definition of Weak n-Categories — Exercises

These exercises drill the payoff of the chapter: a weak $n$-category is an [[Def - Opetopic Set|opetopic set]] in which every many-in, one-out niche has a **universal** filler, the chosen composite. The aim is to understand why this single condition is the right one, by validating it against known cases and stress-testing the hypotheses. The first exercise verifies the $n = 1$ calibration (universal fillers with trivial $2$-cells give exactly a [[Def - Category|category]]); the second verifies the harder $n = 2$ calibration (genuine $2$-cells give a [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory|bicategory]], with the associator, unitors, and pentagon/triangle all forced by universality); the third stress-tests universality itself, showing that mere existence of fillers gives a multivalued, non-canonical composition. The reflex being trained is to replace composition-as-operation by composition-as-universal-property, and to see that universality manufactures both the data and the coherence. These are conceptual validation problems with full rigorous solutions.

- [[Ex - At n equals 1 the universal filler condition gives a category]] (⭐⭐) — unwind universal fillers at $n = 1$ to recover a unique associative unital composite ([[Thm - Baez-Dolan Opetopic Weak n-Categories]], [[Def - Opetopic Set]], [[Def - Category]], [[Def - Limit and Colimit]])
- [[Ex - At n equals 2 the universal fillers reproduce a bicategory]] (⭐⭐⭐) — extract weak composition, associator, and unitors from universal fillers and show the coherence axioms are forced ([[Thm - Baez-Dolan Opetopic Weak n-Categories]], [[Def - 2-Category and Bicategory]], [[Def - Opetopic Set]], [[Def - Limit and Colimit]])
- [[Ex - A niche without a filler is the obstruction to composition]] (⭐⭐) — exhibit non-universal fillers giving multivalued composition, establishing why universality cannot be weakened to existence ([[Thm - Baez-Dolan Opetopic Weak n-Categories]], [[Def - Opetopic Set]], [[Def - Limit and Colimit]])
