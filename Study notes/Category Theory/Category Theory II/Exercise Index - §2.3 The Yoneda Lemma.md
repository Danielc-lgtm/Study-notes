## §2.3 The Yoneda Lemma — Exercises

These exercises exercise the most important result in the chapter, the Yoneda lemma, in three complementary registers. The first specializes it to a one-object category to recover Cayley's theorem, revealing the classical "every group embeds in a symmetric group" as a shadow of "every category embeds in its presheaves" — with the left/right handedness as the key subtlety. The second is the workhorse computation: identify a representable domain and read off the natural-transformation set as a functor value, then interpret the abstract elements as concrete operations (power maps, polynomial maps, precompositions). The third degenerates the lemma to thin and one-object categories, showing it becomes an order-embedding for posets and the regular representation for monoids. The unifying refrain: a natural transformation out of a representable is pinned down by where it sends the identity, and "the identity" plays the role of the universal element everywhere.
