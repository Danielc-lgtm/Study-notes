## §2.4 Universal Elements and the Category of Elements — Exercises

These exercises make precise the chapter's punchline: "universal" is a synonym for "initial or terminal", realized in the category of elements. The first computes the category of elements of a representable functor, recognizes it as a slice category, and locates the universal element as the identity sitting at the terminal apex — the transparent case that models the general representability criterion. The second identifies the universal element of the contravariant power-set functor as the subset $\{1\}$ (not the point $1$), with the type discipline as the lesson, and recognizes the structure as the subobject classifier of $\mathbf{Set}$. The third is the chapter's algebraic-geometry capstone: full faithfulness of $\mathbf{Spec}$ gives $\mathbf{AffSch} \simeq \mathbf{CRing}^{op}$ and the slogan "a scheme is determined by its functor of points". The common thread: a universal element is a terminal (or initial) object of the category of elements, and representability is exactly its existence.

- [[Ex - The category of elements of a representable functor]] (⭐⭐) — $\int \mathcal{C}(-, A)$ is the slice $\mathcal{C}/A$ with terminal object $(A, 1_A)$, the universal element; the "easy half" of the representability criterion ([[Def - Category of Elements]], [[Def - Hom-Functor and Representable Functor]], [[Def - Universal Element]], [[Def - Initial and Terminal Object]])
- [[Ex - Universal element of the power-set functor]] (⭐⭐) — the universal element of the contravariant power-set functor is the subset $\{1\} \in \mathcal{P}(\Omega)$; the subobject classifier of $\mathbf{Set}$ ([[Def - Universal Element]], [[Def - Hom-Functor and Representable Functor]], [[Def - Category of Elements]])
- [[Ex - A scheme is determined by its functor of points]] (⭐⭐⭐) — full faithfulness of $\mathbf{Spec}$ gives $\mathbf{AffSch} \simeq \mathbf{CRing}^{op}$ and "a scheme is its $R$-points across all rings"; arrows reverse ([[Thm - The Yoneda Lemma]], [[Thm - The Yoneda Embedding is Fully Faithful]], [[Def - Hom-Functor and Representable Functor]], [[Def - Ring]])
