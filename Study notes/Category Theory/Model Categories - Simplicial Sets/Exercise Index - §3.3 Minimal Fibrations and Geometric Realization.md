## §3.3 Minimal Fibrations and Geometric Realization — Exercises

These exercises develop the two technical pillars of the [[Thm - Geometric Realization is a Quillen Equivalence|Quillen equivalence]] $\mathbf{sSet} \simeq_Q \mathbf{Top}$: the rigidity of [[Def - Minimal Fibration|minimal fibrations]] and the product-preservation of geometric realisation. The unifying techniques are "reduce to representables, then triangulate" (for facts about $|{-}|$) and "homotopy from connectivity, equality from minimality" (for facts about minimal objects). One exercise isolates the product-preservation lemma via the shuffle triangulation of the prism; one shows minimality collapses a contractible complex to a point; one deduces the unit half of the Quillen equivalence from the counit half by the triangle-identity trick. Together they show how the single geometric input — that realisation respects products and sends minimal fibrations to bundles — propagates formally into the full equivalence of homotopy theories.

- [[Ex - Realization preserves products of standard simplices]] (⭐⭐) — the key product-preservation lemma, proved by density reduction plus the shuffle triangulation of the prism; why one works in compactly generated spaces ([[Thm - Geometric Realization is a Quillen Equivalence]], [[Thm - Geometric Realization is Left Adjoint to the Singular Nerve]], [[Def - Kan Complex and the Nerve]], [[Thm - The Yoneda Lemma]])
- [[Ex - A minimal Kan complex with trivial homotopy is a point]] (⭐⭐⭐) — the rigidity of minimality: connectivity supplies a homotopy, minimality upgrades it to equality, collapsing the complex by induction on dimension ([[Def - Minimal Fibration]], [[Def - Simplicial Homotopy Group]], [[Def - Kan Complex and the Nerve]])
- [[Ex - The unit map into the singular complex is a weak equivalence]] (⭐⭐) — deduces the unit half of the equivalence from the counit by a triangle identity and two-out-of-three; isolating the single geometric input ([[Thm - Geometric Realization is a Quillen Equivalence]], [[Thm - Geometric Realization is Left Adjoint to the Singular Nerve]], [[Def - Simplicial Homotopy Group]], [[Def - Quillen Adjunction and Quillen Equivalence]])
