## §4.1 Filtrations and Martingales — Exercises

The exercises of §4.1 drill the foundational martingale structures. The random walk carries three canonical martingales — the walk itself, the compensated square, and the exponential — each verified by the same "freeze the known, average the increment" computation. Convex functions of martingales are submartingales (the bridge connecting Doob's inequalities to martingales), and the Doob decomposition shows every adapted process splits uniquely as a martingale plus a predictable drift — the discrete forerunner of the Doob-Meyer decomposition in stochastic calculus.

- [[Ex - Martingales of the random walk]] (⭐⭐) — $S_n$, $S_n^2-n\sigma^2$, and the exponential martingale ([[Def - Martingale]], [[Def - Filtration]])
- [[Ex - Convex functions of martingales]] (⭐) — a convex function of a martingale is a submartingale; the bridge to Doob's inequalities ([[Def - Martingale]], [[Thm - Properties of Conditional Expectation]])
- [[Ex - The Doob decomposition]] (⭐⭐) — every adapted process is uniquely a martingale plus a predictable drift ([[Def - Martingale]], [[Def - Filtration]], [[Thm - Properties of Conditional Expectation]])
