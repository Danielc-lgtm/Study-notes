## §1.2 Independence, Borel–Cantelli, the 0–1 Law — Exercises

The exercises of §1.2 drill independence and its strict gradations. Bernstein's XOR example separates pairwise from mutual independence; the infinite monkey theorem combines disjoint blocks with the second Borel-Cantelli to show a fixed string appears infinitely often a.s.; and independence collapses double expectations into products via Fubini on the product measure. The unifying observation: independence is a strict refinement of "uncorrelated," and second Borel-Cantelli (requiring independence) is what converts "$\sum \mathbb{P}(A_n) = \infty$" into "$\mathbb{P}(\limsup A_n) = 1$."
