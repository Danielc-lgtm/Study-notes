## §2.1 Modes of Convergence and Uniform Integrability — Exercises

The exercises of §2.1 drill the hierarchy of convergence modes (almost-sure, in probability, in $L^p$, in distribution) and the role of uniform integrability. The four modes have specific implications and counterexamples calibrating each gap; convergence in distribution to a *constant* upgrades to convergence in probability (the unique deterministic-limit collapse); and uniform integrability is the precise condition under which "convergence implies convergence of expectations." The unifying recipe: pick the weakest mode that suffices, then check UI for the integral conclusion.

- [[Ex - Convergence in distribution to a constant]] (⭐⭐) — the one case where the weakest mode upgrades to convergence in probability ([[Def - Modes of Convergence]], [[Def - Weak Convergence]])
