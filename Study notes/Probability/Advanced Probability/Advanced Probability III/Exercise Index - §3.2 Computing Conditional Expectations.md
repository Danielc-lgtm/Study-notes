## §3.2 Computing Conditional Expectations — Exercises

The exercises of §3.2 drill the explicit computation of conditional expectations in three canonical settings: jointly Gaussian variables (linear regression, with residual independent of the conditioning variable by the Gaussian uncorrelated-equals-independent magic), variables with a joint density (integration against the conditional density), and conditional analogues of Jensen and variance decomposition. Each exercise practices the same recipe — guess a candidate, verify the two characterising properties of conditional expectation — but the concrete guess varies with the setting.

- [[Ex - The Gaussian conditional expectation]] (⭐⭐) — for jointly Gaussian variables, conditional expectation is linear; the residual is independent ([[Def - Conditional Expectation]], [[Thm - Conditional Expectation as L2 Projection]])
- [[Ex - Conditional expectation via the conditional density]] (⭐⭐) — conditional expectation as integration against the conditional density ([[Def - Conditional Density]], [[Def - Conditional Expectation]], [[Thm - Fubini-Tonelli Theorem]])
- [[Ex - Conditional Jensen and the variance decomposition]] (⭐⭐) — the $L^2$-contraction and the law of total variance ([[Thm - Properties of Conditional Expectation]], [[Thm - Conditional Expectation as L2 Projection]])
