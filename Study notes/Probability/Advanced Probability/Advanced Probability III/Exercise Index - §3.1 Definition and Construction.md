## §3.1 Conditional Expectation — Definition and Construction — Exercises

The exercises of §3.1 drill the abstract definition of conditional expectation and its working properties. The discrete case calibrates the intuition (block-average random variable, with extremes $\mathbb{E}[X]$ for trivial $\sigma$-algebra and $X$ for full $\sigma$-algebra); the tower property is the master computational tool ("condition on an auxiliary variable, compute, average"); and independence rules let irrelevant information be averaged out. The universal computational method is *propose and verify* — guess a candidate, check $\mathcal{G}$-measurability and the averaging identity, invoke uniqueness.
