## §1.2 Construction of Measures — Exercises

- [[Ex - Null sets are Carathéodory measurable]] — every outer-measure-zero set passes the Carathéodory criterion; the construction is automatically complete ([[Def - Outer Measure]], [[Def - Carathéodory Measurable Sets]], [[Def - Null Set and Completion]])
- [[Ex - Uniqueness fails without σ-finiteness]] — a pre-measure with no finite windows admits two distinct extensions; $\sigma$-finiteness is necessary ([[Thm - Hahn-Carathéodory Extension Theorem]], [[Thm - Uniqueness of the Hahn-Carathéodory Extension]], [[Def - σ-Finite Measure]])
- [[Ex - Approximating measurable sets by the algebra]] — every measurable set is, up to small measure, a finite union of algebra sets ([[Thm - Hahn-Carathéodory Extension Theorem]], [[Def - Outer Measure]], [[Def - σ-Finite Measure]])
