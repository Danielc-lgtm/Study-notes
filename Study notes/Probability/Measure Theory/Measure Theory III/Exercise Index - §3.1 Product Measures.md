## §3.1 Product Measures — Exercises

- [[Ex - Lebesgue measure is a product measure]] — $\lambda_{m+n}=\lambda_m\otimes\lambda_n$; Borel $\sigma$-algebras multiply, Lebesgue ones do not ([[Thm - Product Measure]], [[Def - Lebesgue Measure]], [[Thm - Dynkin's π-λ Theorem]])
- [[Ex - Slices of measurable sets are measurable]] — every slice of a product-measurable set is measurable, by the minimality principle ([[Def - Product σ-Algebra]], [[Thm - Dynkin's π-λ Theorem]])
- [[Ex - The diagonal has product measure zero]] — a thin set is planar-null; $\sigma$-finiteness is a genuine Tonelli hypothesis ([[Thm - Product Measure]], [[Thm - Fubini-Tonelli Theorem]])
