## §2.1 The Fundamental Group — Exercises

This section drills the basic definitions: homotopy of paths rel endpoints, the path-product operation, the fundamental group $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ as a group, and the first non-trivial computation $\pi_1(T^n) = \mathbb{Z}^n$. The exercises here focus on understanding the *group structure* of $\pi_1$ via explicit computations in product spaces and topological groups, building the foundation for the more substantive covering-space computations in §2.2 and §2.3. The recurring techniques drilled: the product formula $\pi_1(X \times Y) = \pi_1(X) \times \pi_1(Y)$, the Eckmann-Hilton argument forcing commutativity, the universal cover viewpoint $\pi_1 = \mathrm{Deck}(\widetilde X / X)$, and the use of $\pi_1(S^1) = \mathbb{Z}$ as a building block.

- [[Ex - Pi_1 of the Torus is Z Squared]] (⭐⭐) — product formula for $\pi_1$ via projection homomorphisms and explicit lifts to the universal cover $\mathbb{R}^n$ ([[Thm - Pi_1 of S^1 is Z]], [[Def - Path-Product and the Fundamental Group]], [[Def - Covering Space]])
- [[Ex - Pi_1 of a Topological Group is Abelian]] (⭐⭐) — Eckmann-Hilton argument: two unital binary operations on $\pi_1(G, e)$ with the interchange law force commutativity ([[Def - Path-Product and the Fundamental Group]], [[Def - Group]], [[Def - Homotopy of Paths]])
- [[Ex - The Brouwer Fixed Point Theorem in Dimension 2 via Pi_1]] (⭐⭐) — functoriality of $\pi_1$ as obstruction tool: the no-retraction theorem from $\pi_1(D^2) = 0 \neq \mathbb{Z} = \pi_1(S^1)$ ([[Thm - Pi_1 of S^1 is Z]], [[Def - Path-Product and the Fundamental Group]], [[Def - Continuous Map]])
