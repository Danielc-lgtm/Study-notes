## §2.1 Fibre Bundles and Principal Bundles — Exercises

This section drills the foundational definitions: general fibre bundles, principal $G$-bundles, the intrinsic right $G$-action, and the equivalence between the fibre-bundle and free-right-action formulations. The exercises illustrate triviality (and the failure thereof) by constructing global sections explicitly, with the moral that "principal bundle is trivial iff a global section exists". The patterns drilled here recur throughout the chapter: choosing a local section to give a local trivialization, using the right action to organize computations on the principal bundle, and recognizing nontriviality via topological obstructions (Euler characteristic, first Chern class, Stiefel-Whitney classes).

- [[Ex - Frame Bundle of R^n is Trivial]] (⭐) — Triviality of the principal $\mathrm{GL}(n)$-bundle of frames on $\mathbb{R}^n$ via the global coordinate frame; the prototype of all "principal bundle on a contractible base is trivial" arguments ([[Def - Frame Bundle of a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Principal G-Bundle]])

- [[Ex - The Tangent Bundle of S^2 from the SO(3) Hopf Fibration]] (⭐⭐) — Identification of $\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{SO}}(S^2)$ with $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ as a principal $\mathrm{SO}(2)$-bundle, recovery of $TS^2$ as the associated bundle, and nontriviality from $\chi(S^2) = 2$ ([[Def - Orthonormal Frame Bundle]], [[Def - Homogeneous Bundle]], [[Def - Associated Bundle]], [[Thm - Gauss-Bonnet for Closed Surfaces (Chern's Proof)]])

- [[Ex - The Möbius Strip as an Associated Bundle to a Double Cover]] (⭐⭐) — Construction of the Möbius line bundle as an associated bundle, with the connected double cover $S^1 \to S^1$ as the principal $\mathbb{Z}/2$-bundle; nontriviality of the principal bundle via failure of global section ([[Def - Principal G-Bundle]], [[Def - Associated Bundle]], [[Thm - Principal Bundles are Locally Trivial via G-Action]])
