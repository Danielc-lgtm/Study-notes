## §2.4 Topological Quantization and Berry Phase — Exercises

This section drills the **Berry phase** in concrete physical settings and the **Hopf bundle** as the underlying geometric object. The exercises connect quantum-mechanical observables (geometric phases acquired by wavefunctions in adiabatic evolution) to topological invariants (Chern numbers of line bundles). The recurring techniques are: computing the Berry connection from explicit eigenstates, integrating curvature to extract topological numbers (Chern numbers, solid angles), and exploiting the quaternionic / complex structure of $S^3 = \mathrm{SU}(2)$ to make the Hopf-bundle geometry transparent. The exercises culminate in the spin-$\tfrac{1}{2}$ half-solid-angle result, the experimental signature of geometric phase.

- [[Ex - Berry Phase for a Spin-Half in a Magnetic Field]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Explicit computation of the Berry phase $\gamma(C) = -\Omega(C)/2$ for a spin-$\tfrac{1}{2}$ particle in a magnetic field traversing a closed loop on the sphere of field directions; the half-solid-angle answer reflects the $\mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathrm{SO}(3)$ double cover and identifies the Berry line bundle with the Hopf bundle ([[Thm - Berry Phase Equals Holonomy of the Berry Connection]], [[Def - Berry Connection]], [[Thm - First Chern Class of the Hopf Bundle is One]])

- [[Ex - Hopf Fibration Computed Explicitly via Quaternions]] (⭐⭐) — Quaternionic description of the Hopf map $\pi(q) = q\mathbf{i}q^{-1}$ from $S^3$ (unit quaternions) to $S^2$ (unit imaginary quaternions); verification that fibres are great circles and that two distinct fibres link with linking number $\pm 1$ — the Hopf invariant ([[Def - The Hopf Bundle]], [[Ex - SU(2) is Diffeomorphic to S^3]])

- [[Ex - Computing the Euler Class of the Tangent Bundle of CP^n]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Computation $\chi(\mathbb{CP}^n) = n+1$ via two complementary methods (CW-decomposition and Chern-class formula); illustrates the Chern-class machinery that generalizes the Hopf-bundle Chern-number integration ([[Thm - Gauss-Bonnet-Chern Theorem]], [[Def - The Hopf Bundle]], [[Def - The Euler Class of a Real Oriented Vector Bundle]])
