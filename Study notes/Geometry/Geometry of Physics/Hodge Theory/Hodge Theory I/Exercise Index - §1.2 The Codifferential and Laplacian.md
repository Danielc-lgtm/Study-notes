## §1.2 The Codifferential and Hodge Laplacian — Exercises

This section drills the codifferential $\delta = \pm\star d\star$ and the Hodge Laplacian $\Delta = d\delta + \delta d$, the central operators of the chapter. The exercises focus on coordinate computation of $\delta$ and $\Delta$ in low dimensions, the identification of $\delta$ as the form-version of divergence on $\mathbb{R}^n$, and the form-language reformulation of source-free Maxwell's equations as $dF = 0$ and $\delta F = 0$. The recurring techniques are integration by parts (the adjoint identity $\langle d\alpha,\beta\rangle = \langle\alpha,\delta\beta\rangle$ on a closed manifold), composition $\star d\star$ with sign-tracking through signature, and the equivalence "harmonic $\iff$ closed and coclosed" on a closed Riemannian manifold.

- [[Ex - Maxwell's Equations Use the Codifferential]] (⭐⭐) — translates source-free Maxwell equations on Lorentzian $4$D into the form-language equations $dF = 0$, $\delta F = 0$, showing the Hodge star unifies the four classical equations into two ([[Def - The Hodge Star Operator]], [[Def - The Codifferential]], [[Def - Exterior Derivative on a Manifold]], [[Def - Minkowski Space and the Metric]])
- [[Ex - Computing the Hodge Star on S^2]] (⭐⭐) — computes the Hodge Laplacian on $S^2$ as $\Delta = -\star d\star d$ on functions, recovering the classical Laplace–Beltrami operator in spherical coordinates ([[Def - The Hodge Star Operator]], [[Def - Hodge Laplacian]], [[Def - The Codifferential]], [[Def - Riemannian Volume Form]])
- [[Ex - Hodge Star on R^3 Recovers Cross Product and Scalar Triple Product]] (⭐) — establishes the connection between $\star$ and classical $\mathbb{R}^3$ vector calculus, motivating $\delta$ as the divergence and $\Delta$ as the negative Laplacian on $1$-forms ([[Def - The Hodge Star Operator]], [[Thm - Properties of the Hodge Star]], [[Def - Musical Isomorphism (Flat and Sharp)]])
