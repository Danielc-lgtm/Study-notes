## §2.2 Covering Spaces and Lifting — Exercises

This section drills the basic theory of covering spaces and the lifting theorems. The exercises here focus on *recognising* a quotient as a covering map, *constructing* covers explicitly (the orientation cover, the spin cover), and *applying* the lifting criterion to deduce the topology of the base from the cover. The recurring techniques: identification of a quotient by a free properly discontinuous action with a covering map, the computation $\pi_1 = \mathrm{Deck}(\widetilde X / X)$ via simply-connected total space, and the use of the orientation double cover to study non-orientable surfaces. These exercises form the technical backbone of the Galois correspondence in §2.3.

- [[Ex - SU(2) is the Universal Cover of SO(3)]] (⭐⭐) — explicit construction of the spin double cover as conjugation $q \mapsto (v \mapsto qvq^{-1})$; kernel $\{\pm I\}$ identifies $\pi_1(\mathrm{SO}(3)) = \mathbb{Z}/2$ ([[Def - Universal Cover]], [[Def - Lie Group]], [[Ex - SU(2) is Diffeomorphic to S^3]], [[Thm - Galois Correspondence for Covering Spaces]])
- [[Ex - The Orientable Double Cover of the Möbius Strip]] (⭐) — explicit construction of the orientable double cover as a cylinder, with deck transformation the antipodal-and-flip; orientation character $w_1$ is the non-trivial $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2$ ([[Def - Orientable Double Cover]], [[Def - Covering Space]], [[Def - Deck Transformation Group]])
- [[Ex - Pi_1 of RP^n is Z over 2 for n at least 2]] (⭐⭐) — universal cover $S^n \to \mathbb{RP}^n$ via the antipodal quotient; uses simple-connectedness of $S^n$ for $n \geq 2$ ([[Def - Covering Space]], [[Def - Universal Cover]], [[Def - Simply Connected Space]], [[Thm - Galois Correspondence for Covering Spaces]])
