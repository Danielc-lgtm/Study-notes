# Problem Statement

On $P=M\times U(1)$, construct the principal connection determined by
$A\in\Omega^1(M;i\mathbb R)$, recover $A$ with the section $s(x)=(x,1)$, and compute the change under $s'=s e^{i\chi}$.

# Solution

> [!solution]- Solution
> Let $z$ denote the $U(1)$ coordinate and set
> $$\omega_{(x,z)}=A_x+z^{-1}dz.$$
> Because $U(1)$ is abelian, the usual formula
> $\operatorname{Ad}_{z^{-1}}A+z^{-1}dz$ reduces to this expression. On the
> fundamental vertical vector generated by $\xi\in i\mathbb R$, the first term
> vanishes and $z^{-1}dz(z\xi)=\xi$. For constant $u\in U(1)$,
> $R_u^*\omega=A+(zu)^{-1}d(zu)=\omega=\operatorname{Ad}_{u^{-1}}\omega$.
> Hence $\omega$ is a principal connection.
>
> Since $s^*(z^{-1}dz)=0$, one has $s^*\omega=A$. For
> $s'(x)=(x,e^{i\chi(x)})$,
> $$A'=(s')^*\omega=A+e^{-i\chi}d(e^{i\chi})=A+i\,d\chi.$$
> If physicists write the connection as $d+iqA_{\rm phys}$ with a real
> potential, this same formula becomes
> $A_{\rm phys}'=A_{\rm phys}+q^{-1}d\chi$ for the chosen sign convention.

# Rederivation Scaffold

A connection on a trivial bundle is “base potential plus Maurer–Cartan form.”
