## §1.1–1.2 Connections and Curvature — Exercises

These exercises move from a connection whose matrix vanishes in a preferred frame to a geometrically nontrivial connection whose curvature survives every frame change.

- [[Ex - Curvature of a Trivial Bundle with Trivial Connection is Zero]] (⭐) — verify the connection axioms, compute $A=0$, and recover flatness as the Maurer–Cartan identity after a moving-frame change.
- [[Ex - Connection on the Tangent Bundle of S^2 from the Round Metric]] (⭐⭐) — solve Cartan's first equation for the Levi–Civita connection, compute curvature, and integrate it to obtain $\chi(S^2)$.
- [[Ex - The Round Metric on the Sphere via Restriction]] (⭐, prerequisite review) — derive the metric used in the preceding calculation.
