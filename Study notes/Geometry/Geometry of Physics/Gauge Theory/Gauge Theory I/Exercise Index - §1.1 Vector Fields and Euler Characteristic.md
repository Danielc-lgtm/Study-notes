## §1.5 Vector-Field Enrichment — Exercises

This optional enrichment illustrates a second local-to-global mechanism: isolated zeros carry degrees whose sum is the Euler characteristic.

- [[Ex - Index of the Source-Sink Vector Field on the Sphere]] (⭐) — compute the two indices of the smooth height-gradient field and distinguish a genuine zero from a coordinate singularity.
- [[Ex - Stiefel Vector Field on the Odd Sphere is Nowhere-Zero]] (⭐⭐) — use the ambient complex structure to construct a unit tangent field on every odd sphere.
- [[Ex - Hairy Ball Theorem from Poincare-Hopf]] (⭐⭐, bridge) — recover the two-dimensional hairy-ball theorem from the surface index formula.
