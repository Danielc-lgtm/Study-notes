## §1.3 Electromagnetic Connection — Exercises

Use the fixed convention $\nabla=d+iqA$, $\psi'=e^{-iq\chi}\psi$, and $A'=A+d\chi$.

- [[Ex - Gauge-Invariant Coupling of Schrödinger to EM Field]] (⭐⭐) — prove the intertwining identity for every spacetime component and derive minimal coupling without expanding the Hamiltonian.
- [[Ex - Maxwell's Equations as Two Form Equations on Minkowski Space]] (⭐⭐, bridge) — distinguish the geometric identity $dF=0$ from the dynamical equation $d\star F=J$.
- [[Ex - A Form that is Closed but Not Exact on the Punctured Plane]] (⭐⭐, prerequisite review) — compute the de Rham class which supports flat but nontrivial abelian holonomy.
