## §1.4 Monopoles, Aharonov-Bohm, and Topological Effects — Exercises

This section's exercises are the flagship demonstrations of *topological* effects in $U(1)$-gauge theory — situations where the global structure of the bundle (or the topology of the configuration space) has *measurable physical consequences*. The Aharonov-Bohm exercise computes a *holonomy* on a flat connection over a non-simply-connected domain, producing an observable phase shift even when the field strength vanishes everywhere along the electron's trajectory. The Dirac-monopole exercise constructs a *non-trivial bundle* on $S^2$ from physical monopole data, deriving the quantization condition $\frac{2eg}{\hbar} \in \mathbb{Z}$ both from local cocycle consistency and from integrality of the first Chern number. Together they exhibit the two topological dimensions of $U(1)$-gauge theory: (a) flat connections on non-simply-connected base (parameterized by holonomy in $\mathrm{Hom}(\pi_1, U(1))$), and (b) non-flat connections on simply-connected base with non-trivial bundle (classified by integer Chern numbers).

- [[Ex - The Aharonov-Bohm Phase from the Magnetic Solenoid]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Computes the holonomy of the EM connection $A = \frac{\Phi}{2\pi}d\phi$ around a closed loop encircling an infinite solenoid, giving the phase shift $e^{ie\Phi/\hbar}$. Even though $\vec B = 0$ on the electron path, the gauge potential $A$ has measurable effects through its line integral. Proves that the vector potential is a physical field in QM, not just a mathematical convenience ([[Def - Wilson Line and Holonomy of a Connection]], [[Def - U(1) Gauge Field and Electromagnetic Connection]], [[Def - Curvature of a Vector-Bundle Connection]])
- [[Ex - Dirac Monopole as a Non-Trivial Bundle over S^2]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Constructs the Dirac monopole bundle $L_g \to S^2$ via two-patch trivialization, computes the transition function $c_{SN} = e^{-2ieg\phi/\hbar}$, and shows single-valuedness requires $\frac{2eg}{\hbar} \in \mathbb{Z}$. Independently computes the first Chern number $c_1 = \frac{2eg}{\hbar}$ via curvature integration. Identifies the bundle as $H^{\otimes n}$ for the Hopf line bundle $H$ ([[Def - The Dirac Monopole Bundle]], [[Thm - Dirac Quantization Condition]], [[Def - Complex Line Bundle]])
- [[Ex - A Form that is Closed but Not Exact on the Punctured Plane]] (⭐⭐) — *Supplementary, from [[Differential Geometry VIII — Differential Forms]].* The 1-form $d\phi$ on $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{0\}$ is the simplest example of a closed-but-not-exact form, generating $H^1(\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{0\}, \mathbb{R}) = \mathbb{R}$. This is the *de Rham cohomology* class realized by the Aharonov-Bohm potential $A = \frac{\Phi}{2\pi}d\phi$, and the analogous $H^2(S^2 \setminus \{0\}, \mathbb{R}) = \mathbb{R}$ generator is realized by the Dirac monopole's magnetic flux. The topological obstruction to gauge potentials being globally defined sits in these cohomology groups ([[Def - Closed and Exact Forms]], [[Def - de Rham Cohomology]])
