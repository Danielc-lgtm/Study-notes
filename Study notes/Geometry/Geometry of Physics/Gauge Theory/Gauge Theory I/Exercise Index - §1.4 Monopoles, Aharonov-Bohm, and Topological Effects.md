## §1.4 Flux and Holonomy — Exercises

The two principal global obstructions are deliberately contrasted: curvature flux can obstruct a global potential even on simply connected $S^2$, whereas a flat connection can retain monodromy on a nonsimply-connected base.

- [[Ex - Dirac Monopole as a Non-Trivial Bundle over S^2]] (⭐⭐⭐) — construct two smooth local potentials, derive the transition function, and prove that its degree equals $(q/2\pi)\int F$.
- [[Ex - The Aharonov-Bohm Phase from the Magnetic Solenoid]] (⭐⭐⭐) — compute holonomy of a flat connection on $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}$ and determine exactly when a single-valued gauge removes it.
- [[Ex - A Form that is Closed but Not Exact on the Punctured Plane]] (⭐⭐, prerequisite review) — verify the cohomological obstruction underlying the Aharonov–Bohm example.
