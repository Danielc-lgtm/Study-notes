# The Definition

> [!definition] Spin group
> Inside the even Clifford algebra $\operatorname{Cl}^0(\mathbb R^n)$, $\operatorname{Spin}(n)$ is generated by products of an even number of unit vectors. Its action on $\mathbb R^n$ by Clifford conjugation defines an exact sequence
> $$1\to\{\pm1\}\to\operatorname{Spin}(n)\to SO(n)\to1.$$

For $n\ge3$ this is the connected universal double cover. The central element $-1$ acts trivially on vectors but nontrivially on spinors, explaining why spinors require two full turns to return to their original state.

# Dimensions Three and Four

Identifying $\mathbb R^3$ with imaginary quaternions, unit quaternions act by $x\mapsto qxq^{-1}$. The kernel is $\{\pm1\}$, so
$$\operatorname{Spin}(3)\cong Sp(1)\cong SU(2).$$
On $\mathbb H\cong\mathbb R^4$, $(q_+,q_-)$ acts by $x\mapsto q_+xq_-^{-1}$, yielding
$$\operatorname{Spin}(4)\cong SU(2)_+\times SU(2)_-.$$
The two factors correspond to the self-dual and anti-self-dual summands of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$.

# Calibration

A spin rotation is not an extra rotation of the tangent space: it is a lift of one. The two lifts of a given rotation differ by the central sign.
