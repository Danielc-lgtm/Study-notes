## §2.2 Frame and Associated Bundles — Exercises

This section drills the **construction of bundles** via two complementary mechanisms: the frame bundle of a given vector bundle (extracting the principal bundle data from a vector bundle), and the associated-bundle construction (building bundles from a principal bundle plus a $G$-space). The exercises illustrate the **universality** of the principal bundle — every bundle with structure group $G$ is recovered from its principal $G$-bundle via the associated-bundle construction — and the **homogeneous-space construction** $G \to G/H$ as a primary source of principal bundles. The recurring technique is to identify a manifold as a homogeneous space, exhibit the principal bundle, and use associated-bundle constructions to recover the geometry.

- [[Ex - The Tangent Bundle of S^2 from the SO(3) Hopf Fibration]] (⭐⭐) — Building $TS^2 = \mathrm{SO}(3) \times_{\mathrm{SO}(2)} \mathbb{R}^2$ from the principal $\mathrm{SO}(2)$-bundle $\mathrm{SO}(3) \to S^2$ via the standard $\mathrm{SO}(2)$-action on $\mathbb{R}^2$; prototype of associated-bundle construction ([[Def - Frame Bundle of a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Orthonormal Frame Bundle]], [[Def - Associated Bundle]], [[Def - Homogeneous Bundle]], [[Thm - Associated-Bundle Construction Yields a Bundle]])

- [[Ex - The Möbius Strip as an Associated Bundle to a Double Cover]] (⭐⭐) — Building the Möbius line bundle as $S^1 \times_{\mathbb{Z}/2}\mathbb{R}$ from the connected double cover (principal $\mathbb{Z}/2$-bundle) via the sign action on $\mathbb{R}$; illustrates that the structure group can be discrete and the associated bundle nontrivial ([[Def - Associated Bundle]], [[Def - Principal G-Bundle]], [[Thm - Associated-Bundle Construction Yields a Bundle]])

- [[Ex - Frame Bundle of R^n is Trivial]] (⭐) — Construction of the global trivialization of $\mathrm{Fr}(T\mathbb{R}^n)$ from the coordinate global frame; foundational example of the section-trivialization correspondence ([[Def - Frame Bundle of a Vector Bundle]], [[Thm - Principal Bundles are Locally Trivial via G-Action]])
