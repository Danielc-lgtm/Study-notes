## §5.4 The BPST Solution — Exercises

This section drills the *concrete construction and properties of the BPST instanton*: computing its field strength, verifying its self-duality and topological charge, and probing its geometry via holonomy. The exercises here build hands-on familiarity with the simplest non-trivial Yang-Mills solution — the quaternionic ansatz, the Maurer-Cartan equation, the 't Hooft-symbol formalism, the Chern-Simons transgression. The reusable principle: *explicit gauge-theory computations rely on a small set of algebraic identities (Pauli matrices, 't Hooft symbols, Maurer-Cartan, Chern-Simons) repeated many times*.

- [[Ex - Computing the Field Strength of the BPST Instanton]] (⭐⭐⭐) — compute $F = dA - iA\wedge A$ for $A = (\rho^2/(\rho^2+r^2))g^{-1}dg$ via quaternionic and Pauli-matrix algebra, and verify $F = \star F$ ([[Def - The BPST Instanton]], [[Def - The Yang-Mills Field Strength]], [[Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Connection]]).
- [[Ex - Verifying the Second Chern Number of BPST is 1]] (⭐⭐) — compute the instanton number $k = \int\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)/8\pi^2$ by both bulk integration and the Chern-Simons boundary integral, and confirm $k = 1$ ([[Def - The BPST Instanton]], [[Def - Instanton]], [[Thm - Existence of the BPST Instanton]]).
- [[Ex - SU(2) Holonomy of the BPST Instanton on a Loop]] (⭐⭐⭐) — compute the path-ordered holonomy of BPST around an equatorial circle, exploiting the $SO(2)$ loop symmetry to collapse the path-ordering to an ordinary matrix exponential ([[Def - The BPST Instanton]], [[Def - Gauge-Covariant Derivative]]).
- [[Ex - 't Hooft Symbols and Self-Duality]] (⭐⭐) — verify the algebraic properties of the 't Hooft symbols that underlie the explicit BPST computation, including the self-duality of $\bar\eta^a_{\mu\nu}$ ([[Def - Self-Dual and Anti-Self-Dual Connection]], [[Def - The BPST Instanton]]).
