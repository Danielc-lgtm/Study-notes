## §3.3 Comparison Theorems — Exercises

This section drills the three signature global theorems of comparison geometry — **Cartan–Hadamard** (nonpositive sectional curvature on simply-connected complete manifolds gives a diffeomorphism with $\mathbb{R}^n$ via $\exp_p$), **Bonnet–Myers** (positive Ricci curvature on a complete manifold gives compactness, a diameter bound, and finite $\pi_1$), and the application of these to specific manifolds. The principal technique is **the Jacobi-field-second-variation argument**: choose a clever variation field on a geodesic (or closed geodesic), apply Synge's second-variation formula, derive a sign-contradiction with minimality. Most exercises route through Bonnet–Myers applied to a manifold where the Ricci bound is verified from a sectional bound; one exercise (Schwarzschild) directly computes curvature on a physically-motivated geometry.

- [[Ex - Bonnet-Myers Gives a Finite Fundamental Group of CP^n]] (⭐⭐) — Use the pinching $K \in [1/4, 1]$ on $\mathbb{CP}^n$ to bound Ricci below; apply Bonnet–Myers; strengthen to $\pi_1 = 0$ via Synge or the Hopf fibration ([[Thm - Bonnet-Myers Theorem]], [[Thm - Synge's Theorem]], [[Def - Sectional Curvature]], [[Def - Ricci Tensor]]).
- [[Ex - Schwarzschild Sectional Curvatures (Statement)]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Compute the orthonormal-frame sectional curvatures of the Schwarzschild metric via Cartan; verify $\mathrm{Ric} = 0$; deduce Schwarzschild is a non-flat Ricci-flat vacuum solution exhibiting the $M/r^3$ Newtonian tidal pattern ([[Def - Sectional Curvature]], [[Def - Ricci Tensor]], [[Def - Riemann Curvature Tensor]]).
- [[Ex - Sectional Curvature of the Round Sphere is 1]] (⭐⭐) — The model-space curvature calculation; combined with Bonnet–Myers, gives the sharpness of the diameter bound $\pi/\sqrt{\kappa}$ at $\pi$ for $S^n$, which is realised as the actual diameter ([[Def - Sectional Curvature]], [[Def - Constant Sectional Curvature]], [[Thm - Bonnet-Myers Theorem]]).
