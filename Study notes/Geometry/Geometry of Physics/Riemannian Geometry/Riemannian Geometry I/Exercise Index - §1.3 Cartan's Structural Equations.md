## §1.3 Cartan's Structural Equations — Exercises

The exercises of §1.3 drill [[Thm - Cartan's First Structural Equation|Cartan's first]] and [[Thm - Cartan's Second Structural Equation|second structural equations]] as the standard practical tool for computing the [[Def - Connection 1-Forms (Cartan)|connection 1-forms]] and [[Def - Curvature 2-Forms (Cartan)|curvature 2-forms]] of Riemannian and Lorentzian metrics in orthonormal frames. The first exercise (2-sphere) establishes the routine in the simplest non-trivial setting; the second (Schwarzschild) shows the same recipe applied to a 4D Lorentzian metric — the standard general-relativity computation. Each entry below names the exercise, summarises the technique it practices in one line, and lists in parentheses every definition and theorem invoked in that exercise's solution.

- [[Ex - Cartan Structural Equations on S^2]] (⭐⭐) — use the orthonormal coframe $(d\theta, \sin\theta\,d\varphi)$ on $S^2$, apply Cartan's first structural equation to extract $\omega^1{}_2 = -\cos\theta\,d\varphi$, apply the second to get $\Omega^1{}_2 = \sigma^1 \wedge \sigma^2$ and read off Gaussian curvature $K = 1$ ([[Thm - Cartan's First Structural Equation]], [[Thm - Cartan's Second Structural Equation]], [[Def - Connection 1-Forms (Cartan)]], [[Def - Curvature 2-Forms (Cartan)]])
- [[Ex - Computing Curvature 2-Forms in an Orthonormal Frame]] (⭐⭐⭐) — apply the Cartan structural equations to the Schwarzschild metric in 4D Lorentzian signature; compute all six independent curvature 2-forms; read off the $M/r^3$ tidal Riemann components — the standard route for computing curvature in general relativity ([[Thm - Cartan's First Structural Equation]], [[Thm - Cartan's Second Structural Equation]], [[Def - Connection 1-Forms (Cartan)]], [[Def - Curvature 2-Forms (Cartan)]], [[Def - Semi-Riemannian Metric and Signature]])
