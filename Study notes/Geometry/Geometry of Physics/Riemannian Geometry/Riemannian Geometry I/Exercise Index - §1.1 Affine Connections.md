## §1.1 Affine Connections — Exercises

The exercises of §1.1 drill the foundational toolkit for [[Def - Affine Connection on a Vector Bundle|affine connections on vector bundles]] — the construction of connections from frames, the verification of the connection axioms, the computation and interpretation of [[Def - Torsion Tensor|torsion]], and the recognition that flatness and torsion-freeness are independent properties. The Lie-group example exhibited here (the Weitzenböck connection) is the canonical illustration that the connection axioms admit non-Levi-Civita connections with interesting geometric content. Each entry below names the exercise, summarises the technique it practices in one line, and lists in parentheses every definition and theorem invoked in that exercise's solution.

- [[Ex - The Tangent Bundle of a Lie Group has a Canonical Flat Connection]] (⭐⭐) — construct a connection from a global left-invariant frame, compute its torsion as the (negative) Lie bracket, verify the curvature vanishes; recognise that "flat with torsion" and "curved without torsion" are independent ([[Def - Affine Connection on a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Torsion Tensor]], [[Def - The Lie Bracket of Vector Fields]])
