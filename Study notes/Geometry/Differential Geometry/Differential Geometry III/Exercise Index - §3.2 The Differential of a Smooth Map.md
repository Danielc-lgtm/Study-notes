## §3.2 The Differential of a Smooth Map — Exercises

This section drills the abstract algebra of the differential: linearity, the chain rule, the identity rule, and the [[Def - Diffeomorphism|diffeomorphism]] corollary. The exercises verify properties from the precomposition definition and deduce structural consequences (such as: diffeomorphic manifolds have equal [[Def - Dimension|dimension]]). The reusable skills are: (i) unfolding the precomposition definition $(dF_{p}(v))(f) = v(f \circ F)$ to reduce abstract identities to algebraic ones, (ii) using the chain rule to factor compositions into pieces, (iii) recognizing that a [[Def - Diffeomorphism|diffeomorphism]]'s differential is an isomorphism — a constantly-used input throughout differential geometry.

- [[Ex - The Differential is Linear and Functorial]] (⭐) — Verifying the precomposition definition's algebraic properties: $dF_{p}$ is $\mathbb{R}$-linear, the chain rule holds, and the differential of the identity is the identity. The two functor axioms together ([[Def - The Differential of a Smooth Map]], [[Def - The Tangent Space]], [[Thm - Chain Rule for the Differential]]).
- [[Ex - The Differential of a Diffeomorphism is an Isomorphism]] (⭐) — Using the chain rule applied to $F \circ F^{-1} = \mathrm{id}$ to conclude $dF_{p}$ is a vector-space isomorphism; concluding diffeomorphic manifolds have the same [[Def - Dimension|dimension]] ([[Def - Diffeomorphism]], [[Thm - Chain Rule for the Differential]], [[Thm - Dimension of the Tangent Space]]).
- [[Ex - Computing the Differential in Local Coordinates]] (⭐⭐) — Translating the abstract differential into the Jacobian matrix in coordinate bases via the multivariate chain rule; the operational core of differential-geometric calculations ([[Def - The Differential of a Smooth Map]], [[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - Partial Derivatives and the Jacobian Matrix]]).
