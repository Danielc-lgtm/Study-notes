## §3.3 Computations in Coordinates — Exercises

This section drills the chart-based calculations that ground abstract tangent-vector theory in familiar multivariate calculus. The exercises convert manifold-level statements into Jacobian computations in coordinates and identify the resulting tangent vectors with their abstract counterparts. The reusable skills are: (i) reading off coordinate components $v^{i} = v(x^{i})$ from a tangent vector, (ii) using the chart isomorphism to identify $T_{p}M$ with the chart-image's standard basis, (iii) computing differentials as Jacobian matrices in chart coordinates, (iv) recognizing the chart-by-chart calculation as coordinate-independent via the change-of-chart formula.

- [[Ex - Tangent Vectors as Velocities of Coordinate Curves]] (⭐) — Grounding the abstract $\partial/\partial x^{i}|_{p}$ as the velocity of a specific curve along the $i$-th coordinate axis; the geometric intuition behind the algebraic definition ([[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - Velocity of a Curve]]).
- [[Ex - Tangent Space to a Sphere at the North Pole]] (⭐⭐) — Computing the coordinate basis at a specific point using stereographic-projection coordinates, and identifying with the embedded tangent plane in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ ([[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - The Tangent Space to a Submanifold]], [[Def - Velocity of a Curve]]).
- [[Ex - Computing the Differential in Local Coordinates]] (⭐⭐) — Working out the Jacobian-matrix representation of $dF_{p}$ in coordinate bases via the multivariate chain rule on the coordinate representative ([[Def - The Differential of a Smooth Map]], [[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - Partial Derivatives and the Jacobian Matrix]]).
