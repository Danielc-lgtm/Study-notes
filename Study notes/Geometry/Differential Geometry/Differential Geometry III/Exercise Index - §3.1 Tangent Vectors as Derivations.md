## §3.1 Tangent Vectors as Derivations — Exercises

This section drills the algebraic content of the tangent space — derivations, the equivalence of definitions, and the dimension theorem. The exercises convert abstract definitions into concrete computations and back: identifying $T_{p}M$ in specific manifolds, verifying that constructions produce derivations, and recognizing the three pictures (derivation, curve, chart-tuple) as the same object. The reusable skills are: (i) computing with the precomposition definition $(dF_{p}(v))(f) = v(f \circ F)$ at the abstract level, (ii) using charts to convert abstract identities to coordinate calculations, (iii) realizing tangent vectors as velocities of curves to get geometric intuition.

- [[Ex - Tangent Space to a Sphere at the North Pole]] (⭐⭐) — Identifying the abstract $T_{N}S^{2}$ with the embedded horizontal plane in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ via stereographic-projection coordinates ([[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - Velocity of a Curve]], [[Def - The Tangent Space to a Submanifold]], [[Thm - Equivalence of Tangent Vector Definitions]]).
- [[Ex - Tangent Vectors as Velocities of Coordinate Curves]] (⭐) — Showing the coordinate basis vectors are velocities of coordinate-axis-aligned curves; grounds the algebraic definition in geometric motion ([[Def - Coordinate Tangent Vectors]], [[Def - Velocity of a Curve]]).
- [[Ex - Tangent Space of the General Linear Group at the Identity]] (⭐⭐) — Identifying $T_{I}\mathrm{GL}(n) \cong M_{n}(\mathbb{R})$ via the open-subset principle, plus a preview of the Lie algebra bracket via the commutator-curve calculation ([[Def - The Tangent Space]], [[Def - Velocity of a Curve]], [[Thm - Equivalence of Tangent Vector Definitions]]).
