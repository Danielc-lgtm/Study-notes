## §6.3 The Cotangent Bundle and 1-Forms — Exercises

This section drills the construction and basic properties of the cotangent bundle, the dual coordinate coframe, and the 1-form structure on a manifold. The central exercise constructs $T^*M$ explicitly via the vector-bundle construction lemma applied to inverse-transpose-Jacobian transition functions — the canonical example of multi-bundle building from cocycle data. The dual-coframe exercise validates the coordinate-1-form formalism that every concrete computation with 1-forms relies on. The exercise on the conservative form on the punctured plane previews the topological obstruction to exactness that drives de Rham cohomology.

- [[Ex - Constructing the Cotangent Bundle from Transition Functions]] (⭐⭐) — Build $T^*M$ as a smooth rank-$n$ vector bundle by applying the construction lemma to the inverse-transpose Jacobian cocycle. ([[Thm - Vector Bundle Construction Lemma]], [[Def - Cotangent Space and Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Transition Function of a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Dual Basis]])
- [[Ex - The Dual Frame on a Coordinate Chart]] (⭐) — Verify $(dx^j)$ is a smooth local frame for $T^*M$ in a chart, with the dual-basis property $dx^j(\partial/\partial x^i) = \delta^j_i$. ([[Def - Cotangent Space and Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Local Frame]], [[Def - Dual Basis]])
- [[Ex - A Conservative 1-Form on R² Minus Origin]] (⭐⭐) — Show the 1-form $(x dy - y dx)/(x^2+y^2)$ on the punctured plane is closed but not exact, via a unit-circle integral of $2\pi$. ([[Def - Covector Field and Differential 1-Form]], [[Def - The Differential of a Function as a 1-Form]], [[Def - Line Integral of a 1-Form]], [[Thm - A Closed 1-Form on a Simply Connected Manifold is Exact]])
