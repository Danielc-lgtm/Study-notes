## §6.2 Sections and Frames — Exercises

This section drills the algebraic and computational machinery of sections and local frames. The central exercise establishes the dual coordinate coframe $(dx^j)$ as a smooth local frame for $T^*M$ on a chart, providing the concrete tool used in every coordinate computation involving 1-forms. The two companion exercises (the triviality of $TS^1$ and the non-triviality of the Möbius bundle from §6.1) drill the global-frame existence question — which is what distinguishes trivial from nontrivial bundles, and is the single most-asked diagnostic for line-bundle structure. Together these exercises ensure the reader can compute components of sections in frames, recognize local frames in coordinate charts, and test global trivializability by frame-existence arguments.

- [[Ex - The Dual Frame on a Coordinate Chart]] (⭐) — Verify that the coordinate covector fields $(dx^j)$ on a chart form a smooth local frame for $T^*M$ on the chart's domain. ([[Def - Cotangent Space and Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Local Frame]], [[Def - Dual Basis]], [[Def - Covector Field and Differential 1-Form]])
- [[Ex - The Tangent Bundle of the Circle is Trivial]] (⭐) — Use the global vector field $\partial/\partial\theta$ as a global frame for $TS^1$, certifying triviality. ([[Def - Local Frame]], [[Def - Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Vector Field on a Manifold]])
- [[Ex - The Möbius Bundle is Nontrivial]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Show no global frame exists for the Möbius bundle, via a sign-flip argument on the universal cover. ([[Def - Local Frame]], [[Def - Section of a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Transition Function of a Vector Bundle]])
