## §6.1 Vector Bundles — Exercises

This section drills the foundational machinery: constructing vector bundles, detecting triviality, and understanding the role of transition functions and the cocycle condition. The exercises range from a single-line global frame argument for $TS^1$ (a positive triviality test) to the prototypical non-triviality example, the Möbius bundle, and the canonical application of the construction lemma to assemble $T^*M$ from coordinate data. Each exercise reinforces the link between the abstract bundle structure and the operational tools — local trivializations, transition functions, global frames — used in computation.

- [[Ex - The Tangent Bundle of the Circle is Trivial]] (⭐) — Construct a global frame on $TS^1$ via the angular vector field $\partial/\partial\theta$, certifying triviality. ([[Def - Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Local Frame]], [[Def - The Tangent Bundle]])
- [[Ex - The Möbius Bundle is Nontrivial]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Construct the Möbius bundle as $\mathbb{R}^2/\sim$ and prove non-triviality via a lift-to-universal-cover sign-flip argument. ([[Def - Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Local Trivialization]], [[Def - Transition Function of a Vector Bundle]], [[Thm - Vector Bundle Construction Lemma]])
- [[Ex - Constructing the Cotangent Bundle from Transition Functions]] (⭐⭐) — Apply the vector-bundle construction lemma to inverse-transpose-Jacobian transition functions to assemble $T^*M$. ([[Thm - Vector Bundle Construction Lemma]], [[Def - Cotangent Space and Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Transition Function of a Vector Bundle]], [[Def - Dual Basis]])
