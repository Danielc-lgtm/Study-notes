## §10.2 The Poincaré Lemma and Homotopy Invariance — Exercises

This section's exercises drill the Poincaré lemma (closedness implies exactness on contractible domains) and homotopy invariance (homotopy equivalent manifolds have isomorphic cohomology). The unifying theme is reducing global cohomology computations to local triviality via contractibility, and using homotopy equivalences to replace complicated manifolds with simpler ones (deformation retracts onto submanifolds). The Mayer–Vietoris exercise from §10.1 — particularly [[Ex - Computing H^1 of S^1 via Mayer-Vietoris]] — is the canonical demonstration of how the Poincaré lemma plus homotopy invariance combine in concrete computations.

- [[Ex - The de Rham Cohomology of R^n is Trivial in Positive Degrees]] (⭐) — the direct Poincaré-lemma application: contractibility of Euclidean space forces vanishing of $H^k_{dR}$ in positive degrees ([[Def - de Rham Cohomology]], [[Thm - The Poincaré Lemma on a Star-Shaped Region]])
- [[Ex - Computing H^1 of S^1 via Mayer-Vietoris]] (⭐⭐) — combines Poincaré lemma on each piece of a two-arc cover with the Mayer–Vietoris machinery; identifies $[d\theta]$ as the non-trivial generator by integration ([[Thm - The Poincaré Lemma on a Star-Shaped Region]], [[Thm - The Mayer-Vietoris Sequence]])
- [[Ex - The de Rham Cohomology of the Torus]] (⭐⭐⭐) — uses homotopy invariance implicitly via the Künneth formula (each $S^1$ factor's cohomology, computed via Poincaré + Mayer–Vietoris on a two-arc cover, is what enters the tensor product) ([[Thm - Homotopy Invariance of de Rham Cohomology]], [[Thm - The Mayer-Vietoris Sequence]])
