## §9.3 Stokes's Theorem on Manifolds — Exercises

This section's exercises drill the central theorem of the topic — Stokes's theorem on manifolds, $\int_M d\omega = \int_{\partial M}\omega$ — and its applications. The unifying skill is recognizing when an integral problem is *really* a Stokes problem: the integrand is the differential of something simpler, or the integral is over a boundary that bounds a tractable region, or the integrand is exact on a closed manifold so the integral vanishes by topology. The exercises here range from elementary specializations (Green's theorem on the plane) to deep applications (de Rham cohomology of $S^1$, Maxwell's equations as form equations on Minkowski space). Each exercise reinforces the principle that Stokes is the universal mechanism linking differential structure ($d$) to boundary structure ($\partial$).

- [[Ex - Stokes' Theorem Recovers Green's Theorem on R^2]] (⭐) — Specializes Stokes's manifold theorem to $n = 2$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$, recovering Green's theorem $\oint_{\partial D}P\,dx + Q\,dy = \int_D(\partial_xQ - \partial_yP)\,dA$. Verifies the induced boundary orientation matches the standard counterclockwise convention. ([[Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]], [[Thm - Green's Theorem]], [[Def - Exterior Derivative on a Manifold]])
- [[Ex - The de Rham Cohomology of S^1 is R]] (⭐⭐) — Identifies $H^1_{dR}(S^1) \cong \mathbb{R}$ generated by the angular form $d\theta$, using Stokes to show the integration functional $\int_{S^1} : H^1_{dR}(S^1) \to \mathbb{R}$ is well-defined (it kills exact forms) and an isomorphism. ([[Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]], [[Def - Closed and Exact Forms]], [[Def - Differential k-Form on a Manifold]])
- [[Ex - Maxwell's Equations as Two Form Equations on Minkowski Space]] (⭐⭐⭐) — Packages all four classical Maxwell equations into $dF = 0$ and $d{\star}F = J$ on Minkowski space, deduces charge conservation $dJ = 0$ from $d^2 = 0$, and uses Stokes on a 4-volume to derive the integral charge conservation law. ([[Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]], [[Def - Differential k-Form on a Manifold]], [[Def - Exterior Derivative on a Manifold]], [[Def - Closed and Exact Forms]], [[Def - Minkowski Space and the Metric]])
- [[Ex - A Form that is Closed but Not Exact on the Punctured Plane]] (⭐⭐) — *(from [[Differential Geometry VIII — Differential Forms]])* Shows that on $\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\}$ the angular form $d\theta$ is closed but not exact (its integral over the unit circle is $2\pi$), generating $H^1_{dR}(\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\}) = \mathbb{R}$. The deformation argument here is a foundational use of Stokes. ([[Def - Closed and Exact Forms]], [[Thm - Stokes' Theorem on Manifolds]])
