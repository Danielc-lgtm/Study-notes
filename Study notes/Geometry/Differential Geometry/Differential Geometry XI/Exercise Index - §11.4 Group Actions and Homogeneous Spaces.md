## §11.4 Group Actions and Homogeneous Spaces — Exercises

This section drills smooth Lie group actions, the orbit-stabilizer realization $M \cong G/G_p$ of [[Def - Homogeneous Space|homogeneous spaces]], and the computation of [[Def - Adjoint Representation|adjoint representations]] on Lie algebras. The exercises develop the recognition of familiar manifolds (spheres, projective spaces) as homogeneous spaces of classical [[Def - Group|groups]], and the computation of how [[Def - Group|groups]] act on their own Lie algebras. The most reusable technique drilled is: given a manifold with a recognizable Lie group of symmetries, verify transitivity and identify the stabilizer to realize the manifold as $G/H$.

- [[Ex - S^2 as a Homogeneous Space of SO(3)]] (⭐⭐) — realizes $S^2 \cong \mathrm{SO}(3)/\mathrm{SO}(2)$ via the natural rotation action; prototype for $S^n = \mathrm{SO}(n+1)/\mathrm{SO}(n)$ ([[Def - Smooth Action of a Lie Group]], [[Def - Homogeneous Space]], [[Thm - Orbit-Stabilizer for Lie Group Actions]])
- [[Ex - The Adjoint Representation of SO(3) is the Defining Representation]] (⭐⭐⭐) — computes $\mathrm{Ad}_g(\widehat v) = \widehat{gv}$, identifying $\mathrm{Ad}$ on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ as the defining $3$-dimensional representation; uses $\mathrm{SO}(3)$-equivariance of the cross product ([[Def - Adjoint Representation]], [[Def - The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]], [[Ex - The Lie Algebra of SO(3) is Antisymmetric Matrices]])
- [[Ex - SU(2) is Diffeomorphic to S^3]] (⭐⭐) — $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ acts on $\mathfrak{su}(2) \cong \mathbb{R}^3$ via $\mathrm{Ad}$, producing the double cover $\mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathrm{SO}(3)$; alternative perspective on $S^2$ as homogeneous space ([[Def - Lie Group]], [[Def - Adjoint Representation]], [[Def - Homogeneous Space]])
