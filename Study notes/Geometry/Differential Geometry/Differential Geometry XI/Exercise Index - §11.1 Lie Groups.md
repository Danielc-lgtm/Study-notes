## §11.1 Lie Groups — Exercises

This section drills the foundational notion of a [[Def - Lie Group|Lie group]] — a smooth manifold with smooth multiplication and inversion — and the principal examples. The exercises develop the recognition of when a familiar mathematical object (a matrix group, a sphere, a torus) carries a Lie group structure, and how the smooth-manifold structure of the underlying manifold relates to the algebraic structure of the group. The most reusable technique drilled is: identify a Lie group as a level set or open subset of a familiar manifold, verify smoothness of multiplication and inversion in coordinates, read off the dimension.

- [[Ex - The Lie Algebra of GL(n,R) is the Space of n by n Matrices]] (⭐) — verifies $\mathfrak{gl}(n, \mathbb{R}) = T_I \mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{R}) = M(n, \mathbb{R})$ via the open-submanifold structure of $\mathrm{GL}(n)$ ([[Def - Lie Group]], [[Def - The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]], [[Def - The Tangent Space]])
- [[Ex - SU(2) is Diffeomorphic to S^3]] (⭐⭐) — quaternion parametrization realizing $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ as $S^3$, identifying $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ as traceless skew-Hermitian matrices, previewing the double cover ([[Def - Lie Group]], [[Def - Diffeomorphism]], [[Def - The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]])
- [[Ex - The General Linear Group is a Smooth Manifold]] (⭐) — verification that $\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{R})$ is an open submanifold of $M(n, \mathbb{R})$, with smooth matrix multiplication and inversion ([[Def - Lie Group]], [[Def - Smooth Manifold]])
