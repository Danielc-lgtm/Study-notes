# Statement

> **Theorem (homotopy-class decomposition, 3-manifolds; Belyaev–Huseynli 7.1).** For a geometrically finite hyperbolic 3-manifold $X=\Gamma\backslash\mathbb{H}^3$, $\gamma\in\mathcal P_X$ with [[Def - Hyperbolic 3-Space, Kleinian Groups, and Complex Length|loxodromic]] representative $\tau$ (standard form) and $m\ge1$,
> $$\mu^E_X(C_X(\gamma^m))=\int_0^\infty\frac{dt}{t}\int_{\mathcal F_\tau}p^E_{\mathbb{H}^3}(t,w,\tau^m w)\,d\!\operatorname{vol}_{\mathbb{H}^3}(w),\qquad \mathcal F_\tau=\{(z,y):1\le y<e^{\ell_\gamma}\}.$$

**In one line.** The 3-manifold analogue of [[Thm - Mass of a Free Homotopy Class|Theorem 3.2]]: identical structure (isolate conjugacy class, unfold over cosets, reassemble onto the fundamental slab), now with the loxodromic standard form. Proof: as Theorem 3.2, verbatim.

**Full treatment:** [[Paper - Brownian Loops — Homotopy and Homology — §7 Hyperbolic 3-Manifolds|§7.1]].
