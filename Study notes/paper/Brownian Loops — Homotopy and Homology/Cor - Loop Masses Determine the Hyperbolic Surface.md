# Statement

> **Corollary (loop masses determine the surface; Belyaev–Huseynli 3.12).** Let $X$ be a closed hyperbolic surface, $g_1,g_2$ hyperbolic metrics on it, and $\kappa\ge-\frac14$ fixed. If
> $$\mu^\kappa_{X,g_1}(C_X(\gamma^m))=\mu^\kappa_{X,g_2}(C_X(\gamma^m))\quad\text{for every free homotopy class },$$
> then $(X,g_1)$ and $(X,g_2)$ are isometric by an isometry isotopic to the identity — the same point of Teichmüller space.

**In one line.** The killed loop masses are a complete invariant of a closed hyperbolic surface's geometry: knowing them for all classes pins down the metric up to isotopy. Proof: masses ⇒ [[Def - Marked Length Spectrum|marked length spectrum]] ([[Prop - Loop Masses Determine the Length Spectrum|Prop 3.11]]) ⇒ metric (Otal–Croke rigidity).
