# Statement

> **Corollary (Polyakov via loop measure; Belyaev–Huseynli 5.4).** For $g=e^{2\sigma}g_{\mathrm{hyp}}$ any smooth metric in the conformal class of a closed hyperbolic surface $X$,
> $$\log\det_\zeta\Delta_X=P_X(\sigma)+\operatorname{Area}(X)E+\log Z_X'(1),$$
> where $P_X(\sigma)=-\frac{1}{12\pi}\int_X|\nabla\sigma|^2\,dA_{\mathrm{hyp}}+\frac{1}{6\pi}\int_X\sigma\,dA_{\mathrm{hyp}}+\log\frac{\operatorname{vol}_g(X)}{4\pi(g-1)}$ is the Polyakov correction relative to the hyperbolic metric.

**In one line.** Combining [[Thm - Determinant as Renormalised Loop Mass|Theorem 5.1]] (which computes the determinant on the hyperbolic representative as a loop mass) with Polyakov's conformal anomaly formula extends the loop-measure expression of $\det_\zeta\Delta$ to *every* metric in the conformal class — spectral/geodesic content plus an explicit local conformal correction $P_X(\sigma)$.
