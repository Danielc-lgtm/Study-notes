# Definition

> **Definition (hyperbolic element; translation length).** For $\tau\in\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})\setminus\{1\}$:
> **(D1) Hyperbolic.** $\lvert\operatorname{tr}\tau\rvert>2$; equivalently $\tau$ has exactly two fixed points on $\partial\mathbb{H}^2$ and none in $\mathbb{H}^2$.
> **(D2) Translation length.** $\ \ell_\gamma:=\min_{z\in\mathbb{H}^2}d(z,\tau z)>0$, the minimum attained exactly on $\operatorname{axis}(\tau)$.
> **(D3) Trace relation.** $\ \lvert\operatorname{tr}\tau\rvert=2\cosh(\ell_\gamma/2)$.

> **Definition (primitive).** $\tau\in\Gamma$ hyperbolic is **primitive** if
> $$\forall\sigma\in\Gamma,\ \forall k\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq1}:\quad \tau=\sigma^k\ \Longrightarrow\ k=1 .$$
> Every hyperbolic element of a torsion-free Fuchsian group is $\tau^m$ for a **unique** primitive $\tau$ and unique $m\geq1$, up to orientation.

> **Correspondence with geodesics.** $\mathcal{P}_X$ is the set of primitive **oriented** closed geodesics on $X$. Each $\gamma\in\mathcal{P}_X$ corresponds to a primitive hyperbolic conjugacy class in $\Gamma$ with translation length $\ell_\gamma$: $\pi(\operatorname{axis}(\tau))=\gamma$, and $\operatorname{length}(\gamma)=\ell_\gamma$.

> **(F1) Displacement function.** $z\mapsto d(z,\tau z)$ is strictly convex in the distance to $\operatorname{axis}(\tau)$; hence (D2)'s minimum is attained exactly on the axis.
>
> **(F2) Where primitivity is used.** Twice, both structurally. **(a)** $C_\Gamma(\tau^m)=\langle\tau\rangle$ — generated by the *primitive* $\tau$, not by $\tau^m$ — which is [[Def - Centraliser and Coset Enumeration of a Conjugacy Class]] and produces the factor $1/m$ in every mass formula. **(b)** The Euler products of [[Def - Selberg Zeta Function]] and [[Def - Ruelle Zeta Function and its Twist]] run over primitives only, mirroring the Riemann zeta function's product over primes.

# Type card

> [!abstract] Type card — primitive hyperbolic element
> **Given.** **(H1)** $\Gamma$ torsion-free Fuchsian. **(H2)** $\tau\in\Gamma$ with $\lvert\operatorname{tr}\tau\rvert>2$.
>
> **Produces.** An axis $\operatorname{axis}(\tau)\subseteq\mathbb{H}^2$; a number $\ell_\gamma\in(0,\infty)$, invariant under conjugation; and, if $\tau$ is primitive, an element $\gamma\in\mathcal{P}_X$ with $\operatorname{length}(\gamma)=\ell_\gamma$.
>
> **Lets you.** Attach a single positive real number to each free homotopy class — via $(\gamma,m)\mapsto m\ell_\gamma$ — which is the only geometric input to every mass formula in the paper.

# Checks

**Instance.** $\tau:z\mapsto e^{\ell}z$, matrix $\begin{pmatrix}e^{\ell/2}&0\\0&e^{-\ell/2}\end{pmatrix}$. (D1): $\lvert\operatorname{tr}\rvert=2\cosh(\ell/2)>2$; fixed points $0,\infty$; axis the imaginary half-line. (D2): $d(iy,\tau(iy))=d(iy,ie^{\ell}y)=\ell$ on the axis, strictly greater off it by (F1). (D3): immediate. **Every hyperbolic element is $\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})$-conjugate to one of this form**, which licenses [[Constr - Standard-Form Representative and the Fundamental Strip|the standard-form normalisation]].

**Non-instance (fails D1).** The parabolic $z\mapsto z+1$: $\lvert\operatorname{tr}\rvert=2$, one fixed point ($\infty$), **no axis**, and $\inf_zd(z,z+1)=0$ **not attained** — the displacement $\to0$ as $\operatorname{Im}z\to\infty$. So no translation length exists, its class contains no closed geodesic, and every §3 formula involving $\ell_\gamma$ is meaningless. This is the group-theoretic face of *peripheral*.

**Non-instance (fails primitivity).** $\tau^2$ for $\tau$ primitive: hyperbolic, translation length $2\ell_\gamma$, same axis — but **not primitive**, and $C_\Gamma(\tau^2)=\langle\tau\rangle$, generated by $\tau$ and not by $\tau^2$. Getting this wrong over-counts $[\tau^2]_{\mathrm{conj}}$ by a factor $2$ and destroys the $1/m$ in the mass formula — see [[Def - Centraliser and Coset Enumeration of a Conjugacy Class]].

# Commentary

> [!note]- Commentary (skippable)
> The trichotomy of [[Def - Fuchsian Group and the Quotient Surface|(F2) there]] is visible in $\lvert\operatorname{tr}\rvert$, and torsion-freeness deletes the elliptic case. What remains is parabolic (no axis, no length — cusps) and hyperbolic (axis, length — closed geodesics and funnels). So the two surviving types map exactly onto the two kinds of end a quotient can have.
>
> The word *primitive* is worth more attention than it usually gets, because both its uses are load-bearing and neither is cosmetic. (F2)(a) is where the factor $1/m$ — the factor that later turns $\sum_m$ into a logarithm and hence produces the Selberg zeta function — comes from. (F2)(b) is the structural analogy with primes: the Euler product runs over primitives, and the iterates are supplied by the logarithmic expansion, exactly as $\log\zeta(s)=\sum_p\sum_m p^{-ms}/m$.
>
> Quoted rather than derived: that an elliptic element of a *discrete* subgroup of $\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})$ has finite order — the hinge that makes "torsion-free" equivalent to "no elliptics".
