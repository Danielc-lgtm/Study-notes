## §7.3 Photon Motion and the Velocity of Light — Exercises

The exercises of §7.3 drill photon kinematics and the experimental tightening of the constancy of light. Because a photon's null tangent cannot be normalised, one adapts a null vector by $\ell \cdot U_0 = 1$, and the null condition then forces the propagation direction $N$ to be a unit vector — so the speed of light is $c$ for *every* observer at a point of their worldline, a theorem rather than a postulate. The qualification "at a point of their worldline" is the seam where special relativity meets gravity: an accelerated observer measures a position-dependent coordinate speed for distant light. The three historical experiments form a programme: Arago (1810) detected the gross constancy, Michelson–Morley (1887) the directional independence (isotropy), and Kennedy–Thorndike (1932) the magnitude independence — together establishing the full constancy of the speed of light and forcing the complete Lorentz transformation.

- [[Ex - The velocity of light is c for every observer]] (⭐⭐) — prove the propagation direction is a unit vector from the null condition, confirm the proof needs only $U_0\cdot U_0 = 1$ and $\ell\cdot\ell = 0$ (so it holds for any observer), verify boost-invariance of the speed while the direction changes (aberration), and find the Rindler horizon where an accelerated observer's coordinate speed of light vanishes ([[Thm - Invariance of the Velocity of Light]], [[Def - Photon Propagation Direction and Velocity]], [[Def - Observer and Local Rest Space]]).

- [[Ex - Michelson-Morley null result]] (⭐⭐) — compute the aether-model fringe shift $\propto LV^2/c^3$ from the direction-dependent round-trip times, show relativity predicts zero shift because $\|\mathbf V_{\mathrm{light}}\| = c$ in every direction, identify that the experiment establishes the *isotropy* of $c$ (not its magnitude-independence), and explain why the FitzGerald–Lorentz length contraction is automatic in relativity rather than ad hoc ([[Thm - Invariance of the Velocity of Light]], [[Def - Photon Propagation Direction and Velocity]], [[Thm - Length Contraction]]).

- [[Ex - Kennedy-Thorndike and the speed independent of observer speed]] (⭐⭐) — explain why Michelson–Morley is blind to magnitude-dependence, show unequal arms give a phase $\propto (L_1-L_2)$ sensitive to the Earth's seasonal speed variation, derive the relativistic null prediction, and argue that the two experiments together (isotropy plus magnitude-independence) force the full Lorentz transformation including time dilation ([[Thm - Invariance of the Velocity of Light]], [[Def - Photon Propagation Direction and Velocity]], [[Thm - Time Dilation (General Observer)]], [[Thm - Length Contraction]]).

- [[Ex - Arago and the first relativistic null result]] (⭐) — show the corpuscular model predicts a $28''$ directional/seasonal variation in a prism's deflection of starlight, confront it with Arago's null measurement (equal within $5''$), explain it via the invariance of light, and recognise it as the first detection of the constancy of $c$ — 95 years before special relativity, unexplained for lack of the concept ([[Thm - Invariance of the Velocity of Light]], [[Def - Photon Propagation Direction and Velocity]]).
