## §24.1 The Perfect Fluid and Conservation Laws — Exercises

The exercises of §24.1 establish the three pillars on which all of relativistic hydrodynamics rests: the perfect-fluid energy–momentum tensor, the conservation of baryon number, and the thermodynamic equation of state. The first drills the meaning of $T^{\mu\nu} = (\rho+p)u^\mu u^\nu - p\,\eta^{\mu\nu}$ by evaluating it in the rest frame, where it reveals its content — energy density and isotropic pressure — and where the trace $\rho - 3p$ exposes radiation as the traceless case. The second turns the covariant conservation law $\nabla_\mu(nu^\mu) = 0$ into the ordinary continuity equation (with no relativistic correction, the relativistic content hiding in the density $N = \Gamma n$) and introduces the key identity $\nabla_\mu u^\mu = \dot V/V$ for the comoving expansion rate. The third derives the thermodynamic relations — the Euler relation $\rho + p = Ts + \sum\mu_a n_a$ that identifies $\rho + p$ as the proper enthalpy density, and the polytropic equation of state of degenerate matter. Together they supply the tensor, the conservation laws, and the closure that the dynamical chapter §24.2 will project and the exterior-calculus chapter §24.3 will reformulate.

- [[Ex - The energy-momentum tensor of a perfect fluid in the rest frame]] (⭐) — evaluate the perfect-fluid tensor in the rest frame to get $\mathrm{diag}(\rho,p,p,p)$, verify $\rho = T_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu$ and vanishing momentum density, and use the trace $\rho - 3p$ to identify radiation as the traceless fluid ([[Def - Perfect Fluid]], [[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]]).

- [[Ex - Baryon conservation as the relativistic continuity equation]] (⭐⭐) — decompose $\nabla_\mu(nu^\mu) = 0$ relative to an inertial observer into the classical-form continuity equation, establish the comoving expansion identity $\nabla_\mu u^\mu = \dot V/V$, and conclude the comoving baryon count $nV$ is conserved ([[Def - Baryon Four-Current and Its Conservation]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]]).

- [[Ex - The proper enthalpy density and the polytropic equation of state]] (⭐⭐) — derive the Euler and Gibbs–Duhem thermodynamic relations identifying $\rho + p$ as the proper enthalpy density, specialise to barotropic matter where the chemical potential equals the enthalpy per baryon, and compute the polytropic pressure $p = \kappa n^\gamma$ ([[Def - Equation of State and Speed of Sound]], [[Def - Perfect Fluid]]).
