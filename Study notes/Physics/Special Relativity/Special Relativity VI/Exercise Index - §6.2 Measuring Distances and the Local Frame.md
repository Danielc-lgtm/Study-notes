## §6.2 Measuring Distances and the Local Frame — Exercises

The exercises of §6.2 drill the chronometric measurement of distance and the construction of the observer's frame and its domain. Synge's formula expresses spatial length as the geometric mean of the two one-way light times — the Minkowskian power of a point with zero radius, because the photon events lie on the light cone of the target; the Born-rigidity exercise computes, for the uniformly accelerated Rindler congruence, that the proper distance between neighbouring observers is constant in time (so the congruence is Born-rigid), and contrasts the rotating disk where global simultaneity fails; and the frame-coordinates exercise builds the observer's coordinate grid from the slicing by rest spaces and locates the radius $a^{-1} = c^2/g$ beyond which the slices cross and the coordinates fold over. The unifying technique: time is primary, length and rigidity are read off from light round-trip times, and an accelerated frame is only local.

- [[Ex - Synge's formula and the power of a point]] (⭐⭐) — derive $\|\overrightarrow{AB}\| = c\sqrt{(t-t_1)(t_2-t)}$ from the radar scalar square, prove the power-of-a-point identity $\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|^2 = \overrightarrow{AA_1}\cdot\overrightarrow{AA_2}$, and show the Minkowskian version is the zero-radius limit because the photon legs are null ([[Def - Synge World Function and Spatial Distance]], [[Def - Einstein-Poincaré Simultaneity]], [[Thm - Euclidean Character of the Local Rest Space]], [[Def - The Null Cone and the Time Arrow]]).

- [[Ex - A Born-rigid congruence and the constant round-trip time]] (⭐⭐⭐) — for the Rindler congruence with graded acceleration $a(\xi) = 1/\xi$, verify constant proper acceleration, identify the common rest spaces as rays through the origin, compute the constant proper neighbour-distance $d\xi$, and confirm Born's criterion chronometrically while contrasting the non-integrable rotating disk ([[Def - Born Rigidity Criterion]], [[Def - Synge World Function and Spatial Distance]], [[Def - Observer and Local Rest Space]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]]).

- [[Ex - Frame coordinates and the slicing by rest spaces]] (⭐⭐) — show the constant-time surfaces of the observer's frame are the local rest spaces, that for an accelerated observer neighbouring rest spaces tilt and intersect at proper distance $a^{-1}$, and that the frame coordinates are single-valued only for $r \ll a^{-1} = c^2/g\approx 1$ light-year at $g = 10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ ([[Def - Local Frame and Four-Rotation]], [[Def - Observer and Local Rest Space]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]], [[Thm - Nonexistence of Absolute Time]]).
