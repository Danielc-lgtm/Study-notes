## §16.2 Physics in an Accelerated Frame — Exercises

The exercises of §16.2 put physics inside the accelerated frame and find that every familiar relativistic effect returns, reshaped by the position-dependent clock rate $\mathrm{d}\tau = (1+ax)\,\mathrm{d}t$. The first computes the spectral shift of light exchanged between comoving observers — a shift with no Doppler effect, governed entirely by the lapse ratio $1+ax_{\mathrm{em}}$ — and reinterprets it through Einstein's elevator as the gravitational redshift, the first crack in flat spacetime. The second works out clock desynchronization and Born rigidity: comoving clocks at different positions drift apart, a rigid rod's trailing end must accelerate harder than its leading end ($a' = a/(1+ax_0)$), no rigid body can reach the horizon, and Bell's spaceship paradox dissolves once "equal acceleration" is distinguished from "rigid". The third tracks a free (inertial) particle in the Rindler frame and shows it "falls" with a conserved energy $E + mgx$ — exactly a body in uniform gravity, the embryo of the geodesic principle. The unifying observation is the equivalence-principle dictionary: every factor $1+ax$ is $1 + \Phi/c^2$ with $\Phi = gx = c^2ax$, so the redshift is $\Delta\Phi/c^2$, the time dilation is $1+\Phi/c^2$, the potential energy is $mgx$, and the accelerated frame is the flat-spacetime rehearsal of gravitation that motivates [[Special Relativity XXV — Toward Relativistic Gravitation]].

- [[Ex - Redshift in an accelerated frame and the Einstein elevator]] (⭐⭐) — write the photon four-momentum $P = E_{\mathrm{em}}(e_0^*\pm e_1^*)$, find the reception time from the Rindler trajectory, compute $E_{\mathrm{rec}} = P\cdot U(t_{\mathrm{rec}}) = E_{\mathrm{em}}(1+ax_{\mathrm{em}})$ and the redshift $z = 1/(1+ax_{\mathrm{em}})-1$ (red below, blue above, $z\to\infty$ at the horizon), reinterpret in Einstein's elevator as the gravitational redshift $z\simeq gh/c^2$, and recover the proper acceleration from $z$ and the round-trip light time as $a = \mp\ln(1+z)/(cT)$ — the equivalence-principle bridge, with no Doppler effect because the observers are comoving ([[Thm - Spectral Shift in an Accelerated Frame]], [[Def - Rindler Coordinates and the Accelerated Frame]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]]).

- [[Ex - Clock desynchronization and Rindler rigidity]] (⭐⭐) — show a comoving observer at Rindler $x_0$ is uniformly accelerated with $a' = a/(1+ax_0)$ and proper time $t' = (1+ax_0)t$, deduce that fixed comoving clocks desynchronize (unlike inertial ones), prove a coplanar rigid rod has constant rest length $\ell_0 = |x_0|$ with the trailing end accelerating harder ($a'\to\infty$ at the horizon), and resolve Bell's spaceship paradox by distinguishing equal-acceleration (congruent hyperbolae, string snaps) from rigid motion (confocal hyperbolae, position-dependent $a'$) ([[Thm - Clock Synchronization and Desynchronization in an Accelerated Frame]], [[Def - Rindler Coordinates and the Accelerated Frame]], [[Thm - Worldline of a Uniformly Accelerated Observer]]).

- [[Ex - A free particle in the Rindler frame mimics uniform gravity]] (⭐⭐) — track a free (inertial) particle in the Rindler frame, derive its trajectory $x(t) = (b+a^{-1})/\cosh(act) - a^{-1}$, take the low-velocity limit to get the free-fall parabola $x\simeq b - \tfrac12 gt^2$, compute the non-conserved energy $E = mc^2(1+ab)/(1+ax(t))$, and prove $E' = E + mgx$ is a constant of motion — exactly the total energy of a body in uniform gravity, with the "fall" exposed as a frame artifact (the particle is on a geodesic; the observer accelerates) — the cleanest statement of the equivalence principle and the embryo of the geodesic principle ([[Def - Rindler Coordinates and the Accelerated Frame]], [[Thm - Worldline of a Uniformly Accelerated Observer]], [[Def - Uniformly Accelerated Observer (Hyperbolic Motion)]]).
