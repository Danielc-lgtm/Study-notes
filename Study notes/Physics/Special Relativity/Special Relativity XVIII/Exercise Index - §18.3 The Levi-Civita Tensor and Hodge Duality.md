## §18.3 The Levi-Civita Tensor and Hodge Duality — Exercises

The exercises of §18.3 build to the chapter's summit: the self-dual / anti-self-dual decomposition of the electromagnetic field. They begin with the [[Def - The Levi-Civita Tensor|Levi-Civita]] components and contraction identities ($\varepsilon_{0123} = +1$, $\varepsilon^{0123} = -1$, $\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} = -24$) — the algebraic engine of every Hodge computation, all signs traceable to $\mathrm{sgn}(\det g) = -1$. They then compute concrete [[Def - The Hodge Star|Hodge duals]] of $2$-forms (the quarter-turn $(\mathbf E, \mathbf B) \to (-\mathbf B, \mathbf E)$), establish the keystone $\star^2 = -1$ on $2$-forms and trace its minus sign to the Lorentzian signature, and finally complexify to obtain the $\pm i$ eigenspaces of $\star$ — the self-dual and anti-self-dual $2$-forms $\mathbf E \pm i\mathbf B$, which are the $(1,0)\oplus(0,1)$ representation of the Lorentz group and the two helicities of the photon. The unifying observation: $\star^2 = -1$ makes the Hodge star a complex structure, and the chirality of electromagnetism is the Lorentzian sign of the Hodge star.

- [[Ex - Components of the Levi-Civita tensor and the epsilon identities]] (⭐⭐) — establish $\varepsilon_{0123} = +1$ (signature-independent) and $\varepsilon^{0123} = -1$ (from $\det\eta = -1$ on raising four indices), prove $\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} = -24$ by counting signed permutations, and derive $\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\alpha}\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\beta} = -6\delta^\alpha{}_\beta$ with a consistency check ([[Def - The Levi-Civita Tensor]], [[Def - Metric Duality and Index Manipulation]]).
- [[Ex - Computing the Hodge dual of a 2-form]] (⭐⭐) — compute $\star(e^0\wedge e^1) = -e^2\wedge e^3$ and $\star(e^2\wedge e^3) = +e^0\wedge e^1$ with full sign-tracking, tabulate $\star$ on all six basis $2$-forms (electric-type $\to$ minus magnetic-type, and conversely), and show $\star$ acts on the field strength as the quarter-turn $(\mathbf E, \mathbf B) \to (-\mathbf B, \mathbf E)$ generating a $U(1)$ duality ([[Def - The Hodge Star]], [[Def - The Levi-Civita Tensor]], [[Def - Metric Duality and Index Manipulation]]).
- [[Ex - The Hodge star squares to minus one on 2-forms]] (⭐⭐) — specialise $\star\star = (-1)^{p+1}$ to $\star^2 = -1$ on $2$-forms, verify directly that $\star\star(e^0\wedge e^1) = -e^0\wedge e^1$, trace the sign to $\mathrm{sgn}(\det g) = -1$ (the combinatorial factor being trivial at the middle degree), and contrast with the Euclidean $\star^2 = +1$ and its real instanton decomposition ([[Def - The Hodge Star]], [[Thm - Hodge Star and the Exterior Product]], [[Def - The Levi-Civita Tensor]]).
- [[Ex - Self-dual and anti-self-dual decomposition of the field strength]] (⭐⭐⭐) — complexify $\mathscr{A}_2(E)$, build the eigenprojectors $P^\pm = \tfrac12(1 \mp i\star)$ onto the $\pm i$ eigenspaces, identify the self-dual and anti-self-dual parts with the Riemann-Silberstein combinations $\mathbf E \pm i\mathbf B$, and match the decomposition to the $(1,0)\oplus(0,1)$ representation of the Lorentz algebra via $\mathbf J \pm i\mathbf K$ — the chirality of the field strength ([[Thm - Orthogonal Decomposition of 2-Forms]], [[Def - The Hodge Star]], [[Thm - The Complexification of so(1,3) and the (A,B) Decomposition]], [[Def - Lie Algebra of the Lorentz Group]]).
