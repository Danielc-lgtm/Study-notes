## §23.2 Energy-Momentum of the Electromagnetic Field — Exercises

The exercises of §23.2 cover the *construction*, *interpretation*, and *structure* of the electromagnetic energy-momentum tensor. The first exercise carries out the chapter's central derivation: routing the Lorentz four-force density through the two Maxwell equations to *force* $T^{\text{em}}_{\alpha\beta} = \varepsilon_0(F_{\mu\alpha}F^\mu{}_\beta - \tfrac14\eta_{\alpha\beta}F^2)$ as the unique tensor whose divergence equals $-\mathcal F$, and seeing the $\tfrac14$ coefficient *doubly* determined by cancellation and tracelessness. The second computes the measured energetics — energy density, Poynting vector, Maxwell stress — for three illustrative fields (static Coulomb, plane wave, Feynman-disk crossed configuration), exposing both the field-line tension/pressure picture of stress and the static-Poynting subtlety that nonzero $\mathbf S$ need not mean radiation. The third proves tracelessness of $T^{\text{em}}$ directly, traces its dimension dependence to the special-to-four-dimensions conformal invariance of Maxwell theory, and uses it together with the perfect-fluid form to derive the **equation of state of radiation** $p = \rho/3$ that governs the radiation-dominated early universe. Together: one derivation that *constructs* the tensor, one calculation that *reads* it, one structural property that *constrains* it.

- [[Ex - Constructing the EM energy-momentum tensor from conservation]] — start from the Lorentz four-force density $\mathcal F_\alpha = F_{\alpha\mu}J^\mu$, eliminate $J$ via the inhomogeneous Maxwell equation, split the resulting $F\nabla F$ product into a divergence plus a leftover (Leibniz), absorb the leftover via the cyclic Bianchi identity $\mathrm dF = 0$ (which gives the key identity $F^{\mu\beta}\nabla_\beta F_{\mu\alpha} = -\tfrac14\nabla_\alpha F^2$), and read off $T^{\text{em}}_{\alpha\beta} = \varepsilon_0(F_{\mu\alpha}F^\mu{}_\beta - \tfrac14\eta_{\alpha\beta}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})$; verify symmetry, tracelessness, and the energy-density recovery, then identify the $\tfrac14$ coefficient as doubly forced (by cancellation and by $\eta^{\alpha\beta}\eta_{\alpha\beta} = 4$) (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Def - Energy-Momentum Tensor of the Electromagnetic Field]], [[Thm - Maxwell Equations]], [[Thm - Energy-Momentum Conservation]], [[Def - The Electromagnetic Field Tensor]], [[Def - The Lorentz Four-Force]]).

- [[Ex - The EM stress tensor in a Coulomb field and the Poynting vector]] — compute the energy density, Poynting vector, and Maxwell stress for three fields: (i) a static Coulomb field, recovering the field-line tension/pressure structure of the stress and the electrostatic self-energy $W = q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 a)$ outside cut-off radius $a$; (ii) a plane wave, finding $\rho_{\text{em}} = \varepsilon_0 E^2$, $\mathbf S = \rho_{\text{em}}c\hat{\mathbf n}$, and the field invariant $F^2 = 0$ (null radiative field); (iii) crossed static charge-and-magnet ("Feynman disk"), where $\mathbf S \ne 0$ but $\vec\nabla\cdot\mathbf S = 0$ identically, so the configuration stores angular momentum without radiating — exposing the topic page's illegal operation 5 ("nonzero $\mathbf S \Rightarrow$ radiation" is false; radiation requires *time-dependent* $1/r$ field) (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Energy-Momentum Tensor of the Electromagnetic Field]], [[Def - The Electromagnetic Field Tensor]], [[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]]).

- [[Ex - Tracelessness of the EM tensor and the radiation equation of state]] — compute $T^\mu{}_\mu$ directly using $\eta^{\alpha\beta}\eta_{\alpha\beta} = 4$ to verify tracelessness, generalise to dimension $d$ (the trace-term coefficient $1/d$ is the special-to-$d=4$ statement of conformal invariance of Maxwell theory), then combine tracelessness with the [[Def - Perfect Fluid|perfect-fluid]] form $T^{\mu\nu} = (\rho+p)U^\mu U^\nu - p\eta^{\mu\nu}$ for a thermal photon bath and force the **equation of state $p = \rho/3$** that governs radiation in cosmology; preview the quantum **trace anomaly** in curved or bounded geometries (Casimir effect) (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Energy-Momentum Tensor of the Electromagnetic Field]], [[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Def - Perfect Fluid]]).
