## §23.3 Radiation by Accelerated Charges — Exercises

The exercises of §23.3 cash out the chapter's most spectacular application of the energy-momentum tensor: the radiation of an accelerated charge. The unifying technique is to identify the *radiative* ($1/r$) part of the electromagnetic field, compute its Poynting flux, and integrate over a sphere at infinity — the universal recipe by which the [[Thm - Radiation by an Accelerated Charge (Larmor Formula)|Larmor formula]] emerges, together with its [[Thm - Angular Distribution of Radiation|angular distribution]] and the corresponding [[Def - Synchrotron Radiation|synchrotron]] specialisation to circular motion. The first exercise drills the canonical derivation: rest-frame radiative field, dipole-donut $\sin^2\theta$ angular pattern, the universal $\int\sin^3\theta\,\mathrm d\Omega = 8\pi/3$ angular integral, and the lift to the Lorentz-invariant form $\mathcal P = q^2|A|^2/(6\pi\varepsilon_0 c)$ that exposes the Liénard $\Gamma^6$ as kinematic bookkeeping rather than new physics. The second specialises Liénard to a magnetic helix, obtains the **synchrotron power** and the famous **$\Gamma^4 \propto m^{-4}$ scaling** of energy loss per turn, and applies the verdict to LEP vs LHC — explaining why future high-energy electron–positron colliders must be linear. The third analyses the angular distribution, derives the universal **$1/\Gamma$ beaming cone** from the Doppler factor's asymptotics, finds the collinear lobes at $\pm 1/(\sqrt5\Gamma)$, and quantifies the astrophysical Doppler boosting that makes intrinsically two-sided AGN jets appear one-sided. The reusable thread of the section is that *only acceleration radiates, and the power is an invariant*: the $\Gamma$-factors that intimidate are the cost of expressing the frame-independent $|A|^2$ in laboratory three-vectors, and disciplined problem-solving computes the invariant first and only then specialises to a frame.

- [[Ex - The Larmor formula from the radiative field flux]] — identify the radiative ($1/r$) part of the Liénard-Wiechert field in the instantaneous rest frame as $\mathbf E_{\text{rad}} = q\hat{\mathbf n}\times(\hat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf a)/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 c^2 r)$ with magnitude $q a\sin\theta/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 c^2 r)$ (transverse to $\hat{\mathbf n}$, dipole-donut pattern), compute the Poynting flux $\mathrm d\mathcal P/\mathrm d\Omega = q^2 a^2\sin^2\theta/(16\pi^2\varepsilon_0 c^3)$, evaluate the universal angular integral $\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = 4/3$ (so $\int\sin^3\theta\,\mathrm d\Omega = 8\pi/3$) to recover the Larmor formula $\mathcal P = q^2 a^2/(6\pi\varepsilon_0 c^3)$, and lift to the Lorentz-invariant form $\mathcal P = q^2|A|^2/(6\pi\varepsilon_0 c)$ with $|A|^2 = -A\cdot A$, showing the Liénard $\Gamma^6$ is just the invariant expressed in laboratory three-velocity and three-acceleration (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Radiation by an Accelerated Charge (Larmor Formula)]], [[Def - Energy-Momentum Tensor of the Electromagnetic Field]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]]).

- [[Ex - Synchrotron power and energy loss per turn]] — solve the Lorentz-force equation in a uniform magnetic field to get the helical trajectory with orbit radius $R = \Gamma m V\sin\alpha/(|q|B)$, relativistic cyclotron frequency $\omega_B/\Gamma$, and centripetal acceleration $|\boldsymbol\gamma| = \omega_B V\sin\alpha/\Gamma$; specialise the Liénard formula to $\boldsymbol\gamma\perp\mathbf V$ to derive the **synchrotron power** $\mathcal P = q^4 B^2\Gamma^2 V^2\sin^2\alpha/(6\pi\varepsilon_0 c^3 m^2)$; multiply by the orbital period to get **energy lost per turn** $\Delta E = (q^2/3\varepsilon_0 R)(V/c)^3(E/mc^2)^4$, exhibiting $\Gamma^4 \propto m^{-4}$; apply numerically to LEP electrons ($\Delta E \approx 2.4$ GeV per turn, crippling) vs LHC protons ($\Delta E \approx 4.3$ keV per turn, negligible), and conclude $(m_p/m_e)^4 \approx 1.13\times 10^{13}$ is the verdict that forces future high-energy electron–positron colliders to be linear (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Def - Synchrotron Radiation]], [[Thm - Radiation by an Accelerated Charge (Larmor Formula)]], [[Def - The Lorentz Four-Force]]).

- [[Ex - Relativistic beaming and the one-over-Gamma cone]] — expand the Doppler factor for $\Gamma\gg 1$, $\theta\ll 1$, to get $1 - V\cos\theta/c \approx (1 + \Gamma^2\theta^2)/(2\Gamma^2)$, defining the universal **beaming cone of half-angle $1/\Gamma$**; for the collinear case $\boldsymbol\gamma\parallel\mathbf V$ maximise $u^2/(1+u^2)^6$ (with $u = \Gamma\theta$) to find off-axis lobes at $\theta_\pm = \pm 1/(\sqrt5\Gamma)$ with on-axis null forced by the dipole donut; for the orthogonal (synchrotron) case identify the forward-pointing cone sweeping with the velocity like a lighthouse, explaining the broad synchrotron spectrum via short pulses of duration $\sim 1/(\Gamma^3\Omega)$ with Fourier content up to $f_c\sim\Gamma^3 c/R$; for **astrophysical Doppler boosting**, compute the intensity ratio $(2\Gamma)^8 \sim 256\Gamma^8$ between approaching and receding jets (about $10^{10}$ at $\Gamma = 10$), the reason intrinsically two-sided AGN jets appear one-sided in our images (⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Angular Distribution of Radiation]], [[Thm - Radiation by an Accelerated Charge (Larmor Formula)]], [[Def - Synchrotron Radiation]]).

- [[Ex - Angular distribution of synchrotron radiation and the headlight effect]] — derive the laboratory differential-power formulas for parallel and perpendicular acceleration via the master Jacobian and the inverse-aberration substitution, obtaining $\mathrm d\mathcal P/\mathrm d\Omega \propto \sin^2\theta/(1-\beta\cos\theta)^5$ for the linac (peak at $\theta_{\max} \approx 1/(2\Gamma)$, off-axis because the rest-frame dipole vanishes along $\hat{\mathbf v}$) and the longer azimuth-dependent formula for the synchrotron (peak at $\theta_{\max} = 0$, exactly forward); the contrast between parallel and perpendicular acceleration is the diagnostic by which an observer can infer whether the source is linearly or circularly accelerated without knowing $\Gamma$ (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Angular Distribution of Radiation]], [[Thm - Radiation by an Accelerated Charge (Larmor Formula)]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]]).
