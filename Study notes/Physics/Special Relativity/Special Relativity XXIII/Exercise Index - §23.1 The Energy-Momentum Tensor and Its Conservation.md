## §23.1 The Energy-Momentum Tensor and Its Conservation — Exercises

The exercises of §23.1 drill the chapter's basic literacy: how to *write down* the energy-momentum tensor of a continuous medium, how to *read off* the measured energetics by contraction, and how to *project* the divergence-free law into the recognisable scalar/vector continuity equations. The first exercise builds the dust tensor and watches its conservation split into rest-energy conservation plus the geodesic equation — the prototype of the chapter's "find $T$, take its divergence" workflow. The second proves a structural property of $T$ — its symmetry — from angular-momentum conservation, illustrating that a *symmetry* of a tensor can be *forced* by a conservation law via a derivative-of-the-lever-arm Kronecker-delta mechanism, and verifying there is no circularity. The third performs the central projection technique on the tensor conservation law, splitting $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$ into energy continuity and momentum continuity, and recovering **Poynting's theorem** as the electromagnetic special case. Together these three set the chapter's stylistic baseline: every problem reduces to "what is $T^{\mu\nu}$, and what does its divergence say?"

- [[Ex - The energy-momentum tensor of dust]] — build the rank-one tensor $T^{\mu\nu} = \varepsilon_0 U^\mu U^\nu$ for pressureless dust, contract with an observer to get the boosted energy density $\gamma^2\varepsilon_0$ (one $\gamma$ for energy per particle, one for Lorentz-contracted volume), confirm there is no rest-frame pressure, and split the conservation law $\nabla_\mu(\varepsilon_0 U^\mu U^\nu) = 0$ by parallel/orthogonal projection into the continuity equation $\nabla_\mu(\varepsilon_0 U^\mu) = 0$ and the geodesic equation $U^\mu\nabla_\mu U^\nu = 0$ (⭐⭐) ([[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]], [[Thm - Energy-Momentum Conservation]]).

- [[Ex - The symmetry of the energy-momentum tensor from angular momentum conservation]] — define the angular-momentum density $J^{\mu\nu\rho} = (x-x_C)^\mu T^{\nu\rho} - (x-x_C)^\nu T^{\mu\rho}$, take its divergence (the lever-arm derivative $\nabla_\rho(x-x_C)^\mu = \delta^\mu_\rho$ produces the Kronecker delta that collapses $J$ down to $T^{\nu\mu} - T^{\mu\nu}$, with the leftover lever-arm terms killed by energy-momentum conservation), conclude angular-momentum conservation $\Leftrightarrow$ symmetry of $T$, and check there is no circularity since $\nabla\cdot T = 0$ was proved without using symmetry (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Thm - Energy-Momentum Conservation]], [[Def - Angular Momentum Four-Tensor]]).

- [[Ex - Energy and momentum conservation from the divergence]] — substitute the observer decomposition $T = \varepsilon U_0^\flat\otimes U_0^\flat + c(\varpi\otimes U_0^\flat + U_0^\flat\otimes\varpi) + S$ into $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$, use $\nabla U_0 = 0$ for an inertial observer, and project the resulting $1$-form equation along $U_0$ (giving energy continuity $\partial_t\varepsilon + c^2\vec\nabla\cdot\varpi = 0$) and orthogonally with $\perp_{U_0}$ (giving momentum continuity $\partial_t\varpi + \vec\nabla\cdot S = 0$); apply to the electromagnetic field with $\mathcal F = F(\,\cdot\,, J)$ and recover **Poynting's theorem** $\partial_t\rho_{\text{em}} + \vec\nabla\cdot\mathbf S = -\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E$ (⭐⭐) ([[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Thm - Energy-Momentum Conservation]], [[Def - Observer and Local Rest Space]]).
