## §15.3 Hamiltonian Formulation — Exercises

The exercises of §15.3 drill the passage from the Lagrangian to the Hamiltonian picture for a relativistic particle, where a characteristic obstruction — the reparametrisation gauge symmetry — makes the naive construction fail. The first exercise takes the non-covariant route: parametrise by an observer's time so the Lagrangian is non-degenerate, perform an honest Legendre transform, and obtain the physical Hamiltonian $H = \sqrt{\mathbf{p}^2 + m^2}$, including the charged-particle generalisation by minimal substitution. The second confronts the covariant route head-on: the naive Hamiltonian vanishes identically, the Legendre map is non-invertible, and a primary constraint $P\cdot P = m^2$ appears — all from the degree-one homogeneity of the Lagrangian — with Dirac's constraint Hamiltonian as the resolution and the vanishing Hamiltonian interpreted as the absence of absolute time. The third applies the energy Hamiltonian to a concrete field (a uniform magnetic field), solving the helical motion at the relativistic cyclotron frequency and using Poisson brackets to verify conservation laws and reveal the non-commuting kinetic-momentum algebra. The unifying theme: the relativistic Hamiltonian is the energy-momentum relation, the degeneracy of the covariant Lagrangian is the reparametrisation gauge symmetry, and the symplectic structure (Poisson brackets, phase space) connects this chapter to geometric mechanics and to quantisation.

- [[Ex - Legendre transform to the relativistic Hamiltonian]] (⭐⭐) — Legendre-transform the inertial-observer Lagrangian $-m\sqrt{1 - \mathbf{v}^2}$, invert $\mathbf{p} = m\gamma\mathbf{v}$ using the identity $\gamma^2\mathbf{v}^2 = \gamma^2 - 1$, obtain $H = \sqrt{\mathbf{p}^2 + m^2}$, verify Hamilton's equations and that $H = E$, and add potentials via minimal substitution to recover the Newtonian limit ([[Def - Generalized Four-Momentum and the Relativistic Hamiltonian]], [[Def - Relativistic Action of a Free Particle]], [[Def - The Legendre Transform]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]]).

- [[Ex - The Dirac Hamiltonian and the primary constraint]] (⭐⭐⭐) — show the naive Hamiltonian vanishes by Euler's identity, prove the Legendre map is non-invertible by exhibiting $\dot x^\mu$ in the kernel of the Hessian, derive the primary constraint $P\cdot P = m^2$, verify Dirac's constraint Hamiltonian reproduces free motion and fixes $\lambda = \tau$, and interpret $H \equiv 0$ as the reparametrisation gauge symmetry (the absence of absolute time) ([[Thm - Hamiltonian Formulation (Relativistic Particle)]], [[Def - Generalized Four-Momentum and the Relativistic Hamiltonian]], [[Def - Relativistic Action of a Free Particle]], [[Def - The Legendre Transform]]).

- [[Ex - Relativistic motion in a uniform magnetic field via the Hamiltonian]] (⭐⭐) — use $H = \sqrt{(\mathbf{p} - q\mathbf{A})^2 + m^2}$ to show the magnetic field does no work (so $\gamma$ is constant), solve the helical motion at the relativistic cyclotron frequency $\omega = qB/(m\gamma)$, and compute the Poisson brackets $\{p_z, H\} = 0$ and $\{\Pi_x, \Pi_y\} = qB$ (the seed of Landau levels) ([[Def - Generalized Four-Momentum and the Relativistic Hamiltonian]], [[Def - Lagrangian for a Particle in a Vector Field]], [[Thm - Hamiltonian Formulation (Relativistic Particle)]], [[Def - Poisson Bracket]]).
