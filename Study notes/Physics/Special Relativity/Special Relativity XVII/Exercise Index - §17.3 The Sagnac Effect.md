## §17.3 The Sagnac Effect — Exercises

The exercises of §17.3 drill the Sagnac effect — the time difference between counter-propagating signals around a rotating loop — from its derivation through its observable phase to its technological applications. The unifying technique is to recognize the Sagnac delay as twice the clock-desynchronization gap, $\Delta t' = 2\Delta t'_{\text{desync}} = 4\omega A/c^2$, and hence as the enclosed vorticity flux: a prograde signal accumulates the gap with one sign, a retrograde signal with the other. The circular-loop exercise derives the delay directly from the helical signal worldlines and the velocity-composition law, with the signal speed cancelling to expose the effect's metric (not propagation) origin; the interferometer exercise converts the delay to the observable phase $\propto\omega A/(c\lambda)$, derives the $\sim 10^8$ matter-wave enhancement, and shows that matter waves discriminate relativity from the aether; and the Hafele–Keating exercise measures the desynchronization with flown atomic clocks, isolating the rotation effect through the east–west asymmetry and motivating the inertial-reference definition of International Atomic Time. Throughout, the $c^2$ is the metric factor, never the propagation speed — the discipline that the speed-independence of the Sagnac delay enforces.

- [[Ex - The Sagnac delay around a circular loop]] (⭐⭐) — write the two signal worldlines as helices with inertial angular velocities $\Omega_\pm$, solve the meeting conditions $\Omega_\pm t = \omega t \pm 2\pi$ for the return times, apply the relativistic velocity-composition law, and watch the signal speed $v$ cancel to give $\Delta t' = 4\pi\Gamma r^2\omega/c^2 = 2\Delta t'_{\text{desync}}$ ([[Thm - The Sagnac Effect]], [[Thm - Relativistic Velocity Addition]], [[Def - Uniformly Rotating Observer]]).

- [[Ex - The matter-wave Sagnac interferometer and gyrometers]] (⭐⭐⭐) — derive the optical phase $(8\pi/c\lambda)\vec\omega\cdot\vec{\mathcal{A}}$ (Sagnac's $0.21$ rad) and the matter-wave phase $4\Gamma_p(m/\hbar)\vec\omega\cdot\vec{\mathcal{A}}$ (enhanced by $\sim 10^8$), explain why matter waves confirm relativity over the aether, and describe the fibre-optic and ring-laser gyrometers ([[Thm - Sagnac Delay and the Optical Sagnac Interferometer]], [[Thm - The Sagnac Effect]]).

- [[Ex - The Hafele-Keating experiment and International Atomic Time]] (⭐⭐⭐) — split the around-the-world clock difference into a symmetric time-dilation term $\pi rv/c^2$ and an antisymmetric desynchronization $\pm 2\pi r^2\omega/c^2$, evaluate the east–west asymmetry for the Earth, identify the sign-reversing rotation term as the novel content, and explain how International Atomic Time bypasses the impossibility of global synchronization by referring to a central inertial time ([[Thm - Impossibility of Global Clock Synchronization on a Rotating Disk]], [[Def - Uniformly Rotating Observer]]).
