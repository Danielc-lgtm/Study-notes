## §2.3 The Photon and Relativistic Dynamics — Exercises

- [[Ex - Pair production and the photon-photon threshold]] — prove a single photon cannot pair-produce in vacuum (its invariant mass is zero, a pair's is positive, and invariant mass is conserved), then derive the threshold $E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)\ge 2m_e^2c^4$ for two colliding photons, the mechanism by which energetic gamma rays are absorbed by background light ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]]).

- [[Ex - The relativistic rocket]] — derive the photon-rocket equation $M_0/M = e^\phi$ by conserving four-momentum for the closed rocket-plus-exhaust system and squaring away the null exhaust four-vector, showing the result is the Tsiolkovsky equation with velocity promoted to rapidity and exhaust speed equal to $c$ ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Def - Rapidity]]).

- [[Ex - Photon emission and recoil]] — compute the exact energy of a photon emitted by a recoiling atom by isolating and squaring the recoil atom's four-momentum, showing the photon falls short of the level gap by the recoil kinetic energy $(\Delta E)^2/2Mc^2$, the shift the Mössbauer effect eliminates by binding the atom in a crystal ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]]).
