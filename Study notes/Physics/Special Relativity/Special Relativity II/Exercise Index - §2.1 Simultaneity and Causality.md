## §2.1 Simultaneity and Causality — Exercises

The exercises of §2.1 drill the foundational fact of the whole chapter — that "now" is a frame-dependent slicing of spacetime — and its bounded consequence for cause and effect. The first builds the relativity of simultaneity from Einstein's train, separating the *constancy* of light's speed (which produces the effect) from its *finiteness* (a mere signal delay that does not). The second establishes the light cone and proves that the order of timelike-separated events is absolute while the order of spacelike-separated events is negotiable, welding faster-than-light travel to backward-in-time signalling. The third is a qualifying-exam problem on two simultaneous explosions, where a single invariant — the sign of the interval — controls the order, the uniqueness of the simultaneous frame, and the impossibility of a causal link. The unifying technique across all three: recast every "at the same time" as a pair of events, transform them, and read the disagreement off the tilted simultaneity line.

- [[Ex - The relativity of simultaneity (Einstein's train)]] (⭐⭐) — derive that a bulb at the middle of a moving carriage reaches both walls together in the carriage frame but the back wall first on the platform, via both a direct "chase" argument and a Lorentz transformation, and extract the general rule $\Delta t = \gamma v\,\Delta x'$ for events simultaneous in one frame; argue the effect is the constancy, not finiteness, of $c$ ([[Def - The Relativity of Simultaneity]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Causal structure and the light cone]] (⭐⭐) — describe the light cone and its future/past/elsewhere regions, prove they are Lorentz-invariant via the invariant interval, show timelike order is frame-independent while spacelike order reverses (threshold $v = \Delta t/\Delta x$), and conclude that faster-than-light influence equals backward-in-time signalling, hence no rigid bodies ([[Def - Causality and the Light Cone]], [[Def - The Spacetime Interval]], [[Thm - Invariance of the Spacetime Interval]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Order of two events and the simultaneity qualifying problem]] (⭐⭐⭐) — for two firecrackers simultaneous in $S$ at separation $L$, compute the order ($\Delta t' = -\gamma vL$) and stretched separation ($\gamma L$) in a boosted frame, distinguish event-separation from rod-length to dodge the contraction trap, prove $S$ is the unique simultaneous frame, and find the minimum delay $t_B = L$ for a possible causal link from the spacelike interval $\Delta s^2 = -L^2$ ([[Def - The Relativity of Simultaneity]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Causality and the Light Cone]], [[Def - The Spacetime Interval]], [[Thm - Length Contraction]]).
