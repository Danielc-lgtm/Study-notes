## §2.2 Four-Momentum and Relativistic Dynamics — Exercises

- [[Ex - Threshold energy for particle production]] — find the minimum collision energy to create a new particle by evaluating the invariant $(\sum P)^2$ in two frames, using that at threshold the products are at rest in the centre-of-momentum frame; the lab-frame threshold scales as $M^2/m$ while the colliding-beam threshold scales only as $M$ ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]]).

- [[Ex - Compton scattering]] — derive the frequency shift of a photon scattering off a stationary electron by isolating the recoil electron's four-momentum and squaring it into the invariant $m_e^2c^2$, with the photons' nullity deleting two further terms; the scattered photon is always redshifted ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]]).

- [[Ex - Two-body decay kinematics]] — show a particle decays only if heavier than its products, then fix the products' energies, Lorentz factors, and common momentum entirely from the three rest masses by isolating one product and squaring; a two-body decay is monoenergetic ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]]).

- [[Ex - Mandelstam variables for two-body scattering]] — define the Mandelstam invariants $s,t,u$ as Minkowski squares of four-momentum sums and differences for $1+2\to3+4$, prove they are Lorentz invariant and obey the sum rule $s+t+u=\sum m_i^2$ by expanding the squares and collapsing the cross terms with conservation, identify $\sqrt s$ as the centre-of-mass energy, and compute $s$ for a collider versus a fixed target ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]]).

- [[Ex - Whether a particle reaction is kinematically allowed]] — use conservation of four-momentum as a filter on invariant mass: a free photon cannot decay to $e^+e^-$ because a null four-vector cannot equal the timelike total of a massive pair, two photons pair-produce only above $E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)\ge2m_e^2$, and the fixed-target antiproton-production threshold is a beam kinetic energy of $6m_p$ ([[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]]).
