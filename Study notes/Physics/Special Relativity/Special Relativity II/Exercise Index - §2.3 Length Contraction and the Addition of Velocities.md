## §2.3 Length Contraction and the Addition of Velocities — Exercises

The exercises of §2.3 drill the two effects that finish the chapter: a moving rod is short by $\gamma$, and collinear velocities combine by the relativistic law with $c$ as both fixed point and ceiling. The first is the ladder-in-the-barn paradox, the chapter's hardest and most famous problem, where contraction alone yields contradictory verdicts and only the relativity of simultaneity — the two door-closings being simultaneous in the barn frame but not the ladder frame — resolves them. The second derives the velocity-addition law twice (by dividing Lorentz-transformed coordinates and by adding rapidities), proves the fixed-point and ceiling properties through the factorisation $c - u = (c-u')(c-v)c/(c^2 + u'v)$, and shows $N$ boosts approach $c$ without reaching it. The third is a qualifying-exam closing-speed problem distinguishing the unbounded Earth-frame coordinate rate from the bounded relative speed, and inverting the relativistic law to find the constituent speeds. The unifying technique: treat every length and every "fit" as a statement about simultaneously-located endpoints, and route every velocity composition through rapidity.

- [[Ex - Length contraction and the ladder-in-the-barn paradox]] (⭐⭐⭐) — show a ladder of proper length $2L$ contracts to $2L/\gamma$ and fits a barn of length $L$ (for $\gamma\ge2$) in the barn frame while the barn contracts to $L/\gamma$ and the ladder never fits in the ladder frame, then resolve by transforming the two door-closing events and showing the far door shuts $\gamma vL$ before the near door in the ladder frame ([[Thm - Length Contraction]], [[Def - The Relativity of Simultaneity]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Addition of velocities and the speed-of-light ceiling]] (⭐⭐) — derive $u = (u'+v)/(1+u'v)$ by dividing transformed coordinates, verify $u = c$ when $u' = c$, prove the ceiling via $c - u = (c-u')(c-v)c/(c^2+u'v) > 0$, and redo through rapidity to show the law is $\tanh(\varphi_{u'}+\varphi_v)$ and that $N$ boosts give $c\tanh(N\varphi_w) \to c$ without equality ([[Thm - Relativistic Velocity Addition]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Rapidity]]).

- [[Ex - Two spaceships and the relativistic closing speed]] (⭐⭐) — distinguish the Earth-frame closing rate $2u$ (a coordinate gap, allowed up to $2c$) from the relative speed $2u/(1+u^2/c^2)$ (an object's speed, always $< c$), then invert $0.70c = 2u/(1+u^2/c^2)$ to find the Earth-frame speed $u \approx 0.41c$, contrasting with the naive Galilean $0.35c$, and confirm via halving the relative rapidity ([[Thm - Relativistic Velocity Addition]], [[Def - Rapidity]]).
