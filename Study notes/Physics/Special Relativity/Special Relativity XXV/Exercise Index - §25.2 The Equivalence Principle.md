## §25.2 The Equivalence Principle — Exercises

The exercises of §25.2 work the most consequential single principle of the chapter — that a uniform gravitational field is locally indistinguishable from a uniformly accelerated frame — and trace its scope and its boundary. The Einstein elevator thought-experiment makes the principle's content operational: in a windowless cabin, no local measurement (test-particle free fall, accelerometer reading, light-beam trajectory, clock-rate comparison) can distinguish a real gravitational field from uniform acceleration in empty space. Universality of free fall, the experimental input that the equivalence principle quantifies, is then traced through the Nordström action to show how setting the gravitational charge equal to the inertial mass automates the equivalence-principle behaviour at the equation-of-motion level. Tidal forces locate the *boundary* of the equivalence principle: in any region where the gravitational field is non-uniform, two test particles separated by $\vec\xi$ have a relative acceleration $-E\cdot\vec\xi$ that no choice of accelerated frame can mimic, and the residual is the Newtonian tidal tensor — which in general relativity is encoded in the Riemann curvature. Together, the three exercises establish that the equivalence principle is exact at a point and to first order, that universality of free fall is what makes gravity *geometrisable*, and that the irreducible content of gravity (the part no acceleration can transform away) is curvature. The arc points directly at general relativity: a metric field $g_{\mu\nu}(x)$ in which geodesics are mass-independent (universality), the metric is locally Minkowski (equivalence principle), and the obstruction to global flatness is curvature (tidal forces).

- [[Ex - The Einstein elevator thought-experiment]] — establish operationally that no *local* experiment in a windowless cabin can distinguish a uniform gravitational field from uniform acceleration in empty space; predict from the equivalence principle (without any field equation) that test particles fall identically regardless of composition, accelerometers read the same in both situations, horizontal light beams trace parabolic arcs of curvature $g/c^2$ (Sun grazing: $0.87''$, half the GR value), and ceiling-to-floor clocks show a redshift $\Delta\nu/\nu = -gh/c^2$; locate the boundary of the principle by introducing two test particles whose convergent free-fall tracks tidal inhomogeneity (⭐) ([[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]], [[Def - Uniformly Accelerated Observer (Hyperbolic Motion)]], [[Thm - Gravitational Redshift]], [[Thm - Light Deflection]]).

- [[Ex - Universality of free fall from the equality of masses]] — show that $m_G = m_I$ implies trajectories independent of mass and composition (Newton's $m_I\ddot{\vec r} = m_G\vec g$ collapses to $\ddot{\vec r} = \vec g$), contrast with electromagnetism (Lorentz force has $\ddot{\vec r} = (q/m)\vec E$, trajectory depends on $q/m$), state the Eötvös experimental bound ($10^{-13}$ from torsion balances, MICROSCOPE, lunar laser), and deduce the geometric content: a mass-independent trajectory must be a *geodesic* of an unknown metric; verify this is automatic in the Nordström action because $m_a$ multiplies both kinetic and interaction terms and cancels from the Euler-Lagrange equations (⭐⭐) ([[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]], [[Def - Nordström's Scalar Theory of Gravity]], [[Riemannian Geometry II — Geodesics, the Exponential Map, and Variational Principles]]).

- [[Ex - Tidal forces and the limits of the equivalence principle]] — compute the Newtonian tidal tensor $E_{ij} = \partial_i\partial_j\Phi = (GM/r^3)(\delta_{ij} - 3x_i x_j/r^2)$ with eigenvalues $(+2, -1, -1)\cdot GM/r^3$ (prolate stretching toward source), show $\text{tr}(E) = \nabla^2\Phi = 0$ in vacuum (always stretch-and-compress, never uniform contraction), demonstrate that in a uniformly accelerated frame in empty space $\ddot{\vec\xi} = 0$ for any pair while in a real field $\ddot{\vec\xi} = -E\cdot\vec\xi \neq 0$ — so the equivalence principle fails over a region containing two separated particles; evaluate magnitudes for Earth ($\sim 0.5\,\mu g$ over an astronaut's body in low orbit, the microgravity floor); identify the Newtonian tidal tensor as $R^i{}_{00j}$ — a specific component of the Riemann curvature tensor — making "curvature = tidal" precise (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]], [[Def - Uniformly Accelerated Observer (Hyperbolic Motion)]], [[Riemannian Geometry III — Riemann Curvature and Topology]], [[General Relativity I — Einstein's Equations and Schwarzschild]]).
