## §25.3 Gravitational Redshift and the Road to General Relativity — Exercises

The exercises of §25.3 take the gravitational redshift through its full arc: from quantitative derivation (the equivalence principle applied to accelerated-frame Doppler bookkeeping), to structural use (Schild's argument forcing the metric to be position-dependent), to everyday application (GPS clock corrections, the one technology that fails without relativistic gravity). The first exercise is the calculation: three independent derivations — accelerated-frame Doppler, photon energy conservation, exact accelerated-clock period — all yielding $\Delta\nu/\nu = -\Delta\Phi/c^2$, with the Pound-Rebka tower experiment (1960, ${}^{57}\mathrm{Fe}$ Mössbauer gamma rays over $22.5\,\mathrm{m}$) the laboratory verification. The second is the structural payoff: Schild's argument, the cleanest impossibility proof in physics, showing that the redshift in a static field contradicts the Minkowski metric — and the only escape is to let the metric become a position-dependent field $g_{\mu\nu}(x)$, which is general relativity. The third is engineering practice: the GPS satellite clock runs fast by $+38.6\,\mu\mathrm{s/day}$ (gravitational redshift dominates kinematic time dilation by a factor of $\sim 6$, with the two effects having opposite signs), is mis-tuned by $-4.4647\times 10^{-10}$ at the factory to compensate, and would accumulate $\sim 12\,\mathrm{km/day}$ of position error if relativity were ignored — exceeding the $10\,\mathrm{m}$ specification within one minute. The arc is complete: the redshift is the *measurement* that the equivalence principle is real, the *lever* that pries spacetime loose from flatness, and the *daily-life consequence* that demonstrates general relativity is not theoretical. Together with the §25.2 exercises on universality and tidal forces, §25.3 closes the chapter and the entire special-relativity sequence by stating exactly where the flat-metric edifice ends and the curved-metric theory begins.

- [[Ex - Gravitational redshift from the equivalence principle]] — derive the formula $\Delta t = \Delta t'/(1 + \gamma x_{\mathrm{em}}/c^2)$ three independent ways (accelerated-frame Doppler bookkeeping, photon energy conservation, exact accelerated-clock period from hyperbolic motion), verify they all give $\Delta\nu/\nu = -\Delta\Phi/c^2$; evaluate for Pound-Rebka ($22.5\,\mathrm{m}$ Jefferson tower, ${}^{57}\mathrm{Fe}$ at $14.4\,\mathrm{keV}$, predicted shift $-2.45\times 10^{-15}$); identify why the Mössbauer effect is required (suppresses recoil and thermal broadening by $\sim 10^7$, allowing the $10^{-15}$ shift to be resolved); fix the sign convention $\Delta\nu/\nu = -\Delta\Phi/c^2$ (climbing the potential is redshift) (⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Gravitational Redshift]], [[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]], [[Def - Uniformly Accelerated Observer (Hyperbolic Motion)]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]]).

- [[Ex - Why the gravitational redshift forces a position-dependent metric]] — run Schild's argument in full: two successive light signals in a static field are time-translates of each other (staticity), the Minkowski metric is itself time-translation invariant (so the two signals' Minkowski proper-time periods must be equal), but the redshift gives different periods — contradiction; identify which assumption gives way (the equivalence principle is empirically locked at $10^{-13}$, staticity is by hypothesis, the redshift follows; the casualty is "proper time measured by $\eta$"); conclude the metric must be a *field* $g_{\mu\nu}(\vec r)$ with $g_{00}(\vec r) = 1 + 2\Phi(\vec r)/c^2$ in the weak-field limit, accommodating the redshift through altitude-dependent clock rates $d\tau \propto \sqrt{g_{00}}\,dt$; note the argument's striking independence from the photons' worldline detail — the model-independence that makes it airtight (⭐⭐⭐) ([[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]], [[Thm - Gravitational Redshift]], [[Def - Minkowski Space and the Metric]], [[General Relativity I — Einstein's Equations and Schwarzschild]], [[Def - Semi-Riemannian Metric and Signature]]).

- [[Ex - The relativistic clock corrections in GPS]] — compute the two competing relativistic effects on a GPS satellite clock: kinematic time dilation $\Delta\nu/\nu = -v_{\mathrm{sat}}^2/(2c^2) = -8.3\times 10^{-11}$ ($-7.2\,\mu\mathrm{s/day}$, slows the clock) and gravitational redshift $\Delta\nu/\nu = +GM_\oplus(1/R_\oplus - 1/r_{\mathrm{sat}})/c^2 = +5.3\times 10^{-10}$ ($+45.6\,\mu\mathrm{s/day}$, speeds it up); net $+4.45\times 10^{-10}$ ($+38.4\,\mu\mathrm{s/day}$, matching the GPS specification $+38.6\,\mu\mathrm{s/day}$), with the gravitational effect dominating the kinematic by $\sim 6\times$ and the two having opposite signs; verify the satellite-clock factory offset $\Delta f/f = -4.45\times 10^{-10}$ matches the GPS Interface Specification $-4.4647\times 10^{-10}$; compute the uncorrected position error $\sim 12\,\mathrm{km/day}$, exceeding the $10\,\mathrm{m}$ specification within one minute (⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Gravitational Redshift]], [[Def - Time Dilation]], [[Thm - The Equivalence Principle and the Failure of a Flat-Spacetime Gravity]]).
