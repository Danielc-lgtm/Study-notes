## §25.1 Gravity in Minkowski Spacetime and Its Failure — Exercises

The exercises of §25.1 drill the *negative* enterprise of the chapter: showing that the three kinds of relativistic field — scalar, vector, and rank-2 tensor — each fail as a theory of gravity in flat Minkowski space. The unifying technique is variational: each candidate theory is written as a least-action principle, the source is identified by varying with respect to the field, and the resulting field equation is tested against the requirements of a relativistic gravity theory (reduces to Newton; bends light; positive field energy; couples to matter). The scalar theory (Nordström) fails because light has a traceless stress tensor and so does not couple to a scalar field — predicting *no* light deflection, the experimental death of the theory. The vector theory copies electromagnetism with a sign flip and finds the field energy density becomes negative, making the theory radiatively unstable. The tensor theory (Fierz-Pauli, the linearised massless spin-2 field) couples correctly to matter only when promoted to general relativity, in which case the Minkowski background is demoted to an unobservable scaffold. The reusable thread: a relativistic gravity theory's spin is forced by the requirements of universal coupling and positive energy, and only spin-2 — that is, the tensor theory, that is, the linearisation of general relativity — survives.

- [[Ex - Deriving the Nordström field equation]] — vary the action $S = S_{\mathrm{field}} + S_{\mathrm{inter}}$ with $q_a = m_a$ to obtain $\Box\Phi = -4\pi G T/c^2$, recognise the source as the trace $\mathcal{S} = -T/c^2$ via the Dirac-measure rewriting of the worldline integral, and verify the Newtonian limit $\Delta\Phi = 4\pi G\rho$; recover the perfect-fluid trace $T = \varepsilon - 3p$ and explain why the source is the trace and *not* the energy density (only the trace is a Lorentz scalar) (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Nordström's Scalar Theory of Gravity]], [[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]], [[Def - Perfect Fluid]]).

- [[Ex - Why scalar gravity does not bend light]] — show $T^{\mathrm{em}} = T^\mu{}_\mu(\text{EM}) = 0$ for the electromagnetic stress tensor by direct trace computation, hence light produces no Nordström field and feels no scalar gravitational acceleration; conclude that scalar gravity predicts zero deflection of starlight by the Sun, in stark conflict with the 1919 Eddington measurement of $1.75''$, killing scalar gravity observationally; contrast with general relativity which gives the full deflection (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Nordström's Scalar Theory of Gravity]], [[Thm - Light Deflection]], [[Def - The Energy-Momentum Tensor]], [[Special Relativity XXI — The Electromagnetic Field]]).

- [[Ex - The negative field energy of vector gravity]] — perform the substitution $\varepsilon_0 \to -1/(4\pi G)$ in the electromagnetic energy density to obtain $\rho_{\mathrm{grav}} = -(1/8\pi G)(\vec E\cdot\vec E + c^2\vec B\cdot\vec B) < 0$ for the vector-gravity field, argue this makes the theory radiatively unstable (an oscillating mass loses energy *toward* itself), and identify the deep reason: gravity's source is the rank-2 energy-momentum tensor, not a rank-1 current, so the field must be spin-2, not spin-1 (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Vector and Tensor Theories of Gravity]], [[Special Relativity XXII — Maxwell's Equations]], [[Special Relativity XXIII — The Energy-Momentum Tensor and Field Energy]]).
