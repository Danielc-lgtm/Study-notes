## §13.1 Four-Momentum, Mass and Energy — Exercises

The exercises of §13.1 build fluency with the single object on which the whole chapter turns — the four-momentum $P = mU = (E, \mathbf{p})$ — and with the three readings of it: energy $E = \gamma m$ (the time component, relative to an observer), momentum $\mathbf{p} = \gamma m\mathbf{u}$ (the spatial part, relative to an observer), and rest mass $m$ (the Minkowski length, absolute, $P\cdot P = m^2$). The recurring technique is to evaluate the invariant $P\cdot P$ in the most convenient frame — the rest frame for a single particle, the centre-of-momentum frame for a system — and to remember that *length is not additive*, so the invariant mass of a composite system exceeds the sum of its parts' masses whenever they move relative to one another. The unifying observation: energy, momentum, and mass are not three independent quantities with three conservation laws (as Newton had them) but three views of one four-vector, and the apparent paradoxes of relativistic mass all dissolve into the elementary fact that $|P_1 + P_2| \ne |P_1| + |P_2|$.

- [[Ex - Rest energy, kinetic energy and the Newtonian limit]] — verify $E^2 = \mathbf{p}^2 + m^2$ by squaring the four-momentum in the rest frame, expand $E = \gamma m = m + \tfrac12 mu^2 + \cdots$ to recover the rest energy and the Newtonian kinetic energy with its relativistic corrections, and compute the Lorentz factor and kinetic energy of an LHC proton ($7$ TeV) and an ultra-high-energy cosmic ray ($10^{20}$ eV) (⭐) ([[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]], [[Def - Four-Velocity and Four-Acceleration]]).

- [[Ex - The invariant mass of a system of particles]] — show the invariant mass $M^2 = (\sum P_i)\cdot(\sum P_i)$ of a system's total four-momentum is Lorentz invariant, equals the total energy in the centre-of-momentum frame, and strictly exceeds $\sum m_i$ whenever the parts move relative to one another; apply it to two photons (nonzero invariant mass from two null vectors) and to two massive particles, and connect the non-additivity to nuclear binding energy (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]], [[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Thm - Mass-Energy Equivalence]], [[Def - The Four-Momentum of a Photon]]).

- [[Ex - Energy and momentum of a system and the comoving observer]] — show that the energy and momentum of a system are additive, $E = \sum E_a$ and $\mathbf{p} = \sum\mathbf{p}_a$, that a comoving (centre-of-momentum) observer measures zero total three-momentum and total energy equal to the system's invariant mass, and that for an isolated system measured by an inertial observer both the total energy and the total momentum are constant in time (⭐⭐) ([[Thm - Energy and Momentum Relative to an Observer]], [[Thm - Conservation of Four-Momentum]], [[Def - Observer and Local Rest Space]], [[Def - Four-Momentum and Rest Mass]]).
