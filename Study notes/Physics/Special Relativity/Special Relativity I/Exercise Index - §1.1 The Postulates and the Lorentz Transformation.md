## §1.1 The Postulates and the Lorentz Transformation — Exercises

The exercises of §1.1 drill the Lorentz transformation and its kinematic consequences. The boost is derived from first principles (linearity + relativity + constancy of light forces $\gamma = (1 - v^2)^{-1/2}$); length contraction $L = L_0/\gamma$, time dilation $T = \gamma T_0$, and relativity of simultaneity all follow as different facets of the same coordinate transformation; and famous "paradoxes" (ladder-and-barn, train-and-lightbulb) resolve by tracking which simultaneity is being used. The unifying observation: every kinematic effect in special relativity comes from re-reading the same spacetime interval in different inertial frames.

- [[Ex - Deriving the Lorentz transformation from the postulates]] — reconstruct the boost from first principles by imposing the two postulates one at a time: inertia forces linearity, the moving origin forces the form $\gamma(x-vt)$, the relativity principle forces the same $\gamma$ in the inverse and $\gamma$ even in $v$, and the constancy of light fixes $\gamma = (1-v^2)^{-1/2}$ and the temporal law (⭐⭐) ([[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Length contraction]] — transform the worldlines of a rod's endpoints into a frame where it moves, impose that frame's simultaneity on the length measurement, and obtain $L = L_0/\gamma$; then resolve the ladder-and-barn paradox by recognising "fits inside" as a frame-dependent statement about the order of two spacelike-separated events (⭐) ([[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]], [[Def - Classification of Four-Vectors]]).

- [[Ex - Time dilation]] — transform a clock's tick events into a frame where it moves to get $T = \gamma T_0$, confirm via the invariant interval, and explain the abundance of atmospheric muons at sea level by time dilation (ground frame) and length contraction (muon frame), checking the two viewpoints agree (⭐) ([[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - The Spacetime Interval]], [[Thm - Invariance of the Spacetime Interval]]).

- [[Ex - The relativity of simultaneity]] — show events simultaneous in one frame are not in another via the $t' = \gamma(t-vx)$ clause, find the tilted lines of simultaneity on the spacetime diagram, and analyse the train-and-lightbulb gedankenexperiment to see that the disagreement is forced by the constancy of the speed of light (⭐⭐) ([[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]], [[Def - The Spacetime Interval]], [[Def - Classification of Four-Vectors]]).
