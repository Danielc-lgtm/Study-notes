## §1.3 Spacetime Diagrams and the Constancy of c — Exercises

The exercises of §1.3 turn the [[Def - Spacetime Diagram|spacetime diagram]] into a precise, derived tool and read the constancy of $c$ off its geometry. The first derives the boosted axes from the [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation|Lorentz transformation]] — the $ct'$-axis ($x'=0$) and $x'$-axis ($t'=0$) scissoring symmetrically toward the $45^\circ$ light line — and identifies the tilt of the space axis as the relativity of simultaneity. The second justifies the $45^\circ$ light convention and proves it frame-independent (light lines are the boost's eigendirections), with transverse light aberrating in direction but keeping speed $c$. The third proves the speed limit causally: a worldline flatter than $45^\circ$ is spacelike, runs backward in time in some frame, and would permit signalling into one's own past — so every worldline is timelike or null, and the $45^\circ$ light cone defines an invariant causal structure. The unifying observation: the light cone, fixed at $45^\circ$ in every frame, is the frame-independent scaffolding from which simultaneity, causality, and the speed limit all follow.

- [[Ex - The boosted axes scissor toward the light ray]] (⭐⭐) — derive the $S'$ axes from the boost ($ct'$-axis slope $1/v$, $x'$-axis slope $v$), show they scissor symmetrically toward the $45^\circ$ line (the second postulate) and are pseudo-orthogonal (angles $\alpha$ and $\pi/2-\alpha$), and read the relativity of simultaneity off the tilted $x'$-axis; warning about Euclidean ruler-measurements on a Minkowski diagram ([[Def - Spacetime Diagram]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Light travels at 45 degrees in every frame]] (⭐) — justify plotting $ct$ vertically so light has slope $1$, prove $x=\pm t \Rightarrow x'=\pm t'$ (light at $45^\circ$ in every frame, the boost's eigendirections), describe the frame-independent light cone, and show transverse light keeps speed $c$ while its direction aberrates by $\tan\theta' = \gamma v$ ([[Def - Spacetime Diagram]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]]).

- [[Ex - Why nothing can be drawn flatter than 45 degrees]] (⭐⭐) — show a worldline's slope is $c/u$ so massive worldlines are steeper than $45^\circ$, prove a superluminal (spacelike) signal runs backward in time in some frame via $\Delta t' = \gamma\Delta t(1-vu)$, build the tachyonic anti-telephone causal paradox to forbid faster-than-light motion, and establish the invariant future/past/elsewhere partition from the frame-independence of the cone ([[Def - Spacetime Diagram]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]]).
