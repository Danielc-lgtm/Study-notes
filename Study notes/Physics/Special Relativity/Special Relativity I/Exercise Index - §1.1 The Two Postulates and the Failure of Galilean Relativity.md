## §1.1 The Two Postulates and the Failure of Galilean Relativity — Exercises

The exercises of §1.1 drill the conceptual foundations: what the two postulates say, how the constancy of light breaks the [[Def - Galilean Spacetime and Its Failure|Galilean transformation]], and how an inertial coordinate system is *operationally* built. The low-speed exercise pins down that the Galilean transformation is the $c \to \infty$ limit and that the relativity of simultaneity lives in a single term $\frac{v}{c^2}x$; the reciprocity exercise shows that the evenness of the Lorentz factor follows from isotropy and the relativity principle *before* light is invoked; and the synchronisation exercise constructs the coordinates from a clock and a flashlight, exposing simultaneity as a frame-internal convention. The unifying lesson: every relativistic surprise traces to abandoning absolute time, and absolute time was an assumption ($t' = t$) hiding in plain sight.

- [[Ex - Recovering the Galilean transformation in the low-speed limit]] (⭐) — expand $\gamma = 1 + \tfrac12\beta^2 + \cdots$ and take $c \to \infty$ to recover $x' = x - vt$, $t' = t$; identify the position-dependent clock term $\frac{v}{c^2}x$ as the relativity of simultaneity and the only thing that vanishes, and estimate the negligible corrections for a train ([[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Galilean Spacetime and Its Failure]]).

- [[Ex - Reciprocity and the evenness of the Lorentz factor]] (⭐⭐) — prove $\gamma_v = \gamma_{-v}$ two independent ways (isotropy: $\gamma$ depends only on $v^2$; reciprocity: the reflected-frame construction), discard the spurious reflection root by continuity to the identity, and show that without evenness the forward and inverse boosts would carry different scales and break the principle of relativity ([[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]], [[Thm - Uniqueness of the Lorentz Transformation from the Postulates]]).

- [[Ex - The operational synchronisation of clocks]] (⭐⭐) — build inertial coordinates with a clock and light by the radar method (time $\tfrac12(t_1+t_2)$, position $\tfrac12 c(t_2-t_1)$), define Einstein synchronisation, show two observers slice simultaneity differently (the tilted $x'$-axis), and verify the radar simultaneity line matches the Lorentz transformation's $t'=0$ locus ([[Def - Inertial Frame and the Postulates of Special Relativity]], [[Def - The Lorentz Transformation]], [[Def - Spacetime Diagram]]).
