## §1.3 Poisson Brackets and Conservation Laws — Exercises

The exercises in this section drill **the Poisson algebra structure on $C^\infty(M)$** and its use in identifying and propagating conserved quantities. The unifying theme is **conservation laws as Poisson-commuting functions with the Hamiltonian**, with the Lie-algebra structure of the bracket producing new conserved quantities from old ones (Jacobi) and revealing the **moment map** as the systematic mechanism for symmetry-induced conservation. The canonical example is angular momentum on $T^*\mathbb{R}^3$, whose components form the $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ Lie algebra under the Poisson bracket — the classical-mechanical realization of the rotation algebra, and the classical limit of the quantum angular momentum algebra.

- [[Ex - Poisson Bracket of Angular Momentum Components]] (⭐⭐) — compute $\{L^i, L^j\} = \epsilon^{ijk}L^k$ on $T^*\mathbb{R}^3$ from canonical brackets, identify $L^2$ as a Casimir (Poisson-commuting with each $L^i$), conclude conservation under rotationally invariant Hamiltonians ([[Def - Poisson Bracket]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Vector Field]]).

- [[Ex - Pendulum on a Sphere (Symplectic Treatment)]] (⭐⭐) — also illustrates conservation laws: explicit verification of $\{p_\phi, H\} = 0$ for the rotational symmetry of the spherical pendulum, leading to reduction to a 1D problem ([[Def - Poisson Bracket]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Function]]).

- [[Ex - Jacobi's Principle for a Particle in a Central Potential]] (⭐⭐⭐) — central potential has both energy and angular momentum conserved; use both to derive Binet's equation for the orbit and recover Kepler conic sections; demonstrates the symplectic-reduction picture in concrete physical context ([[Def - Poisson Bracket]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Function]], [[Def - Lagrangian Submanifold]]).
