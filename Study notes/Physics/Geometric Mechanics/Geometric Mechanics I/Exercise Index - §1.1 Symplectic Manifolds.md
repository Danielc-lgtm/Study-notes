## §1.1 Symplectic Manifolds — Exercises

The exercises in this section drill the foundational notions of symplectic geometry: symplectic vector spaces and manifolds, the canonical structure on cotangent bundles, Lagrangian submanifolds, and Darboux's theorem on local triviality. The unifying theme is **closed nondegenerate 2-forms as the geometric arena for Hamiltonian dynamics**: every exercise is either an explicit computation in canonical coordinates (verifying the abstract definitions concretely) or a structural analysis showing how the closedness and nondegeneracy axioms force key consequences (Liouville volume, Lagrangian-submanifold structure, no local invariants). For the elementary calibration, the harmonic oscillator is the universal worked example; for the constrained case, the pendulum on a sphere demonstrates how symmetry reduction works concretely.

- [[Ex - Harmonic Oscillator in Phase Space]] (⭐) — set up the canonical symplectic form on $T^*\mathbb{R}$, derive Hamiltonian vector field and Hamilton's equations, solve explicitly, compute symplectic area $\oint p\,dq = 2\pi E/\omega_0$ ([[Def - The Canonical Symplectic Form on a Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Vector Field]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Function]]).

- [[Ex - Pendulum on a Sphere (Symplectic Treatment)]] (⭐⭐) — Lagrangian and Hamiltonian on $T^*S^2$, identify conserved angular momentum $p_\phi$ via rotational symmetry, reduce to 1D effective potential problem ([[Def - The Canonical Symplectic Form on a Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Hamiltonian Function]], [[Def - Poisson Bracket]], [[Def - The Legendre Transform]]).

- [[Ex - The Lagrange Submanifold of a Magnetic Field]] (⭐⭐⭐) — graph of the magnetic vector potential $A$ in $T^*\mathbb{R}^3$ is Lagrangian for canonical $\omega_0$ iff $F = dA = 0$; for the twisted form $\omega = \omega_0 + e\pi^*F$ it is always Lagrangian; connects symplectic geometry to gauge theory ([[Def - Lagrangian Submanifold]], [[Def - The Canonical Symplectic Form on a Cotangent Bundle]], [[Def - Closed and Exact Forms]]).
