## Architecture and convention decisions

1. The presentation is independently derived: existing vault pages supply reusable facts, filenames, frontmatter, and link structure, but not prose, pedagogical order, or proof architecture.
2. The series begins with the abelian/vector-bundle prototype, then separates bundle topology from connection geometry, before moving to characteristic forms and field theory. Analysis and spin geometry precede Seiberg–Witten theory; topology and Donaldson theory close the series.
3. Every source definition, named result, worked calculation, and substantive exercise/example is assigned either an atomic page or an explicit treatment on a chapter page. Routine background already proved elsewhere is recalled precisely and linked rather than duplicated wholesale.
4. Connection conventions will be declared per chapter. Matrix-valued curvature uses $F_A=dA+A\wedge A$; right principal actions use the corresponding $\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}$ equivariance. Physics normalizations and Riemannian/Lorentzian Hodge signs will never be mixed implicitly.
5. The current Gauge Theory IV instanton material will move to Gauge Theory V when IV becomes the Chern–Weil chapter; incoming links will be audited and updated before the rename.
